## 3: Face it (third person)

### What are we trying to create or experience?

Wendell and the Six Faces are turning the provisional design ontology from a strong, manually usable corpus into a repo-local alpha that can guide one real game-design cycle, survive adversarial comprehension tests, preserve six distinct lenses, and work from a clean session without this conversation.

### What is life like now?

`ontology/manifest.yaml`, `ontology/v0.1/QUICKSTART.md`, the design-ontology skill, the council skill, binding decisions, and initial tests exist. The shipping-readiness report predates much of that work. There are no explicit Shaman–Sage agent packages, no semantic fixture suite, no clean-session proof, and no completed dogfood cycle under the current package.

## 2: Talk to it (second person)

### Round 1

Wendell authorized the alpha-shipping process and asked the six Game Masters to guide it. The reach test produced no further question: the smallest safe dogfood task and release gates can be resolved from the ontology and council record.

### What the dialogue found

- **What will that get us?** Confidence grounded in use rather than corpus size; a repeatable path from design question to implementation brief.
- **How does it feel to live here now?** The ontology stops being a body of work waiting for permission to be finished and becomes a tool the designer can play with.
- **What would have to be true for someone to feel this way?** One small design cycle must produce an observable improvement, preserve refusal and dissent, and end without requiring ontology completion.
- **What reservations do we have?** Packaging can mimic readiness; tests can become word searches; six faces can collapse into one voice; clean-session success can secretly depend on this conversation.

### The test, and what it showed

Stage 0's cheap test is whether the current package can produce a bounded Roadblock Encounter specification that keeps observation, hypothesis, permission, outcome, and completion distinct without requiring real player data. Its result is recorded in `spec.md` and the first dogfood artifact.
