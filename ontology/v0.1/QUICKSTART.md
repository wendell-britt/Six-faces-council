# Design Ontology v0.1 — Council Quickstart

This is the compact entry point for the Six Faces council when it designs or builds games. The manifest controls which deeper files to load. Historical records preserve provenance but do not outrank current sources or binding decisions.

## Constitutional center

1. **The Game Master is also a player.** Facilitation must preserve the Game Master's pleasure, agency, discovery, and replenishment.
2. **The game designer is also a player.** The act of designing should itself be playable and intrinsically rewarding.
3. **Begin with authentic pleasure; translate outward later.** If it is fun for Wendell, it is a valid first signal for people in his psychographic neighborhood. Wider transfer is tested in later drafts.
4. **The body gets a vote.** Somatic evidence participates in design judgment without becoming a single correct interpretation.
5. **Mechanics claim reality.** What a game tracks, rewards, gates, remembers, permits, and changes is what it treats as real.
6. **Development changes possibility.** It is contextual change within a named line, not whole-person rank.
7. **Relationship can create capability.** Consensual agency may combine into possibilities no participant has alone.
8. **Play precedes complete understanding.** Design conditions for discovery; do not require conceptual mastery before participation.
9. **Mystery rewards inquiry.** Preserve inferability, multiple interpretations, and optional explanation.
10. **Players author personal meaning.** The system may seed conditions and offer interpretations; it does not own a player's inward truth.
11. **Preserve lineage.** The origin, path, revisions, actions, relationships, and consequences remain part of the object.
12. **The helper remains inside the field of care.** Helping must not require self-erasure or limitless obligation.
13. **A design tool should practice what it teaches.** Tools may themselves be games that remediate the designer's predictable shadows.
14. **Composting is valid completion.** A possibility may be honored, learned from, and released without implementation.
15. **Motivation serves meaning; it does not prove it.** Engagement systems are judged by the experience and capacity they support.

The fifteen are the current provisional constitution. The original eight remain the provenance-bearing seed.

## Depth over flatland

Binding since 2026-10-24 (`binding_decisions.depth_over_flatland`): every play surface is an inviting image or an explorable space, never a grid, gauge or panel. Data shows as world state (size, light, places grown, creatures living there), never as a number on the play surface. Haze, scale and light lead inward; the world grows as inner work is done; every scene plays in 2D and 3D; every named inspiration gets real reference images before a mood still. The Diplomat's shadow is flatland: "Our job is to bring depth back into green" (Wendell).

## Required order of inquiry

Reconstruct **what exists, where it operates, and when it changes** before deciding how it should work. Do not return questions to Wendell when sources, evidence, or bounded council deliberation can answer them. Escalate only irreducible personal meaning, values, consent, money, canonical prose, or owner authority.

## AQAL attention

For a major game proposal, declare:

- **UL / I:** intended inner experience and player-authored meaning;
- **UR / It:** behavior, somatic expression, and observable action;
- **LL / We:** relationship, culture, consent, and shared meaning;
- **LR / Its:** rules, roles, resources, institutions, data, and persistent consequences;
- developmental lines made consequential;
- states invited but not converted into traits;
- types favored or burdened;
- exclusions, risks, evidence, rest, refusal, and exit.

One game does not have to mechanize the whole ontology. It must declare what it foregrounds, supports implicitly, and leaves outside scope.

## Emotional Alchemy boundaries

- The five **Basic Moves** are Wake Up, Open Up, Clean Up, Grow Up, and Show Up.
- **WAVE** names only the Open Up breathing practice.
- Wake → Clean → Grow → Show is the **within-channel progression**.
- The fifteen canonical moves are five Transcend, five Generative, and five Control moves.
- Legacy lowercase Epiphany `translate`/`transcend` values use a different grammar and must remain namespaced.
- Player-reported satisfaction governs self-directed inner validity.
- In allyship, satisfaction does not establish helpfulness to another person; consent, relational attestation, and relevant external consequences remain necessary.

## Trust, agency, and relationship

Trust is modeled through **capability** and **integrity** within a relationship and domain. Credentials or badges speak to demonstrated capabilities; they never become whole-person rank.

Access to an ally is not command. Relationship progression requires agency, consent, kept commitments, demonstrated capability, and the possibility of refusal. The player may gain access before earning permission to call on every move.

## Lifecycle grammar

> **Moves resolve; encounters end; quests complete; arcs conclude.**

None of those alone proves satisfaction, transformation, development, trust, or worth. Rest, refusal, retreat, witnessing, interruption, and no-shift may validly end participation or an encounter while leaving a quest incomplete.

## Representations and findings

- The player lives the tension; a **Tension Map** is a correctable representation.
- `TensionConfiguration` is the formal dissatisfied configuration.
- `TensionSnapshot` records exactly what was persisted.
- `TransformationTrace` preserves the path without requiring success.
- A `DerivedMovementProposal` may guide a `MovementRoutingPlan`; neither proves that movement occurred.
- Actual play may support a `MovementFinding`: shift, no-shift, uncertainty, reinterpretation, refusal, or unassessed.
- Structured extraction is not anonymization. Collection, persistence, derivation, adaptation, sharing, analytics, model improvement, correction, deletion, and revocation are separate permission purposes.

## Recoverability

The Yellow Brick Road is mythic reassurance, not infallibility:

> **No path has to be wasted.**

A wrong move can remain wrong while becoming information, repair, story, or another road. Divergence does not prove hidden desire. A revised destination does not retroactively fulfill the prior one. Some blockers may remain unresolved indefinitely while continuing to constrain a named route.

## Roadblocks and intention

A roadblock is first a scoped observation: something prevents a named action, route, capacity, or destination under declared conditions. Its classification, causal account, meaning, response, and outcome are separate records.

> **A roadblock may reveal a mismatch among intention, method, conditions, and capacity; it does not prove misaligned intention.**

AI may propose an `IntentionAlignmentHypothesis` only against a declared intention and relevant evidence. It must not psychologize a technical defect, infer hidden desire, or treat refusal as confirmation. At the technical layer, metabolization converts failure into repair, verification, safer routing, reusable learning, or provenance; it does not require emotional processing. A blocker may instead be rested, accommodated, rerouted, left unresolved, abandoned, or composted.

## Session depth, performance, and disclosure

Gameplay performance is real design material: performed practices can create capacity, relationship, consequence, and good stories. Record the named performance without treating it as proof of an unseen inner state.

Answer length, intimate specificity, fear naming, and mask identification do not establish genuineness, depth, readiness, transformation, or deftness. Use a correctable `ResponseAffordanceProfile` to describe what the supplied material can support. More relevant material may permit more specific tailoring, but it must not buy better baseline craft, core progress, power, credentials, or social standing.

Keep artifact specificity, richness, quality, and fit separate. **Intimate disclosure is not progression currency.** Offer optional follow-up, presets, fictionalized or symbolic input, player selection, and private-local routes where possible. The system must never weaken an artifact to pressure disclosure. Pacing adaptation remains visible, reversible, and player-correctable.

## BAR scope

A `BAR` is an intentionally captured, portable kernel of potential with stable identity and provenance. Every BAR has provenance; not every provenance-bearing object is a BAR. A note, artifact, quest, rule, specification, log, or decision receives BAR identity only when it is explicitly captured as a reusable kernel.

Charge is optional. A `ChargedBAR` is one specialization or role, alongside kernels originating in requests, observations, opportunities, design ideas, decisions, rules, lore, imported artifacts, or forks.

Keep the kernel distinct from its `BARExpression`. A BAR may bloom as a quest, rule, lore, feature specification, plan, norm, branch, or artifact without becoming reducible to that expression. Capture creates lineage, not implementation debt. Portability carries a permission policy; it never means unrestricted reuse.

A game asset carries its source and licence; only own work and CC0 ship in a page, and AI concept art is a reference until rebuilt (a painted-over capture is a rebuild only when a person or our code paints it).

## Composting

Composting is a governed lifecycle disposition that ends a named active obligation. It does not require a new artifact, lesson, transformation, fuel, later reuse, or recovered value.

> **Compost may nourish future growth, but it does not owe the garden a harvest.**

Keep `CompostingDisposition`, optional `CompostResidue`, and any later `RegenerationEvent` separate. After composting, active backlog pressure, overdue status, progress penalties, required review, and automatic completion prompts stop. Reactivation requires a new explicit decision.

Composting is not deletion, archival, supersession, or completion of the original quest. Preserve only the minimum permitted lineage; privacy or deletion rights may require a tombstone or no recoverable content. Later reuse needs its own authority and provenance.

## Creative reuse and personal meaning

BARS may frame AI remixing of a creator's archive as creative composting and “not necromancy.” That is a `ProjectMeaningFrame`, not a required interpretation.

> **The project may name its ritual; it may not name the player's experience for them.**

A player or creator may find the reuse generative, uncanny, inappropriate, exploitative, sacred, playful, grief-laden, “not mine,” or unresolved. Keep personal meaning, source authorship, generated authorship, attribution, identity or likeness, reuse permission, and shared-world consequence separate.

A `DerivedVoice` is a character informed by a permitted corpus; it is not the source person, past self, or archived speaker. “Not mine” is a scoped correction that may reject meaning, authorship, attribution, identity, aesthetics, relationship, or permission. It must never be treated as resistance. Archive-based adaptation remains optional, with non-AI and non-reuse routes preserving the core game and quality floor.

## Privacy and consent authority

Consent is situated authorization for an operation now. A `ConsentRecord` is evidence of a past grant, refusal, or condition; it is not permanent proof of present willingness. `PermissionGrant` binds subject, data category, purpose, operation, processor, audience, context, duration, retention, descendant policy, correction, revocation, and deletion. Omitted purposes are not granted.

`PermissionEnvelope` is the effective intersection of applicable grants, refusals, boundaries, source restrictions, and duties. It travels through `DerivationRecord` lineage to snapshots, psychographs, inferences, world material, BARs, council syntheses, analytics, exports, caches, and model artifacts. Structured extraction, aggregation, or generation does not automatically anonymize or release a derivative.

Correction changes or contests a claim. Revocation ends future authority for a purpose. Deletion disposes of named content and descendants; it is not composting, archival, hiding, or account deactivation. Propagate each through reachable lineage, preserve only minimum permitted residual obligations, and report irreversible disclosures or technical limits honestly. Optional sensitive uses must not buy baseline quality, progress, power, credentials, or belonging.

## Evidence authority

- Player report establishes reported meaning, experience, satisfaction, or intention; it does not establish external effect.
- Somatic report establishes reported sensation; it does not establish one compulsory interpretation.
- Behavioral observation establishes behavior under observed conditions; it does not establish motive or global capacity.
- Relational attestation establishes another agent's experience; it does not establish the first agent's inward truth.
- System trace establishes recorded transitions; it does not establish why they mattered.
- Artifact establishes that something was produced; it does not establish transformation.
- AI inference and council synthesis are proposals; they do not establish user belief, consent, or empirical proof.

## Council use

Each face applies its bounded lens from `council/faces.yaml` while honoring this ontology. A shared archetypal name does not merge altitude, runtime role, player identity, review discipline, or council lens. Sage synthesizes and preserves dissent; Sage does not decide. Council convergence increases interpretive support, not empirical certainty.
