# Design Ontology — Framework Translation, Round 1

**Owner:** Wendell Britt
**Status:** Provisional translation; not constitutional ratification
**Source:** `design-ontology-pattern-extraction-round-1.md`

## Translation rule

Each framework is a lens with a bounded job. No framework may claim the whole design, settle every tension, or overwrite the source aesthetic.

The ontology is integral; an individual game is selectively expressive. Every design should declare what it makes mechanically real, what it carries through narrative or social practice, what it excludes, and what those choices may obscure.

---

## 1. AQAL translation

### What AQAL clarifies

AQAL prevents a design from confusing one perspective on development with the whole event.

| Candidate pattern | UL — interior | UR — enacted | LL — relational/cultural | LR — systemic |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Development as changed possibility | Felt choice, meaning, identity, charge | Demonstrated action, restraint, skill | Trust, shared meaning, coordination | Permissions, roles, access, persistent consequences |
| Relationship as causal medium | Felt connection or distance | Help, repair, refusal, kept promise | Norms, consent, alliance, mutual recognition | Relationship rules, governance, group capacities |
| Mystery and interpretation | Intuition, hypothesis, felt resonance | Testing, prediction, action | Shared readings and disagreement | Evidence distribution, hidden-state design, revelation structure |
| Performance as cultivation | Identification may follow action | Rehearsed behavior and transfer | Witnessing, response, negotiated meaning | Incentives, repetition, rewards, safeguards |
| Adaptive otherness | Personal resonance and sovereignty | Player choices and somatic response | Encounter with distinct agents | Data use, world persistence, access controls |
| Developmental provenance | Remembered intention and interpretation | Recorded choices and artifacts | Witnessed lineage and contested accounts | Traceability, state history, BAR disposition |

AQAL also keeps developmental lines independent. Emotional, cognitive, moral, relational, somatic, strategic, creative, and helping capacities may develop unevenly. A level describes demonstrated complexity within a line and context, never the total worth or maturity of a person.

States are temporary conditions a design may invite. Types are patterned differences in orientation or playstyle. Neither should be mistaken for developmental level.

### What AQAL cannot decide

- which experience should be fun;
- which line a particular game should foreground;
- what an inner event means to the player;
- whether a mechanic is elegant or alive;
- whether one interpretation is emotionally satisfying;
- whether comprehensive coverage is worth the cost to playability.

### AQAL requirement

Every design produces an **integral declaration of mechanical attention**:

- foregrounded perspectives and lines;
- narratively or socially supported perspectives and lines;
- intentionally unmodeled dimensions;
- types favored or burdened;
- states invited or discouraged;
- complexity demanded for entry and mastery;
- evidence by which intended and actual experience will be compared.

## 2. Emotional Alchemy translation

### What Emotional Alchemy clarifies

Emotional Alchemy explains how charge can become awareness, interpretation, capacity, action, relationship, or artifact without requiring the feeling to disappear.

It supports:

- transformation as a playable process rather than a narrative assertion;
- internally satisfying movement as primary evidence for self-directed change;
- performance before identification, because an enacted form may open later meaning;
- somatic information as part of the evidence field;
- differentiated results: insight, action, repair, boundary, refusal, creation, rest, or compost;
- provenance from original charge through process to outcome.

### What Emotional Alchemy cannot decide

- whether another person consents to receive the result;
- whether an intervention is effective or safe for someone else;
- whether the game’s external economy is sustainable;
- whether a satisfying transformation transfers across contexts;
- which interpretation of charge is metaphysically true;
- whether every play experience should seek transformation.

### EA evidence rule

For self-directed play, first-person satisfaction establishes transformation validity. Lack of change is data, not failure.

For allyship, transformation validity remains distinct from application fitness. The affected person’s agency and the observable consequences constrain whether the capacity is appropriate here.

### EA guardrail

The game may invite performance of an alchemical move before the player fully possesses the capacity. It must not prescribe one correct emotional arc or turn performed vulnerability into compulsory currency.

## 3. Octalysis translation

### What Octalysis clarifies

Octalysis identifies why a player might enter, repeat, intensify, or abandon a designed activity. It can inspect whether motivation supports the intended aesthetic or quietly replaces it.

The current pattern map aligns most strongly with:

- **Epic Meaning and Calling:** stewardship, allyship, contribution, and consequential world participation;
- **Development and Accomplishment:** changed capability and visible lineage of growth;
- **Empowerment of Creativity and Feedback:** enacted intuition, interpretive testing, and player-authored meaning;
- **Ownership and Possession:** persistent artifacts, authored seeds, and stewardship—without converting allies into property;
- **Social Influence and Relatedness:** trust, coordination, shared interpretation, and collective capacity;
- **Unpredictability and Curiosity:** coherent mystery, hidden patterns, and uncanny relevance.

Scarcity and Loss/Avoidance may create stakes but require scrutiny, especially where vulnerability, access, or relationship could be coerced.

### What Octalysis cannot decide

- whether engagement is meaningful;
- whether a transformation is satisfying;
- whether a relationship is consensual;
- whether a mystery is epistemically fair;
- whether a metric represents what it claims;
- whether more participation is better for the player or helper.

### Octalysis requirement

For each motivational mechanism, state:

- the intended aesthetic or developmental function;
- the behavior it rewards in practice;
- the likely optimization pattern;
- who benefits and who bears the cost;
- what would indicate proxy capture;
- how the player can rest, refuse, or leave.

Motivation remains subordinate to meaning.

## 4. Somatic verification translation

### What somatics clarifies

Somatic verification lets the body participate in design knowledge before, during, and after explicit interpretation.

It supports:

- enacted intuition: action before complete conceptual certainty;
- recognition of attraction, resistance, enclosure, invitation, activation, and relief;
- comparison between intended and actual emotional movement;
- detection of designs that are conceptually elegant but experientially dead;
- distinguishing productive tension from depletion or coercion;
- allowing unchanged or ambiguous bodily response to remain evidence.

### What somatics cannot decide

- the single correct meaning of a sensation;
- whether an external claim is factually true;
- whether another person experienced benefit or harm;
- whether discomfort is always danger or always growth;
- whether one person’s embodied response should govern all users.

### Somatic evidence rule

Record sensation and change separately from interpretation. Ask what was noticed, what shifted, what action became available, and which meanings remain plausible.

The council’s useful distinction becomes a design probe:

> Does recognition feel enclosing, or does it feel like an invitation onward?

## 5. BAR lifecycle translation

### What BAR clarifies

BAR gives possibilities and evidence a life cycle so that capture does not become permanent obligation.

It can preserve:

- the original seed or charge;
- provenance across interpretations, actions, relationships, and artifacts;
- differences between unresolved, developing, active, reference, delegated, completed, and composted material;
- lineage even when a possibility is merged, transformed, or released;
- the legitimacy of good-enough completion and deliberate non-implementation.

### What BAR cannot decide

- whether an idea is aesthetically alive;
- whether the body consents to continued work;
- whether the current steward is the right person;
- whether a possibility should become a mechanic;
- whether retaining provenance justifies retaining sensitive data.

### BAR requirement

Every captured item needs enough context to answer:

- where it came from;
- what kind of thing it is;
- what evidence supports it;
- who may interpret or act on it;
- what it currently asks, if anything;
- how it can be revised, handed off, completed, rested, or composted.

## 6. Equivalences and near-equivalences

The following concepts overlap enough to relate but should not be collapsed without care:

- **changed possibility / affordance change / development:** same central pattern viewed through design, action, and developmental language;
- **lineage / provenance / persistent trace:** all connect origin to result, but provenance also includes evidence, custody, and interpretation;
- **adaptive otherness / relational agency:** both prevent reduction to a player model, while otherness also applies to worlds and systems;
- **latent commitment / enacted intuition:** intuition may seed a commitment, but a commitment can also be deliberate and explicit;
- **proxy capture / metric colonization / reification:** related failures; proxy capture is the preferred broad term, while reification names mistaking representation for reality and metric colonization names the spread of measurement logic;
- **transformation validity / satisfying action:** transformation validity concerns inner evidence; satisfying action is one possible expression or test;
- **capability credential / badge:** a badge is one representation of demonstrated, situated capability, not the capability itself.

---

## 7. Tensions that must remain distinct

- First-person satisfaction cannot be replaced by external approval; external impact cannot be excused by inner satisfaction.
- Performance need not prove authentic identity; repeated rewarded performance can still become empty optimization.
- Mystery should resist premature explanation; hidden systems still require epistemic fairness and governance.
- Adaptation can create resonance; player data and inner imagery remain consent-sensitive.
- Relationship can create shared capacity; distinct agents retain refusal and independent growth.
- Integral awareness seeks broad perspective; individual designs require disciplined selection.
- Development creates new possibility; rest and unchanged experience remain legitimate.

---

## 8. Candidate revisions before philosophy drafting

1. Replace any blanket warning against “performing the metric” with the narrower concept of **proxy capture**.
2. State that performance can precede identity, understanding, or stable capacity.
3. Treat integral coverage as a declaration and audit discipline, not a demand that every game mechanize everything.
4. Make provenance a core bridge across Go, BAR, Emotional Alchemy, trust, and adaptive systems.
5. Treat the Game Master’s and designer’s play as a source of world material and evidence, not only a labor-protection concern.
6. Preserve “development changes possibility” as the shared definition across frameworks.
7. Keep inner meaning, relational consent, enacted behavior, and systemic consequence as distinct evidence authorities.

## 9. Readiness for the design-philosophy draft

The material is ready for a first design-philosophy draft because:

- every initial specimen has contributed at least one reusable pattern;
- recurring patterns now appear across multiple sources;
- framework responsibilities and limits have been differentiated;
- major tensions are named without forced resolution;
- the council has tested two central propositions;
- recent over-specification has been corrected with a stopping rule.

The next draft should articulate a compact constitutional layer, followed by provisional commitments, design questions, and verification rules. It should not yet formalize the complete ontology schema or perform active/reference/compost governance.
