# Design Ontology — Cross-Specimen Pattern Extraction, Round 1

**Owner:** Wendell Britt
**Status:** Provisional pattern map; not yet constitutional ratification or ontology schema
**Inputs:** Psychograph seed; Pokémon; Monster Rancher disc generation; *Inception*; Go; *Dungeon Crawler Carl*; two six–Game Master council consultations

## Purpose

This document extracts recurring design patterns after every initial aesthetic specimen has received a dialogical pass. It remains upstream of framework translation, design-philosophy drafting, formal ontology modeling, council governance, and playable verification.

It does not require every game to implement every pattern.

---

## 1. Central design movement

Across the five specimens, Wendell is drawn toward a recurring movement:

> **A partially hidden or intuitive pattern enters play, gains meaning through relationship and consequential action, and becomes a developed capacity whose lineage remains visible.**

This movement links mystery, development, relationship, performance, and provenance. It also explains why mere accumulation, decorative symbolism, arbitrary surprise, and engagement without meaning feel insufficient.

---

## 2. Recurring aesthetic forces

### 2.1 Development as changed possibility

Development is real when prior play changes what can be perceived, chosen, sustained, coordinated, repaired, refused, or created.

It should not be confused with:

- replacement by a stronger asset;
- an accumulating score without changed affordance;
- a global ranking of the player or character;
- an authorial assertion that growth occurred.

Development may be intrinsic, relational, interpretive, embodied, narrative, configurational, collective, or systemic. These remain distinct lines.

### 2.2 Relationship as a causal medium

Relationship does not merely decorate progression. It changes capability and makes some stories possible.

Agents retain separateness. Access is not command; alliance does not erase agency. Trust is situated and can be evidenced through demonstrated capability, kept commitments, honored permissions, repair, and voluntary coordination.

### 2.3 Mechanics declare what is real

What a game tracks, differentiates, rewards, and allows to alter future play gains ontological weight.

The design question is not simply whether to quantify. It is which dimensions of reality receive mechanical consequence and which remain implicit, decorative, or absent.

### 2.4 Performance as cultivation

Gameplay is performance. Performing care, courage, inquiry, restraint, vulnerability, stewardship, or alliance for a reward is not automatically counterfeit.

Structured performance can:

- create good stories;
- rehearse an unfamiliar capacity;
- produce real in-game relational effects;
- generate evidence before identity or understanding catches up;
- let meaning emerge after action.

The relevant failure mode is **proxy capture**, not performance: optimization displaces or harms the story, relationship, capacity, or experience the reward was meant to cultivate.

### 2.5 Mystery with epistemic fairness

Hidden systems and uncanny relevance are desirable when they preserve causal coherence. Attentive players should be able to infer important truths from evidence, even if formal recognition comes later.

Revelation should deepen interpretation rather than merely announce the author’s answer. Multiple readings may coexist when they remain accountable to evidence, prediction, satisfying action, and usefulness.

### 2.6 Enacted intuition and resolution depth

A design can allow tacit or embodied knowing to enter play before explicit explanation.

Low-resolution gestures require enough temporal, spatial, and causal room to develop into high-resolution consequences. Excessive compression may teach tactics while hiding the larger aesthetic those tactics serve.

### 2.7 Latent commitment

A meaningful choice can constrain the future while remaining ambiguous enough for later relationships and events to help determine what it becomes.

This is not simply foreshadowing. Later play participates in producing the earlier choice’s meaning.

### 2.8 Developmental provenance

Emergent results feel meaningful when artifacts of their lineage remain inspectable.

The ontology should distinguish:

- the originating seed or commitment;
- the path of actions and relationships;
- transformations along that path;
- the resulting capability, artifact, relationship, or world state;
- the interpretations attached by different agents.

Outcome value and process value may both matter without becoming identical.

### 2.9 Adaptive otherness

An adaptive system may read the player and transform that reading into explorable material, but it should not reduce the world to a personality test.

> **The player may seed the world, but does not exhaust it. The system reads provisionally; the world answers with otherness; the player authors what it means.**

The world needs causes, agents, resistance, persistence, or meanings that exceed the player model. The player must remain larger than the system’s interpretation.

### 2.10 Layered authorship

The designer authors conditions. The Game Master’s world-making can itself be original play. Players introduce additional seeds through character creation, choice, interpretation, and action. Adaptive systems may select, combine, or transform material.

These forms of authorship overlap without becoming interchangeable. Personal meaning remains player-authored.

### 2.11 Transformation validity and application fitness

For self-directed Emotional Alchemy, internally satisfying change is primary evidence that transformation occurred.

When a capacity is used in allyship, another agent’s consent and the observable consequences constrain whether that capacity is fit for this application. External success does not define inner truth; inner satisfaction does not override another’s agency.

### 2.12 Expertise as stewardship

Power players should perceive useful patterns without requiring complete certainty. Expertise becomes most aligned when it helps others inquire and act rather than claiming authority over their inner truth.

Shared charge provides relevance. Demonstrated transformation and helpful outcomes provide situated credibility. Neither grants universal authority.

## 3. Integral coverage without maximalism

AQAL coverage should not require every game to mechanize every quadrant, line, level, state, and type. That would reproduce the framework-abundance shadow and make every design carry the whole ontology.

Instead, the ontology should require an **integral declaration of mechanical attention**:

1. Which perspectives and developmental lines does this design make mechanically consequential?
2. Which does it support narratively or socially without formal mechanics?
3. Which does it intentionally leave outside its scope?
4. What experiences, players, or consequences might those choices obscure?
5. What evidence would show that the declared emphasis is producing the intended experience?

Integral practice becomes conscious selection plus awareness of exclusions—not compulsory inclusion of everything.

---

## 4. Provisional guardrail families

Guardrails remain hypotheses to test through play rather than restrictions assumed correct in advance.

- **Agency:** affected agents can respond, refuse, reinterpret, reciprocate, or withdraw.
- **Consequence:** rewarded performance changes later possibility rather than only increasing a meter.
- **Provenance:** the system preserves the path from action to result.
- **Plurality:** more than one enactment or interpretation can remain viable.
- **Situated scope:** evidence in one context or line does not define the whole person.
- **Corrigibility:** the system can be contradicted and its readings revised.
- **Player sovereignty:** privacy, explanation, personalization, retention, and sharing are separable choices.
- **Non-coercion:** rest, refusal, unchanged experience, and composting can be legitimate outcomes.
- **Relational accountability:** another person’s consent cannot be farmed as a unilateral resource.
- **Playability:** safeguards must preserve uncertainty, experimentation, performance, and fun.

Possible evidence against proxy capture includes diverse viable strategies, memorable downstream consequences, preserved agency, transfer of capacity beyond the exact reward prompt, legible links between action and result, and the absence of a single narratively empty optimization routine.

## 5. Productive polarities

These tensions should become inspectable design dimensions rather than universally resolved rules:

- mystery ↔ explanation;
- authored structure ↔ emergence;
- intuitive action ↔ explicit understanding;
- performance ↔ identification;
- outcome value ↔ process value;
- personal resonance ↔ world otherness;
- adaptation ↔ sovereignty;
- intrinsic satisfaction ↔ external consequence;
- access ↔ autonomy;
- strategic breadth ↔ tactical immediacy;
- simulation detail ↔ narrative consequence;
- legibility ↔ reduction;
- development ↔ rest or composting.

---

## 6. Anti-patterns and shadows

- **Replacement progression:** advancement makes prior relationships or work disposable.
- **Possession-as-consent:** acquiring an ally is treated as total command.
- **Globalized credentials:** situated capability becomes whole-person rank.
- **Decorative relationship:** bonds have no effect on possibility or consequence.
- **Arbitrary mystery:** hidden results lack inferable causal lineage.
- **Closed revelation:** the author’s explanation terminates inquiry.
- **Solipsistic adaptation:** the world becomes nothing but a mirror of the player model.
- **Proxy capture:** the rewarded indicator displaces the living quality it represents.
- **Metric totalization:** a local measure becomes a moral, psychological, or developmental verdict.
- **Compressed depth:** accessibility removes the scale required for strategic or interpretive emergence.
- **Outcome erasure of process:** only the result survives; how it came to be becomes irrelevant.
- **Framework maximalism:** integral coverage becomes an obligation to include every distinction in every game.

## 7. Candidate design-philosophy propositions

These are ready for the next framework-translation stage but are not yet constitutional principles:

1. **Mechanics make claims about reality. Choose their attention deliberately.**
2. **Development should change possibility without assigning total worth.**
3. **Relationship is a source of capability, not merely reward or decoration.**
4. **Play may precede understanding; consequential performance can cultivate capacity.**
5. **Let intuition enter as a gesture and let the world answer it.**
6. **Preserve lineage from seed through process to emergent result.**
7. **Hide enough to create discovery, reveal enough to support inference.**
8. **Let adaptive systems propose relevance while players author personal meaning.**
9. **The player may influence the world without becoming the world’s sole cause.**
10. **Treat inner satisfaction and relational application as distinct forms of evidence.**
11. **Design for all quadrants by examining exclusions, not by forcing every game to model everything.**
12. **When the ontology raises a risk, it must propose a playable test rather than transfer the burden back to the designer.**
