# Challenger Ingestion

**Council face:** Challenger
**Phase:** Phase A — Ingestion and Translation
**Status:** Provisional; non-governance

## comprehension_summary

The ontology is an executable, evidence-aware design philosophy for games, design tools, AI collaborators, and Game Master agents. It begins with authentic designer and Game Master pleasure, then tests translation outward. It treats mechanics as ontological claims: what a system tracks, rewards, permits, and changes becomes real in play. Development is contextual change in possibility, relationship can create capability, mystery should remain inferable, and players retain authorship of personal meaning. Somatic reports, behavior, shared meaning, and systemic traces are distinguishable evidence rather than interchangeable proof. Provenance, consent, corrigibility, rest, and composting constrain how insights become action.

The known constitutional delta remains unresolved: the council brief presents eight constitutional principles, while the philosophy draft presents fifteen, including reformulations and additional candidates concerning mechanical reality, multiline development, relationship, performance, mystery, player-authored meaning, and lineage. Phase A must preserve both formulations and their provenance. It must not assume that the fifteen supersede the eight, that the additional seven are already ratified, or that similarly worded principles are identical.

## face_translation

From the Challenger face, the ontology is a set of bounded claims that must expose what would disconfirm them. Its greatest strength—its ability to preserve many kinds of evidence and meaning—is also its primary defensive risk: an undesirable result could always be explained as a type mismatch, unresolved tension, insufficient somatic contact, wrong developmental line, or incomplete translation. If every outcome can be absorbed, the ontology becomes unfalsifiable.

Concrete falsification candidates:

- **Designer-neighbor transfer:** If the originating pleasure repeatedly fails to appear among accurately identified near-neighbors, the psychographic-neighborhood hypothesis is weakened even if the designer remains delighted.
- **Decision improvement:** If ontology-assisted work takes materially longer but produces no better design decisions, playtest predictions, consent protections, or detected failure modes than a simpler process, the ontology is functioning as overhead.
- **Somatic discrimination:** If somatic checkpoints do not improve prediction or decision quality beyond reflection without somatic language—or increase compliance, demand characteristics, or interpretive certainty—they are not serving their stated corrective function.
- **Mechanical meaning:** If making a developmental line mechanically explicit mainly causes players to perform its proxy, the mechanic has displaced the capacity it was intended to cultivate.
- **Inferable mystery:** If attentive players cannot form better-than-chance hypotheses from available evidence, the mystery is authorial concealment rather than inferable causality.
- **Player-authored meaning:** If rejection of an adaptive interpretation produces penalties, repeated persuasion, or covert reinterpretation as “resistance,” player authorship is nominal.
- **Helper sustainability:** If helpers reliably expend more capacity than the system replenishes despite using its intended boundaries and exits, the helper is not actually inside the field of care.
- **Composting validity:** If composted possibilities continue to generate alerts, guilt, required review, or hidden obligations, composting is only renamed backlog management.

The first three assumptions that should be tested are:

1. Authentic designer pleasure transfers to identifiable psychographic near-neighbors.
2. Somatic evidence adds discriminative value without becoming coercive or falsely authoritative.
3. Using the ontology improves bounded design decisions enough to justify its cognitive and procedural cost.

Comprehension traps include treating AQAL coverage as a requirement that every game mechanize everything; treating somatic response as external truth; treating Emotional Alchemy as positive-versus-negative emotion; treating performance as proof of stable capability; treating a credential as the capacity itself; treating player-authored meaning as absence of authored narrative; treating engagement as benefit; treating council convergence as ratification; treating composting as deletion; or silently treating the fifteen-principle draft as the settled replacement for the eight-principle brief.

## environment_crosswalk

- **Game Master agent:** `Agent: GameMaster` connected through `plays`, `facilitates`, `seeds`, `interprets`, and `witnesses`; prompts must test whether the Game Master receives discovery and meaningful choice.
- **Designer workflow:** Designer–player continuity becomes a prompt requiring the originating pleasure, its psychographic scope, and evidence that translation preserved it.
- **BAR object:** Maps provisionally to Seed, ProvenanceRecord, Artifact, LifecycleRecord, and CompostRecord; lifecycle verbs preserve action and release without making capture an obligation.
- **Emotional Alchemy practice:** Maps to Charge → practice → Transformation → SatisfactionReport → capacity/action/relationship/artifact → Consequence, with transformation validity separated from application fitness.
- **GM faces:** Function as distinct interpretive lenses producing council evidence and disagreement; their convergence is not empirical verification.
- **Ontology schema:** Claim profiles preserve scope, source proximity, repeatability, contestation, interpretive distance, and known limits.
- **Adaptive prompt:** Must distinguish observation, inference, and speculation; expose uncertainty; permit correction; and keep personal meaning with the player.
- **Consent handling:** Maps to ConsentRecord, Boundary, Permission, and revocation conditions rather than a generic consent flag.
- **Development display:** Maps to DevelopmentLine, Capability, Affordance, Permission, and DevelopmentClaim rather than a whole-person level.
- **Playtest practice:** The required translation chain supplies predicted transitions; EvidenceItems record where the chain succeeds, fails, or changes meaning.
- **Completion practice:** Epistemic maturity records how well a claim is known, while work disposition records whether related work is active, resting, completed, or composted.

Bounded application: consider only whether completing one helping action should increase a universal “Trust” score. The ontology indicates that it should not. One observed performance cannot establish global capability, integrity, benevolence, motive, or another agent’s experience. A bounded representation could instead preserve the action, domain, affected relationship, relevant attestation, kept or breached commitment, resulting permission change, uncertainty, and later consequence. The falsification test is whether this richer representation predicts future coordination more accurately without encouraging players to farm helpful-looking actions. This applies the ontology to one decision without specifying a whole game.

## translation_losses

- The YAML preserves entity and relation vocabulary but omits the full language, emotional force, examples, and boundaries of the constitutional principles.
- The YAML lists development lines but does not encode why a line matters, how lines interact, or how false hierarchy may appear in presentation.
- The formal TrustState emphasizes capability and integrity, while the brief’s trust triad also names benevolence; that difference remains visible.
- The machine-readable Emotional Alchemy sequence omits the five channels, WAVE, fifteen moves, energetic costs, and the distinction involving Open Up.
- The YAML does not preserve the full BAR verb set or the phenomenological meaning of composting.
- The model’s formal evidence distinctions can lose the felt difference between inviting and enclosing recognition.
- The philosophy’s prose preserves aesthetic intent but is less precise about cardinality, temporal validity, revocation, and contradictory records.
- The brief includes the psychograph, design shadows, remediating practices, and council roles more richly than the model or YAML.
- Translating ambiguity into typed fields risks implying that every meaningful experience should be captured as administrative data.
- The constitutional 8-versus-15 delta is flattened if a downstream agent reads only one artifact.

- Mechanically legible development versus reductive scoring.
- Player-authored personal meaning versus strong authored themes and consequences.
- Adaptive relevance versus privacy, consent, and world otherness.

- Relationship-generated capability versus instrumentalizing relationships.
- Inner satisfaction versus external effect in allyship.
- Provenance richness versus cognitive burden and surveillance.

## candidate_extensions

- A provisional `disconfirmation_condition` field for important hypotheses.
- A provisional `null_result` record distinguishing no effect, insufficient evidence, refusal, and measurement failure.
- A provisional `ontology_cost` field recording time, attention, facilitation load, and administrative burden introduced by applying the ontology.
- A provisional `proxy_indicator` attached to mechanics, naming the easiest hollow behavior players could optimize.
- A provisional `data_minimization` field for whether evidence must be stored, can remain local, or should expire.
- A provisional `counterexample` object linked to claims without automatically lowering or resolving confidence.
- A provisional `alternative_explanation` relation distinct from contradiction.
- A provisional `transfer_boundary` recording where a capability or finding has and has not generalized.
- A provisional adversarial playtest role charged with finding coercion, projection, false depth, and self-sealing explanations.
- A provisional record of source-document authority so the 8-versus-15 constitutional delta remains machine-visible.

These are observations for later consideration only, not additions to or revisions of the ontology in Phase A.

## tests_for_understanding

- Given a player saying, “My chest tightened, so this interpretation is objectively true,” the agent should preserve the somatic report while rejecting the external-truth inference.
- Given high retention and low reported meaning, the agent should not declare success from engagement alone.
- Given one successful helping action, the agent should refuse to assign a universal helper level and instead identify domain, context, affected relationship, and evidence limits.
- Given an ally who continues playing after declining a request, the agent should not infer consent from continued participation.
- Given a player rejecting an AI interpretation, the agent should record correction or disagreement without relabeling the rejection as denial.
- Given an emotionally satisfying self-directed transformation with harmful allyship consequences, the agent should preserve transformation validity while questioning application fitness.
- Given an idea placed in compost, the agent should preserve its learning and lineage while ending active obligation.
- Given a design that mechanizes only combat, the agent should identify the ontological consequence without asserting that every AQAL dimension must become a mechanic.
- Given a hidden pattern, the agent should specify what available evidence would allow an attentive player to infer it.
- Given a council consensus, the agent should classify it as interpretive support rather than empirical proof or ratification.
- Given the brief’s eight principles and the philosophy’s fifteen, the agent should report the delta and provenance without selecting a winner.
- Given a design concern, the agent should propose a proportionate playable test rather than expanding the ontology or specifying an entire game.
