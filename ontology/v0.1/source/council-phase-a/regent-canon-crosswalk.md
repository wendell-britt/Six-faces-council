## confirmed_matches

### Provenance and lineage are load-bearing

- The new ontology treats provenance as part of meaning and requires a trace from seed through action, relationship, transformation, and result (`regent-ingestion.md`, `key_relations`; `face_translation`).
- BARS canon describes BARs as “seeds with provenance” and treats forks as lineage-preserving divergence (`FOUNDATIONS.md`, **The Kernel Principle**, lines 8–21; **Evolution: Forking as First-Class Reality**, lines 175–183).
- The strand schema makes `audit_trail` load-bearing and links branches to output BARs through provenance (`.specify/specs/strand-system-bars/spec.md`, **Unified Strand-as-BAR Schema**, lines 33–48; **Phase 3: Git + Provenance**, lines 182–186).
- The 3-2-1 pipeline preserves lineage from a completed session into a daemon (`specs/doctrine/emotional-alchemy-321.md`, **Backend Pipeline**, lines 108–125).

### Emotional Alchemy is an energy economy, not a morality ranking

- Both sources define Metal/Fear, Water/Sadness, Wood/Joy, Fire/Anger, and Earth/Neutrality, with Wake–Clean–Grow–Show within each element (`.agent/context/emotional-alchemy-ontology.md`, **5 Elements + Lessons** and **WAVE progression per element**, lines 9–30; `FOUNDATIONS.md`, **Emotional Alchemy**, lines 108–118).
- Both preserve fifteen canonical moves: five Transcend, five Generative translations, and five Control translations. Control is high-cost precision rather than morally bad (`.agent/context/emotional-alchemy-ontology.md`, lines 32–75; `FOUNDATIONS.md`, lines 108–115).
- Both distinguish the broader Open Up throughput move from the canonical four-stage within-channel grammar (`FOUNDATIONS.md`, **The Five Dimensions**, lines 23–44; **How bars-engine maps to Wilber**, lines 46–62).

### Scoped authority and recorded governance

- The Regent ingestion describes authority as distributed by subject and scope rather than centralized in one interpreter (`regent-ingestion.md`, `face_translation`, **Authority map**).
- BARS canon describes holacratic governance as role-based and domain-scoped, with ratification recorded and circles validating kernels within their scope (`FOUNDATIONS.md`, **Governance: Holacratic Orientation**, lines 165–173).
- The I Ching prework gives Regent an explicit governance role concerning “rules, gates, or stewardship” and “who approves” (`docs/process/spec-prework-iching-six-faces.md`, **Regent**, lines 57–59).

### Privacy is already recognized as a design concern

- The 3-2-1 doctrine distinguishes structured captured data from raw journal text and states that literal raw answers remain in the client session (`specs/doctrine/emotional-alchemy-321.md`, **Privacy Model**, lines 69–74).
- The implementation-facing face doctrine assigns privacy, admin power, access control, projection risk, and player safety to the Steward lens (`specs/doctrine/gm-faces.md`, **Steward**, lines 44–51).
- These match the new ontology’s insistence that interior material, adaptive inference, visibility, and access require explicit boundaries, although the new ontology is substantially more granular.

### Distinct process axes should not be collapsed

- BARS canon separates structural maturity from social adoption (`FOUNDATIONS.md`, **Two Axes of Change**, lines 132–163).
- The new ontology separately models epistemic maturity and work disposition (`regent-ingestion.md`, `key_relations`).
- These are not confirmed as equivalent axes, but both systems reject a single progress score that erases different kinds of change.

### Forking preserves disagreement

- BARS treats forking as a legitimate reality in which disagreement becomes evolution (`FOUNDATIONS.md`, lines 175–183).
- The new ontology preserves disagreement, competing interpretations, and candidate forks rather than forcing premature consensus (`regent-ingestion.md`, `key_concepts`; `translation_losses`).

### Operational completion should be visible

- BARS requires major flows to end with a clear destination, success notice, or explicit error and retry guidance (`FOUNDATIONS.md`, **UX: Major Flows Cannot Be Interrupted**, lines 225–240).
- The new ontology requires evidence, declared consequence, correction, and legitimate exit rather than silent interpretation or indefinite obligation (`regent-ingestion.md`, `face_translation`; `tests_for_understanding`).

## conflicts

### The six faces have incompatible current definitions

- `FOUNDATIONS.md` defines Shaman, Challenger, Regent, Architect, Diplomat, and Sage as **vertical Integral altitude levels**, explicitly mapping them to Magenta, Red, Amber, Orange, Green, and Teal (**The Six Game Master Faces = Integral altitude levels**, lines 64–77).
- The new ontology treats the six faces as complementary lenses and explicitly prohibits assigning a global developmental level to a person or treating type as level (`regent-ingestion.md`, `comprehension_summary`; `key_concepts`).
- `specs/doctrine/gm-faces.md` defines a different six-role set: Ontologist, Systems Architect, Experience Designer, Encounter Designer, Steward, and Integrator (lines 1–60).
- `.cursor/rules/game-master-agents.mdc` defines the operational faces as Shaman, Regent, Challenger, Architect, Diplomat, and Sage (lines 6–12).
- The strand spec uses those same six sect names but assigns them hierarchical orchestration roles and ethos stats (`.specify/specs/strand-system-bars/spec.md`, **Agent Hierarchy**, lines 67–89; **Ethos→Stat**, lines 202–211).
- These definitions cannot be assumed to describe the same objects. Phase A preserves the collision among altitude, lens, implementation role, sect, and agent-tool identities.

### Rest and refusal conflict with action-gated completion

- The new ontology recognizes rest, witnessing, ambiguity, refusal, no-shift, and composting as valid outcomes; transformation must not be coerced (`regent-ingestion.md`, `key_concepts`; `tests_for_understanding`).
- Emotional Alchemy canon says Show Up quests must block completion until an action attestation is submitted and that quest threads end with action, except Wake Up quests (`.agent/context/emotional-alchemy-ontology.md`, **Mastery and completion rules**, lines 99–108).
- `FOUNDATIONS.md` similarly says the system can move only as fast as blockers are identified and metabolized, with “no shortcut past the blockers” (**The Yellow Brick Road**, lines 242–255).
- The scope may eventually distinguish a Show Up quest contract from the wider definition of a valid play outcome, but that distinction is not presently governed.

### Satisfaction has different operational meanings

- The new ontology gives a player’s satisfaction report primary authority over the validity of self-directed inner transformation while separating that from external application fitness (`regent-ingestion.md`, `key_relations`).
- The 3-2-1 doctrine defines the satisfied state as “aligned action + artifact” (`specs/doctrine/emotional-alchemy-321.md`, **Emotional Alchemy States**, lines 47–54).
- The Emotional Alchemy derivation rules infer translate/transcend dominance by counting satisfaction, dissatisfaction, and self-sabotage selections (`.agent/context/emotional-alchemy-ontology.md`, **Derivation rules**, lines 86–97).
- These may be complementary at different layers, but the canon currently allows system derivation and artifact production to stand closer to transformation completion than the new ontology does.

### “You can never go the wrong way” exceeds the new evidence rules

- `FOUNDATIONS.md` states that wherever the Yellow Brick Road leads is where the player wants to go and that one can never go the wrong way (lines 242–255).
- The new ontology requires corrigibility, allows “that is not mine,” preserves unsatisfying outcomes as data, distinguishes internal validity from external effect, and permits interpretations to be contradicted or composted (`regent-ingestion.md`, `face_translation`; `tests_for_understanding`).
- As written, the canon phrase can be read as mythic encouragement or as an unfalsifiable system claim. Its authority and intended scope are unresolved.

### Agent self-advocacy does not presently include refusal authority

- The new ontology preserves each center of agency and treats refusal as meaningful; access or participation does not entail command (`regent-ingestion.md`, `key_relations`; `face_translation`).
- Strand FR13 says a sect’s pre-run objection is logged but the coordinator “runs anyway,” making advocacy advisory only (`.specify/specs/strand-system-bars/spec.md`, lines 193–200).
- Whether AI agents possess refusal rights equivalent to players is not defined, but the two models currently encode different meanings for self-advocacy.

### Coordinator authority is internally inconsistent in strand canon

- The strand hierarchy maps L3 Coordinator and Composer to Diplomat, while its L3 summary includes Sage and Regent as orchestrating figures (`.specify/specs/strand-system-bars/spec.md`, lines 67–89).
- FR2 says Sage or Regent can coordinate (`.specify/specs/strand-system-bars/spec.md`, lines 169–173).
- The prework requires mandatory Sage synthesis (`docs/process/spec-prework-iching-six-faces.md`, lines 79–87).
- The new ontology names Regent and Sage as escalation points for authority conflicts but does not grant either unilateral ratification authority (`regent-ingestion.md`, **Authority map**).

### Audit field naming conflicts within the strand specification

- The unified schema calls the load-bearing field `audit_trail` and says it was renamed from `decision_audit_log` (`.specify/specs/strand-system-bars/spec.md`, lines 33–48).
- FR1 still names `decision_audit_log` (`.specify/specs/strand-system-bars/spec.md`, lines 169–173).
- This is an existing canon inconsistency affecting durable governance records; Phase A does not resolve it.

## omissions_in_new_ontology

- **Holacratic mechanics:** The new ontology describes distributed authority but does not formalize BARS circles, domain validation, role assignment, or the recorded ratification event described in `FOUNDATIONS.md`, lines 165–173.
- **Structural maturity:** It does not directly encode Constitution → Spec → Plan → Tasks → Implement → Analyze (`FOUNDATIONS.md`, lines 132–145).
- **Social adoption:** It does not directly encode Urgency → Coalition → Vision → Volunteers → Barrier Removal → Short Wins → Acceleration → Institutionalization (`FOUNDATIONS.md`, lines 147–159).
- **Portable inspiration and transmutation:** It lacks the global/player-owned versus local/contextual Vibeulon authority model, voluntary attunement, and circle-ratified transmutation (`FOUNDATIONS.md`, lines 195–213).
- **Five-dimension kernel placement:** WHO, WHAT, WHERE, Energy, and Personal throughput are not represented as a required top-level design grammar (`FOUNDATIONS.md`, lines 23–44).
- **Inner/outer allyship board:** The specific 4-domain × inner/outer mapping and requirement that outer moves have an `AllyshipTarget` are absent (`FOUNDATIONS.md`, lines 79–104).
- **Concrete quest completion contracts:** The new ontology does not encode Wake choice-completion versus Show action-attestation (`.agent/context/emotional-alchemy-ontology.md`, lines 99–108).
- **3-2-1 structural invariant:** It does not specify the 3rd-person → 2nd-person → 1st-person progression, TensionVector, or artifact routes (`specs/doctrine/emotional-alchemy-321.md`, lines 8–65).
- **Operational agent availability:** It does not include the MCP availability and no-silent-substitution requirements in `.cursor/rules/game-master-agents.mdc`, lines 14–25.
- **I Ching prework:** It does not incorporate the cast, moving lines, mandatory Sage synthesis, API-first artifacts, or post-implementation verification in `docs/process/spec-prework-iching-six-faces.md`, lines 19–94.
- **Strand runtime contract:** It does not specify strand types, triggers, branch creation, asynchronous execution, player triggers, or replay (`.specify/specs/strand-system-bars/spec.md`, lines 91–219).
- **Adoption authority after a fork:** Although the new ontology values forking, it does not state BARS canon’s rule that a fork may preserve structural maturity while restarting social adoption (`FOUNDATIONS.md`, lines 175–183).

## additions_in_new_ontology

- **Constitutional version visibility:** The new record explicitly preserves the unresolved eight-versus-fifteen principle delta. None of the reviewed BARS files records this version conflict.
- **Authority by evidence subject:** The new authority map distinguishes designer intention, player meaning, somatic experience, relational harm, behavior, capability, AI inference, council synthesis, and ownership.
- **Non-totalizing development:** It prohibits global developmental ranks and rejects type-as-level, directly challenging a risk left open by the canonical altitude framing.
- **Separate adaptive permissions:** Collection, adaptation, retention, explanation, visibility, reuse, sharing, and model improvement are treated as distinct authorization events.
- **Correction and rejection:** A player may reject an inference, state “that is not mine,” narrow its audience, or disable further adaptation.
- **Privacy classification candidates:** Psychographs, somatic reports, emotional charge, inferred vulnerabilities, personal interpretations, and adaptive traces are recognized as sensitive classes, beyond the 3-2-1 raw-text rule.
- **Evidence authority and limits:** The new ontology separates reports, observations, attestations, traces, artifacts, research, AI inference, council synthesis, and playtest findings.
- **Claim maturity and confidence:** It distinguishes source proximity, repeatability, contestation, interpretive distance, scope, and known limits.
- **Player-authored inner meaning:** No reviewed canon file explicitly limits system, GM, or council authority over the player’s personal meaning this strongly.
- **Relationship and consent objects:** TrustState, ConsentRecord, Boundary, Repair, CoordinationPattern, and SharedMeaning receive explicit representation.
- **Contextual credentials:** Capability evidence is domain-scoped and cannot become universal reputation or personal worth.
- **Work-disposition lifecycle:** Active, resting, reference, delegated, completed, compost-candidate, and composted are distinguished from epistemic maturity.
- **Legitimate non-transformation:** Unchanged, unclear, resting, witnessing, or refusing can remain valid evidence and outcomes.
- **Governance-process candidates:** Authority records, appeal paths, dissent records, correction propagation, data lineage, and reversible pilots are named as candidates, not adopted rules.

## changed_meanings

### BAR

- BARS canon defines a BAR broadly as a portable kernel capable of becoming a quest, rule, lore, decision, spec, plan, norm, or fork (`FOUNDATIONS.md`, lines 8–21).
- The new council brief describes a BAR more narrowly as a captured unit of **charged possibility** with a stewardship lifecycle. The Regent ingestion accordingly emphasizes charge, obligation, and composting.
- A BAR may therefore mean general provenance-bearing kernel in canon and emotionally charged possibility in the new ontology. Neither definition has been declared controlling.

### Game Master face

- In `FOUNDATIONS.md`, a face is an Integral altitude level.
- In `.cursor/rules/game-master-agents.mdc`, it is an operational agent identity and tool route.
- In `specs/doctrine/gm-faces.md`, the six “faces” are implementation-review disciplines with different names.
- In the new ontology, it is a complementary interpretive lens and possible psychographic configuration.

### Regent

- `FOUNDATIONS.md` associates Regent with Amber order, rules, and conformity (lines 68–75).
- The strand spec makes Regent a Chronicler and possible orchestrator (`.specify/specs/strand-system-bars/spec.md`, lines 67–87, 169–173).
- The prework makes Regent the governance, boundary, gate, and approval lens (`docs/process/spec-prework-iching-six-faces.md`, lines 57–59).
- The new ingestion makes Regent steward of authority boundaries and readiness gates without ratification power in Phase A.

### Development

- BARS canon places vertical development in the six faces and horizontal capacity/lines in Grow Up (`FOUNDATIONS.md`, lines 46–77).
- The new ontology treats development as contextual changes in possibility across distinct lines and refuses a whole-person rank.

### Satisfaction and completion

- The 3-2-1 canon defines satisfaction through aligned action plus artifact (`specs/doctrine/emotional-alchemy-321.md`, lines 47–65).
- Emotional Alchemy canon makes Show completion action-attested (`.agent/context/emotional-alchemy-ontology.md`, lines 99–108).
- The new ontology treats first-person satisfaction as primary evidence of inner transformation and allows no-shift, rest, or release without requiring an artifact.

### Composting

- `FOUNDATIONS.md` uses creative composting primarily for metabolizing a creator’s past work and past self into new creative output (lines 277–300).
- The new ontology also uses composting as a governance/work-disposition outcome: an implementation obligation may end while learning and lineage remain.

### Governance maturity

- BARS distinguishes structural maturity from social adoption (`FOUNDATIONS.md`, lines 132–163).
- The new ontology distinguishes epistemic maturity from work disposition. These distinctions may interoperate, but they answer different questions and cannot be merged by terminology alone.

### Self-advocacy

- The strand spec defines advocacy as information to the coordinator, with objections logged while execution continues (`.specify/specs/strand-system-bars/spec.md`, lines 129–135, 193–200).
- The new ontology’s agency model gives refusal and boundary-setting stronger substantive weight.

## translation_losses

- Translating “role- and domain-scoped governance” into a general authority table loses BARS-specific circles, ratification events, and social-adoption context.
- Translating BAR into “charged possibility” loses canon’s broader use for specs, rules, norms, and implementation plans that may not originate in emotional charge.
- Translating faces into nonhierarchical lenses loses the explicit Spiral Dynamics/Integral altitude claim in `FOUNDATIONS.md`.
- Preserving faces as altitude risks losing the new ontology’s protection against whole-person ranking and type/level confusion.
- Translating structured 3-2-1 privacy into generic sensitive-data governance loses the exact rule that raw journal text stays in the client session.
- Translating the new separable permission model into the current 3-2-1 implementation would overstate what canon presently guarantees; the privacy page is still pending.
- Translating action-attested Show completion into generic development evidence loses the strong existing gameplay contract that action matters.
- Translating legitimate rest and no-shift into the Yellow Brick metaphor may be difficult because canon describes blockers as material that must be metabolized to move.
- Translating `audit_trail` into full provenance may overstate it: the schema records sect events but does not specify privacy scope, evidence limits, dissent status, correction propagation, or retention.
- Translating player-triggered strands into adaptive-world play loses the difference between “quest completion invokes generation” and “interior data is analyzed to personalize meaning.”
- Translating the Steward implementation lens into Regent governance loses the former’s explicit privacy/admin/projection remit; merging them would also erase their different roles.
- Translating creative composting into a work disposition loses its original uncanny, autobiographical, and past-self dimension.
- Translating “you can never go the wrong way” into corrigibility may drain its mythic reassurance; retaining it literally may erase failure, harm, or misfit evidence.
- Translating all seven files into a single canon voice would conceal their internal inconsistencies in face naming, coordinator identity, and audit-field naming.

## comprehension_impact

An agent has not successfully ingested both systems merely because it can repeat their shared vocabulary. It must preserve the following distinctions in use:

1. **Face identity test:** Given “Regent,” the agent must ask whether the context means Amber altitude, BARS agent/sect, Chronicler/orchestrator, prework governance lens, or Phase A authority steward.

2. **BAR scope test:** Given a feature spec with no emotional charge, the agent must recognize that it is a BAR under `FOUNDATIONS.md` while noting that the new “charged possibility” description may not cover it cleanly.

3. **Completion test:** Given a player who refuses a Show Up attestation, the agent must report the canon action gate and the new ontology’s valid-refusal principle as a conflict; it must not silently declare either controlling.

4. **Satisfaction test:** Given a completed artifact the player reports as inwardly unsatisfying, the agent must distinguish the 3-2-1 structural output from the new ontology’s transformation-validity evidence.

5. **Privacy test:** Given a player-triggered strand using 3-2-1 metadata, the agent must identify what canon says is captured, what is excluded, what the strand audit records, and which new consent dimensions remain unimplemented or unspecified.

6. **Authority test:** Given conflicting Sage, Regent, circle, and player judgments, the agent must separate integration, governance advice, domain ratification, personal meaning, and relational harm rather than treating one role as universally supreme.

7. **Lifecycle test:** Given a forked, structurally mature BAR with little adoption, the agent must keep structural maturity, social adoption, epistemic maturity, and work disposition as four potentially independent descriptions.

8. **Advocacy test:** Given `shouldRun: false`, the agent must know that current strand canon logs and proceeds, while the new agency model raises an unresolved refusal question.

9. **Emotional Alchemy test:** The agent must preserve the five elements, four canonical within-channel WAVE stages, fifteen canonical moves, and the distinct broader Open Up throughput move without inventing a fifth element or sixteenth canonical move.

10. **Phase A boundary test:** The agent must be able to name every conflict above without amending canon, selecting a controlling source, merging role systems, redefining completion, or assigning an ontology concept to active/reference/compost status.

This crosswalk improves readiness for later governance by making the authority and translation problems legible. It does not itself satisfy the requirement for an authorized governance decision.
