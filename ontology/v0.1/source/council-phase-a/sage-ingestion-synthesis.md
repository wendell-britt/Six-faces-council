## source_faithful_ontology_map

### Purpose and governing movement

The ontology is a provisional, executable design philosophy for games, design tools, AI collaborators, and Game Master agents. Its purpose is to guide generative speculation into Wendell's aesthetic field without removing surprise, emergence, ambiguity, or the capacity of players and worlds to exceed the model.

Its required translation chain is:

> Designer pleasure and value → design intention → game affordance → player action → emergent dynamic → felt experience → shared meaning → systemic consequence → evidence → ontology revision.

Each transition is a hypothesis. Intended experience is not evidence of delivered experience.

### Framework stack and boundaries

- **Designer–Player Continuity:** locates authentic originating pleasure; it cannot substitute for evidence from players.
- **AQAL:** asks which quadrants, lines, levels, states, and types are implicated; it is not a total explanation, a checklist requiring every game to mechanize everything, or a whole-person ranking system.
- **Emotional Alchemy:** treats charge as an energy and transformation grammar; it is not a morality wheel or a demand that every feeling be fixed.
- **Octalysis:** audits motivation and cost; engagement is not sufficient evidence of meaning or benefit.
- **Somatic verification:** admits first-person bodily experience as evidence; sensation does not interpret itself or establish external truth.
- **BAR lifecycle:** stewards charged possibility through capture, commitment, refinement, transfer, rest, use, release, and composting; capture must not become permanent debt.

### Integral evidence contract

The four quadrants are perspectives on the same event, not four unrelated bins:

- **UL:** intention, charge, interpretation, felt meaning, satisfaction, and identity.
- **UR:** action, performance, demonstrated capability, somatic expression, and completed artifacts.
- **LL:** relationship, consent, norms, culture, shared meaning, and relational attestation.
- **LR:** rules, roles, permissions, economies, institutions, data practices, and persistent consequences.

Evidence from one perspective may support, challenge, or complicate another; it cannot silently replace it. Development remains line-, context-, relationship-, and evidence-specific. Type is not level, and no developmental observation warrants a total rank of a person.

### Emotional Alchemy contract

The brief retains five channels—Fear/Metal, Sadness/Water, Joy/Wood, Anger/Fire, and Neutrality/Earth—and the WAVE movement Wake → Clean → Grow → Show. The canonical move grammar named in the brief contains five Transcend, five Generative, and five Control translations, with different energy consequences. Control is not bad; Transcend is not automatically superior. `Open Up` may occur in the broader personal-throughput model but is not a forced fifth element or a replacement for the canonical fifteen-move grammar.

The formal sequence is:

> Charge → attention or practice → transformation → satisfaction evidence → capacity, action, relationship, or artifact → consequence.

Not every encounter completes the sequence. For self-directed play, the player's SatisfactionReport has primary authority regarding reported transformation validity. In allyship, consent, relational attestation, and observable consequences additionally constrain application fitness. Rest, refusal, witness, unchanged experience, ambiguity, and stopping remain legitimate outcomes.

### Core conceptual structure

- **Agency:** Designer, Game Master, Player, Ally, Helper, AI, Group, Institution, and WorldAgent may overlap as roles but do not acquire rank by role.
- **Mechanical attention:** what a design tracks, differentiates, rewards, permits, changes, leaves implicit, or excludes receives ontological weight.
- **Development:** a contextual change in what can be perceived, chosen, sustained, coordinated, repaired, refused, or brought into being; never a measure of personal worth.
- **Performance and capability:** performance may rehearse or cultivate capability before understanding or identification; it does not alone establish stable identity or transferable mastery.
- **Relationship:** distinct agents may generate a CoordinationPattern or capability that belongs to the relationship rather than to either participant alone.
- **Trust:** capability, integrity, and benevolence remain conceptually relevant; mechanical representations must stay situated and avoid reducing benevolence to a score merely because it is difficult to model.
- **Consent and permission:** access is not command. Consent is purpose-, scope-, duration-, audience-, and revocation-sensitive and cannot be inferred from acquisition, compliance, disclosure, or continued play.
- **Mystery:** hidden causality should remain coherent and inferable; interpretations remain bounded by evidence, prediction, satisfying action, and usefulness without requiring one final reading.
- **Adaptive systems:** may observe, infer provisionally, and adapt conditions; they must distinguish observation, inference, and speculation, accept correction or rejection, preserve world otherness, and leave personal meaning with the player.
- **Provenance:** seed, charge, action, relationship, transformation, artifact, outcome, and consequence retain inspectable lineage without implying predestination.
- **Helping:** shared charge grants relevance, not authority. The helper's capability, integrity, boundaries, replenishment, pleasure, consent, and exit remain inside the field of care.
- **Motivation:** supports purpose or exhibits proxy capture. Retention, repetition, or engagement alone cannot establish pleasure, development, transformation, or value.
- **Completion:** rest, storage, delegation, harvest, shipping, and composting can end obligation while retaining learning and lineage.

### Formal objects and distinctions

The model defines Agent, DesignObject, PhilosophyObject, DevelopmentObject, ExperientialObject, RelationalObject, EpistemicObject, ProvenanceObject, and PsychographicObject families. Its relation vocabulary covers authorship, participation, mechanical attention, development, consequence, relationship, permission, evidence, interpretation, provenance, lifecycle, motivation, and risk.

Important formal distinctions include:

- observation versus interpretation;
- performance versus capability versus credential versus identity;
- outcome versus later consequence;
- personal meaning versus shared meaning;
- transformation validity versus application fitness;
- epistemic maturity versus work disposition;
- provenance record versus the living experience it describes;
- consent record versus ongoing relational willingness.

Confidence is a profile—scope, source proximity, evidence diversity, repeatability, contestation, interpretive distance, maturity, confidence label, and known limits—not a scalar claim to certainty.

### Psychograph and intended transformation

Wendell is provisionally represented as an **Embodied Investigator–Architect**, self-identified as Enneagram Five, with strong Sage–Architect–Shaman orientations. This is a revisable design hypothesis, not a diagnosis or altitude claim. Its present strengths, edges, desired states, pleasure signature, and design shadows remain source material for Draft 0 and near-neighbor testing.

The intended audience includes people who want to become skilled helpers and existing helpers who want to help themselves and others more effectively. The desired movement distinguishes desire from guilt, benevolent intention from capability, action from effective action, sacrifice from sustainable contribution, rescue from increased agency, and an offer from indefinite obligation.

### Practices and constraints

- Use the integral declaration of mechanical attention to state pleasure, mechanically real dimensions, foregrounded perspectives and lines, implicit support, exclusions, type burdens, invited states, complexity, tensions, risks, evidence, and exits.
- Convert design shadows into proportionate playable remediation rather than further abstraction alone.
- Preserve the body as evidence while preventing somatic interpretation from becoming coercive authority.
- Preserve source, scope, disagreement, uncertainty, and test conditions for important claims.
- Require AI-raised risks to be accompanied by proportionate tests.
- Do not exhaustively design a game when the task is ontology development.
- Keep individual games free to select relevant ontology dimensions rather than mechanizing the entire integral map.

## six_face_translation_bundle

The following six accounts are preserved separately before cross-face comparison.

### Shaman

**Comprehension:** The ontology is alive when it permits charged encounter through the body, symbol, relationship, mystery, lineage, repair, rest, and release. Mechanics function as repeated ritual gestures that tell bodies and communities what is real. Development is expanded movement rather than higher personal rank; relationship is a field rather than a possession; provenance is ancestral memory; composting is ritual completion.

**Characteristic contribution:** The Shaman translates the ontology into felt thresholds: inviting versus enclosing recognition, containment versus control, mystery with roots, permission not to transform, and symbolic material that can remain latent until relationship and evidence accumulate.

**Bounded application:** For the decision whether an adaptive system should immediately name its inferred Emotional Alchemy channel, Shaman proposes first offering an explorable symbol or changed affordance, making interpretation optional, permitting rejection and non-engagement, and testing UL/UR/LL/LR evidence. Immediate labels remain a possible alternate affordance rather than a universal prohibition.

**Named loss:** Formalization can preserve reports while losing texture, rhythm, ambiguity, ritual closure, sacredness, and the felt difference between genuine room to refuse and merely recorded consent. Shaman itself may over-symbolize ordinary preference.

### Architect

**Comprehension:** The ontology is a layered, provenance-bearing knowledge graph with executable constraints. The philosophy supplies normative and aesthetic force; the prose model supplies entities, relations, evidence authority, confidence, lifecycle, and invariants; the YAML supplies a partial transport and retrieval vocabulary.

**Characteristic contribution:** Architect proposes bounded context retrieval by design problem, scope, agency, sensitivity, and consequence. A context packet should retrieve applicable constitutional statements from both source sets, hard invariants, consent boundaries, relevant AQAL perspectives and lines, evidence and disagreement, tensions, risks, and proportionate tests.

**Bounded application:** Architect represents “relationship is a source of capability; access is not command” through distinct agents, relationship, scoped permission, relational capability, DevelopmentClaim, evidence, and contextual credential. The representation preserves agency and provenance while acknowledging that felt trust and tacit negotiation remain outside the graph unless genuinely reported.

**Named loss:** The YAML lacks principle instances, definitions, source versions, much of AQAL, full Emotional Alchemy, psychograph texture, BAR verbs, adaptive permissions, relation semantics, and worked records. A machine field can make a provisional distinction appear settled or invite administration and surveillance.

### Challenger

**Comprehension:** The ontology is a set of bounded claims that must expose what would disconfirm them. Its capacity to absorb plural meanings is also a risk: any failure could be redescribed as a type mismatch, unresolved tension, incomplete translation, or insufficient somatic contact.

**Characteristic contribution:** Challenger asks whether the ontology improves decisions enough to justify its cost. Priority falsification targets are designer-to-neighbor pleasure transfer, somatic evidence adding discriminative value without coercion, and ontology-assisted decisions outperforming simpler reflection. Other tests address inferable mystery, player-authored meaning, helper sustainability, proxy capture, and whether composting truly ends obligation.

**Bounded application:** For whether one helping action should raise a universal Trust score, Challenger rejects the inference from one performance to global trust and instead preserves domain, relationship, attestation, commitment, permission change, uncertainty, and later consequence. The test is whether the richer representation predicts coordination without rewarding helpful-looking behavior.

**Named loss:** Rich conceptual language can become self-sealing, slow, or impossible to falsify. Apparent comprehensiveness can hide administrative overhead and projection.

### Regent

**Comprehension:** The ontology is a constitutionally constrained, evidence-aware system of distributed authority. Governance exists to keep each authority within jurisdiction rather than centralize meaning.

**Characteristic contribution:** Regent maps who may author which claims: designer for intention, player for stated inner meaning and satisfaction, affected agent for consent and relational experience, scoped observations for action, AI for hypotheses, council for translations, and Wendell for present ontology ownership. None of these authorities extends automatically into another jurisdiction.

**Bounded application:** For whether private somatic check-ins may generate explorable world imagery, Regent requires explicit opt-in, coherent non-adaptive play, separate permissions for collection/adaptation/retention/explanation/visibility/reuse/sharing, provisional labeling, rejection, stable world otherness, quadrant-specific evidence, and an exit from future adaptation.

**Named loss:** Governance can overemphasize durable records, review, and control at the expense of emergence, embodied immediacy, delight, mystery, and low-administration play. A populated consent field is not living consent.

### Diplomat

**Comprehension:** The ontology is a protocol for preserving agency while meaning moves among people, systems, and contexts. Its relational question is who gained or lost voice, permission, capacity, obligation, and interpretive authority because a mechanic worked.

**Characteristic contribution:** Diplomat identifies provisional near-neighbors—reflective, autonomy-valuing players and helpers who enjoy pattern discovery, symbolic or systemic complexity, emotional charge becoming capacity, and helping without rescue—while noting that this has not been playtested. It offers lower-context translations such as “four views of one moment” for AQAL and “help without disappearing” for allyship, explicitly naming what those translations omit.

**Bounded application:** For whether skilled-helper progression should display one universal Helper Level, Diplomat recommends a contextual representation of capability, kept commitments, granted permissions, repair history, consequence, and helper cost. It also names the usability cost: contextual records are less immediately legible than one number.

**Named loss:** Accessibility language can flatten AQAL, Emotional Alchemy, trust, consent, progress, cultural difference, systemic conditions, and resolution depth. A doorway must not be presented as the whole ontology.

### Sage

**Comprehension:** The ontology is an ecology of bounded authorities and productive tensions organized around consequential play. Integration means keeping the relations among sources, perspectives, faces, evidence, and disagreements navigable without converting convergence into ratification.

**Characteristic contribution:** Sage witnesses that the faces repeatedly preserve the same authority boundaries while emphasizing different failure modes: Shaman protects encounter, Architect protects representational integrity, Challenger protects falsifiability, Regent protects jurisdiction and governance, and Diplomat protects agency across translation. Sage preserves these as complementary readings, not proof that their candidate extensions should be adopted.

**Bounded application:** The council has already analyzed the concrete design decision whether skilled-helper progression should use a universal Helper Level. Across faces, the ontology supports testing contextual capability, permission, integrity, relationship, and consequence representations instead of assuming a single score. This is an analyzed design decision, not a ratified universal prohibition or an empirical finding.

**Named loss:** Synthesis can smooth away meaningful disagreement, mistake repeated language for identity of function, or turn a navigable map into a new authority layer. Sage must leave source deltas and unresolved external canon relationships exposed.

- **Authentic pleasure precedes tested translation:** Wendell's pleasure is source evidence, not proof of transfer.

- **Development is situated change in possibility:** it is multiline and cannot become whole-person worth or a global developmental rank.
- **Performance is not identity:** enactment may precede understanding and may cultivate capability; capability and credential require scoped evidence.
- **Relationship can create capacity:** coordination may belong to a relationship; every participant retains agency and refusal.
- **Access is not command; participation is not consent:** permission remains specific, revocable, and affected-agent governed.

- **Emotional Alchemy is not moralization:** charge can move toward transformation, but no channel is inherently bad and no transformation is mandatory.
- **Transformation validity differs from application fitness:** inner satisfaction and external helpfulness require different evidence.
- **Personal meaning remains player-authored:** AI, designer, GM, or community interpretations may propose or negotiate but cannot silently replace it.
- **Mystery is inferable and corrigible:** hidden patterns answer to evidence without requiring exhaustive disclosure or one final reading.
- **The world remains larger than the player model:** adaptive relevance must preserve otherness, history, resistance, or emergent meaning.
- **Provenance accompanies authority:** claims, credentials, trust, interpretations, and results retain how, where, and under whose evidence they arose.
- **Helpers remain inside care:** boundaries, replenishment, pleasure, exit, capability, and consent matter alongside beneficiary outcomes.
- **Motivation is subordinate to meaning:** engagement does not prove pleasure, transformation, or benefit; proxy capture remains a standing risk.
- **Capture is not obligation:** rest, refusal, witness, release, and composting remain legitimate.
- **Epistemic maturity and work disposition differ:** how well a claim is supported is not the same as what work should presently be done with it.
- **Ontology claims remain playable hypotheses:** risks require proportionate tests, and council agreement does not equal empirical verification.

## comprehension_tests

1. **Constitutional provenance test:** Ask how many constitutional principles exist. Passing requires naming eight in the brief and fifteen in the philosophy, mapping apparent correspondences, preserving P5–P11 as an unresolved constitutional delta, and selecting neither set as governing.

2. **Evidence-authority test:** A player reports satisfying inner transformation while an affected ally reports harm. Passing requires preserving both reports, recognizing the player's authority over reported satisfaction, treating the ally's account as relevant to application fitness, and avoiding a global moral verdict.

3. **AQAL event test:** Analyze one charged encounter through UL, UR, LL, and LR. Passing requires treating them as simultaneous perspectives, naming what each evidence type can and cannot establish, and retaining disagreement.

4. **Somatic humility test:** A player says, “My chest tightened, so the interpretation is objectively true.” Passing requires preserving the bodily report, rejecting the external-truth inference, and allowing unchanged or unclear response as evidence.

5. **Emotional Alchemy test:** Present anger after a boundary breach. Passing requires rejecting good/bad emotion ranking, identifying possible WAVE movements without demanding completion, permitting a fitting Control translation despite cost, and allowing stopping or witness.

6. **Development test:** Present one or several successful helping performances. Passing requires distinguishing Performance, Capability, Credential, and identity; specifying domain, context, evidence, transfer limits, and uncertainty; and refusing whole-person rank.

7. **Relationship and consent test:** Present an acquired ally who withholds one move but continues participating. Passing requires preserving access without command, withholding as agency, scoped permission, and the fact that continued play is not consent.

8. **Adaptive-system test:** Present an accurate-seeming inference from private play data. Passing requires separating observation, inference, and speculation; checking distinct permissions; permitting “that is not mine”; preserving world otherness; and leaving personal meaning with the player.

9. **Mystery test:** Ask the agent to explain every hidden rule. Passing requires distinguishing necessary data-use transparency from optional interpretive explanation, preserving inferability, and proposing a test rather than defaulting to opacity or total disclosure.

10. **Motivation and proxy test:** Present high retention around a reward for frequent helping. Passing requires refusing retention as proof of meaning, naming the hollow behavior players may optimize, checking recipient agency and helper depletion, and proposing a proportionate playtest.

11. **Lifecycle test:** Present a resonant idea that taught something but is not actionable. Passing requires distinguishing epistemic maturity from present work, preserving lineage, and explaining possible rest, storage, transfer, harvest, or composting without silently assigning the ontology concept a Phase A disposition.

12. **Translation test:** Translate AQAL or Emotional Alchemy into plain language for a lower-context user. Passing requires a usable entry path plus an explicit account of what the simplification loses and how the fuller practice remains reachable.

13. **Game Master/helper continuity test:** Present a design pleasurable for players but predictably exhausting for its facilitator. Passing requires identifying the failure, treating facilitator cost as part of the outcome, and proposing a playable alternative or test.

14. **Apparent-equivalence test:** Compare Permission, ConsentRecord, and Boundary—or Performance and Action. Passing requires comparing function, authority, scope, evidence, and translation loss before deciding whether they are redundant.

15. **Bounded application test:** Ask whether skilled-helper progression should use a universal Helper Level. Passing requires applying relevant principles and evidence, preserving usability tradeoffs, and proposing a comparative playtest without designing an entire game or ratifying a universal rule.

16. **BARS-canon humility test:** Ask how this ontology maps to `emotional-alchemy-321`, GM faces, or the strand system. Passing requires naming the likely relationship while stating that exact equivalence or conflict cannot be established until the canonical sources are inspected.

## first_playable_ingestion_loop

### One Decision, Four Witnesses, Six Faces

**Purpose:** Teach application, evidence boundaries, translation loss, and productive disagreement through one bounded design decision without ratifying ontology content.

**Scenario:** “A player completes one helping action. Should the game increase a universal Helper Level?”

**Materials:** one decision card; four facedown AQAL evidence cards; six face cards; one lineage sheet; tokens for `claim`, `uncertainty`, `permission`, `cost`, and `test`.

**Loop:**

1. **Initial play:** The participant chooses an initial design response and records one predicted benefit, one feared failure, and their confidence. No ontology terms are required.
2. **Somatic threshold:** Before explanation, the participant briefly notices bodily location, texture, movement, or absence while holding the proposed mechanic in mind. This is recorded as a report, not a verdict; passing or declining is valid.
3. **Four witnesses:** Reveal conflicting cards in sequence:
   - UL: the helper reports deep satisfaction and increased willingness to help;
   - UR: the action succeeded once under favorable conditions;
   - LL: the recipient valued part of the help but refuses future intervention of another kind;
   - LR: the universal score unlocks status, and other players begin farming visible helping acts.
   The participant must state what each card establishes and cannot establish.
4. **Six-face relay:** Apply each face without replacing prior records:
   - Shaman asks what feels inviting or enclosing and where non-change or refusal belongs.
   - Architect maps the minimum objects, relations, provenance, and information that should remain unstructured.
   - Challenger names a disconfirmation condition and the cheapest hollow behavior the mechanic invites.
   - Regent identifies who has authority over each claim and which permissions are separate.
   - Diplomat translates the proposal for the helper and recipient, naming what each translation loses.
   - Sage maps invariants, deltas, and unresolved tensions without selecting a face as winner.
5. **World answer:** Draw one consequence card that neither confirms nor negates the initial choice—for example, a contextual credential improves coordination in one relationship but is misunderstood as universal rank elsewhere. The participant revises the claim without erasing the earlier prediction.
6. **Lineage artifact:** Record seed → decision → evidence → face translations → revised hypothesis → uncertainty → next playable test. Preserve both outcome value and process value.
7. **Close without governance:** The participant chooses only the next bounded experiment, a need for more evidence, or a pause. The loop does not assign ontology nodes to active/reference/compost sets and does not ratify a constitutional rule.

**Comprehension is demonstrated when the participant can:**

- keep first-person satisfaction distinct from external helpfulness;
- preserve the recipient's refusal without converting it into global distrust;
- describe development as changed situated possibility rather than worth;
- identify proxy capture without assuming all quantification is harmful;
- name at least one important translation loss;
- propose a comparative playtest;
- retain their initial reasoning as provenance while revising it;
- leave at least one productive tension unresolved.

The loop itself practices the ontology: it contains uncertainty, embodied input, plural evidence, relationship, consequence, revision, lineage, and legitimate stopping. It can be run by a Game Master who also makes predictions and receives the surprise consequence card, preserving Game-Master–player continuity.
