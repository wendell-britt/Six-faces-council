# Regent Decision — Constitutional Evolution from Eight to Fifteen

**Face:** Regent
**Decision scope:** Relationship between the original eight constitutional principles and the later fifteen
**Authority:** Targeted decision explicitly delegated by Wendell to the six Game Masters
**Status:** Bounded constitutional-version decision; no broader pruning, merging, deprecation, composting, or work-disposition sorting

## documented_timeline

1. **The eight began as a provisional constitutional seed, not an immutable final constitution.**
   The original council brief labels itself a “Provisional ontology seed for council ingestion and cross-environment translation” and names eight principles under **2. Constitutional principles**: Game Master as player, designer as player, psychographic-neighborhood design, somatic authority, helper care, self-practicing tools, composting, and meaning over motivation (`outputs/design-ontology-six-face-council-brief.md`, **Status**, **Council mandate**, and **2. Constitutional principles**). Its mandate explicitly anticipates candidate changes and later governance rather than claiming textual finality.

2. **The ontology process then moved through research and dialogical excavation before returning to constitutional language.**
   The documented sequence places psychograph, intake, research, excavation, pattern extraction, and framework translation before the design-philosophy draft (`outputs/design-ontology-process-log.md`, **Ontology-development sequence**). This establishes that later constitutional language was produced after additional evidence rather than contemporaneously with the seed.

3. **Pokémon excavation supplied development and relationship claims.**
   Development first emerged as the requirement that prior work remain consequential, then separated into intrinsic, configurational, player, and relational forms. Relationship was identified as the carrier of development; access was distinguished from command; and trust became situated capability and integrity (`outputs/aesthetic-source-research-round-1.md`, **Pokémon — Excavation responses 1–7**). These materials became the basis for later principles 6 and 7.

4. **Monster Rancher and Inception excavation supplied inferable mystery, stewardship, and personal-meaning authority.**
   Monster Rancher established useful pattern recognition under incomplete certainty, adaptive curation with player sovereignty, and optional contestable explanation (`outputs/aesthetic-source-research-round-1.md`, **Monster Rancher — Excavation responses 2, 7–9**). Inception established evidence-bounded plurality, player-authored inner meaning, adaptive reading as explorable world material, and the requirement that the world acquire otherness (`outputs/aesthetic-source-research-round-1.md`, **Inception — Excavation responses 3–8**). These became major sources for later principles 9 and 10.

5. **The first pattern checkpoint already recognized most of the future inserted principles as recurring ontology candidates.**
   After Pokémon, Monster Rancher, and Inception, the checkpoint named development as consequential work, relationship as developmental medium, situated trust, inferable mystery, player-authored meaning, adaptive otherness, player sovereignty, and provenance (`outputs/design-ontology-pattern-checkpoint-1.md`, **2. Provisional ontology candidates**). At this stage they were explicitly candidates rather than constitutional rules.

6. **Go supplied developmental provenance, and Dungeon Crawler Carl supplied mechanical attention and performance-before-understanding.**
   Go clarified that an emergent result becomes meaningful when the lineage from intuitive gesture through later relations remains perceivable (`outputs/aesthetic-source-research-round-1.md`, **Go — Excavation responses 2–4**). Dungeon Crawler Carl clarified that the problem is not quantification but a narrow ontology of mechanical attention, and that rewarded performance may cultivate capacity before identity or understanding catches up (`outputs/aesthetic-source-research-round-1.md`, **Dungeon Crawler Carl — Excavation responses 1–3**). These became later principles 5, 8, and 11.

7. **Cross-specimen extraction converted source findings into an explicit pre-constitutional proposition set.**
   The pattern extraction names twelve candidate design-philosophy propositions while stating that they are “not yet constitutional principles” (`outputs/design-ontology-pattern-extraction-round-1.md`, **7. Candidate design-philosophy propositions**). The future additions appear there almost verbatim: mechanical reality, development as changed possibility, relational capability, performance as cultivation, inferable mystery, player-authored adaptive meaning, world otherness, and provenance.

8. **Framework translation tested and bounded those candidates before promotion.**
   AQAL supplied perspective and multiline-development limits; Emotional Alchemy separated inner transformation validity from relational application fitness; Octalysis subordinated motivation to meaning; somatics limited interpretation of bodily evidence; and BAR supplied provenance and lifecycle boundaries (`outputs/design-ontology-framework-translation-round-1.md`, **1–5**). Its **Candidate revisions before philosophy drafting** explicitly directs the next draft to preserve performance-before-identity, development as changed possibility, provenance, Game Master/designer play, and differentiated evidence authority.

9. **The philosophy draft promoted a coherent fifteen-principle successor set.**
   The philosophy states that it comes “After aesthetic excavation and framework translation; before formal ontology modeling and council governance” and calls the fifteen “the most stable commitments in the current design philosophy” (`outputs/design-philosophy-draft-v0.1.md`, **Position in process** and **I. Constitutional principles**). It retains and refines all eight seed commitments while inserting seven evidence-derived principles as 5–11.

10. **The formal model then operationalized the fifteen-principle structure.**
    The model represents mechanical attention, contextual DevelopmentClaims, relationship and consent objects, Performance, inferable and corrigible claims, personal-meaning authorship, and provenance (`outputs/design-ontology-model-v0.1.md`, **Modeling commitments**, **Entity families**, **Relationship vocabulary**, **Development model**, **Adaptive-system model**, and **Hard invariants**). This does not itself ratify the philosophy, but it demonstrates that principles 5–11 were structurally integrated rather than appended as commentary.

11. **Phase A correctly preserved the version question until authority was supplied.**
    Regent and Sage identified the eight-versus-fifteen delta and refused to infer amendment authority merely from later wording (`outputs/council-phase-a/regent-ingestion.md`, `comprehension_summary`, `possible_equivalences`, and `questions`; `outputs/council-phase-a/sage-ingestion-synthesis.md`, **Translation deltas**, **Comprehension tests**, and **Readiness assessment**). Canon reconciliation confirmed that BARS contains no shared source-version registry capable of deciding the relationship (`outputs/council-phase-a/regent-canon-crosswalk.md`, **additions_in_new_ontology** and **unresolved_authority_questions**; `outputs/council-phase-a/sage-canon-reconciliation.md`, **source_inventory_and_authority** and **Eight versus fifteen constitutional principles**).

12. **The present targeted delegation supplies the previously missing decision authority.**
    Wendell has now delegated this bounded constitutional-evolution question to the six Game Masters. That delegation authorizes a decision about version relationship, but not general reduction, disposition sorting, resolution of BARS conflicts, or final verification of each principle through play.

## evidence_of_evolution

### The changes follow the documented process rather than contradict it

The process roadmap explicitly placed philosophy drafting after excavation, pattern extraction, and framework translation (`design-ontology-process-log.md`, **Ontology-development sequence**). The seven additions move through the expected maturity sequence:

> source response → excavation hypothesis → recurring pattern → candidate proposition → framework-bounded commitment → constitutional draft → formal model

That is the ontology’s own provenance grammar in action.

### Every inserted principle has multiple layers of support

- **P5 mechanical reality:** direct user correction in DCC; cross-specimen proposition; AQAL mechanical-attention requirement; model relations and declaration.
- **P6 development:** Pokémon response; DCC council refinement; AQAL multiline boundary; DevelopmentClaim model and hard invariant.
- **P7 relationship:** Pokémon relational progression and ally agency; cross-specimen causal-medium pattern; RelationalObjects and access-is-not-command invariant.
- **P8 performance:** direct Wendell correction in DCC; proxy-capture analysis; Performance entity and capability relation.
- **P9 mystery:** Monster Rancher and Inception evidence; epistemic-fairness pattern; Claim/Hypothesis/Test model and preserve-mystery invariant.
- **P10 player meaning:** direct Inception response; six-face adaptive-world synthesis; adaptive-system authority boundary and correction/rejection controls.
- **P11 lineage:** direct Go excavation; cross-specimen developmental provenance; ProvenanceObjects and minimum-record requirements.

### The original eight also evolved rather than remaining textually fixed

The later philosophy does not merely insert seven headings. It refines the older principles using excavation findings: world-making expands Game Master play; intuition clarifies somatic evidence; consent and contextual credentials refine helper care; rest and witnessing expand composting; and proxy capture sharpens the motivation principle. Treating the original eight as the only valid text would discard these documented refinements as well as principles 5–11.

### The fifteen form a coherent constitutional sequence

The successor order has an intelligible architecture:

1. principles 1–4 establish whose play and evidence initiate design;
2. principles 5–11 govern what games make real through development, relationship, performance, mystery, meaning, and lineage;
3. principles 12–15 govern care, remediation, completion, and motivational integrity.

The inserted principles therefore supply the missing bridge between originating epistemology and downstream ethical practice. They are not a miscellaneous middle list.

### The formal model depends on the inserted layer

Mechanical attention, DevelopmentClaim, CoordinationPattern, Performance, player-authored meaning, AdaptiveSystem permissions, and ProvenanceRecord are structural parts of `design-ontology-model-v0.1.md`. Treating principles 5–11 as external commentary would create an unexplained gap between the constitution and its formal model.

### Prior Phase A non-decision was procedural, not negative evidence

Phase A declined to choose because its mandate prohibited governance and because explicit authority had not yet been supplied. Its preservation of the delta demonstrates procedural integrity; it does not show that the two lists were intended to remain coequal indefinitely. The present delegation cures that authority gap for this single question.

## decision

**The fifteen-principle philosophy is the amended and expanded successor to the original eight-principle constitutional seed.**

The relationship is **constitutional evolution by provenance-bearing amendment**, not replacement without lineage, not mere commentary, and not permanent coexistence as two coequal constitutions.

For version interpretation:

- The eight-principle set is the **constitutional seed/baseline**: the historical source that preserves the original commitments and their earlier wording.
- The fifteen-principle set is the **current v0.1 constitutional draft**: it carries forward all eight lineages, refines their language, and adds seven principles developed through the documented research process.
- For future references within this ontology package, “the constitutional principles” should mean the fifteen-principle v0.1 set unless a record explicitly refers to the original eight-principle seed or compares versions.
- The original eight remain authoritative as provenance and amendment history. They are not erased, silently rewritten, or treated as a rival operative enumeration.
- Where the later wording materially changes an original principle, the later wording governs the current draft while the earlier wording remains inspectable as source history.

This decision establishes **version relationship and current-reference precedence only**. It does not declare the fifteen empirically verified, permanently immutable, universally applicable, reconciled with every BARS canon conflict, or exempt from later authorized amendment. The philosophy and model remain v0.1 and provisional in maturity.

## rationale

1. **Same owner and same declared purpose:** Both documents belong to Wendell and address the same design ontology for games, tools, AI collaborators, and Game Masters. There is no evidence that the fifteen were authored for a separate constitutional domain.

2. **Complete lineage preservation:** Every original principle has a recognizable successor. None disappears. Renumbering follows insertion, not abandonment.

3. **Documented evidence for every addition:** Principles 5–11 can each be traced to direct excavation, cross-specimen recurrence, framework translation, and formal modeling.

4. **Later document position is substantively meaningful:** Later chronology alone would not establish authority, as Phase A correctly observed. Here, chronology is joined by explicit process position, shared ownership, traceable derivation, coherent restructuring, and the present delegated decision authority.

5. **The successor is more executable:** The added principles explain the model’s central objects and hard invariants. Keeping only eight would leave mechanical attention, development, relational agency, performance, mystery, meaning authority, and provenance without constitutional grounding despite their repeated evidence.

6. **Co-equal coexistence would create avoidable governance ambiguity:** Agents would be unable to answer how many principles govern or whether principles 5–11 are optional. A provenance-bearing successor preserves history without producing two simultaneous constitutional counts.

7. **“Replacement” would misdescribe the actual change:** The later set retains all eight commitments and expands them. Amendment is the accurate governance term because it preserves continuity while recognizing substantive additions and textual refinements.

8. **The decision is reversible through the ontology’s own governance values:** The eight remain available for comparison, and the fifteen remain provisional and contestable. Current-reference precedence does not require historical deletion or metaphysical finality.

## residual_uncertainty

- This decision does not resolve conflicts between the fifteen-principle draft and local BARS canon, including face-as-altitude, action-gated completion, system-derived movement, or Yellow Brick Road transformation pressure (`outputs/council-phase-a/regent-canon-crosswalk.md`, **conflicts**).
- The documents still lack a general amendment protocol specifying proposal, consultation, approval threshold, effective date, appeal, and later supersession. The present explicit delegation authorizes this decision but does not automatically create that reusable protocol.
- “Constitutional” still names a provisional v0.1 commitment layer rather than a play-verified final constitution. Playable verification may later justify revision.
- Principle 12’s changed treatment of benevolence warrants future clarification: benevolence remains conceptually important, but the later constitutional wording foregrounds capability, integrity, boundaries, consent, and contextual credentials.
- Principle 10’s player authority concerns inward personal meaning. It does not settle disputes about shared facts, consent, relational harm, or system consequences.
- Principle 11’s provenance requirement does not yet define how much trace is sufficient or when privacy requires deletion, anonymization, or deliberately broken lineage.
- The exact version label for the eight-principle seed was never formally assigned. Calling it the “seed/baseline” describes its decided relationship without creating a new machine-readable version identifier.
- The other five Game Master decisions may identify different losses, boundaries, or wording needs. Their later synthesis may refine the council’s collective rationale, but should preserve the documented provenance before altering this Regent decision.
