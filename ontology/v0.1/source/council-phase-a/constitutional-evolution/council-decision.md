# Six-Face Council Decision — Constitutional Evolution

**Decision date:** 2026-10-10
**Decision scope:** The constitutional relationship between the original eight principles and the later fifteen
**Authority:** Wendell's targeted delegation to the six Game Master council
**Status:** Decided for version lineage and current-reference precedence only
**Source face records:** Architect, Challenger, Diplomat, Regent, and Shaman decisions in this directory, synthesized here by the Sage

## decision

The six-face council decides that the fifteen principles in `outputs/design-philosophy-draft-v0.1.md` are the **current provisional v0.1 constitution**, produced through a **provenance-preserving amendment and restatement** of the original eight-principle constitutional seed in `outputs/design-ontology-six-face-council-brief.md`.

The precise relationship is:

```text
eight-principle constitutional seed
  --amended, expanded, and restated as-->
fifteen-principle provisional v0.1 constitution
```

Accordingly:

1. The original eight remain the attributed constitutional seed and historical provenance layer.
2. All eight lineages continue in the fifteen; none was repealed or rejected.
3. Seven principles were added after aesthetic excavation, cross-specimen extraction, and framework translation.
4. The fifteen supersede the eight **only as the complete current membership set for v0.1**.
5. The eight are not a co-equal operative alternative, but they remain authoritative evidence of origin, earlier wording, and constitutional development.
6. Where inherited wording differs, the fifteen-principle wording governs present v0.1 interpretation while the earlier wording remains inspectable.

This is not a decision that the fifteen are final, universal, empirically verified, or superior to every independent BARS source. It is a bounded decision about succession, membership, and lineage inside the new design-ontology package.

### Six-face convergence and dissent

The five preserved face decisions and the Sage synthesis converge on the same substantive judgment:

- **Architect:** additive constitutional amendment and successor version; `amends_and_expands`.
- **Challenger:** expanded successor restatement, after testing accidental rewrite, unrelated constitutions, total invalidation, and chronology-only explanations.
- **Diplomat:** superseding amendment by preservation and expansion, with the fifteen as the current reciprocal play contract.
- **Regent:** amended and expanded successor, with later wording governing the current draft.
- **Shaman:** amended-and-restated living successor that preserves the seed as ancestry.
- **Sage synthesis:** provenance-preserving amendment and restatement, with current-reference supersession but no historical erasure.

There is **no substantive dissent** about the constitutional relationship. The faces use different governance metaphors—amendment, successor restatement, superseding amendment, and living continuation—but those differences concern emphasis rather than outcome. All reject three alternatives: two permanently co-equal constitutions, a merely explanatory elaboration, and replacement that invalidates or erases the eight.

## what_changed

The constitutional membership changed from eight principles to fifteen.

The later set retained and refined the original eight while inserting seven new constitutional commitments:

5. **Mechanics make claims about reality.**
6. **Development changes possibility, not personal worth.**
7. **Relationship is a source of capability.**
8. **Play may precede understanding.**
9. **Mystery must reward inquiry.**
10. **The player authors personal meaning.**
11. **Preserve lineage from seed to result.**

The change was not only numerical. It added a constitutional middle layer connecting the original participatory and somatic commitments to the original care, practice, completion, and meaning commitments. That middle layer governs:

- what mechanics make ontologically consequential;
- how development may change capability without ranking a whole person;
- how relationship, consent, trust, and distinct agency create capability;
- how performance may cultivate capacity before identity or explanation;
- what epistemic fairness requires from mystery;
- where authority over a player's personal meaning resides;
- how an emergent result remains connected to its developmental history.

The inherited principles also acquired clarified scope. Game Master play includes world-seeding and discovery; designer play includes learning through creation; psychographic-neighborhood design becomes authentic pleasure followed by outward translation; somatic evidence is explicitly fallible; helper care gains consent and contextual credentials; self-practicing tools become playable remediation; composting includes rest, witness, ambiguity, delegation, and release; and meaning-over-motivation gains the more precise failure concept of proxy capture.

## authority_effect

This decision has the following authority effects inside the new design-ontology package:

1. **Default retrieval:** “The constitutional principles” means the fifteen principles in `outputs/design-philosophy-draft-v0.1.md` unless a request explicitly asks for the original eight, the seed, or constitutional history.
2. **Current interpretation:** The later wording controls v0.1 design, AI, Game Master, and ontology interpretation where it differs from the seed wording.
3. **Provenance preservation:** The eight remain attached as the origin record. They must not be deleted, silently overwritten, or described as errors merely because the fifteen are current.
4. **No optional middle:** Principles 5–11 are constitutional additions, not commentary that an agent may omit while claiming to apply the complete v0.1 constitution.
5. **No dual constitution:** The two lists are not co-equal operative enumerations. Teaching may be layered or simplified, but simplification must preserve the function and protections of the complete set.
6. **Provisional maturity:** Current precedence does not change the philosophy's draft status or exempt any principle from playable verification and later authorized amendment.
7. **Bounded jurisdiction:** The ruling does not decide precedence between this v0.1 constitution and independent BARS foundations, implemented Emotional Alchemy doctrine, runtime rules, or strand specifications.
8. **No collateral governance:** The ruling does not merge, prune, deprecate, compost, or assign active/reference/compost dispositions to any other ontology material.

## residual_uncertainty

- **Playable verification:** The relationship is decided, but the fifteen have not thereby been proven equally effective in play.
- **Exact wording:** Individual principles remain revisable; this decision fixes collection lineage and current precedence, not immutable sentences.
- **General amendment protocol:** The ontology still lacks a reusable procedure for proposing, testing, approving, appealing, and dating future constitutional changes.
- **Exact seed version label:** The brief calls itself a provisional seed but does not formally declare `v0.0`. “Eight-principle seed” is authoritative here; `v0.0` should remain an explanatory shorthand unless separately adopted.
- **Effective-date precision:** The documentary promotion point is Stage 7 and the current relationship decision is dated 2026-10-10, but no separate contemporaneous ratification timestamp exists for the philosophy draft.
- **Other normative layers:** The relationship among constitutional principles, hard invariants, guardrails, framework boundaries, and runtime rules still needs explicit authority and exception semantics.
- **BARS canon:** Face/altitude doctrine, WAVE vocabulary, completion requirements, consent granularity, disclosure signals, and other canon deltas remain outside this ruling.
- **Representation completeness:** The YAML does not yet encode the collections or their amendment lineage, so agents reading only that artifact may still retrieve an incomplete constitutional picture.
- **Future grouping and teaching:** The complete content is decided for v0.1, but its best clusters, sequence, metaphors, and lower-context teaching interfaces remain subject to playtesting.
- **Provenance limits:** The requirement to preserve lineage still needs privacy, retention, anonymization, and deliberate-deletion boundaries.

None of these uncertainties reopens the decided relationship: the eight are the preserved seed; the fifteen are the current provisional v0.1 constitution; and the relationship is amendment-and-restatement with provenance-bearing succession.
