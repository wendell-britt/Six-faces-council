# Constitutional Evolution Decision — Shaman

**Decision scope:** Relationship between the original eight constitutional principles and the later fifteen
**Authority for this decision:** Wendell’s targeted delegation to the six Game Masters
**Status:** Decided for constitutional version lineage; no broader pruning, canon precedence, or work-disposition decision
**Method:** Reconstruct what changed, where it changed, and when in the documented process before deciding the relationship

## documented_timeline

The sources do not provide a reliable calendar timestamp for every conceptual change. “When” below therefore means the explicit stage order documented in `outputs/design-ontology-process-log.md` § “Ontology-development sequence,” supported by each artifact’s declared status and cross-references.

1. **Seed constitution — before the completed aesthetic excavation.**
   `outputs/design-ontology-six-face-council-brief.md` § “Constitutional principles” records eight principles and identifies the document as a “Provisional ontology seed.” They establish the initial vessel: Game Master and designer participation, psychographic-neighborhood design, somatic evidence, care for helpers, reflexive tools, composting, and meaning over engagement. The later excavation already refers back to “The Game Master is also a player” as an existing constitutional principle (`outputs/aesthetic-source-research-round-1.md` § “Excavation response 8 — nested seeding joins Game Master and player”), confirming that the eight-principle set preceded that finding.

2. **Pokémon excavation — development and relationship become ontological rather than merely thematic.**
   `outputs/aesthetic-source-research-round-1.md` §§ “Excavation response 1 — development makes work consequential,” “Excavation response 3 — relationship is the carrier of development,” “Excavation response 4 — alliance combines rather than absorbs agency,” and “Excavation response 5 — access is not command” establish that development must preserve consequential work, relationship creates capability, allies retain agency, and access is not command. These are the source lineage of later principles 6 and 7.

3. **Monster Rancher excavation — inferable mystery, stewardship, and player sovereignty become explicit.**
   `outputs/aesthetic-source-research-round-1.md` §§ “Excavation response 2 — pattern literacy becomes stewardship,” “Excavation response 4 — Emotional Alchemy satisfaction has priority,” “Excavation response 8 — adaptive curation with player sovereignty,” and “Excavation response 9 — optional explanation as a reflective layer” establish mystery with incomplete certainty, situated expertise, separate inner and external evidence, opt-out, and contestable interpretation. These findings feed later principles 9 and 10 and deepen the original helper-care principle.

4. **Inception excavation and its six-face consultation — personal meaning and adaptive otherness become constitutional candidates.**
   `outputs/aesthetic-source-research-round-1.md` §§ “Excavation response 3 — concealed truth must remain inferable,” “Excavation response 4 — revelation preserves evidence-bounded plurality,” “Excavation response 6 — designed conditions, player-authored inner meaning,” “Excavation response 7 — adaptive reading becomes playable world material,” and “Six Game Master council response — a living world acquires otherness” establish epistemically fair mystery, evidence-bounded plurality, player authority over inner meaning, and a world larger than the player model. The provisional synthesis explicitly remained unratified at that stage. “Excavation response 8 — nested seeding joins Game Master and player” also expands original principle 1: the Game Master’s play is not only labor protection but a source of ontological depth.

5. **Go excavation — intuition and lineage become visible design commitments.**
   `outputs/aesthetic-source-research-round-1.md` §§ “Excavation response 3 — intuition enters play before it becomes explicit” and “Excavation response 4 — lineage matters more than proving prior intent” establish enacted intuition and developmental provenance. The result can grow beyond conscious prior intent while remaining causally descended from the original gesture. These findings feed later principles 8 and 11 and deepen original principle 4, “The body gets a vote.”

6. **Dungeon Crawler Carl excavation and six-face consultation — mechanical attention and performance are corrected and promoted.**
   `outputs/aesthetic-source-research-round-1.md` §§ “Excavation response 1 — the limitation is what the system models,” “Six Game Master council response — development changes possibility, not personal worth,” and “Excavation response 2 — gameplay performance is generative, not counterfeit” establish that mechanics confer ontological weight, development changes possible action rather than personal value, and performance may cultivate capacity before identity or understanding. “Excavation response 3 — guardrail adequacy emerges through play” narrows the failure from performance itself to proxy capture. ## evidence_of_evolution

- **No original constitutional commitment was discarded.** The mapping is complete: brief 1–4 survive as philosophy 1–4, and brief 5–8 survive as philosophy 12–15. This exact mapping was independently recognized during Phase A in `regent-ingestion.md` § `possible_equivalences`, `diplomat-ingestion.md` §§ `comprehension_summary` and `possible_equivalences`, and `sage-ingestion-synthesis.md` § “apparent_equivalences.”

- **The numerical expansion has a coherent structural location.** Seven additions occupy philosophy positions 5–11 between the original somatic/participatory opening and the original care/process/completion closing. They answer what became newly visible through play: what mechanics make real, what development means, how relationship creates capacity, how performance relates to understanding, what mystery owes inquiry, who authors inner meaning, and how results retain ancestry.

- **The additions were not generated in a single abstract drafting act.** Each appears first as user evidence or council-tested excavation, then as a provisional candidate, then as a cross-specimen pattern, then as a framework-bounded proposition, and only afterward as constitutional language. The process sequence in `design-ontology-process-log.md` §§ “Ontology-development sequence” and “Current decision” confirms this order.

- **The intermediate documents explicitly prevent retrospective inflation.** `design-ontology-pattern-checkpoint-1.md` says its candidates are not ratified; `design-ontology-pattern-extraction-round-1.md` says its twelve propositions are not yet constitutional; `design-ontology-framework-translation-round-1.md` remains non-ratifying but declares readiness for a philosophy draft. Therefore the promotion point is documentable rather than implied.

- **The later principles change the explanatory center of the constitution without repudiating its seed.** The eight principles primarily establish who must be cared for and how design should be practiced. The seven additions establish what the designed reality is allowed to claim about development, relationship, performance, mystery, meaning, and lineage. The later constitution is therefore more than commentary on the eight, but it remains genetically continuous with them.

- **The formal model treats the new principles as load-bearing.** `design-ontology-model-v0.1.md` operationalizes mechanical attention, contextual DevelopmentClaims, CoordinationPattern, Performance, adaptive meaning authority, and provenance. This would be disproportionate if principles 5–11 were merely marginal annotations.

- **Phase A uncertainty concerned authority, not missing lineage.** The face records consistently map the sets and identify 5–11 as additions; they withheld only the relationship decision. `sage-canon-reconciliation.md` § “owner_corrections_requested,” question 3, presents supersede/amend/elaborate/candidate as the unresolved alternatives. The present delegation permits selection among those alternatives.

## decision

**The later fifteen are the amended-and-restated successor to the original eight.**

For the new design ontology’s version lineage:

- the eight-principle set is the **seed constitution (v0.0 lineage)**;
- the fifteen-principle set in `outputs/design-philosophy-draft-v0.1.md` § “I. Constitutional principles” is the **current provisional constitution (v0.1)**;
- the later set **supersedes the earlier set as the current complete statement**, while preserving the original eight as attributed ancestors rather than deleting or composting them;
- principles 1–4 and 12–15 are carried-forward principles, some with clarified scope;
- principles 5–11 are evidence-bearing constitutional additions produced by the documented excavation and translation process;
- the two lists are **not co-equal competing constitutions**, and the seven additions are **not merely commentary** on the original eight.

“Amended and restated” is chosen deliberately. **Amended** recognizes that seven constitutional commitments were added and several inherited commitments were materially clarified. **Restated** recognizes that the philosophy draft presents one complete fifteen-principle layer rather than an eight-principle text plus a detached amendment sheet.

This decision resolves only the internal version relationship between these two new-ontology documents. It does not declare the fifteen empirically verified, immutable, superior to BARS canon in every domain, or exempt from later revision through the ontology’s own evidence and governance processes.

## rationale

From the Shaman face, a living constitution should preserve ancestry without requiring the seed to remain the final visible form of the organism.

Calling the fifteen a mere elaboration would deny that the excavation changed what the ontology treats as constitutionally real. Mechanics, development, relationship, performance, mystery, meaning authority, and lineage are not footnotes; they became the grammar through which the original commitments can be enacted without collapsing into good intentions.

Calling the fifteen a replacement would erase ancestry. Every original principle survives, and several later principles grew by exposing latent implications already carried by the seed: the body’s vote becomes enacted intuition; Game Master play becomes world-seeding; care for helpers becomes situated helping authority; meaning over motivation becomes the proxy-capture boundary; composting becomes legitimate rest and release.

Calling both lists co-equal would create avoidable ambiguity every time an agent is asked which constitution to apply. It would also break developmental provenance: the documented process shows one sequence of growth, not two independent constitutional traditions.

Amendment-and-restatement best preserves all four relevant truths:

1. **continuity:** none of the eight was discarded;
2. **real change:** seven commitments were promoted into the constitutional layer;
3. **lineage:** the earlier form remains inspectable and credited;
4. **current usability:** agents can retrieve one complete provisional constitutional set.

This relationship also practices the ontology it expresses. The early gesture was allowed to develop through encounter; later evidence helped determine what it became; and artifacts preserve the route from seed to current form.

## residual_uncertainty

- **Substantive ratification remains provisional.** This decision establishes version lineage and the currently applicable draft set. It does not convert every principle into empirically verified or permanently ratified BARS canon.

- **External canon precedence remains unresolved.** The relationship between the fifteen-principle v0.1 constitution and BARS foundations, implemented Emotional Alchemy doctrine, runtime rules, strand specifications, and functional face doctrine remains governed by the separate conflicts recorded in `outputs/council-phase-a/sage-canon-reconciliation.md`. This decision does not silently resolve them.

- **The exact effective date is documentary rather than calendrical.** The promotion occurs at Stage 7, the philosophy draft, in the process sequence. The files do not record a separate ratification timestamp.

- **“Constitutional principle,” “hard invariant,” “guardrail,” and “framework boundary” still have unresolved differences in amendment authority and exception handling.** The fifteen-principle list is now the current complete constitutional statement, but its relationship to those other normative forms remains open.

- **Some inherited wording may continue to evolve.** The decision preserves the mapping and succession relationship, not every sentence as immutable language.

- **Later evidence may amend the fifteen again.** Such change should create a new version with a visible map from the fifteen, just as this decision preserves the map from eight to fifteen.

- **The labels `v0.0 lineage` and `v0.1` describe the relationship decided here.** The source files themselves call the brief a provisional seed and the philosophy “Draft v0.1”; they do not contain an explicit `v0.0` field for the eight. The lineage label should therefore be understood as a reconstruction, not a retroactive claim that an earlier formal release event occurred.
