## documented_timeline

### 1. The eight principles formed the pre-excavation constitutional seed

The first explicit constitutional collection appears in `outputs/design-ontology-six-face-council-brief.md`, **2. Constitutional principles**:

1. The Game Master is also a player.
2. The game designer is also a player.
3. Design first for the designer’s psychographic neighborhood.
4. The body gets a vote.
5. The helper belongs inside the field of care.
6. The tool practices what it teaches.
7. Composting is a valid completion.
8. Motivation does not substitute for meaning.

This seed was already an integrated response to Wendell’s early design intentions, psychograph, Emotional Alchemy, AQAL, Octalysis, somatics, helping orientation, and known design shadows. The brief calls it a “provisional ontology seed” and instructs the council not to prune, merge, or govern it during Phase A. See `design-ontology-six-face-council-brief.md`, **Council mandate**, **Purpose**, and **2. Constitutional principles**.

In the process sequence, this material precedes the completed aesthetic excavation and later philosophy draft. `outputs/design-ontology-process-log.md`, **Ontology-development sequence**, places aesthetic intake, research, excavation, pattern extraction, and framework translation before the formal design-philosophy draft.

### 2. Excavation produced seven additional constitutional-grade claims

The additional material did not appear as an arbitrary rewrite. It emerged specimen by specimen:

- **Relationship and development:** Pokémon excavation established consequential development, relationship as the carrier of development, access distinct from command, situated trust, and capability-specific credentials. See `outputs/aesthetic-source-research-round-1.md`, **Pokémon / Excavation responses 1–7** and **Provisional close of Pokémon excavation pass 1**.
- **Mystery and player meaning:** Monster Rancher excavation established coherent hidden mappings, inferable patterns, stewardship under uncertainty, adaptive curation, optional explanation, and player authority to correct or reject an interpretation. See `aesthetic-source-research-round-1.md`, **Monster Rancher / Excavation responses 1–9**.
- **Player authorship and adaptive otherness:** Inception excavation established bounded ambiguity, designed conditions with player-authored inner meaning, and player-derived imagery becoming explorable world material. The six-face consultation concluded that the player may seed the world without exhausting it. See `aesthetic-source-research-round-1.md`, **Inception / Excavation responses 4–7**, **Six Game Master council response — a living world acquires otherness**, and **Council synthesis candidate**.
- **Lineage:** Go excavation established that a later outcome need not prove fully conscious prior intent, but it must retain inspectable artifacts of how the forest developed from the seed. See `aesthetic-source-research-round-1.md`, **Go / Excavation response 4 — lineage matters more than proving prior intent** and **Ontology candidate — developmental provenance**.
- **Mechanical reality, contextual development, and performance:** Dungeon Crawler Carl excavation corrected the problem from “too much quantification” to a narrow ontology of mechanical attention; the council formulated development as changed possibility rather than personal worth; Wendell corrected the council’s suspicion of rewarded performance by asserting that gameplay performance generates story and may cultivate capability. See `aesthetic-source-research-round-1.md`, **Dungeon Crawler Carl / Excavation response 1 — the limitation is what the system models**, **Six Game Master council response — development changes possibility, not personal worth**, and **Excavation response 2 — gameplay performance is generative, not counterfeit**.

### 4. Cross-specimen extraction converted the findings into twelve candidate propositions

After all five specimens, `outputs/design-ontology-pattern-extraction-round-1.md` identified a central movement linking mystery, development, relationship, performance, and provenance. See **1. Central design movement** and **2. Recurring aesthetic forces**.

- hide enough for discovery and reveal enough for inference;
- let adaptive systems propose relevance while players author personal meaning;
- let the player influence the world without becoming its sole cause.

At this stage these were expressly “not yet constitutional principles.”

### 5. Framework translation tested and revised the candidates

`outputs/design-ontology-framework-translation-round-1.md` examined the candidates through AQAL, Emotional Alchemy, Octalysis, somatic verification, and BAR lifecycle. It preserved framework limits, evidence authorities, and tensions rather than treating pattern recurrence alone as sufficient.

Important transitions appear in:

- **2. Emotional Alchemy translation:** performance may precede identification, while inner satisfaction and allyship application remain distinct.
- **3. Octalysis translation:** development, creativity, relatedness, ownership, and curiosity are retained while engagement is denied authority to define meaning.
- **5. BAR lifecycle translation:** lineage and non-obligatory capture become operational lifecycle concerns.
- **8. - **9. Readiness for the design-philosophy draft:** the material is judged ready for a first compact constitutional layer.

This is the point at which the seven later additions moved from specimen-specific findings to cross-framework-supported design commitments.

### 6. The philosophy draft promoted the seven additions while retaining all eight originals

`outputs/design-philosophy-draft-v0.1.md`, **I. Constitutional principles**, presents fifteen principles as “the most stable commitments in the current design philosophy” and says later design choices should answer to them explicitly.

The draft does not discard any original principle. It retains the original four opening principles, inserts seven newly supported principles as 5–11, and then retains the original final four as 12–15 with added precision.

Its process metadata places it **after aesthetic excavation and framework translation** and **before formal ontology modeling and council governance**, confirming that the larger set is a later synthesis rather than an alternative early draft.

## evidence_of_evolution

The record supports genuine constitutional evolution rather than accidental duplication for five reasons.

### 1. The additions have traceable empirical-aesthetic provenance

Each new principle can be traced backward through named specimens, Wendell’s corrections, council consultations, and intermediate synthesis. They were not introduced only in the philosophy draft.

### 2. The additions recur across more than one specimen

The cross-specimen central movement explicitly links mystery, development, relationship, performance, and provenance. See `design-ontology-pattern-extraction-round-1.md`, **1. Central design movement**. The philosophy’s new principles are separate constitutional expressions of that recurrent structure.

### 3. The additions passed through an explicit promotion pipeline

Their recorded maturity path is:

```text
excavation finding
  → checkpoint candidate
  → cross-specimen aesthetic force
  → candidate design-philosophy proposition
  → framework translation and revision
  → constitutional principle in philosophy v0.1
  → formal ontology objects, relations, and invariants
  → Phase A council ingestion
```

This sequence is documented across `design-ontology-process-log.md`, `design-ontology-pattern-checkpoint-1.md`, `design-ontology-pattern-extraction-round-1.md`, `design-ontology-framework-translation-round-1.md`, `design-philosophy-draft-v0.1.md`, and `design-ontology-model-v0.1.md`.

### 4. The later set preserves identity continuity

All eight seed commitments survive recognizably. Their order is preserved in two blocks—1–4 and 12–15—and their later wording adds constraints learned during excavation. This is an additive amendment pattern, not replacement by an unrelated constitution.

### 5. The ontology model depends on the added principles

Removing principles 5–11 would leave major modeled objects and invariants without an explicit constitutional source: mechanical attention, contextual development, relational agency, performance/capability separation, epistemic fairness, player meaning authority, adaptive otherness, and provenance. The formal model therefore supplies downstream evidence that the later philosophy was intended as a coherent expanded foundation.

## decision

**The later fifteen principles are an additive constitutional amendment and successor version of the original eight.**

More precisely:

1. The original eight constitute the **constitutional seed collection**.
2. The later fifteen constitute the **v0.1 expanded constitutional collection**.
3. The relationship is `amends_and_expands`, not simple duplication and not merely explanatory elaboration.
4. For work using the v0.1 design philosophy and ontology, the fifteen-principle collection **supersedes the eight-principle list as the current provisional membership set**.
5. That supersession does not invalidate or erase the original eight. They remain the provenance-bearing ancestral collection, and all eight continue inside the fifteen by mapped identity and elaboration.
6. The seven inserted principles are constitutional additions, not provisional commitments accidentally placed in the wrong section. Their multi-stage derivation and later formal modeling justify their promotion.

Formal lineage:

```text
constitutional_set_8
  --amended_and_expanded_by-->
constitutional_set_15_v0.1

constitutional_set_15_v0.1
  --preserves_principle_identity_of-->
constitutional_set_8.principles[1..8]

constitutional_set_15_v0.1
  --adds-->
[mechanical_reality,
 development_as_changed_possibility,
 relational_capability,
 performative_cultivation,
 epistemically_fair_mystery,
 player_authored_personal_meaning,
 developmental_provenance]
```

This decision resolves the relationship between the two collections. It does not change the provisional status of the philosophy as a whole, declare the principles empirically verified, or decide their authority over independent BARS canon.

## rationale

“Elaboration” alone is too weak because principles 5–11 impose independent obligations that cannot be derived merely by restating the original eight. For example, none of the original eight alone requires epistemically fair mystery, preserves player authority over personal meaning, or defines development as changed possibility rather than worth.

“Mere candidate additions” is inconsistent with the documented process. The additions were candidates during pattern extraction, but after framework translation they were deliberately placed in the constitutional section, described as the current philosophy’s most stable commitments, used as the source of formal modeling, and successfully ingested by the council.

“Supersession” without qualification is also inaccurate because it suggests the eight were displaced or invalidated. The later collection retains every seed principle and strengthens rather than repudiates them.

The precise architectural relationship is therefore **versioned additive amendment**:

- amendment explains the changed membership;
- expansion explains the seven new independent commitments;
- successor version identifies which collection the v0.1 ontology references;
- preserved provenance prevents historical erasure and allows later review of exactly what changed.

This structure also resolves the retrieval problem identified in `outputs/council-phase-a/architect-ingestion.md`, **face_translation**: an AI should no longer retrieve two coequal constitutional collections without a relationship. It should retrieve the fifteen-principle v0.1 set for current ontology work, attach the eight-principle seed as provenance when constitutional history or wording matters, and continue to label the entire philosophy provisional until later verification changes that status.

## residual_uncertainty

- The wording of individual principles remains revisable. `design-philosophy-draft-v0.1.md`, **I. Constitutional principles**, explicitly says wording may improve.
- This decision establishes collection lineage and current v0.1 membership; it does not establish that every principle has survived playable verification.
- The seven additions are distinct enough to remain separate at this stage, but later evidence may reveal overlaps or boundary changes. This decision does not authorize merging or pruning them.
- The relationship between the fifteen-principle v0.1 constitution and existing BARS canon remains outside this targeted decision. Phase A canon records preserve those authority questions separately.
- The machine-readable YAML does not yet instantiate either constitutional collection, their membership, identity mappings, or their `amends_and_expands` relation. That is a representation gap, not uncertainty about this decision.
- The philosophy’s status remains “provisional design-philosophy draft.” “Current constitutional collection” therefore means current within v0.1, not eternally fixed or universally binding.
- The present decision does not determine whether a future v0.2 should retain all fifteen, add others, or revise their grouping. Any such change should preserve this lineage rather than silently overwrite it.
