## evidence_of_evolution

### Evidence supporting genuine evolution

- **Explicit pipeline:** The process log names pattern extraction, framework translation, and philosophy drafting as successive ontology-development stages. Source: `outputs/design-ontology-process-log.md` § “Ontology-development sequence,” steps 5–7, and § “Current decision.”
- **Promotion language:** Pattern extraction explicitly labels the twelve statements candidates “not yet constitutional,” while framework translation explicitly prepares the next constitutional draft. The later philosophy then labels a selected subset constitutional. This is documentary evidence of selection and promotion, not an inference from filenames.
- **Conservation of the seed:** Every original principle remains recognizable in the later set, with the same two opening principles, the body principle in the same fourth position, and the final four shifted intact to positions 12–15.
- **Traceable novelty:** Each of the seven additions has a source-specific excavation history, a cross-specimen abstraction, and framework-level refinement before appearing in the constitutional section.
- **Downstream uptake:** The formal model operationalizes the seven additions rather than ignoring them. The model’s stated purpose is to translate the philosophy, not the earlier brief, into ontology vocabulary.
- **Independent council recognition:** Architect, Challenger, Regent, and Sage Phase A records separately identify the same 8-preserved/7-added structure. This convergence supports the textual map, though it does not by itself create authority.

### Challenger attempts to falsify the evolution claim

1. **Alternative: the fifteen are merely an accidental longer rewrite.**
   Not supported. The seven additions closely match named candidate propositions and framework revisions upstream.

2. **Alternative: the two lists are unrelated constitutions for different environments.**
   Not supported. The fifteen retain all eight in recognizable order, and the philosophy describes the same target domain: games, design tools, AI collaborators, and Game Master agents.

3. **Alternative: the fifteen wholly supersede and invalidate the eight.**
   Not supported. The later list conserves the eight, the ontology values provenance, and no source states that the seed was erroneous or revoked.

4. **Alternative: the seven additions remain only provisional commitments despite their placement.**
   Partly falsified. The philosophy explicitly places them in its constitutional section rather than § “II. Provisional design commitments.” However, the whole v0.1 philosophy remains provisional and awaits playable verification; constitutional placement indicates relative stability, not final truth.

5. **Alternative: chronology alone establishes amendment.**
   Rejected as method. The decision rests on explicit upstream/downstream instructions, candidate-to-principle textual correspondence, conservation of the eight, and formal-model uptake—not on later file order.

## decision

The later fifteen-principle set is the **expanded successor restatement of the original eight-principle seed constitution**.

More precisely:

- the original eight are the **constitutional seed and provenance layer**;
- all eight continue in the later constitution, with limited clarification or elaboration;
- seven principles were intentionally promoted through excavation, cross-specimen pattern extraction, and framework translation;
- the resulting fifteen constitute **Design Philosophy v0.1’s current provisional constitutional set**;
- this is an **evolutionary amendment by expansion**, not a competing constitution, silent replacement, or claim that the original eight were invalid;
- future references to “the constitution” for v0.1 should use the fifteen while retaining the eight-to-fifteen map as constitutional lineage.

This decision resolves only the relationship between the two collections. It does not claim that every principle has been empirically verified, settle conflicts with BARS canon, or authorize broader ontology reduction and disposition sorting.

## rationale

“Expanded successor restatement” best fits all available evidence while making the fewest unsupported claims.

- **Successor** reflects the documented process: the philosophy is downstream of completed excavation and framework translation, and the formal model explicitly follows the philosophy.
- **Expanded** reflects the exact arithmetic and content: all eight survive and seven traceable principles are added.
- **Restatement** preserves wording and scope changes without pretending the eight were copied verbatim in every case.
- **Provisional** respects the draft’s stated status and the ontology’s rule that principles remain hypotheses until they survive play.
- **Provenance layer** prevents the later list from erasing why the project began with the original commitments.

The alternative of maintaining two co-equal constitutions would create artificial ambiguity after the historical trace has become clear. The alternative of declaring supersession would violate the observable conservation of the eight and obscure lineage. The chosen relationship therefore preserves evolution without manufacturing a rupture.

## residual_uncertainty

- No source records a formal amendment event at the moment the seven candidates entered the philosophy draft; the present delegated decision supplies the missing relationship judgment retrospectively.
- The record establishes why the seven were promoted, but not equal empirical support for each. Their constitutional status is provisional and should remain open to playable verification.
- “Begin with authentic pleasure, then translate outward” is clearly descended from the psychographic-neighborhood principle, but the later phrasing may subtly broaden its scope from audience selection to a general translation method.
- “Composting is a valid completion” gains rest, witnessing, and ambiguity in the later philosophy; the boundary between elaboration and expansion inside that single principle remains interpretive.
- The model operationalizes many later principles but does not instantiate the constitutional collection as machine-readable versioned objects, so an AI reading only the YAML could still miss this decision and lineage.
- BARS canon contains independent constitutional and doctrinal authority questions. This decision does not establish precedence between Design Philosophy v0.1 and BARS foundations, implemented doctrine, runtime rules, or strand specifications.
- Play may later show that a promoted principle is too broad, redundant, or poorly worded. Such evidence would support a future amendment; it does not change the documented relationship decided here.
