## evidence_of_evolution

### The later principles answer gaps exposed in actual dialogue

The original eight define who belongs in care, where design begins, and what broad failures to avoid. They do not yet tell an AI or designer:

- why a relationship must change capability rather than merely deliver rewards;
- why access to an ally is not command;
- why development must not become a whole-person ranking;
- why gameplay performance can be valid before identity or understanding;
- what separates living mystery from arbitrary opacity;
- who owns the meaning of adaptive or psychologically resonant content;
- why process lineage must survive the result.

Those questions were not invented during drafting. They arose through the specimen dialogues cited in the timeline and recur across multiple specimens in `outputs/design-ontology-pattern-extraction-round-1.md` → **Recurring aesthetic forces**.

### The seven additions survived framework limits

`outputs/design-ontology-framework-translation-round-1.md` does not merely praise the candidates. It specifies what AQAL, Emotional Alchemy, Octalysis, somatics, and BAR can and cannot decide. The later principles retain these jurisdictional boundaries:

- P6 prevents AQAL or credentials from becoming total rank.
- P7 and P10 prevent relationship and adaptive interpretation from erasing another agent's authority.
- P8 prevents authenticity policing while leaving proxy capture testable.
- P9 preserves both mystery and epistemic fairness.
- P11 makes provenance meaningful without claiming predestination.

Their final wording is therefore narrower and more usable than the raw aesthetic insights from which they emerged.

### The formal model depends on them

`outputs/design-ontology-model-v0.1.md` operationalizes the seven through:

- `makes_mechanically_real` and the integral declaration of mechanical attention;
- DevelopmentClaim, DevelopmentLine, Capability, Affordance, and the no-global-rank invariant;
- Relationship, Permission, ConsentRecord, TrustState, Repair, and CoordinationPattern;
- Performance distinguished from Capability, Credential, and identity;
- Claim, Hypothesis, EvidenceItem, Uncertainty, Interpretation, and Test;
- `authors_personal_meaning`, adaptive-system correction/rejection, and world otherness;
- Seed, Artifact, ProvenanceRecord, `records_path`, and `preserves_trace_of`.

Removing the seven from constitutional status while keeping the model would make the model's most important user protections appear technical or optional rather than expressions of the design philosophy.

### All six Phase A translations treated the added content as central

`outputs/council-phase-a/sage-ingestion-synthesis.md` → **invariant_vocabulary** includes mechanical attention, contextual development, performance-not-identity, relationship-generated capability, access-not-command, personal meaning, inferable mystery, world otherness, provenance, and transformation/application separation. That cross-face stability does not empirically verify the principles, but it demonstrates that the additions are coherent, teachable, and recognizable across the council's lenses.

### The change materially improves translation for users

For users, the move from eight to fifteen changes the constitution from a designer-centered ethical orientation into a reciprocal play contract:

- **P5** tells users that what the game tracks and ignores is a design claim, not neutral bookkeeping.
- **P6** protects them from being ranked as whole people by local performance, psychographs, badges, or developmental evidence.
- **P7** protects allies from becoming inventory and makes relational development mechanically consequential.
- **P8** lets lower-context and exploratory users learn by doing without first producing the correct identity statement or emotional explanation.
- **P9** promises that mystery can be investigated rather than accepted on authority.
- **P10** protects a user's right to reject AI, GM, helper, or community interpretations of their interior.
- **P11** lets users see how their choices and relationships mattered rather than receiving unexplained outcomes or context-free credentials.

These are not specialist embellishments. They determine whether the ontology welcomes users as co-authors and agents or merely applies a designer's values to them.

## decision

The fifteen principles in `outputs/design-philosophy-draft-v0.1.md` are the **current provisional constitutional restatement of the design ontology**.

The original eight in `outputs/design-ontology-six-face-council-brief.md` are the **constitutional seed and provenance layer** of that restatement. They remain historically and interpretively active because they show the ontology's originating commitments, but they are no longer a co-equal alternative constitution for this ontology package.

The relationship is **superseding amendment by preservation and expansion**, not repeal, silent replacement, or parallel coexistence:

1. Original P1–P4 continue as later P1–P4.
2. Original P5–P8 continue as later P12–P15.
3. Later P5–P11 are seven constitutional additions produced by the documented excavation, synthesis, and framework-translation process.
4. The fifteen-principle draft governs interpretation of the companion formal model v0.1 unless a later, explicitly authorized constitutional revision changes it.
5. The eight-principle source remains attached as provenance and may be used to explain the constitution's origin, but must not be presented to users or agents as the complete current constitutional layer.

This decision resolves only membership and succession between these two lists. It does not ratify each principle as empirically verified, decide conflicts with existing BARS canon, or authorize broader active/reference/compost governance.

## rationale

### A parallel-constitution reading would create avoidable user ambiguity

If both lists remained co-equal, users and agents could select the shorter list precisely when the omitted protections are inconvenient. An AI could honor “the body gets a vote” while still claiming authority over what the body means; reward relationship while treating allies as possessed assets; recognize development while ranking the person; or generate uncanny adaptation without making its interpretation rejectable. P5–P11 close those gaps.

### A repeal-and-replace reading would erase lineage

The later list deliberately preserves all eight originating commitments. Treating the eight as obsolete error would contradict P11 and the BAR commitment to provenance. The seed explains why helper care, designer/GM participation, embodiment, composting, and meaning remained constitutional while new principles were inserted around them.

### Superseding amendment matches the actual design process

The process log places philosophy drafting after excavation, pattern extraction, and framework translation. The later document is not an accidental summary; it is the stage where candidate propositions were intentionally converted into constitutional language. The model then assumes that expanded language. Preservation plus expansion accurately describes both chronology and substance.

### The fifteen improve lower-context translation when layered correctly

The original eight are not a safe “simple version” of the fifteen because the seven additions contain user rights and interpretive boundaries. Lower-context translation should simplify vocabulary, not omit those functions. For example:

- “Development changes possibility, not personal worth” can become “Getting better at one thing does not make the game judge your whole self.”
- “Relationship is a source of capability” can become “Some things become possible only when both sides choose to work together.”
- “The player authors personal meaning” can become “The game may offer a reading; you decide whether it is yours.”
- “Preserve lineage” can become “The game should be able to show how your earlier choices helped create what happened.”

Thus the constitution may be taught in layers or clusters, but every user-facing translation must preserve the operative protection carried by all fifteen and name any temporary omission.

### The decision protects helpers as well as recipients

The original helper-care principle remains, but P5–P11 explain the relational environment in which helper care operates. A helper needs contextual rather than global credentials, permission rather than presumed access, freedom to perform and learn without claiming identity, corrigible interpretations, and provenance that distinguishes their inner transformation from effects on another person. The expanded constitution therefore prevents both recipient erasure and helper over-obligation.

## residual_uncertainty

- The fifteen remain a **provisional v0.1 constitutional draft**. This decision establishes their relationship to the eight; it does not convert them into empirically verified universal truths.
- Exact wording may still change after playable verification, especially where “mystery must reward inquiry,” “player authors personal meaning,” and “play may precede understanding” meet multiplayer culture, facilitation, and institutional constraints.
- The threshold and procedure for future constitutional amendment are not yet formally modeled.
- The fifteen-principle philosophy and existing BARS canon still conflict or diverge on face-as-altitude language, four-stage versus five-move WAVE terminology, mandatory action completion, consent granularity, and disclosure-based depth signals. This decision does not resolve those separate authority questions; see `outputs/council-phase-a/diplomat-canon-crosswalk.md` → **conflicts** and `outputs/council-phase-a/sage-canon-reconciliation.md` → **exact_conflicts_and_deltas**.
- The best user-facing grouping of the fifteen is not yet playtested. The constitutional content is decided for this version; the sequence, interface, metaphor, and teaching depth remain design questions.
- Cross-face convergence demonstrates comprehension, not player transfer. Near-neighbor and lower-context users must still test whether the principles feel protective and playable rather than dense, moralizing, or administratively burdensome.
- Filesystem timestamps corroborate the same-day evolution but do not by themselves prove authorial intention. The stronger evidence is the documented stage sequence and the direct conceptual lineage across excavation, extraction, framework translation, philosophy, and model.
