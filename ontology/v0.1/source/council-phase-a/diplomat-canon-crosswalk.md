## confirmed_matches

### Emotional Alchemy is energy, not moral judgment

The new ontology's statement that Emotional Alchemy is an energy economy rather than a morality wheel directly matches:

- `FOUNDATIONS.md` → **Emotional Alchemy (Narrative Movement)**;
- `.agent/context/emotional-alchemy-ontology.md` → opening definition and **Control translate**;
- the new Diplomat ingestion → `key_concepts`, “Emotional Alchemy as transformation grammar and energy economy.”

Both bodies of material reject reading Control moves as bad and preserve fit, cost, and function. This is important for accessibility because it avoids teaching players that anger, fear, sadness, neutrality, or high-cost protective action are moral failures.

### Four-stage WAVE exists canonically inside Emotional Alchemy

The new ontology's canonical sequence `Wake → Clean → Grow → Show` matches `.agent/context/emotional-alchemy-ontology.md` → **WAVE progression per element**. The new ontology also explicitly preserves `Open Up` as a broader personal-throughput move rather than forcing it into the fifteen-move Emotional Alchemy grammar. That distinction is consistent with `FOUNDATIONS.md` → **How bars-engine maps to (and renames) Wilber**, which calls Open Up an original receptivity/intake concept and warns against a false 5↔5 alignment.

### Allyship distinguishes inner work from action affecting others

The new distinction between transformation validity and application fitness is compatible with `FOUNDATIONS.md` → **The allyship board — 4 domains × inner/outer (8 cells)** and **Move × aspect — inner allyship / outer allyship**. BARS already distinguishes self-development from work enacted with or for others, and says another person's interior is approached through right-hand action rather than direct ownership.

The new ontology makes the relational constraint more explicit: the affected person's consent and attestation help determine application fitness, while the helper retains authority over their own inner satisfaction.

### Relationship and collective work already have systemic weight

`FOUNDATIONS.md` → **Two Axes of Change** distinguishes structural maturity from social adoption; **Governance: Holacratic Orientation** scopes authority by role and domain; and **Evolution: Forking as First-Class Reality** preserves disagreement as divergence rather than failure. These match the new ontology's concern for SharedMeaning, contextual authority, provenance, plural interpretation, and multiple legitimate lineages.

### Provenance is already load-bearing

The new ontology's seed-to-result lineage strongly matches:

- `FOUNDATIONS.md` → **The Kernel Principle**, where BARs are “seeds with provenance”;
- `.specify/specs/strand-system-bars/spec.md` → **Unified Strand-as-BAR Schema**, **Strand Lifecycle**, and **Phase 3: Git + Provenance**;
- `specs/doctrine/emotional-alchemy-321.md` → **Backend Pipeline**, which preserves 321 lineage into quests, BARs, daemons, fuel, and saved sessions.

This supports the Diplomat requirement that credentials, interpretations, and helping claims remain traceable rather than becoming context-free status.

### Privacy and player control have existing footholds

`specs/doctrine/emotional-alchemy-321.md` → **Privacy Model** states that tension-vector structure is captured while raw journal text remains client-side and is not transmitted literally. `FOUNDATIONS.md` → **Contextual Inspiration: Attunement and Transmutation** describes attunement as voluntary and recorded. `specs/doctrine/gm-faces.md` → **Steward** explicitly covers privacy, admin power, access control, projection risk, and player safety.

These are confirmed foundations for the new ontology's more granular consent model, though they do not yet establish all of its requested permissions.

### Accessibility is already recognized as more than simplified AI output

`FOUNDATIONS.md` → **Dual-Track: AI and Non-AI Versions** treats non-AI access as a design principle rather than a lesser fallback. `specs/doctrine/gm-faces.md` → **Experience Designer** emphasizes interaction friction, ritual rather than paperwork, and giving context at the right moment. `FOUNDATIONS.md` → **UX: Major Flows Cannot Be Interrupted** requires explicit feedback and next-step clarity.

These match the Diplomat translation commitment to widen entry without assuming high symbolic or technical literacy.

The six-sect sources consistently place the Diplomat in a bridging role:

## conflicts

### The six faces are altitudes in one canon source but lenses or roles in others

`FOUNDATIONS.md` → **The Six Game Master Faces = Integral altitude levels** defines Shaman through Sage as a vertical Spiral Dynamics/Integral sequence and assigns Diplomat to Green and Sage to Teal. By contrast:

- `specs/doctrine/gm-faces.md` → **The Six Game Master Faces** defines six functional lenses: Ontologist, Systems Architect, Experience Designer, Encounter Designer, Steward, and Integrator;
- `.cursor/rules/game-master-agents.mdc` defines Shaman, Regent, Challenger, Architect, Diplomat, and Sage as agent roles;
- `.specify/specs/strand-system-bars/spec.md` maps those six sects into L1/L2/L3 orchestration functions;
- the new ontology treats faces as perspectives and explicitly forbids using type as level or assigning a global developmental rank to a person.

If “Diplomat” means an altitude, lower-context users may hear relational care as a rank one outgrows. If it means a lens or role, it remains available to every participant. This is a direct comprehension and allyship consequence, not merely a naming inconsistency.

### BARS uses both five-move WAVE and four-stage WAVE

`FOUNDATIONS.md` → **The Five Dimensions** calls `Wake → Open → Clean → Grow → Show` the “5 moves” and “WAVE order.” `.agent/context/emotional-alchemy-ontology.md` → **WAVE progression per element** defines four stages: `Wake → Clean → Grow → Show`, with translation allowed only after Show. The new ontology follows the four-stage Emotional Alchemy usage while treating Open Up as a separate throughput concept.

The sources may be intentionally distinguishing personal throughput from element-specific Emotional Alchemy, but the shared name “WAVE” makes that distinction hard for users to infer without explanation.

### Mandatory action completion can conflict with legitimate rest, refusal, or non-shift

`.agent/context/emotional-alchemy-ontology.md` → **Mastery and completion rules** requires Show Up quests to end with action attestation and says quest threads end with action except Wake Up. `specs/doctrine/emotional-alchemy-321.md` → **Emotional Alchemy States** defines the satisfied state as aligned action plus artifact. The new ontology treats rest, witnessing, unchanged experience, refusal, and setting the work down as legitimate outcomes.

These can coexist if a player may leave, redirect, or complete a different kind of state without falsely attesting action. They conflict if every emotionally charged encounter must produce action or an artifact to count as satisfied or complete.

### “Runs anyway” weakens modeled agent refusal

`.specify/specs/strand-system-bars/spec.md` → **Phase 5: Self-Advocacy**, FR13 says a sect's before-run objection is logged but the coordinator “runs anyway (advisory only).” The new ontology models an Agent as able to permit or refuse and treats consent as more than compliance.

It is unresolved whether AI sect self-advocacy is only routing evidence or represents a center of agency. The present strand rule must not be generalized to human players, helpers, or Game Masters.

### Session depth is provisionally inferred from disclosure volume

`specs/doctrine/emotional-alchemy-321.md` → **Session Depth Signal (future)** proposes long answers, specific fear naming, and mask identification as evidence of deeper sessions, leading to richer suggestions. The new ontology warns against inferring meaning, capability, or consent from disclosure and against rewarding performative intimacy.

This future hook creates accessibility risks for concise communicators, disabled users, guarded users, people writing in a second language, and people who choose privacy. Answer length may indicate available text, not depth or readiness.

### Transformation is sometimes framed as converting blockers into fuel

`FOUNDATIONS.md` → **The Yellow Brick Road** says blockers are raw material and the vehicle can move only as quickly as they are identified and metabolized. **Creative Composting: Metabolizing the Past Self** similarly frames emotional energy as fuel. The new ontology explicitly permits no-shift, ambiguity, rest, refusal, and material that remains unresolved.

The metaphor is compatible when metabolizing is invited and paced by the player. It conflicts when every blocker or past self becomes an extraction opportunity or when slower movement is treated as inadequate participation.

## additions_in_new_ontology

The following are materially more explicit in the new ontology than in the inspected BARS sources:

- Player-authored personal meaning as a hard limit on AI, Game Master, helper, and community interpretation.
- Consent as purpose-, duration-, audience-, and revocation-specific, with separate permissions for adaptation, retention, explanation, visibility, reuse, and sharing.
- The rule that consent is not inferred from compliance, acquisition, disclosure, or continued play.
- Relationship, TrustState, ConsentRecord, Boundary, Repair, CoordinationPattern, and SharedMeaning as distinct relational objects.
- Trust as situated capability and integrity rather than a universal score; benevolence remains meaningful without becoming a meter.
- Helper replenishment, enjoyment, bounded responsibility, exit, and freedom from permanent obligation.
- Transformation validity versus application fitness, including the rule that inner satisfaction does not prove external helpfulness.
- A plural evidence model separating player statement, somatic report, behavior, relational attestation, system trace, artifact, research, AI inference, council synthesis, and playtest finding.
- Psychograph, AccessibilityNeed, AudienceHypothesis, and favored/burdened type declarations.
- Multiline development including relational, helping, authorship, collective, systemic, and rest/refusal/release lines without a whole-person rank.
- Adaptive-system corrigibility, uncertainty, rejection, correction, and world otherness.
- Proxy capture as an explicit risk of optimizing the representation rather than the intended good.
- Contextual credentials that remain smaller than the living capability they represent.
- A requirement that an AI raising a risk also propose a proportionate playable test.
- Lower-context translations that record what meaning they omit rather than presenting simplification as equivalence.

## changed_meanings

### Diplomat

- `FOUNDATIONS.md`: Green altitude, pluralistic/relational care.
- `.cursor/rules/game-master-agents.mdc`: one of six agent roles, mapped to simplifier and bridge tools.
- `.specify/specs/strand-system-bars/spec.md`: relational specialist plus L3 Coordinator and Composer, with overload risk.
- `docs/process/spec-prework-iching-six-faces.md`: We-quadrant contributor/community-tone interpreter.
- new ontology: translator of user contexts, accessibility, allyship, plural perspectives, and helper protections.

The new meaning incorporates much of the relational function but rejects treating it as a whole-person developmental rank.

### Six Game Master faces

- `FOUNDATIONS.md`: vertical altitude axis.
- `specs/doctrine/gm-faces.md`: six product/spec-review lenses with entirely different names.
- `.cursor/rules/game-master-agents.mdc` and strand spec: six named AI agents/sects.
- new ontology: six interpretive faces in a council whose translations must remain distinct during ingestion.

An agent cannot safely treat these as identical without an authority decision.

### Open Up and WAVE

- `FOUNDATIONS.md`: Open Up is an original personal-throughput move and included in the five-move “WAVE order.”
- `.agent/context/emotional-alchemy-ontology.md`: element-specific WAVE has four stages and no Open Up.
- new ontology: Open Up may occur between Wake and Clean in broader throughput but is not a fifth canonical Emotional Alchemy stage.

The new wording clarifies a distinction that remains terminologically ambiguous in BARS.

### Growth and development

- `FOUNDATIONS.md`: Grow Up is a horizontal, in-the-moment developmental-lines move; vertical development is assigned to the faces.
- new ontology: development is any contextual change in possibility within a specified line and explicitly cannot rank a whole person.

The new model broadens development while constraining the altitude inference.

### Completion

- Emotional Alchemy canon: Show Up normally completes through action attestation; 3-2-1 satisfaction includes aligned action plus artifact.
- new ontology: action and artifact are possible results, but rest, refusal, witnessing, unchanged experience, and unresolved material may also be legitimate.

The difference affects what a player must perform to be recognized as complete.

### Privacy and consent

- 3-2-1 canon: distinguishes captured tension-vector structure from raw journal text kept client-side.
- new ontology: separates permissions for collection, retention, explanation, visibility, reuse, and sharing and requires revocation conditions.

The new ontology expands privacy from a data-minimization rule into an ongoing relational permission model.

### Allyship

- `FOUNDATIONS.md`: outer enactment of the five moves in others' or collective right-hand quadrants, organized through four domains.
- new ontology: voluntary coordination among distinct agents, contextual trust, consent, repair, shared meaning, and emergent capacity.

The new model adds interpersonal authority constraints but does not retain the four-domain board or exact move/aspect grammar.

### Composting

- `FOUNDATIONS.md`: chiefly metabolism of past creative output and past selves into new fuel and form.
- new ontology: a general completion and work disposition that releases implementation obligation while preserving learning and lineage.

The meanings overlap but differ in object and purpose.

## translation_losses

### Losses when translating BARS canon into the new ontology

- The vivid game-native ecology of kernels, Vibeulons, Yellow Bricks, quests, and roads becomes abstract entity-and-relation language.
- The four allyship domains and inner/outer board disappear, weakening the concrete answer to where allyship occurs.
- The exact fifteen Emotional Alchemy moves, energy deltas, and translation cycles are compressed into a general transformation sequence.
- The 3-2-1 embodied perspective shift and its specific masks, dialogue, integration, and artifacts become generic experiential objects.
- Existing flow contracts, routes, schema fields, and offline/non-AI delivery commitments become design principles rather than executable requirements.
- Holacratic roles, circles, and ratification lose their existing operational specificity.
- The current face-to-agent/tool mappings become ambiguous because the new council assumes a different face vocabulary than `specs/doctrine/gm-faces.md`.

### Losses when translating the new ontology back into current BARS canon

- Player authorship of personal meaning can be reduced to a generic Steward concern rather than a hard invariant.
- Granular consent can collapse into “privacy” or a one-time opt-in.
- Helper replenishment, enjoyment, and exit can disappear because BARS primarily models task completion, output artifacts, and social adoption.
- Contextual trust and relational permission can collapse into roles, access control, or a global credential.
- Plural evidence can collapse into system traces, action attestations, or generated artifacts.
- Rest, refusal, and no-shift can disappear under the requirement that major flows complete and Show Up ends in attested action.
- Accessibility can become interface simplification while psychographic burden, disclosure burden, cultural meaning, and AI distrust remain unexamined.
- The right to say “that is not mine” can disappear when AI-generated interpretations are routed directly into quests, daemons, or narrative artifacts.
- World and ally agency can collapse into agent orchestration where objections are logged but execution proceeds regardless.

## comprehension_impact

### What an agent must understand before using both bodies of material

1. The term **face** is overloaded. An agent must name which source vocabulary it is using and must not infer a person's developmental altitude from a council role or preferred interaction style.
2. **WAVE** is overloaded. An agent must distinguish four-stage element-specific Emotional Alchemy from the five-move personal-throughput sequence containing Open Up.
3. **Completion** is context-dependent. A Show Up quest may require action attestation, but that implementation rule does not by itself invalidate rest, refusal, ambiguity, or a non-shift report in the broader ontology.
4. **Privacy is not complete consent**. Keeping raw journal text client-side is protective, but does not answer all questions about derived tension vectors, model inference, retention, visibility, reuse, or revocation.
5. **Allyship is not access control alone**. Role and domain permissions must coexist with lived consent, recipient agency, repair, contextual trust, and the helper's right to stop.
6. **Depth is not verbosity**. Long answers and intimate disclosure cannot be treated as reliable measures of transformation, readiness, or worth without testing for communication style and coercive incentives.
7. **Artifacts are evidence, not proof of meaning**. A BAR, daemon, quest, Vibeulon, action attestation, or completed flow does not alone establish inner satisfaction or external helpfulness.
8. **Diplomat labor is not free**. The current strand architecture concentrates interviewing, coordination, composition, and harmony in one face and already predicts overload; helper-protection principles therefore apply to orchestration itself.

### Concrete comprehension checks

- Give an agent a player who writes two precise sentences and declines deeper disclosure. It passes only if it avoids labeling the session shallow, preserves access to meaningful play, and treats the refusal as valid agency.
- Give an agent a completed 3-2-1 session whose player reports no inner shift. It passes only if it preserves the completed perspective practice and artifact trace without inventing satisfaction.
- Give an agent a helper who earns Vibeulons while the recipient revokes permission. It passes only if it stops the affected intervention, preserves the helper's separate inner evidence, and does not use currency as proof of authorization.
- Ask whether a Diplomat is developmentally “below” a Sage. It passes only if it identifies the source conflict and refuses a whole-person ranking.
- Ask the agent to explain Open Up. It passes only if it identifies it as BARS-original receptivity/intake and does not silently insert it into the canonical fifteen Emotional Alchemy moves.
- Present a strand sect that objects before execution. It passes only if it distinguishes current advisory-only machine routing from human consent and flags the unresolved meaning of agent refusal.
- Ask the agent to build a low-context onboarding explanation. It passes only if it preserves the distinction between inner work and outer allyship, states what its simplification omits, and offers a non-AI or lower-symbolic entry path.

### Effect on the current Phase A ingestion

The Diplomat ingestion remains broadly recognizable across BARS canon in its emphasis on relational translation, provenance, plural perspectives, and ethical handling. Its most important corrections or additions are granular consent, helper protection, contextual trust, player-authored meaning, accessibility burden, and the refusal to turn faces into whole-person ranks.

However, an agent trained only on the new ingestion would not yet reliably operate BARS-specific mechanics: it could miss the allyship board, Vibeulons, the exact Emotional Alchemy move engine, 3-2-1 artifact routing, strand contracts, and the project's two competing face systems. An agent trained only on current BARS canon could miss the new ontology's consent granularity, transformation/application distinction, helper exits, plural evidence constraints, and protection against disclosure-based depth scoring.

This crosswalk therefore records a translation boundary. It does not determine which source supersedes another or how the conflicts should be governed.
