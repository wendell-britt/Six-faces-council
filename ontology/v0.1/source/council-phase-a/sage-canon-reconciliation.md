## confirmed_invariants

“Confirmed invariant” here means a meaning that survives direct comparison across the new package and relevant BARS sources. It does not mean newly ratified constitution.

1. **BARs preserve potential and provenance.**
   **Direct source fact:** `FOUNDATIONS.md` calls a BAR a portable kernel and “seed with provenance”; the strand spec links strand BARs, output BARs, and optional branches.
   **Sage synthesis:** The new ontology's Seed, Artifact, ProvenanceRecord, Revision, LifecycleRecord, and `records_path` preserve this meaning, although its charged-possibility emphasis is narrower than canon's complete BAR class.

2. **Emotional Alchemy is an energy economy, not a morality scale.**
   **Direct source fact:** `FOUNDATIONS.md` and `.agent/context/emotional-alchemy-ontology.md` explicitly state this and treat Control as costly precision rather than bad movement.
   **Sage synthesis:** All six council translations preserve this boundary.

3. **The elemental grammar has five channels, four within-channel stages, and fifteen canonical moves.**
   **Direct source fact:** `.agent/context/emotional-alchemy-ontology.md` defines Metal/Fear, Water/Sadness, Wood/Joy, Fire/Anger, Earth/Neutrality; Wake → Clean → Grow → Show; five Transcend, five Generative, and five Control moves. Translation occurs only after Show.
   **Sage synthesis:** The new prose package preserves this; the YAML does not.

4. **Open Up is distinct from the five elements and the fifteen-move grammar.**
   **Direct source fact:** `FOUNDATIONS.md` calls Open Up a BARS-original receptivity/intake move and explicitly says it does not force a five-to-five alignment.
   **Sage synthesis:** The new ontology's separation of the Basic Moves from element-specific Emotional Alchemy is source-recognizable. The later owner correction reserves “WAVE” for the Open Up breathing practice and supersedes both conflicting source usages.

5. **Development has multiple axes that must not be collapsed.**
   **Direct source fact:** `FOUNDATIONS.md` separates lines, face altitude, structural maturity, and social adoption.
   **Sage synthesis:** The new ontology adds contextual DevelopmentClaims, epistemic maturity, and work disposition. The stable meaning is multiplicity of axes; their exact relation remains unresolved.

6. **Inner work and externally affecting action require distinguishable evidence.**
   **Direct source fact:** `FOUNDATIONS.md` distinguishes inner and outer allyship across domains and requires an AllyshipTarget for outer enactment.
   **Sage synthesis:** The new distinction between transformation validity and application fitness adds evidence authority and consent precision without contradicting the underlying inner/outer separation.

7. **Role- and domain-scoped authority is preferable to undifferentiated authority.**
   **Direct source fact:** `FOUNDATIONS.md` describes holacratic, role-based, domain-scoped governance and recorded ratification; contextual Vibeulon transmutation requires circle ratification.
   **Sage synthesis:** The new authority map, contextual credentials, and consent boundaries extend this invariant into epistemic and relational domains.

8. **Forking and divergence can preserve lineage without becoming failure.**
   **Direct source fact:** `FOUNDATIONS.md` makes forking first-class; the strand spec links branches and BARs by provenance.
   **Sage synthesis:** The council's preservation of separate face translations and disagreement is consistent with this invariant.

9. **Privacy requires at least some data minimization.**
   **Direct source fact:** `emotional-alchemy-321.md` stores structured TensionVector material while excluding raw journal text from literal transmission; `FOUNDATIONS.md` makes attunement voluntary and recorded; the Steward lens covers privacy and projection risk.
   **Sage synthesis:** The new package extends but does not contradict the invariant by separating permissions for collection, inference, adaptation, retention, explanation, visibility, reuse, and sharing.

10. **Agent work should be routed through named roles and retain an audit/provenance trail.**
    **Direct source fact:** `gm-faces.md` requires primary/secondary/review routing; the runtime rule maps agents to tools; the strand spec records sequence, outputs, advocacy, decisions, and provenance.
    **Sage synthesis:** Council output remains attributed interpretation rather than anonymous consensus.

11. **Completion and failure must be legible to participants.**
    **Direct source fact:** `FOUNDATIONS.md` requires major flows to end with a destination, success feedback, or explicit error guidance and calls for instrumentation.
    **Sage synthesis:** This is compatible with the new demand for observable consequences and evidence. It does not by itself settle what counts as valid transformational completion.

12. **Adoption does not prove structural quality.**
    **Direct source fact:** `FOUNDATIONS.md` makes social adoption independent of structural maturity.
    **Sage synthesis:** This supports the new rule that engagement and external approval do not substitute for meaning, transformation, or design quality.

## exact_conflicts_and_deltas

### A. Direct or operational conflicts

1. **Face ontology and anti-ranking**
   **Direct source fact:** `FOUNDATIONS.md` declares the named Shaman–Sage faces to be vertical Integral/Spiral-Dynamics altitude levels.
   **Direct source fact:** The new model prohibits global developmental ranking of a person and treating type as level, while using the same names as co-present council lenses and psychographic orientations.
   **Conflict:** A named face cannot be assumed to be both a nonhierarchical selectable lens and a person's vertical altitude without a scoping rule.

2. **Show Up action gate and valid refusal/rest — resolved by council decision**
   **Direct source fact:** `.agent/context/emotional-alchemy-ontology.md` requires action attestation for Show Up completion and says quest threads end with action except Wake Up.
   **Direct source fact:** The new ontology treats stopping, rest, witnessing, refusal, unchanged experience, and non-transformation as legitimate outcomes.
   **Resolution:** A refusal may validly end participation or the encounter while leaving an action-attested Show Up quest incomplete. Encounters end; quests complete. See `completion-boundaries/council-decision.md`.

3. **System-defined satisfaction and player-reported satisfaction**
   **Direct source fact:** `emotional-alchemy-321.md` defines Satisfied as aligned action plus artifact. `.agent/context/emotional-alchemy-ontology.md` counts satisfaction selections in movement derivation.
   **Direct source fact:** The new ontology gives the player's SatisfactionReport primary authority for self-directed transformation validity and permits the sequence to stop before action or artifact.
   **Conflict:** These cannot all be the universal definition of satisfaction. They may describe input selection, implemented dispatch state, and phenomenological report at different layers, but those layers are not formally separated.

4. **TensionVector identity claim and epistemic humility — resolved by council decision**
   **Direct source fact:** `emotional-alchemy-321.md` says the TensionVector “IS the dissatisfied emotional state made explicit.”
   **Direct source fact:** The new ontology requires records and interpretations to remain smaller than the player and lived event.
   **Resolution:** The identity claim is rejected. The player lives the tension; the system may retain a TensionConfiguration derived from reports and a TensionSnapshot of actual persisted fields. Bare `TensionVector` is retired; `BARS321TensionVector` is a deprecated compatibility alias. See `tension-vector/council-decision.md`.

5. **Automatic movement derivation and player meaning authority — resolved by ontology decision**
   **Direct source fact:** `.agent/context/emotional-alchemy-ontology.md` derives translate/transcend dominance from selected satisfaction, dissatisfaction, and self-sabotage counts and supplies default per-node assignments.
   **Direct source fact:** The new ontology requires AI/system interpretations to remain provisional and rejectable.
   **Resolution:** The output is a correctable `DerivedMovementProposal`. It may become an accepted `MovementRoutingPlan` for authored content but cannot establish actual movement. A later `MovementFinding` requires play evidence. Correction stops future use and propagates through downstream lineage without rewriting historical play. See `derived-movement/reconstruction-and-decision.md`.

6. **Self-advocacy and refusal**
   **Direct source fact:** Strand FR13 logs a sect's `shouldRun: false` objection but runs it anyway.
   **Direct source fact:** The new Agent model includes refusal and treats access or participation as insufficient for consent.
   **Conflict:** Advisory routing input and substantive refusal are not the same. The human rule must not be inferred from the software-agent rule.

7. **Internal strand audit-field conflict**
   **Direct source fact:** The unified schema says `audit_trail` replaced `decision_audit_log`; strand FR1 still names `decision_audit_log`.
   **Conflict:** The specification contains two field names for the same required function.

8. **Internal coordinator-role conflict**
   **Direct source fact:** The strand hierarchy maps Coordinator and Composer to Diplomat, the L3 summary also assigns orchestration to Sage and Regent, and FR2 says Sage or Regent can coordinate. The I Ching prework requires Sage synthesis.
   **Conflict:** Coordination, composition, synthesis, order, and approval responsibilities are not governed by one authority contract.

### B. Semantic collisions and scope deltas

9. **At least four meanings of “Game Master face”**
   - `FOUNDATIONS.md`: Shaman–Sage as altitude.
   - `gm-faces.md`: Ontologist–Integrator as functional review disciplines.
   - Runtime rule and strand spec: Shaman–Sage as agents/sects and tool routes.
   - New ontology: Shaman–Sage as interpretive council lenses and psychographic orientations.
   **Delta:** These overlap but differ in membership, category, selection criteria, and authority.

10. **Four-stage and five-move uses of “WAVE” — resolved by owner correction**
    **Direct source fact:** Elemental Emotional Alchemy uses Wake → Clean → Grow → Show. `FOUNDATIONS.md` calls Wake → Open → Clean → Grow → Show the personal-throughput WAVE order.
    **Resolution:** The source collision is now historical rather than normative. The five are the Basic Moves; WAVE is the Open Up breathing practice; Wake → Clean → Grow → Show is the within-channel progression. See `wave-terminology/owner-decision.md`.

11. **Broad BAR kernel and charged possibility**
    **Direct source fact:** `FOUNDATIONS.md` permits any provenance-bearing kernel—quest, rule, lore, design decision, spec, implementation plan, norm, or fork—to be a BAR.
    **Direct source fact:** The council brief describes a BAR as a captured unit of charged possibility.
    **Delta:** Charged possibility may be one BAR source or subtype; it is not demonstrated to exhaust the canonical class.

12. **Composting as generative reuse and as release**
    **Direct source fact:** `FOUNDATIONS.md` emphasizes metabolizing past authored material into new creative fuel and output.
    **Direct source fact:** The new ontology emphasizes ending implementation obligation while retaining learning and lineage.
    **Delta:** Reuse and release may coexist, but neither source states their relationship.

13. **Provenance expansion**
    **Direct source fact:** BARS canon primarily tracks BAR, artifact, strand, branch, fork, and session lineage.
    **Direct source fact:** The new ontology extends provenance to evidence authority, interpretations, trust, psychographs, consent, and helping credentials.
    **Delta:** Expanded provenance increases epistemic clarity and intimate-data risk.

14. **Development expansion and constraint**
    **Direct source fact:** BARS canon distinguishes horizontal lines, vertical face altitude, structural maturity, and social adoption.
    **Direct source fact:** The new ontology treats development as contextual change in possibility and prohibits whole-person rank.
    **Delta:** The new model broadens development while constraining one implication of altitude.

15. **Privacy rule and consent grammar**
    **Direct source fact:** 3-2-1 canon excludes raw journal text from literal transmission but stores structured tension data.
    **Direct source fact:** The new ontology distinguishes collection, inference, adaptation, retention, explanation, visibility, reuse, sharing, correction, and revocation.
    **Delta:** The new grammar is materially broader than implemented canon and must not be described as already implemented.

16. **Completion has multiple non-equivalent scopes**
    BARS sources use completion for major-flow feedback, Wake passage completion, Show action attestation, 3-2-1 satisfied/dispatch state, structural maturity, and social adoption. The new ontology adds valid encounter endings and work completion through rest, shipping, or composting.
    **Delta:** An unqualified completion claim is unsafe.

17. **Yellow Brick Road reassurance and falsifiability — resolved by ontology decision**
    **Direct source fact:** `FOUNDATIONS.md` says players can never go the wrong way and movement depends on identifying and metabolizing blockers.
    **Direct source fact:** The new ontology preserves contradictory evidence, unsuccessful outcomes, refusal, and the possibility that a design or intervention did not work.
    **Resolution:** Retain it as mythic reassurance and a recoverability covenant: no path has to be wasted. It is not an empirical guarantee, cannot erase wrongness or harm, and cannot silently revise the player's destination. Some blockers may remain unresolved indefinitely while continuing to constrain a named route. See `yellow-brick-road/reconstruction-and-decision.md`.

18. **Roadblock as technical fact and intentional diagnosis**
    **Direct source fact:** `FOUNDATIONS.md` calls an Emergent Roadblock a manifestation of misaligned intention while giving build errors and export mismatches as examples.
    **Delta:** The new ontology requires the technical observation to remain separate from the intention interpretation unless evidence connects them.

19. **Session depth and disclosure volume**
    **Direct source fact:** The future 3-2-1 signal associates long answers, specific fear naming, and “genuine mask identification” with deeper sessions and richer outputs.
    **Direct source fact:** The new ontology rejects behavioral proxies as sufficient evidence of inner meaning.
    **Delta/conflict:** The proposal is compatible only if treated as a content-detail signal and tested against verbosity, language, disability, culture, privacy, and reported depth.

20. **Creative composting meaning authority**
    **Direct source fact:** `FOUNDATIONS.md` answers its “necromancy” question with “no” inside the project ontology.
    **Direct source fact:** The new ontology leaves personal inward meaning with the player.
    **Delta:** A project-level ethical framing and an individual player's meaning may differ; their jurisdiction is not stated.

21. **Eight versus fifteen constitutional principles**
    **Direct source fact:** The brief declares eight; the later philosophy declares fifteen; BARS canon does not contain a source-version record for this delta.
    **Conflict:** Constitutional membership and amendment authority remain unresolved. Direct BARS comparison does not decide it.
