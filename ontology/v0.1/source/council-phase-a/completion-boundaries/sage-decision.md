# Sage Decision — Scope-Bound Completion

An encounter can end validly without its move, quest, or arc completing. Completion is always a scope-bound claim supported by declared criteria and the appropriate evidence authority.

The RPG-shaped default—campaign, arc, quest, encounter, move invocation—is a useful orientation but not a universal containment tree. The ontology must also support questless sandbox encounters, moves spanning scenes, multiple quests advanced by one encounter, solo practices, multiplayer exits, technical flows, and retrospectively discovered arcs.

Encounter families should be composable contracts rather than exclusive genre types. Ending protects agency and makes transition legible; resolution records what became of an attempt; fulfillment evaluates a particular objective; conclusion bounds a larger trajectory; meaning remains plural and evidence-bounded.
