# Shaman Decision — The Circle May Close While the Road Remains Open

The encounter container may close through resolution, rest, refusal, retreat, boundary, interruption, or no-shift without claiming that its move, quest, arc, or transformation completed. A Witness Note may preserve what occurred without forcing dispatch or change. Composting may discharge the obligation to continue without pretending the original objective was achieved.

The player, Game Master, facilitator, and helper all retain the right to stop participation. Transformation remains an independently evidenced change, never an inference from a closure flag.

**Shaman formulation:** Every encounter deserves a valid ending, but only scoped evidence can establish resolution, fulfillment, conclusion, or transformation.
