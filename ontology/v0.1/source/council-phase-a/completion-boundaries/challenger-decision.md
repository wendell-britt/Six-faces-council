# Challenger Decision — Falsifying False Completion

The model fails if it cannot represent any of the following:

- an encounter ending while its quest remains active;
- a failed move being fully resolved;
- a participant refusing without being diagnosed or morally penalized;
- a practice ending with no reported shift;
- a retreat preserving future possibility while failing an immediate objective;
- a session stopping while its encounter remains suspended;
- a reloaded branch retaining provenance without retaining canonical world state;
- a sandbox encounter existing without a quest or authored arc.

Metrics, interface flags, Game Master rulings, and AI interpretations cannot substitute for the evidence authority required by the claim. Completion criteria may not be retroactively tightened to force the intended story.
