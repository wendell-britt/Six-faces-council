# Regent Decision — Authority and Completion Contracts

Every participant may end their participation. Only a declared contract can establish completion.

A completion contract names the evaluated object, conditions, acceptable evidence, adjudicating authority, failure and exit states, pause and resumption behavior, propagation rules, consent dependencies, and resulting permissions or consequences. The ruleset or designer declares the contract; the Game Master adjudicates procedural ambiguity within agreed authority; the player governs inward meaning and self-directed satisfaction; affected agents govern their consent and may attest relational impact.

Every ending must state what ended, what remains active, what changed, and what can happen next.
