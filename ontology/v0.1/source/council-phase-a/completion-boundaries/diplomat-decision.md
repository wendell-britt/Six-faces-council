# Diplomat Decision — Closure Without False Consensus

Use accessible, scope-specific language:

- the encounter ended;
- the move resolved with a stated result;
- the quest objective was or was not fulfilled;
- the quest terminated under a named disposition;
- the arc concluded while specified threads remained open.

In multiplayer play, one participant may exit while the encounter continues. Different reports of satisfaction, repair, consequence, and meaning remain separately attributable rather than averaged into consensus. No participant may unilaterally declare a relationship repaired or another person transformed.

Players should be able to understand the encounter’s operative contract, who may stop it, what stopping changes, and what remains unresolved without first learning ontology vocabulary.
