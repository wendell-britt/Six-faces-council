## conflicts

- **The ontological status of the six named faces conflicts.** `FOUNDATIONS.md` § “The Six Game Master Faces = Integral altitude levels” defines Shaman, Challenger, Regent, Architect, Diplomat, and Sage as vertical Integral/Spiral-Dynamics altitude levels. The new ontology uses those same names as co-present functional lenses and describes Wendell as having strong Sage–Architect–Shaman orientations, while also forbidding global developmental ranking and treatment of type as level. Those meanings cannot be treated as identical without distortion.

- **BARS canon also contains a different set called “The Six Game Master Faces.”** `specs/doctrine/gm-faces.md` defines Ontologist, Systems Architect, Experience Designer, Encounter Designer, Steward, and Integrator. Meanwhile `.cursor/rules/game-master-agents.mdc` and `strand-system-bars/spec.md` use Shaman, Regent, Challenger, Architect, Diplomat, and Sage. The new ontology uses the latter six. There are therefore at least three face semantics in the current sources: altitude, player-facing sect/agent, and feature-review function. The source set itself does not provide a single reconciliation.

- **Canonical 3-2-1 defines satisfaction more narrowly than the new ontology.** `specs/doctrine/emotional-alchemy-321.md` § “Emotional Alchemy States” defines Satisfied as “aligned action + artifact.” The new ontology allows a satisfying transformation that does not necessarily reach action or artifact and treats unchanged, unclear, resting, or witnessing outcomes as valid findings. These cannot both be universal definitions of a satisfied Emotional Alchemy result.

- **Canonical quest completion can require action where the new ontology protects non-transformation and stopping.** `.agent/context/emotional-alchemy-ontology.md` § “Mastery and completion rules” says Show Up quests require attested action and that quest threads end with action, with Wake Up as the exception. The new ontology states that a player may stop, witness, set work down, or remain unchanged without failure. This may be a scope conflict—completion of a Show Up quest versus legitimacy of ending an encounter—but the scope boundary is not explicit in the source package.

- **The Yellow Brick Road language universalizes metabolism.** `FOUNDATIONS.md` § “The Yellow Brick Road” says anyone and anything encountered can be transmuted into a Yellow Brick experience, that one can never go the wrong way, and that movement is limited by identifying and metabolizing blockers. The new ontology rejects coerced transformation and permits unresolved material, non-change, rest, and refusal. Read literally and universally, the canonical language conflicts with that permission.

- **The canonical tension vector is asserted as the dissatisfied state itself.** `specs/doctrine/emotional-alchemy-321.md` § “Tension Vector” says the tension vector “IS the dissatisfied emotional state.” The new ontology requires records, interpretations, and AI-derived structures to remain smaller than the lived person and experience. A stored vector may represent or operationalize a dissatisfied state, but treating it as ontologically identical conflicts with the new evidence and corrigibility rules.

- **Automatic derivation can outrun player authority.** `.agent/context/emotional-alchemy-ontology.md` §§ “Derivation rules” and “Per-node assignment” derive translate/transcend dominance from counts of satisfaction, dissatisfaction, and self-sabotage and assign movement patterns. The new ontology permits such output only as provisional system inference that can be corrected or rejected; it gives the player’s SatisfactionReport primary authority over inner transformation. Canon does not state this correction boundary in those sections.

- **Future session-depth signals use behavioral proxies as claims about genuineness.** `specs/doctrine/emotional-alchemy-321.md` § “Session Depth Signal (future)” associates long answers, specific fear naming, and “genuine mask identification” with deep sessions and richer outputs. The new ontology does not allow answer length or specificity alone to establish inner depth, authenticity, or transformation. This proposal conflicts unless constrained to a content-detail signal rather than a truth/depth judgment.

- **Creative-composting canon settles an interpretation that the new ontology would leave player-authored.** `FOUNDATIONS.md` § “Creative Composting: Metabolizing the Past Self,” “The Ethical Frame,” asks whether AI remixing is necromancy and answers no because Integral Theory provides sufficient ontological footing. The new ontology allows the system to propose meaning but reserves personal inner meaning for the player and preserves multiple evidence-bearing interpretations. A system-wide answer may therefore exceed its authority for players who experience the practice differently.

- **Advisory-only self-advocacy may conflict with agency language.** `strand-system-bars/spec.md` § “Phase 5: Self-Advocacy” requires that the coordinator log a sect’s `shouldRun: false` objection but run it anyway. The new ontology preserves every center of agency and emphasizes refusal. Whether an AI sect possesses refusal authority is not established, so this remains a conflict at the level of language and role expectations rather than a resolved rights claim.

## additions_in_new_ontology

- **Somatic evidence has explicit authority and limits.** The new ontology adds SomaticReport, bodily truth-testing, no-shift evidence, and the requirement that sensation not be treated as its own infallible interpretation. The examined canon uses charged and shadow practices but does not formalize somatic reports as a distinct evidence class.

- **Player-authored personal meaning is a hard boundary.** The canon supports player-owned inspiration but does not explicitly state that system interpretation must remain smaller than the player or that “that is not mine” is a valid corrective.

- **Adaptive-system governance is substantially expanded.** The new ontology separately names observation, inference, adapted content, uncertainty, alternatives, correction, rejection, collection, retention, explanation, visibility, reuse, and sharing. The canon’s 3-2-1 privacy section distinguishes raw text from stored tension-vector structure but does not provide this complete permission grammar.

- **World otherness is explicit.** The new ontology requires adaptive worlds to retain causality, history, resistance, agency, and meaning beyond the player model. This is not explicitly present in the examined canon.

- **Evidence authority is plural and typed.** Player statement, somatic report, behavioral observation, relational attestation, system trace, artifact, research, AI inference, council synthesis, and playtest finding receive explicit “can establish/cannot establish alone” limits absent from the canon package.

- **Confidence is profiled rather than scored.** Scope, source proximity, diversity, repeatability, contestation, interpretive distance, maturity, labels, and known limits are new formal additions.

- **Transformation validity and application fitness are explicitly separated.** The inner/outer BARS board anticipates this, but the new ontology specifies distinct evidence and authority for the helper’s inner transformation and the effect of intervention on another person.

- **Trust is modeled as situated capability and integrity.** TrustState, ConsentRecord, Boundary, Repair, CoordinationPattern, and the warning against universal reputation or access-as-command add relational precision not found in the examined canon.

- **Helper and Game Master replenishment are inside the success condition.** Canon assigns roles and allyship actions but does not explicitly state that a helping system fails if it consumes or permanently obligates the helper.

- **Rest, refusal, non-change, ambiguity, and composting are generalized as legitimate outcomes.** Canon contains Witness Note, private stagnation, and creative composting, but the new ontology applies legitimacy more broadly across design and transformation.

- **Development is modeled across many distinct lines without whole-person rank.** The new model adds emotional, somatic, interpretive, epistemic, relational, integrity, strategic, creative, narrative, helping, stewardship, authorship, collective, systemic-participation, and rest/refusal/release lines.

- **Mechanics are explicitly treated as ontological claims.** Canon demonstrates this through its five dimensions, completion rules, and currencies but does not state the general principle that tracking, differentiating, rewarding, permitting, and changing something gives it ontological weight.

- **Performance is separated from identity and capability.** The new ontology permits play to precede understanding and prevents a single enactment from proving stable identity.

- **Psychographs are revisable design hypotheses rather than diagnoses.** The examined canon has Nations, Archetypes, faces, ethos, and stats but does not state this evidence boundary.

- **Design shadows require proportionate playable tests.** The canon contains design practices, but the new ontology makes reflexive remediation a general principle and requires an AI that raises a risk to propose a test.

- **Epistemic maturity is separated from work disposition.** This distinction is new relative to canon’s structural maturity, social adoption, BAR weight, and lifecycle metaphors.

## changed_meanings

- **Shaman:** In `FOUNDATIONS.md` § “The Six Game Master Faces = Integral altitude levels,” Shaman means Magenta altitude characterized by magic, tribal belonging, and ritual. In `strand-system-bars/spec.md` §§ “Agent Hierarchy” and “Ethos→Stat,” Shaman means a mythic/liminal researcher with Compassion/Empathy. In `emotional-alchemy-321.md` § “GM Voice Sequence,” Shaman holds and senses the Face It phase. In the new ingestion, Shaman is a non-exclusive lens for felt truth, charge, ritual, symbol, somatics, mystery, and permission to remain unchanged. These meanings overlap but are not interchangeable.

- **Game Master face:** `specs/doctrine/gm-faces.md` uses “face” for six feature-review functions unrelated by name to the Shaman–Sage sequence. `FOUNDATIONS.md` uses the latter names as altitude. The strand spec uses them as orchestrated sects/agents. The new ontology uses them as a council of interpretive perspectives. “Face” therefore changes category across sources.

- **WAVE:** `FOUNDATIONS.md` § “The Five Dimensions” uses WAVE for five personal-throughput moves including Open Up. `FOUNDATIONS.md` § “Emotional Alchemy” and `.agent/context/emotional-alchemy-ontology.md` use four stages per element without Open Up. The new ontology intentionally preserves both by calling Open Up part of the broader throughput rather than canonical elemental grammar. The same word still carries two scopes.

- **Transcend:** `.agent/context/emotional-alchemy-ontology.md` §§ “Transcend (vertical completion)” and “Transcend vs Translate” use transcend for completing WAVE within one emotional channel and describe an altitude shift. The new ontology treats development as contextual change in possibility and rejects global rank. Transcend can remain a move vector, but it cannot be assumed to establish whole-person altitude.

- **Satisfaction:** Canonical 3-2-1 defines satisfied state as aligned action plus artifact. The new ontology makes the player’s SatisfactionReport primary for inner transformation and allows sequences not to reach action or artifact. Canonical satisfaction is more structurally terminal; the new meaning is more phenomenological and scope-sensitive.

- **Completion:** Emotional Alchemy canon makes Show Up action-attested completion and ends quest threads with action; `FOUNDATIONS.md` requires major flows to complete with explicit feedback. The new ontology expands valid completion to include stopping, witnessing, resting, unchanged experience, good-enough shipping, and composting. “Flow completion,” “quest completion,” “transformational completion,” and “valid encounter ending” are not yet distinguished consistently.

- **Composting:** `FOUNDATIONS.md` § “Creative Composting” chiefly metabolizes the creator’s prior work and past self into new output and energy. The new ontology generalizes composting to any possibility whose implementation obligation can end while lineage and learning remain.

- **Provenance:** Canon primarily uses provenance to link kernels, strands, outputs, branches, and artifacts. The new ontology extends provenance to claims, trust, consent, interpretations, psychographs, transformation, and helping authority. This adds intimacy and privacy risk to a previously production-centered concept.

- **Development:** Canon distinguishes horizontal lines, vertical face altitude, structural maturity, and social adoption. The new ontology centers context-specific changes in possibility and refuses a global rank. The models may be compatible in part, but “development” no longer defaults to ascending a single vertical axis.

- **Allyship:** Canon structurally locates allyship in outer/right-hand enactment and gives every throughput move an outer expression. The new ontology emphasizes consent, distinct agency, trust, application fitness, helper sustainability, and shared charge. It expands the ethical and evidential meaning beyond board location.

- **Neutrality/Earth:** Canon defines Earth as whole-system perspective/detachment. The new Shaman ingestion includes stability or witnessed detachment while explicitly questioning whether neutrality, grounded witnessing, freeze, dissociation, and lack of interest are being conflated. The new ontology makes Earth less self-interpreting.

- **Depth/deftness:** `emotional-alchemy-321.md` proposes response length, fear specificity, and mask identification as future depth signals; `gm-faces.md` assigns measurable deftness to Integrator. The new ontology requires triangulated evidence and known limits before those proxies can imply transformation quality.

- **Agent self-advocacy:** Strand canon treats it as auditable advisory input to coordinator routing. The new ontology’s agency language can be read as including meaningful refusal. Whether software-agent advocacy is consultation, consent, or role-based dissent remains undefined.

## translation_losses

- Translating BARS canon into the new ontology can erase implementation reality: route behavior, stored schemas, attestation requirements, Vibeulon economics, phase gates, and tool prerequisites may disappear into general philosophical language.

- Translating the new ontology into BARS canon can erase authority boundaries: personal meaning, correction rights, non-change, contextual evidence limits, and separate permissions may be absorbed into automatic quest derivation and artifact production.

- Calling every charged process “Emotional Alchemy” risks hiding the exact difference between five-move personal throughput, four-stage element WAVE, fifteen canonical moves, binary legacy translate/transcend, and 3-2-1 perspective integration.

- Mapping the Shaman ingestion onto Magenta altitude would turn a chosen lens into a developmental rank and could make ritual or mythic language appear developmentally lower rather than functionally appropriate.

- Mapping Magenta Shaman, strand Shaman, 3-2-1 Shaman voice, and council Shaman into one object would hide their distinct provenance, permissions, and purposes.

- Treating Witness Note as fully equivalent to rest/witness/non-change could overstate canon; the artifact preserves nondispatch, but the sources do not explicitly call non-transformation successful completion.

- Treating inner/outer board position as equivalent to transformation-validity/application-fitness would lose the new distinction between who can testify to inner change and who can attest relational effect.

- Treating `audit_trail` as full epistemic provenance would lose evidence type, confidence, privacy scope, interpretive distance, and contestation.

- Treating canonical privacy as equivalent to player sovereignty would overlook retention, visibility, reuse, sharing, inference, and explanation permissions.

- Treating Vibeulons as equivalent to all Emotional Alchemy energy would collapse a game currency, a narrative energy model, and lived affect into one thing.

- Treating the Yellow Brick Road as a literal universal process would lose the new ontology’s permission for unresolvedness and could convert a motivating myth into transformation pressure.

- Treating the new ontology’s composting as identical to creative remixing would lose endings where nothing new is produced and obligation simply ceases.

- Treating behaviorally measurable deftness as transformation quality would repeat the proxy-capture risk the new ontology is designed to expose.

- Treating machine-readable permissions or ConsentRecords as living consent would lose power, felt room to refuse, and present-moment willingness.

- The Shaman crosswalk itself may privilege symbolic and ethical discrepancies while underweighting architectural reasons for canonical implementation constraints.

## comprehension_impact

- **The Shaman ingestion remains source-recognizable but is not yet canon-equivalent.** It accurately carries provenance, emotional energy, control-move neutrality, Open Up’s distinct scope, liminal Shaman functions, privacy concern, and strand disagreement. It also introduces commitments that canon does not yet contain and challenges several canonical formulations.
