## meaning_by_environment

### Altitude face

An **altitude face** is a position or archetypal mode on BARS’s vertical developmental map. Its defining property is ordered developmental correspondence: Shaman/Magenta through Sage/Teal (`FOUNDATIONS.md`, **The Six Game Master Faces = Integral altitude levels**). It answers: *What complexity or developmental mode is being represented?*

Its evidence, scope, and risks are unique. Under the new ontology’s anti-totalization rule, an altitude attribution must name its subject, line, context, evidence, and duration. It cannot be inferred merely because a player, agent, or reviewer selected a same-named face.

### Runtime sect

A **runtime sect** is an executable agent identity participating in tools and strand orchestration. It has capabilities, tool mappings, routing behavior, advocacy events, sequence position, and output provenance (`.cursor/rules/game-master-agents.mdc`, **Tool mapping**; `.specify/specs/strand-system-bars/spec.md`, **Strand Lifecycle** and **Agent Hierarchy**). It answers: *Which executable agent is acting, through which tools and operational contract?*

A runtime `sage` may synthesize because the runtime contract assigns that function. This does not prove that the software, its operator, or its output occupies Teal altitude.

### Functional review face

A **functional review face** is a responsibility lens selected by the dominant risk of a feature. Its canonical members are Ontologist, Systems Architect, Experience Designer, Encounter Designer, Steward, and Integrator (`specs/doctrine/gm-faces.md`, each face section and **GM Face Routing Block**). It answers: *What kind of review must this design receive?*

Its membership differs from the named six. Systems Architect may overlap with the Architect runtime sect or council lens, and Integrator may overlap with Sage, but overlap of work does not establish identity, equal authority, or one-to-one mapping.

### Player-facing identity

A **player-facing sect identity** is the school, head, mission, affinity, and developmental lens through which a player participates. Players “serve” a sect, and `active_face` affects trigram alignment and quest emphasis (`.agent/context/game-master-sects.md`, **The Six Sects**, **FACE_TRIGRAM_PREFERENCE**, and **Serving a Game Master**). It answers: *Which symbolic school is the player engaging, and how does that choice shape presentation and available play?*

The current BARS design intentionally makes altitude symbolism playable through sect choice. Ontologically, however, choosing or serving a sect is a performance and affiliation event, not sufficient evidence that the player is located at the corresponding developmental altitude.

### Council lens

A **council lens** is a deliberately selected epistemic stance for inquiry: Shaman examines felt truth and symbolism; Architect structure and representation; Challenger falsification; Regent authority and gates; Diplomat users and translation; Sage integration and loss (`design-ontology-six-face-council-brief.md`, **Council assignments**). It answers: *What questions, evidence, and losses must this deliberation foreground?*

Council lenses are plural and composable. Multiple lenses can be applied to the same decision without claiming that the decision-maker changes altitude, joins a player sect, or invokes a particular runtime tool unless those actions are separately specified.

### Local process voice

Some sources assign faces as voices within a bounded ritual: Shaman, Challenger, Sage, and Integrator in 3-2-1; all six in I Ching prework (`emotional-alchemy-321.md`, **GM Voice Sequence**; `spec-prework-iching-six-faces.md`, **Six Game Master faces**). These are local role assignments. They should reference either a council/process lens or a runtime performer explicitly rather than creating another unqualified meaning of Face.

## shared_structure

The five concepts share real structure, which explains why the collision persisted:

- Four environments reuse the named keys `shaman`, `challenger`, `regent`, `architect`, `diplomat`, and `sage`.
- Each partitions a complex whole into six complementary modes.
- Each mode carries a recurring symbolic center: ritual/threshold, force/testing, order/stewardship, structure/strategy, relationship/translation, and integration/whole-system view.
- Player sects deliberately make the altitude map playable (`.agent/context/game-master-sects.md`, **Spiral Dynamics Correspondence**).
- Runtime agents operationalize related motifs: Challenger is routed toward contrarian pressure, Architect toward drafting/compilation, Diplomat toward bridging, and Sage toward synthesis (`.cursor/rules/game-master-agents.mdc`, **Tool mapping**).
- Council lenses refine those motifs into epistemic responsibilities.
- Functional review faces cover a similar total design field—meaning, systems, experience, encounters, ethics, and integration—but use a different taxonomy optimized for feature review.

This supports a common **symbolic face family** or archetypal correspondence layer. It does not support identity across namespaces. Shared name, motif, or count is evidence for a relation; it is not evidence that permissions, ranks, selection criteria, states, or records should be inherited.

## non_equivalences

- **Selection is not assessment.** Choosing the Shaman player sect or Shaman council lens does not establish Magenta altitude.
- **Execution is not development.** Invoking the Sage runtime agent does not establish Teal consciousness in the model, operator, or output.
- **Review responsibility is not persona.** Assigning Steward review does not make Steward a player identity or Regent runtime role.
- **Shared work is not one-to-one identity.** Systems Architect, Architect sect, Architect council lens, and Orange altitude overlap around structure and strategy but differ in subject, authority, and selection rule.
- **Synthesis is not ratification.** Sage integration in runtime, prework, or council does not confer approval authority.
- **Order is not governance jurisdiction.** Regent’s Amber symbolism, Chronicler role, possible coordinator role, and council authority lens are related but not interchangeable.
- **Player affiliation is not runtime permission.** A player serving Architect does not acquire `architect_draft`, database migration authority, or access to strand internals.
- **Runtime identity is not player-facing identity.** A software sect agent can represent or serve a player-facing sect without being the same entity as the school, its leader-character, or the player’s affiliation record.
- **Council lens is not psychographic fact.** A provisional Sage–Architect–Shaman center records an affinity hypothesis; it is neither a global altitude profile nor proof that only those runtime agents should act.
- **Functional membership differs.** Ontologist–Integrator cannot be silently renamed into Shaman–Sage without losing the functional doctrine’s explicit privacy, UX, encounter, schema, and evaluation responsibilities.
- **The six slots do not align canonically.** No reviewed source defines a governing bijection between the functional six and the named six. Any mapping is presently many-to-many and task-dependent.
- **Process casting is local.** The 3-2-1 sequence’s Integrator voice alongside Shaman/Challenger/Sage demonstrates composability, not a unified face enumeration.

## decision

**Altitude face, runtime sect, functional review face, player-facing sect identity, and council lens are five distinct ontology concepts. They belong to one related Game Master symbolic family, but they are not one concept with five interchangeable uses.**

The decision has four consequences:

1. A bare `Face` is insufficient for durable records, prompts, schemas, permissions, developmental claims, or governance decisions.
2. Reuse of the same named key creates an explicit correspondence relation, not object identity.
3. Authority and properties do not cross namespaces automatically. Developmental ordering, runtime tools, review obligations, player affiliations, and council evidence responsibilities remain local to their concepts.
4. Current implementations may continue using a shared `active_face` or sect identifier, but ontology interpretation must distinguish the player affiliation record from the altitude archetype it makes playable and from any runtime agent it invokes.

The shared family should preserve symbolic continuity. The namespaces should preserve semantic, evidentiary, and permission boundaries.

## naming_or_relation_recommendation

Use qualified terms in prose immediately and treat the following as candidate schema names for later implementation:

| Concept | Recommended qualified name | Required distinguishing fields |
|---|---|---|
| Developmental mapping | `altitude_face` | `face_key`, developmental framework, altitude/color, subject, line/domain, context, evidence, time, uncertainty |
| Executable agent | `runtime_sect` | `sect_key`, tools, capabilities, runtime/provider, routing contract, advocacy, inputs, outputs, provenance |
| Feature-review responsibility | `review_face` | `review_key`, review domain, selection reason, required checks, authority, completion evidence |
| Player-facing school/affiliation | `player_sect_identity` | `sect_key`, player, active/inactive status, mission, presentation, trigram affinity, selected-at, revocation/change history |
| Deliberative perspective | `council_lens` | `lens_key`, question set, evidence foregrounded, predictable losses, assignment context, output provenance |

Use a separate relation-bearing symbolic key rather than inheritance:

- `draws_from_face_archetype`
- `makes_playable`
- `represented_by_runtime_sect`
- `invokes_council_lens`
- `reviewed_by`
- `shares_face_key_with`
- `maps_to_review_face` with cardinality explicitly many-to-many

Recommended interpretation examples:

- `player_sect_identity: architect` **makes_playable** `altitude_face: orange/architect`.
- `runtime_sect: architect` **represents** or **serves** the Architect player-facing sect in a specified workflow.
- `council_lens: architect` **draws_from** the Architect archetype but foregrounds schema, provenance, representation, and translation loss.
- `review_face: systems_architect` may **map_to** `runtime_sect: architect` for one task without becoming identical to it.
- `review_face: steward` may require Regent, Diplomat, Sage, or a dedicated evaluator depending on privacy and authority needs; no fixed bijection should be inferred.

Retrieval and prompting should always include source path, namespace, authority layer, and task. “Ask the Architect” is underspecified; acceptable forms include “invoke the Architect runtime sect,” “apply the Systems Architect review face,” “use the Architect council lens,” or “present the Architect player sect.”

For human development claims, use the altitude namespace only when altitude is actually the subject of the claim. Record a bounded line, context, evidence, and uncertainty. A player’s `active_face`, a chosen council role, stylistic affinity, or agent invocation is not altitude evidence.

## residual_uncertainty

- `game-master-sects.md` explicitly says the sects “ARE” Spiral Dynamics levels made playable. This decision separates the concepts while preserving `makes_playable`; later governance may clarify whether BARS intends any stronger identity claim.
- The correct schema migration for existing `active_face` records is outside this decision. Existing data may currently combine affiliation, developmental lens, content routing, and identity presentation in one field.
- It remains unclear whether “player-facing identity” means sect affiliation, the Game Master head as a character, a player self-description, or all three. Those may require further subtyping.
- `specs/doctrine/gm-faces.md` does not state whether its functional taxonomy is current, legacy, or complementary. This decision preserves it as a distinct review namespace without deciding its lifecycle status.
- The exact correspondence between each named archetype and each functional review face remains many-to-many and ungoverned.
- BARS canon grants Sage unusual mask/delegation behavior (`game-master-sects.md`, **Domain Constraint** and **Why the Sage synthesizes**). Namespacing does not decide whether that runtime privilege should remain or how it is authorized.
- The strand spec variously locates coordination in Diplomat, Sage, and Regent. Distinguishing namespaces prevents semantic leakage but does not resolve coordinator authority.
- The anti-global-ranking invariant constrains how altitude claims may be used in the new ontology. It does not, by itself, decide whether the foundational Spiral Dynamics ordering should be revised.
- The shared symbolic family still needs a minimal definition of what remains invariant across Shaman, Challenger, Regent, Architect, Diplomat, and Sage without turning poetic affinity into inherited permissions.
- Local process voices may merit a separate `process_voice` object if they acquire durable configuration or permissions; for now they should be qualified role assignments rather than a sixth primary face namespace.
