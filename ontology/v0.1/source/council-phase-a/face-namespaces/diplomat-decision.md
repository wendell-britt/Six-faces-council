## meaning_by_environment

| Environment | What a “face” names | Subject represented | Selection or activation | Persistence | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Integral altitude** | A developmental/sense-making pattern ordered from Magenta through Teal | A level or lens of complexity, currently ambiguously applied to persons, moments, or developmental contexts | Assessment or interpretation through Integral/Spiral Dynamics | Potentially developmental and ordered | Changes how growth, inclusion, and vertical complexity are interpreted |
| **Runtime sect/agent** | An executable AI role with tools, prompts, orchestration position, and audit output | A software agent or strand participant | Router, coordinator, MCP tool, explicit sect sequence | Exists for a run or as a configured agent identity | Performs work, emits advocacy, and produces artifacts |
| **Functional review face** | A review discipline such as privacy, schema, UX, encounter design, or integration | A responsibility attached to a feature/spec review | Primary, secondary, and review routing based on task needs | Task-scoped | Determines what kinds of questions, checks, and approvals are requested |
| **Player-facing sect identity** | A chosen school, mission, ethos, stat, trigram affinity, and quest orientation | The player's affiliation or active play lens | Player choice through `active_face` / serving a sect | Persists in story progress until changed | Shapes narrative presentation, quest alignment, and possible identification |
| **Council lens** | A bounded interpretive perspective in multi-perspective inquiry | A contribution or turn in a council process | Assigned by council sequence or invoked for a question | Temporary and inquiry-scoped | Produces one attributable translation that must not replace the source or other lenses |

The same surface name can therefore answer five different questions:

- **Altitude:** What developmental pattern is being modeled?
- **Runtime:** Which executable agent should act?
- **Review:** What responsibility must inspect the work?
- **Player-facing:** Which school or play identity is the player choosing to inhabit?
- **Council:** Which perspective should speak in this inquiry?

## shared_structure

The face environments are not unrelated. Four of the five reuse a common six-name pattern: Shaman, Challenger, Regent, Architect, Diplomat, and Sage. Across sources, those names retain recognizable semantic attractors:

- Shaman: ritual, felt pattern, liminality, belonging;
- Challenger: power, edge, action, falsification;
- Regent: order, rules, governance, record;
- Architect: strategy, structure, systems, blueprint;
- Diplomat: relationship, care, bridge, community meaning;
- Sage: integration, systems, meta-view, synthesis.

This recurring pattern supports one shared **Game Master archetype family** with six stable archetype keys. The family supplies names, motifs, domains, symbolic associations, and provenance.

The environments also share several structural features:

- each decomposes a complex task or developmental field into differentiated perspectives;
- each face has a bounded domain and characteristic questions;
- several faces may contribute to one event;
- routing or selection matters because no single face is intended to perform every function equally;
- synthesis is typically associated with Sage, though the exact operational authority differs;
- face outputs should remain attributable so disagreement and translation loss stay visible.

The functional review set shares the general idea of differentiated review responsibility but not the same names, developmental ordering, symbolic lineage, or one-to-one domains. It is structurally analogous to the archetype family, not demonstrated to be another instance of the same six members.

## non_equivalences

### A chosen sect is not a measured altitude

`game-master-sects.md` currently connects `active_face` to a developmental lens and also says the faces are Spiral Dynamics levels. Those relations do not make the player's selection evidence that the player *is at* that level. Choice may express curiosity, desired practice, narrative identity, mood, aesthetic preference, or the challenge the player wants to meet.

Equating affiliation with altitude would turn a reversible play choice into a developmental judgment and could make Shaman, Challenger, Regent, Architect, or Diplomat feel like inferior identities relative to Sage. That conflicts with the new ontology's no-global-rank and type-is-not-level invariants.

### A runtime agent is not an altitude

A runtime Shaman or Diplomat is selected because its prompt, tools, or specialty fit the work. Its invocation does not mean the software, operator, or subject occupies Magenta or Green altitude. Runtime routing is functional; altitude interpretation is developmental.

### A council lens is not a runtime identity

A council face is an epistemic position temporarily adopted to generate an attributable reading. It may be enacted by an AI, person, or composite process. A runtime agent can embody that lens, but execution identity and interpretive stance have different provenance and success criteria.

### A council lens is not a player identity

Wendell's provisional Sage–Architect–Shaman configuration is a psychographic hypothesis about design orientation, not a required sect choice, a runtime route, or a whole-person altitude. Likewise, temporarily asking the Diplomat question does not make the speaker a Green person.

### Functional review faces are not the named sects

Ontologist, Systems Architect, Experience Designer, Encounter Designer, Steward, and Integrator select responsibilities by feature risk. Some overlaps are strong—Systems Architect with Architect, Integrator with Sage—but the remaining mappings are many-to-many:

- privacy may need Steward, Diplomat, Regent, and Sage;
- onboarding may need Experience Designer, Diplomat, Shaman, and Architect;
- an encounter may need Encounter Designer, Challenger, Shaman, and Diplomat;
- ontology naming may need Ontologist, Sage, Architect, and Regent.

Forcing a one-to-one mapping would either erase functional coverage or assign an altitude implication to a review task.

### Player-facing sect and runtime sect share a definition but not an instance

The same SectDefinition can supply a mission and symbolic identity to both environments. The player's affiliation with Diplomat and a running Diplomat agent are nevertheless different relations involving different agents, permissions, durations, and consequences. One should not silently act for, assess, or speak as the other.

### Sage synthesis does not imply universal authority

Altitude canon says Sage includes and transcends prior levels; runtime and prework sources give Sage synthesis responsibilities. The council brief limits Sage to integration that preserves disagreements and prohibits replacement of source translations. These meanings overlap around whole-system view but do not establish that every Sage output is developmentally superior, empirically correct, or authorized to override other agents.

### Concealed namespace relations are not neutral

`game-master-sects.md` → **Spiral Dynamics Correspondence** says the altitude mapping was intentionally hidden from Diplomat because Green resists hierarchy. Whatever its historical rationale, a system that uses a player's sect selection as an undisclosed developmental classification changes the meaning of consent and identity. A player-facing identity may draw on altitude symbolism, but any assessment consequence must be explicit, contestable, and separable from participation.

## decision

Altitude face, runtime sect, functional review face, player-facing identity, and council lens are **distinct ontology concepts**. They must not be represented by one unqualified `Face` object or treated as interchangeable based on a shared label.

They are related through a two-layer structure:

1. **Shared archetypal lineage:** a `GameMasterArchetype` family carries the six recurring names and their broad symbolic/domain motifs.
2. **Environment-specific objects:** altitude lens, runtime sect/agent, player sect affiliation, and council lens each reference an archetype but retain their own subject, authority, activation, duration, evidence, and consequences. Functional review faces remain a separate `ReviewDiscipline` taxonomy related by capability/routing mappings rather than archetype identity.

The player-facing and runtime uses may reference the same `SectDefinition`, but a player's `SectAffiliation` and an executable `RuntimeAgent` remain distinct instances and relations.

The decision also establishes a user-protection boundary:

> Selecting, enjoying, serving, invoking, or temporarily speaking through a face is not evidence of a person's global developmental altitude.

An altitude interpretation, when used at all, must be scoped to a developmental line, context, and evidence claim. It cannot be inferred solely from sect choice, council assignment, agent route, writing style, aesthetic preference, or functional review responsibility.

This resolves the ontology distinction. It does not abolish the BARS altitude model, choose a preferred developmental theory, or decide whether the functional review taxonomy should later be renamed.

## naming_or_relation_recommendation

### Recommended namespaces

| Namespace / object | Example identifier | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `gm_archetype` | `gm_archetype:diplomat` | Shared semantic and symbolic lineage: relationship, care, bridge, Wind association where applicable |
| `altitude_lens` | `altitude_lens:green_diplomat` | A bounded Integral/Spiral Dynamics developmental pattern; never an unscoped person rank |
| `sect_definition` | `sect:diplomat` | Player-facing school/mission/ethos/trigram and the thematic definition runtime agents may draw from |
| `runtime_agent` | `runtime_agent:diplomat` | Executable role, prompts, tools, capabilities, orchestration rules, and audit identity |
| `player_affiliation` | `player_affiliation:{player}:diplomat` | A player's consented and reversible relation to a sect; not the player ontology itself |
| `council_lens` | `council_lens:diplomat` | Temporary epistemic stance used for an attributable inquiry or translation |
| `review_discipline` | `review_discipline:experience_designer` | Functional review responsibility from `specs/doctrine/gm-faces.md` |

### Recommended relations

- `instantiates_archetype(runtime_agent, gm_archetype)`
- `draws_from_archetype(sect_definition, gm_archetype)`
- `symbolizes_altitude_pattern(gm_archetype, altitude_lens)`
- `affiliates_with(player_affiliation, sect_definition)`
- `uses_lens(council_turn, council_lens)`
- `draws_from_archetype(council_lens, gm_archetype)`
- `routes_to(review_discipline, runtime_agent)` with many-to-many cardinality
- `supports_review(council_lens, review_discipline)` with many-to-many cardinality
- `performed_by(council_lens, agent_or_person)`
- `scoped_to(altitude_claim, developmental_line_and_context)`

Explicitly prohibited shortcut relations:

- `player_affiliation → has_altitude`
- `runtime_agent → is_altitude`
- `council_lens → proves_person_type`
- `review_discipline → equals_named_sect`
- `sage_output → overrides_other_faces`

### Retrieval and interface language

- Never retrieve or display bare “Face: Diplomat” when the layer matters. Say **Diplomat council lens**, **Diplomat runtime agent**, **Diplomat sect**, **Green/Diplomat altitude lens**, or **Diplomat player affiliation**.
- Reserve **functional review face** for the Ontologist–Integrator taxonomy and prefer **review discipline** in new schemas to reduce collision.
- In player interfaces, use **sect**, **school**, or **chosen lens** rather than **level** unless the player has explicitly entered a developmental-assessment context.
- If altitude interpretation influences content, state that it is a model of a perspective in context, disclose how it was inferred, permit correction, and provide participation without accepting the assessment.
- Store the namespace in strand audit trails and council outputs so later readers know whether `architect` meant a runtime agent, council lens, sect, review discipline, or altitude pattern.

### User-facing explanation

A lower-context explanation can preserve the distinction as:

> “The same six names appear in several parts of the system. A sect is a school you can choose, an agent is a tool that can act, a council lens is a way of asking questions, and an altitude lens is a developmental theory. They can share a theme without being the same thing—and choosing one does not rank you.”

This wording preserves access to the symbolic coherence of the six names without requiring Integral vocabulary or concealing a developmental judgment inside play identity.

## residual_uncertainty

- `FOUNDATIONS.md` and `game-master-sects.md` describe the altitude correspondence strongly enough that later governance may need to decide whether the shared archetype is derived from altitude, merely correlated with it, or broader than it. This decision preserves the relation without deciding theoretical priority.
- `game-master-sects.md` uses `active_face` for player selection. Renaming or migrating that implemented field is outside scope; the ontology can namespace its meaning even if code retains the legacy field name.
- The exact duration, reversibility, and multiplicity of player sect affiliations are not specified in the inspected source. A player may eventually be allowed one active sect, several learned sects, or temporary masks.
- The functional-review taxonomy's status—current, legacy, or complementary to the named sect system—remains undocumented. This decision preserves it as a separate review taxonomy rather than choosing whether it should remain sixfold.
- The boundary between a `SectDefinition` and `GameMasterArchetype` may be thin in implementation. They remain conceptually distinct because a sect adds mission, service relationship, quest behavior, ethos/stat, and player-facing consequences.
- Runtime sources disagree about coordination: the strand hierarchy maps Coordinator and Composer to Diplomat, while FR2 allows Sage or Regent to coordinate and prework requires Sage synthesis. Namespace separation makes the conflict visible but does not resolve coordinator authority.
- The meaning of AI sect self-advocacy and refusal remains unresolved. Runtime-agent boundaries must not be copied automatically into human player consent.
- The altitude sources do not clearly specify whether they assess a whole person, a momentary perspective, a line-specific capacity, or a narrative archetype. The new ontology's anti-ranking invariant requires scoped use, but a later altitude-specific decision may need to formalize the evidence model.
- Trigram preferences, ethos stats, and face sentences may belong to SectDefinition, GameMasterArchetype, or both. Their exact placement should follow implementation provenance rather than similarity alone.
