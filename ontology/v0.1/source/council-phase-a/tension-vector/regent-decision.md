# Regent Decision — Purpose-Bound Representation

Interior experience belongs to the player. The system may hold only a purpose-bound, correctable representation of what was reported. Transformation is a later claim requiring separate evidence and consent; the path between them is a trace.

Collection, session processing, persistence, artifact generation, personalization, NPC learning, collective fuel, sharing, analytics, and model improvement require distinct permissions. Player preview, omission, correction, rejection, deletion, and downstream lineage are readiness gates.

Use `TensionRepresentation` and `TensionSnapshot` in new schemas. Preserve TensionVector only as a versioned legacy alias unless a real coordinate model is later declared.
