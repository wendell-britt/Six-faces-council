# Shaman Decision — The Vector Sketches the Knot

TensionVector should stop at the represented dissatisfied configuration. The complete movement belongs to an `AlchemyTrace` linking reports, practice, transformation or no-shift, satisfaction, action, artifact, and consequence.

Lived tension belongs to the player. The mask is a revisable metaphor rather than diagnosis or stable identity. `integrationShift` belongs to a TransformationRecord; somatic echo belongs to a SomaticReport; aligned action and artifacts belong downstream.

“Vector” may survive as evocative game language, but the formal object should be a TensionConfiguration unless direction and magnitude are genuinely defined.
