# Sage Decision — Configuration, Trace, and Consequence

The lived tension is the phenomenon. A structured representation is a lossy, purpose-bound interpretation derived from reports. A transformation is a separate claimed transition; action and artifact are downstream consequences.

The synthesis structure is:

- `TensionConfiguration` — reported source configuration at time A;
- `TensionSnapshot` — exact persisted representation;
- `TransformationTrace` — provenance linking practice, transformation/no-shift finding, satisfaction, intention, action, artifact, and consequence;
- `BARS321TensionVector` — optional namespaced compatibility or player-facing label.

Bare `TensionVector` is unsafe in formal schema and retrieval because current sources use it for incompatible layers and imply precision they do not possess.
