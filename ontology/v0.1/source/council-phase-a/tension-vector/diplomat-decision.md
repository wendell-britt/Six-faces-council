# Diplomat Decision — The Tension Is Lived; the System Maps by Permission

TensionVector should describe only the player-reported dissatisfied configuration at a named moment. A separate TransformationTrace may remain partial and may end in shift, no-shift, uncertainty, refusal, rest, or nondispatch.

Use `TensionRepresentation` in the general ontology and `BARS321TensionVector` as a namespaced subtype preserving product lineage. In ordinary player language, “what you named” or “tension map” is less diagnostic and extractive.

Shared conflict requires separately governed reports, negotiated shared meaning where it exists, and preserved disagreement. One person’s satisfaction cannot overwrite another’s harm, consent, or lack of repair.
