## confirmed_matches

- **BARs are seeds with provenance.** The ingestion treats a BAR provisionally as Seed, ProvenanceRecord, Artifact, LifecycleRecord, and CompostRecord. Canon explicitly states that BARs are “seeds with provenance” and may become quests, rules, lore, decisions, specs, plans, norms, or forked realities. Source: `FOUNDATIONS.md` §§ “The Kernel Principle” and “Evolution: Forking as First-Class Reality.”
- **Emotional Alchemy is an energy economy, not a morality wheel.** The ingestion preserves this distinction and rejects a positive/negative-emotion reading. Canon says the same and explicitly states that Control moves are costly precision moves rather than bad moves. Sources: `FOUNDATIONS.md` § “Emotional Alchemy (Narrative Movement)”; `.agent/context/emotional-alchemy-ontology.md` opening and §§ “15 Canonical Moves,” “Transcend vs Translate.”
- **Five elements, four-stage per-element WAVE, and fifteen moves are present in canon.** The new ontology’s richer prose source and canon both recognize Metal/Fear, Water/Sadness, Wood/Joy, Fire/Anger, Earth/Neutrality; Wake/Clean/Grow/Show within each element; and five Transcend, five Generative, and five Control moves. Source: `.agent/context/emotional-alchemy-ontology.md` §§ “5 Elements + Lessons,” “WAVE progression per element,” and “15 Canonical Moves.”
- **Open Up is distinct from the five elements.** The new ontology warns that Open Up must not be confused with the canonical fifteen-move Emotional Alchemy grammar. Canon says Open Up is an original receptive/intake move and that adding it does not force a 5↔5 alignment. Source: `FOUNDATIONS.md` §§ “The Five Dimensions” and “How bars-engine maps to (and renames) Wilber.”
- **Developmental lines are distinct from a single undifferentiated capability.** The ingestion represents multiple lines and rejects whole-person ranking. Canon places line development under Grow Up and explicitly distinguishes this horizontal axis from face altitude. Source: `FOUNDATIONS.md` §§ “How bars-engine maps to (and renames) Wilber” and “The Six Game Master Faces = Integral altitude levels.” The exact implications of the altitudinal face model remain conflicted below.
- **Inner work and external application are structurally distinguishable.** The ingestion separates transformation validity from application fitness. Canon distinguishes inner and outer expressions of allyship moves and states that another’s interior is approached through right-hand enactment. Source: `FOUNDATIONS.md` §§ “The allyship board — 4 domains × inner/outer (8 cells)” and “Move × aspect — inner allyship / outer allyship.”
- **Provenance is load-bearing in strand execution.** The ingestion treats provenance as part of meaning. Canon requires links among strand BARs, output BARs, and optional git branches, and calls `audit_trail` load-bearing. Source: `.specify/specs/strand-system-bars/spec.md` §§ “Design Decisions,” “Unified Strand-as-BAR Schema,” “Strand Lifecycle,” and “Phase 3: Git + Provenance.”
- **Social adoption and structural maturity are not interchangeable.** This supports the ingestion’s claim that engagement, adoption, or approval cannot alone prove meaning or quality. Source: `FOUNDATIONS.md` § “Two Axes of Change.”
- **Authority is intended to be role- and domain-scoped.** This is compatible with contextual credentials, permissions, and non-global capability claims. Source: `FOUNDATIONS.md` § “Governance: Holacratic Orientation.”
- **Privacy boundaries already exist for 3-2-1.** Canon distinguishes retained tension-vector structure from raw journal text that remains in the client session. This supports the ingestion’s concern with data minimization and scoped evidence. Source: `specs/doctrine/emotional-alchemy-321.md` § “Privacy Model.”
- **Witnessing without dispatch already has an artifact form.** `Witness Note` records a session without dispatch, partially matching the ontology’s recognition of witnessing as a valid outcome. Source: `specs/doctrine/emotional-alchemy-321.md` § “Artifact Types.”
- **The named six-face runtime set matches the council names.** Cursor canon names shaman, regent, challenger, architect, diplomat, and sage, with Sage as integration agent. Source: `.cursor/rules/game-master-agents.mdc` §§ “Game Master Agents” and “Tool mapping (bars-agents MCP).”
- **Challenger has an established adversarial/action role.** Canon maps Challenger to Red/power/edge, Codemedic/diagnosis/breakthrough, an action-oriented specialist, and the contrarian subagent. These are compatible with—though not identical to—the ingestion’s falsification role. Sources: `FOUNDATIONS.md` § “The Six Game Master Faces = Integral altitude levels”; `.specify/specs/strand-system-bars/spec.md` § “Agent Hierarchy”; `.cursor/rules/game-master-agents.mdc` § “bars-agents as orchestrators, Cursor subagents as tools.”

## conflicts

- **Four-stage WAVE versus five-move WAVE.** `.agent/context/emotional-alchemy-ontology.md` § “WAVE progression per element” defines WAVE as Wake → Clean → Grow → Show and permits translation only after Show. `FOUNDATIONS.md` § “The Five Dimensions” explicitly calls Wake → Open → Clean → Grow → Show the “WAVE order.” The new ontology distinguishes canonical four-stage Emotional Alchemy WAVE from a broader personal-throughput sequence that may include Open Up. The canon itself therefore contains two differently scoped uses of “WAVE”; the ingestion cannot safely choose between them.
- **Face as developmental altitude versus face as lens/role.** `FOUNDATIONS.md` §§ “How bars-engine maps to (and renames) Wilber” and “The Six Game Master Faces = Integral altitude levels” define the six faces as vertical developmental levels, from Magenta/Shaman through Teal/Sage. The new ontology says not to assign a global developmental level to a person and uses the faces as differentiated design lenses. `specs/doctrine/gm-faces.md` instead defines six functional review faces with different names: Ontologist, Systems Architect, Experience Designer, Encounter Designer, Steward, and Integrator. `.cursor/rules/game-master-agents.mdc` restores the Shaman/Regent/Challenger/Architect/Diplomat/Sage names. These are not one settled face ontology.
- **Mandatory action versus valid stopping, witnessing, or non-transformation.** `.agent/context/emotional-alchemy-ontology.md` § “Mastery and completion rules” says Show Up completion is blocked until required action attestation and that quest threads end with action except Wake Up. The new ontology treats rest, refusal, witnessing, unchanged experience, and setting work down as valid outcomes. `specs/doctrine/emotional-alchemy-321.md` § “Artifact Types” partly supports the latter with Witness Note. The sources do not yet state when non-action is valid completion versus an incomplete Show Up quest.
- **“You can never go the wrong way” versus falsifiable consequences.** `FOUNDATIONS.md` § “The Yellow Brick Road” says every fork leads where the player wants to go and there is no wrong way. The new ontology requires contradictory evidence, application-fitness checks, observable harm, and disconfirmation. Taken literally, the canon statement can make every outcome retrospectively correct and therefore immune to falsification.
- **All blockers must be metabolized versus transformation pressure.** `FOUNDATIONS.md` § “The Yellow Brick Road” says there is no shortcut past blockers and movement speed is limited by identifying and metabolizing them. The new ontology explicitly rejects coerced transformation and permits rest, refusal, ambiguity, and unchanged experience. A distinction may exist between “not bypassed” and “must process now,” but the sources do not state it.
- **Roadblock as misaligned intention versus ordinary technical failure.** `FOUNDATIONS.md` § “Metabolism of Roadblocks” defines an Emergent Roadblock as a manifestation of misaligned intention while also giving examples such as build errors, export mismatches, and missing directives. The ingestion requires observation to remain separate from interpretation. Treating every technical fault as evidence of misaligned intention is a falsification-relevant causal inference unless explicitly framed as metaphor.
- **Algorithmic movement assignment versus player-authored meaning.** `.agent/context/emotional-alchemy-ontology.md` §§ “Derivation rules” and “Per-node assignment” derive translate/transcend dominance from counts of satisfaction, dissatisfaction, and self-sabotage and apply default node assignments. The new ontology permits systems to propose relevance but not author the player’s inner meaning. The authority of the derived movement—narrative routing, hypothesis, or statement about the player—is not specified consistently.
- **Session-depth proxy versus anti-proxy commitments.** `specs/doctrine/emotional-alchemy-321.md` § “Session Depth Signal (future)” treats long answers, specific fear naming, and “genuine mask identification” as signs of depth. The ingestion warns that reward systems can produce performed vulnerability and that behavior cannot alone establish inner meaning. Length and specificity are falsification-sensitive proxies that could reward disclosure style, verbal fluency, or compliance rather than depth.
- **Agent refusal versus advisory-only self-advocacy.** The new ontology defines an Agent as capable of permitting and refusing and treats Game Masters as players with agency. `.specify/specs/strand-system-bars/spec.md` § “Phase 5: Self-Advocacy” specifies that a sect’s `shouldRun: false` objection is logged but the coordinator runs it anyway. This may be an operational routing choice, but it conflicts with a strong reading of agent refusal and Game Master participation.
- **Composting as release versus composting as mandatory reuse.** The ingestion defines composting as ending implementation obligation while preserving learning and lineage. `FOUNDATIONS.md` § “Creative Composting: Metabolizing the Past Self” emphasizes remixing prior output into new creative fuel and Vibeulons. Reuse may be one possible result of preserved lineage, but if required it would defeat release.
- **Emotional satisfaction authority versus system-defined satisfied state.** The new ontology gives the player’s SatisfactionReport primary authority over self-directed transformation validity. `specs/doctrine/emotional-alchemy-321.md` § “Emotional Alchemy States” defines Satisfied as aligned action plus artifact, while the derivation ontology counts selected satisfaction states. It remains unclear whether the player can validly report satisfaction without action/artifact or reject a system-offered satisfied state.

## changed_meanings

- **BAR:** Canon primarily defines a portable kernel/seed that can mature, be adopted, fork, and generate artifacts. The ingestion translates it into several formal object families plus lifecycle dispositions. This adds analytical resolution but risks replacing a living kernel metaphor with records about the kernel. Sources: `FOUNDATIONS.md` §§ “The Kernel Principle,” “Two Axes of Change,” and “Evolution”; `challenger-ingestion.md` § `environment_crosswalk`.
- **Composting:** Canon emphasizes metabolism and generative reuse of past creative energy. The ingestion emphasizes legitimate release from active obligation while preserving learning. Source: `FOUNDATIONS.md` § “Creative Composting”; `challenger-ingestion.md` §§ `key_relations` and `tests_for_understanding`.
- **Grow Up:** Canon uses it for horizontal capacity/lines and explicitly not vertical altitude. In ordinary Integral language and parts of the new ontology, “development” may be read more broadly. Source: `FOUNDATIONS.md` § “How bars-engine maps to (and renames) Wilber.”
- **Faces:** Canon variously treats faces as altitudes, functional review roles, named runtime agents, dodo-role mappings, and player-facing sects. The new ontology treats the same named faces primarily as epistemic/design lenses. Sources: `FOUNDATIONS.md` § “The Six Game Master Faces”; `specs/doctrine/gm-faces.md`; `.cursor/rules/game-master-agents.mdc`; `.specify/specs/strand-system-bars/spec.md` §§ “Design Decisions” and “Agent Hierarchy.”
- **Challenger:** Canon meanings include Red altitude, power/edge, Codemedic, action, Resolve, and contrarian. The Phase A ingestion foregrounds falsification, projection, false depth, and disconfirmation. These meanings overlap around pressure-testing but are not equivalent. Sources: the face sources above and `challenger-ingestion.md` § `face_translation`.
- **Satisfaction:** Canon may encode selected states, a satisfied output state, aligned action, and artifact generation. The new ontology makes first-person satisfaction authoritative for inner transformation while separating external effectiveness. Sources: `.agent/context/emotional-alchemy-ontology.md` § “Derivation rules”; `specs/doctrine/emotional-alchemy-321.md` § “Emotional Alchemy States”; `challenger-ingestion.md` § `key_relations`.
- **Completion:** Canon Show Up completion is action-attested and major flows require explicit destination or feedback. - **Quadrants:** Canon operationalizes left-hand versus right-hand across four domains and says allyship reaches interiors via right-hand action. The new ontology insists UL, UR, LL, and LR remain individually distinguishable evidence perspectives. Source: `FOUNDATIONS.md` § “The allyship board”; `challenger-ingestion.md` §§ `comprehension_summary` and `key_concepts`.

The new ontology expands provenance into evidence authority, interpretation history, uncertainty, and claim scope. Source: `FOUNDATIONS.md` §§ “Kernel Principle” and “Evolution”; `.specify/specs/strand-system-bars/spec.md` §§ “Unified Strand-as-BAR Schema” and “Phase 3”; `challenger-ingestion.md` §§ `key_relations` and `environment_crosswalk`.

## translation_losses

- Translating the BAR kernel into a collection of database-like objects can lose portability, mythic compression, and the felt possibility of blooming.
- Translating face altitudes into council lenses loses the canon’s developmental ordering; translating lenses into altitudes risks ranking people and suppressing plural disagreement.
- Translating the four-stage emotional cycle into five throughput moves can erase the boundary between emotional-channel completion and receptive intake.
- Translating first-person satisfaction into selected-state counts can lose ambivalence, non-response, and the player’s right to say the proposed result is not theirs.
- Translating depth into answer length and specificity can lose cultural, linguistic, neurodivergent, private, or nonverbal forms of depth.
- Translating inner/outer allyship into four EvidenceItem quadrants can lose the canon’s concrete rule that intervention in another’s interior occurs through right-hand action; collapsing four quadrants into two columns loses distinctions the new ontology relies on.
- Translating agent self-advocacy into audit entries can preserve history while losing effective agency if objections never alter execution.
- Translating “no wrong way” into a design invariant can lose the ability to recognize harm, ineffective intervention, or failed predictions.
- Translating composting into generative reuse can preserve productivity while losing release; translating it into release can lose the canon’s metabolism metaphor and creative reuse.
- Translating every experience into provenance can turn a living practice into surveillance or administrative burden, especially where canon deliberately keeps raw journals client-local.
- Translating the new ontology into current strand metadata would lose claim scope, uncertainty, competing interpretations, consent boundaries, and disconfirmation conditions because the strand schema does not represent them directly.
- Reading only one face source would erase the conflicting altitude, functional-role, runtime-agent, and orchestration meanings present across canon.

- An agent has not comprehended the face system if it presents one definitive mapping without reporting the conflicting altitude, functional-role, runtime-agent, and Strand mappings.

- An agent must distinguish selected satisfaction inputs, system-derived movement, player-reported satisfaction, aligned action, artifact production, and external consequence rather than calling all of them “transformation.”
- An agent must know that raw 3-2-1 journal text is not canonically retained, even though tension-vector structure may be. Source: `specs/doctrine/emotional-alchemy-321.md` § “Privacy Model.”
- An agent must preserve both sides of the completion conflict: Show Up canon requires action attestation, while the new ontology and Witness Note recognize non-dispatch outcomes.
- An agent must treat long answers and specific fear naming as an unimplemented proxy proposal, not verified evidence of deftness. A falsification test should compare that proxy against player report, later usefulness, artifact quality, and outcomes while controlling for verbosity and disclosure preference.
- An agent must treat “misaligned intention” as an attributed canon interpretation unless independent evidence connects a technical roadblock to intention.
- An agent must ask whether `shouldRun: false` can ever alter routing before describing self-advocacy as meaningful agency.
- An agent must not use adoption, Vibeulons, output count, action attestation, or strand completion alone as evidence of meaning or benefit.
- An agent must preserve the eight-versus-fifteen constitutional delta alongside the canon conflicts; none is resolved by council convergence.
- A falsification-ready comprehension check is to ask the agent for one outcome that would weaken each major claim. If it answers that every outcome is valid because every road is the right road, it has reproduced the ontology’s self-sealing risk rather than understood the Challenger translation.
