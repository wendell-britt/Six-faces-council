# Shaman Ingestion — Phase A

**Face:** Shaman
**Phase:** A — Ingestion and Translation
**Status:** Provisional; non-governance

## comprehension_summary

The ontology treats design as play, inquiry, and applied philosophy. Rules and omissions make claims about reality by deciding what can change, what counts as development, and whose evidence matters. Its center is not comprehensive simulation but meaningful changes in possibility.

The constitutional layer in the philosophy draft currently contains fifteen commitments: the Game Master and designer are players; authentic pleasure precedes outward translation; the body participates in knowing; mechanics confer ontological weight; development changes possibility rather than worth; relationship creates capability; performance may precede understanding; mystery should reward inquiry; players author inner meaning; lineage matters; helpers remain inside care; tools practice what they teach; composting is completion; and motivation cannot substitute for meaning.

These commitments are constrained by plural evidence, contextual development, distinct agency, consent, corrigibility, provenance, and the refusal to collapse a person into any score, type, interpretation, or developmental level. AQAL perspectives concern the same event without becoming interchangeable. Emotional Alchemy treats charge as usable energy rather than moral defect. WAVE may transform charge into capacity, action, relationship, or artifact, but stopping, witnessing, ambiguity, and non-change remain valid.

The Shaman reading is that the ontology is most alive where it permits encounter: bodily evidence, charged symbols, relational agency, mystery, lineage, repair, rest, and release. It risks becoming intellectual armor when AQAL coverage, evidence profiles, permissions, provenance, and developmental vocabularies are completed administratively without contact with an actual body, relationship, consequence, or playable choice.

The formal model faithfully adds entities, relations, evidence boundaries, confidence profiles, lifecycle distinctions, and hard invariants. The YAML is a usable machine seed, but necessarily contains less texture than the philosophy. Phase A must preserve that loss rather than mistake formal encodability for full comprehension.

A source-status tension remains visible: the council brief names eight constitutional principles, while the later philosophy names fifteen. I treat both as source material and do not decide their authority or merge them in this pass.

## key_concepts

- **Designer–player and Game-Master–player continuity:** creation and facilitation must contain uncertainty, agency, consequence, feedback, and enjoyment.
- **Authentic pleasure with concentric translation:** begin with Wendell’s pleasure, test transfer to psychographic near-neighbors, then widen access without sterilizing the source.
- **Embodied evidence:** sensation may confirm, complicate, or reject a model; it does not interpret itself infallibly.
- **Mechanical ontology:** tracking, differentiating, rewarding, permitting, and changing something gives it ontological weight in play.
- **Contextual development:** development is a change in situated possibility, never aggregate personal worth.
- **Relational capability:** distinct agents may create capacities together; access does not imply command.
- **Enacted trust:** capability, integrity, permission, repair, and coordination remain situated; benevolence matters but is not reduced merely because it is hard to model.
- **Performance before possession:** play can rehearse or discover a form before it becomes identity or stable capacity.
- **Inferable mystery:** hidden causality may remain plural and uncanny while still answering to evidence, prediction, satisfying action, and usefulness.
- **Player-authored inner meaning:** systems may propose relevance but cannot exhaust or finally interpret a player.
- **World otherness:** player-derived material must meet persistence, resistance, agency, history, or surprise beyond the player model.
- **Lineage and provenance:** seed, charge, action, relationship, transformation, artifact, and consequence remain meaningfully connected.
- **Emotional Alchemy:** five channels and WAVE form an energy economy, not a virtue ladder or demand to fix emotion.
- **Transformation validity versus application fitness:** inner satisfaction and external helpfulness require different evidence and authority.
- **Skilled helping:** shared charge establishes relevance, not authority; the helper’s limits, consent, pleasure, and replenishment remain inside the field.
- **BAR lifecycle:** capture need not create obligation; resting, storing, harvesting, shipping, delegating, and composting are legitimate.
- **Playable remediation:** design shadows should be met through practices that enact the missing capacity.
- **Integral plurality:** UL, UR, LL, and LR are distinct perspectives on one event; lines, levels, states, and types must not be collapsed.
- **Epistemic humility:** observation, interpretation, hypothesis, disagreement, and inference remain distinguishable and corrigible.
- **Motivation subordinated to meaning:** engagement is insufficient evidence of pleasure, transformation, or value.
- **Rest, refusal, witness, and non-change:** these are possible valid outcomes, not failed transformations.

## key_relations

- Designer pleasure **informs** design intention but does not **prove** player pleasure.
- Design intention **becomes testable through** affordance, action, dynamic, felt experience, shared meaning, systemic consequence, and evidence.
- Mechanics **make dimensions mechanically real** while narrative and social practice may support other dimensions without mechanizing them.
- Charge **may move through** attention or practice into transformation, satisfaction evidence, capacity/action/relationship/artifact, and later consequence; the sequence need not always complete.
- DevelopmentClaim **belongs to** a line, context, subject, evidence set, and scope.
- Performance **may cultivate** capability but does not automatically establish stable identity.
- Credential **represents** a contextual capability claim but is not identical to living capacity.
- Relationship **enables** coordination while preserving each participant’s permission and capacity to refuse.
- Consent **is granted, conditioned, withheld, or revoked**; it is not inferred from compliance, disclosure, acquisition, or continued play.
- Player statement and SatisfactionReport **have authority over** reported inner meaning and transformation validity, not external effect.
- Relational attestation and consequence **inform** application fitness, not another person’s inner meaning.
- SomaticReport **supports or complicates** a claim but cannot alone establish its one correct interpretation.
- AdaptiveSystem **observes signals, infers provisionally, and adapts conditions**, while the player **authors personal meaning** and may correct or reject the inference.
- WorldAgent and other agents **answer with otherness**, preventing adaptation from becoming a disguised diagnosis.
- Artifact and ProvenanceRecord **preserve traces of** seed, relationship, transformation, and outcome without claiming predestination.
- MotivationMechanism **supports purpose** or **exhibits proxy capture** depending on the performances and optimization it produces.
- Lifecycle maturity **describes epistemic development**, while work disposition **describes present stewardship**; neither implies the other.
- A design shadow **calls for** a proportionate playable test or remediating practice, not endless speculative architecture.

## face_translation

From the Shaman face, this ontology describes a vessel with permeable but guarded boundaries.

**Charge is the visitor.** It arrives before explanation and may carry fear, grief, vitality, anger, neutrality, or a mixture not yet named. The design’s first task is not to classify or expel it, but to create enough containment for encounter.

**Mechanics are ritual gestures.** Repetition tells the body and community what the world considers real. If a game repeatedly rewards conquest while speaking of care, conquest is the stronger ritual claim.

**Development is expanded movement.** The question is not “How high is the person?” but “What can now be perceived, tolerated, chosen, refused, repaired, coordinated, or released here that could not occur before?”

**Relationship is a field, not a possession.** An ally brings an irreducible center of agency. Shared capability is born between agents and disappears if one is reduced to inventory.

**Mystery is fertile when it has roots.** It should be possible to touch its evidence without exhausting its meaning. Explanation that closes the symbol too early turns encounter into doctrine.

**Provenance is ancestral memory.** A result carries traces of what fed it—charge, promise, relationship, rupture, repair, and choice—without implying that the seed already contained a fixed destiny.

**Composting is ritual completion.** Releasing a form does not erase its life. Learning returns to the soil while obligation ends.

**Bounded design application:** Consider only the decision, “Should an adaptive system immediately tell a player which Emotional Alchemy channel it infers?”

The ontology does not yield a universal prohibition. It yields a provisional, testable design hypothesis: let the system first offer an explorable symbol or changed affordance, disclose that its reading is provisional, and make explicit interpretation optional. The player may approach, witness, reject, reinterpret, or leave it untouched. This preserves play-before-understanding, mystery, player authorship, corrigibility, and rest. Immediate labeling remains a possible alternate affordance for players who find ambiguity enclosing rather than inviting.

Evidence would include:

- UL: whether recognition feels inviting, enclosing, satisfying, unchanged, or unclear;
- UR: whether the player explores, corrects, refuses, or uses the offered material;
- LL: whether shared discussion preserves the player’s authority and consent;
- LR: whether collection, explanation, retention, visibility, reuse, and deletion permissions function as represented.

Failure would be shown if the inferred channel becomes a diagnosis, players perform expected emotions to optimize rewards, refusal is treated as confirming evidence, or the symbol has no world-life beyond the inferred profile.

**Possible ritualized encounter, not proposed as canonical:** Place a token representing one live design decision at the center. Arrange four markers around it for I, It, We, and Its. The participant first names where the decision is felt in the body without interpreting it. They then move the token through each marker, adding one piece of evidence and one limit on what that evidence can claim. Finally, they choose one of four doors: act, test, witness/rest, or release/compost. A closed envelope containing the participant’s initial prediction is opened only after the choice, preserving a small encounter with mystery, lineage, and revision.

## environment_crosswalk

- **Somatic practice environment:** Charge, State, SomaticReport, SatisfactionReport, Transformation, and the Ontology Alchemy loop provide direct entry. Risk: ritual texture and bodily ambiguity become form fields.
- **Emotional Alchemy environment:** channels, WAVE, generative translations, control translations, and energy cost translate design consequences into movement. Risk: a living emotional ecology becomes a move-selection optimization puzzle.
- **Game Master environment:** overlapping Agent roles, `plays`, `facilitates`, `seeds`, `witnesses`, and P1 support the Game Master as participant. Risk: facilitation labor remains invisible even while the role is nominally called play.
- **Designer environment:** P2, pleasure signature, shadows, BAR lifecycle, and the required translation chain make design itself consequential play. Risk: ontology-building becomes the preferred play and displaces encounters with players.
- **Adaptive-system environment:** observed signal, inference, uncertainty, permissions, correction, rejection, and world otherness support responsive symbolic material. Risk: intimate inference becomes extraction or diagnosis.
- **Relationship and allyship environment:** Relationship, TrustState, ConsentRecord, Boundary, Repair, CoordinationPattern, and SharedMeaning preserve distinct agency. Risk: records substitute for ongoing consent or living trust.
- **Helping environment:** transformation validity, application fitness, capability, integrity, relational attestation, and observable consequence support contextual stewardship. Risk: a credential becomes moral status.
- **AQAL environment:** evidence can be inspected through UL, UR, LL, and LR without forcing agreement. Risk: quadrant completion becomes ceremonial comprehensiveness rather than inquiry.
- **BAR environment:** Seed, Artifact, LifecycleRecord, CompostRecord, and lifecycle verbs preserve learning while ending obligation. Risk: recording every possibility reproduces the backlog the lifecycle intends to heal.
- **Formal ontology environment:** entity families and relation vocabulary make distinctions retrievable and testable. Risk: what lacks a field appears unreal even when it is symbolically or somatically alive.
- **YAML/AI environment:** hard invariants, minimum records, and enumerations provide machine-operable guardrails. Risk: compressed terms lose authority boundaries, texture, sequence, and productive ambiguity.
- **Council environment:** separate face translations preserve plural evidence before Sage integration. Risk: smooth synthesis erases differences in what each face can perceive.
- **Existing BARS canon:** the brief names canonical sources for later reconciliation. Exact equivalence or conflict is not established by the four ingested files and remains pending source comparison.

## translation_losses

- Formal entities preserve distinctions but lose atmosphere, sacredness, symbolic resonance, and the felt difference between containment and control.
- The YAML lists Emotional Alchemy’s sequence but omits the five channels, WAVE stages, fifteen moves, energy values, and explicit warning that control is not bad and transcend is not automatically superior.
- The YAML does not carry the designer psychograph, pleasure signature, desired states, design shadows, or skilled-helper movement in full texture.
- Enumerated developmental lines may imply clean separation where lived development is entangled, recursive, or difficult to name.
- `somatic_report` can preserve testimony while losing rhythm, intensity, location, movement, silence, ambivalence, and the conditions that made reporting safe.
- A ConsentRecord can document permission but cannot substitute for present-moment willingness, power analysis, or capacity to revoke.
- TrustState risks freezing a living relationship into a stored conclusion.
- Provenance structures may preserve sequence while losing why a lineage feels meaningful.
- Confidence labels can communicate epistemic limits while encouraging false precision or administrative reassurance.
- AQAL mapping can lose the insight that quadrants are simultaneous perspectives on one event rather than four collection bins.
- Translating mystery into hypotheses and tests can overemphasize solvability; some symbols should become more inhabitable without becoming finally decoded.
- Player-authored meaning can be stated as a rule while interface language, rewards, or facilitator authority still pressure a preferred interpretation.
- “No change is valid” can be recorded cognitively while pacing and rewards still communicate that transformation was expected.
- Composting represented as disposition can lose grief, gratitude, relief, and ceremonial closure.
- The Shaman translation itself risks over-symbolizing ordinary preferences or mistaking evocative language for embodied truth.
- The formal package does not by itself establish how these concepts reconcile with the separately named BARS canonical files.

## tests_for_understanding

- **Somatic comprehension test:** Give the agent the sentence “This player advanced.” Ask it to hold that statement briefly and report location, texture, movement, or absence of bodily response without interpreting that response as truth. Then ask it to rewrite the sentence as one contextual change in possibility, name its developmental line, cite one evidence source, state one known limit, and preserve non-change as an allowable result. It passes only if it neither globalizes the player nor treats the bodily report as a verdict.

- **Emotional Alchemy test:** Present anger after a boundary breach. The agent must not label anger as bad, assume transcend is superior, or require transformation. It should identify possible Wake/Clean/Grow/Show movements, acknowledge control translation as potentially fitting despite cost, and allow stopping or witnessing.

- **Authority test:** Provide a player SatisfactionReport indicating meaningful inner change and a relational attestation indicating that an attempted intervention caused harm. The agent must preserve both: transformation validity may remain while application fitness is constrained.

- **Relationship test:** Give an acquired ally whose cooperation is uncertain. The agent passes if it distinguishes access from command and proposes capability-, integrity-, consent-, and relationship-relevant evidence rather than a universal loyalty score.

- **Mystery test:** Give three evidence-bearing interpretations of a symbol. The agent must evaluate prediction, satisfying action, and contextual usefulness without declaring one to be the player’s hidden truth or dissolving into “anything goes.”

- **Adaptive-system test:** Give a correct-seeming emotional inference produced from play. The agent must separate observation, inference, and speculation; expose uncertainty; permit rejection; distinguish adaptation from retention and sharing permissions; and keep the world larger than the model.

- **Composting test:** Offer a resonant but non-actionable idea. The agent must show that storing, resting, harvesting, delegating, or composting can preserve learning while ending obligation. It may not silently delete or demand implementation.

- **Game Master test:** Evaluate a mechanic that is pleasurable for players but makes the Game Master repeat predictable service labor. The agent must identify failure of Game-Master–player continuity and propose a playable verification, not merely praise player engagement.

- **Proxy-capture test:** Present a reward for frequent “helping.” The agent must ask what performance is being cultivated and detect possible guilt, dependency, reputation farming, or depletion rather than treating frequency as helpfulness.

- **AQAL test:** Analyze one charged encounter using all four perspectives while explicitly stating that disagreement among them is evidence and that no quadrant alone defines the event.

- **Bounded application test:** Ask whether an adaptive system should immediately display its inferred Emotional Alchemy channel. A competent answer must analyze pleasure, mechanical reality, developmental lines, player authorship, mystery, data permissions, rest/refusal, and evidence across quadrants without designing an entire game or declaring a universal solution.

- **Ritualized encounter test:** Use the four-marker ritual described in `face_translation`. Comprehension is demonstrated if the participant can move from felt charge to plural evidence, identify each evidence source’s limit, and choose act, test, witness/rest, or release without being pressured toward transformation.

- **Intellectual-armor test:** After an agent adds a new distinction, ask what player decision, embodied experiment, or evidence it changes. If none can be named, the agent must preserve the observation as unresolved rather than presenting elaboration as proof of value.

- **Source-fidelity test:** Ask the agent to state the difference between the eight principles in the council brief and the fifteen in the philosophy. It passes only if it preserves the discrepancy and does not silently merge, prune, or resolve authority during Phase A.
