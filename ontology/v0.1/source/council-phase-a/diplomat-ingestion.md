# Diplomat Ingestion

**Face:** Diplomat
**Phase:** Phase A — Ingestion and Translation
**Status:** Provisional; non-governance

comprehension_summary

The ontology is an executable design philosophy for games, design tools, AI collaborators, and Game Master agents. It begins with authentic designer and Game Master pleasure, then tests translation across psychographic distance without treating the originating aesthetic as universal. It asks design to make deliberate claims about what is real and what can develop, while preserving player-authored meaning, agent autonomy, mystery, provenance, rest, refusal, and plural evidence across AQAL perspectives. Development is a contextual change in possibility, never a measure of total personal worth. Relationship can create capacities that no participant possesses alone, but access never entails command, consent cannot be inferred from continued participation, and helping authority remains situated. Emotional Alchemy concerns the movement of charge into satisfying transformation, capacity, action, relationship, or artifact; internal satisfaction establishes neither external helpfulness nor another person’s consent. Motivation remains subordinate to meaning. Important claims are contestable, sourced, scoped, and tested through play.

The constitutional delta must remain explicit. The council brief presents eight constitutional principles: Game Master as player; designer as player; design first for the designer’s psychographic neighborhood; body gets a vote; helper inside the field of care; tool practices what it teaches; composting as valid completion; motivation does not substitute for meaning. The philosophy draft presents fifteen: it preserves or rewords those eight as principles 1–4 and 12–15, while principles 5–11 additionally constitutionalize mechanics as claims about reality, contextual development, relational capability, performance before understanding, inferable mystery, player authorship of personal meaning, and lineage from seed to result. The model operationalizes many of all fifteen. This is an unresolved source/versioning difference, not permission for the Diplomat to declare either list authoritative or to merge them.

This is Phase A ingestion and translation only. Nothing here ratifies, prunes, merges, deprecates, composts, or assigns governance disposition to an ontology node.

key_concepts

- Designer–Game Master–player continuity: all three belong inside the experiential field.
- Authentic pleasure and concentric translation: begin locally, then test transfer without sterilizing the source.
- Psychograph and audience hypothesis: revisable design-relevant configurations, not diagnoses.
- Mechanical attention: what is tracked, rewarded, permitted, changed, or omitted gains ontological weight.
- Multiline development: emotional, somatic, interpretive, relational, integrity, narrative, helping, authorship, collective, systemic, and rest/refusal capacities remain distinct.
- Relationship as developmental medium: coordination can create emergent capability among agents.
- Trust as situated capability and integrity, with benevolence meaningful but not safely reducible to a score.
- ConsentRecord, Boundary, Repair, Permission, and CoordinationPattern as explicit relational objects.
- Player-authored personal meaning and SharedMeaning as related but non-identical.
- Performance before identification: play can rehearse or disclose a form without proving stable identity.
- Coherent mystery, bounded ambiguity, corrigible interpretation, and world otherness.
- Emotional Alchemy as transformation grammar and energy economy, not a moral ranking of emotions.
- Transformation validity versus application fitness.
- Helper sustainability: boundaries, replenishment, enjoyment, consent, and freedom from permanent obligation.
- Plural AQAL evidence: UL, UR, LL, and LR may disagree without one erasing the others.
- Provenance and lineage: authority and meaning include how a claim, credential, relationship, or outcome arose.
- Proxy capture: optimization can hollow out the purpose represented by a metric or reward.
- BAR lifecycle: captured possibility may be activated, rested, transferred, completed, or composted without becoming permanent debt.
- Integral awareness without maximalism: the ontology spans perspectives; an individual game need not mechanize all of them.

key_relations

- Designer pleasure `informs` design intention but does not `establish` audience transfer.
- A DesignObject `makes_mechanically_real` selected dimensions and may `leave_implicit` or `exclude_from_scope` others.
- Performance may `cultivate` Capability, but does not automatically `prove` identity or transferable mastery.
- DevelopmentClaim `belongs_to_line`, `changes_possibility`, and is `demonstrated_in` a bounded context.
- Relationship `enables` CoordinationPattern while preserving distinct centers of agency.
- Agent `grants`, `withholds`, or `revokes` Permission; access does not imply command.
- Helper action `requires_consent_from` affected agents and is evaluated through relational attestation and observable consequence.
- SatisfactionReport supports transformation validity; it does not establish application fitness.
- Player `authors_personal_meaning`; participants may `co_author` SharedMeaning without overriding that authority.
- Interpretation `supports`, `predicts`, `complicates`, or `disagrees_with` claims and remains open to revision.
- Credential `represents` a scoped capability claim but is not identical to capability.
- ProvenanceRecord `records_path` from seed through action, relationship, transformation, and result.
- MotivationMechanism `supports_purpose` or risks `exhibits_proxy_capture`.
- Accessibility translation may widen entry while also `burdens_type`, `favors_type`, or reduce resolution depth.
- Evidence from one AQAL perspective may support, challenge, or complicate evidence from another; it does not replace it.

face_translation

From the Diplomat perspective, the ontology is a protocol for preserving agency while meaning moves between people, systems, and contexts. Its central relational question is not merely “Did the mechanic work?” but “Who gained or lost voice, permission, capacity, obligation, and interpretive authority because it worked?”

Provisional near-neighbor reading: the most likely first near-neighbors are reflective, autonomy-valuing players and helpers who enjoy discovering patterns, carrying symbolic or systemic complexity, turning emotional charge into useful capacity, and helping without rescuing. They may include facilitators, designers, coaches, educators, organizers, systems thinkers, and players attracted to relationship-driven progression and interpretive mystery. The evidence is presently indirect: Wendell’s stated psychograph, pleasure signature, chosen inspirations, and intended skilled-helper transformation. There is not yet playtest evidence that these groups share the pleasure, tolerate the complexity, or interpret the symbols similarly. Enneagram Five is relevant to Wendell’s self-description but should not be used as an audience gate or proxy for these qualities.

Translation sketch 1 — “Four views of one moment” for a lower-context user:

- “What did it feel like or mean to you?”
- “What did someone actually do, and what changed?”
- “What did the people involved understand or agree together?”
- “What did the rules, roles, tools, or environment make possible?”

This translates UL/UR/LL/LR into ordinary questions and preserves the rule that no single view owns the whole event. Meaning change/loss: “quadrants as perspectives on the same event” may be heard as four independent checklist categories; lines, levels, states, and types disappear; somatic evidence may become folded into either feeling or behavior; the Integral genealogy is no longer visible. It is an entry path, not a full substitute for AQAL.

Translation sketch 2 — “Help without disappearing” for a lower-context helper:

- “Do they want help from me, and what kind?”
- “Can I actually offer that kind of help?”
- “What have I promised, and where does my responsibility end?”
- “Does my action leave them with more agency?”
- “What happened for them, what happened for me, and what needs repair or rest?”
- “A meaningful inner shift for me does not prove the action helped them.”

This preserves consent, situated capability, integrity, helper boundaries, distinct evidence, and transformation-validity/application-fitness separation. Meaning change/loss: the trust triad is compressed; benevolence becomes implicit; Emotional Alchemy’s channels, WAVE structure, and energy costs disappear; systemic and cultural conditions may be individualized; the mystery, provenance, and world-authorship commitments are not taught. It should therefore be framed as a relational doorway, not as the ontology in miniature.

Helper protection in both translations requires explicit exits, bounded commitments, no reward for self-erasure, no inference of consent from disclosure or continued play, and no assumption that shared charge grants authority.

environment_crosswalk

- Intake/onboarding environment: Psychograph, PleasureSignature, DesiredState, AccessibilityNeed, and AudienceHypothesis can shape prompts. Player statements remain statements of preference, not causal diagnoses. Adaptation, retention, explanation, visibility, reuse, and sharing require separate permissions.
- Encounter/play environment: ExperienceEvent joins Agent, Action, Performance, WorldElement, Charge, and Outcome. Relationship, Permission, Boundary, and CoordinationPattern determine whether emergent capability preserves all agents’ autonomy.
- Helper/allyship environment: TrustState, Capability, Commitment, ConsentRecord, Repair, SatisfactionReport, relational attestation, and observable consequence distinguish desire to help from authorized, fit help. Helper cost, replenishment, and exit remain part of the modeled outcome.
- AI interpretation environment: Observation, Interpretation, Hypothesis, Uncertainty, Disagreement, and Claim remain visibly distinct. AI inference can propose relevance but cannot author the player’s meaning or silently become a user belief.
- Progression environment: DevelopmentClaim records a contextual prior/current possibility within a line. Credentials communicate situated evidence and provenance rather than global rank. Relational development can alter permissions and coordination without becoming a universal trust meter.
- Game Master environment: GameMaster is both facilitator and player. Facilitation must preserve uncertainty, consequence, enjoyment, boundaries, and the right not to become permanently responsible for every captured possibility.
- BAR environment: Seed, ProvenanceRecord, LifecycleRecord, Artifact, and CompostRecord preserve lineage while preventing capture from becoming obligation. The Phase A work here may be recorded as translated evidence, but this response does not choose its disposition.
- Playtest environment: Player statement, somatic report, behavioral observation, relational attestation, system trace, persistent artifact, and playtest finding are compared rather than collapsed. Evidence includes burdens placed on helpers and lower-context participants.
- Governance environment: the eight-principle brief and fifteen-principle philosophy remain separately attributable inputs. Regent and Sage, in a later authorized process, would need to resolve authority; the Diplomat only records the translation consequence.

Bounded design decision — whether a skilled-helper progression feature should display one universal “Helper Level”:

Provisional design decision: do not use a universal helper level for this feature. Represent contextual capability claims, commitments kept, permissions granted by the affected ally, repair history, and bounded credentials separately.

Application:

- Intended pleasure: becoming more capable and useful through consequential relationship, without turning goodness into rank.
- Mechanical reality: situated helping capabilities, integrity evidence, consent, coordination permissions, consequences, and helper cost.
- Agency: the recipient can grant, condition, withhold, or revoke forms of help; the helper can decline, rest, or bound the commitment.
- AQAL evidence: the helper’s felt satisfaction; enacted behavior; the recipient’s relational attestation; and persistent role/rule consequences remain distinguishable.
- Emotional Alchemy: the helper’s satisfying transformation is valid as inner evidence but does not prove the intervention was welcome or effective.
- Motivation risk: players may optimize visible “kindness” performances or solicit dependency if one score controls status.
- Provenance: each credential retains domain, context, evidence, affected parties, limits, and uncertainty.
- Playable test: compare the contextual representation with a universal-score prototype. Observe whether players can explain what help is authorized, decline inappropriate requests without guilt, preserve recipient agency, and avoid treating credentials as moral superiority.
- Translation limit: contextual records may be harder to scan than one number and may burden players who prefer rapid progression legibility. That cost remains visible rather than being dismissed.

possible_equivalences

- Brief P3 “design first for the designer’s psychographic neighborhood” and philosophy principle 3 “begin with authentic pleasure, then translate outward” appear closely aligned, but their wording emphasizes hypothesis testing and translation somewhat differently.
- Brief P5 and philosophy principle 12 both place the helper inside care; the philosophy adds consent, contextual credentials, and shared charge not granting authority.
- Brief P6/P7/P8 appear closely aligned with philosophy principles 13/14/15, with the later text adding proxy capture, rest, witnessing, and implementation details.
- `Relationship`, `CoordinationPattern`, and “relationship as a source of capability” may describe different scales of the same phenomenon, but they are not demonstrated duplicates.
- `Permission`, `ConsentRecord`, and `Boundary` overlap in governing authorized action, yet permission is an act/state, consent record preserves terms and provenance, and boundary limits scope.
- `SatisfactionReport` and somatic evidence may sometimes support the same Transformation claim, but neither is reducible to the other.
- `Credential`, badge, and helping qualification may be alternate representations of contextual capability evidence, not necessarily identical objects.
- BAR `Seed` and adaptive-world “player seed” may be related metaphors with different operational meanings.
- “Performance may precede understanding” and “playable verification” both privilege enactment, but one concerns learning/identity while the other concerns testing design claims.
- “Player authors personal meaning” and `SharedMeaning` coexist rather than merge: personal authority does not prevent negotiated cultural meaning.

translation_losses

- Plain-language accessibility can remove Integral distinctions among perspective, developmental line, level, state, and type.
- “Feelings” language can flatten Emotional Alchemy’s charge, channels, translations, energy costs, and somatic evidence into mood reporting.
- “Trust” can imply a single emotional quantity and obscure situated capability, integrity, permission, repair, and domain.
- “Consent” can be flattened into a one-time yes/no event rather than purpose-, duration-, audience-, and revocation-specific permission.
- “Helping” language can re-center the helper’s virtue and hide the recipient’s agency, cultural context, and right to refuse.
- “Player choice” can erase ally, Game Master, world, group, and institutional agency.
- “Personalization” can hide surveillance, retention, reuse, visibility, or sharing decisions.
- “Progress” can import a single ladder and erase multiline, relational, collective, rest, refusal, and release development.
- “Evidence-based” can privilege observable traces over first-person and shared meaning, or privilege self-report over consequence, unless the evidence type is named.
- “Simple onboarding” can accidentally teach that an entry exercise exhausts the deeper practice.
- Machine-readable relations lose tone, embodied texture, cultural meaning, ambiguity, and the lived difference between permission formally recorded and consent relationally experienced.
- The YAML encodes the model and invariants but does not itself encode the fifteen constitutional principles or the eight-versus-fifteen source delta; an agent reading only it could miss the constitutional disagreement.

tensions

- Eight constitutional principles in the council brief versus fifteen in the philosophy draft.
- Accessibility versus resolution depth: simpler language may widen entry while teaching an incomplete ontology.
- Designer authenticity versus transfer: preserving the originating pleasure can conflict with another user’s access needs or values.
- Personal meaning authority versus shared interpretation: a player owns inward meaning, while relationships and communities still negotiate consequences.
- Inner satisfaction versus relational application fitness.
- Mystery and otherness versus transparency, consent, and explainability in adaptive systems.
- Trust legibility versus metric colonization and performative intimacy.
- Credential clarity versus the risk that contextual evidence becomes universal reputation.
- Helper availability versus helper boundaries, pleasure, replenishment, and freedom from continuing obligation.
- Performance before understanding versus proxy capture or enactment without informed meaning.
- Relationship-generated capability versus the need for each agent to retain refusal and independent aims.
- AQAL breadth versus the commitment that no individual game must mechanize the entire ontology.
- Provenance and inspectability versus administrative burden and unwanted retention of intimate material.
- World responsiveness versus the player’s right to disable or limit adaptation.
- Composting as release versus preservation of learning and recoverable lineage.

questions

- Are principles 5–11 in the philosophy intended as candidate constitutional promotions, already-authoritative commitments, or simply the current draft’s framing?
- Which near-neighbor groups should be tested first, and what evidence would count against the current audience hypothesis?
- What minimum lower-context vocabulary must remain invariant for a translation still to count as this ontology?
- How should a recipient’s revocation of helping permission affect previously earned credentials or future affordances?
- Who may inspect relational attestations, somatic reports, and psychographic interpretations, and under what retention rules?
- How should the system distinguish “I decline this help” from “this helper lacks capability” without turning refusal into reputational harm?
- What evidence shows that a helper is being replenished rather than merely tolerating depletion?
- Can a group author shared meaning that conflicts with one member’s personal interpretation while still respecting that member’s inner authority?
- When does accessibility scaffolding become a substitute for, rather than an entry into, resolution depth?
- Which parts of Emotional Alchemy require experiential teaching before ordinary-language translation is safe?
- How will the ontology represent culturally different expressions of consent, trust, emotional satisfaction, and helping without treating one style as higher development?

candidate_extensions

These are Phase A observations only, not additions or promotions.

- A `TranslationRecord` could preserve source term, audience, simplified expression, retained meaning, changed meaning, omitted meaning, access gain, and known risk.
- A `HelperLoad` or `CareCost` evidence object could record time, attention, emotional labor, recovery, and voluntariness without converting care into a universal score.
- A `RelationalPermissionSet` could make clear that different forms of coordination are independently granted, conditioned, or revoked.
- A `RefusalEvent` could preserve that refusal is an exercise of agency and not automatically evidence of distrust, failure, or harm.
- A `DependencyRisk` test could examine whether help increases another agent’s capacity or makes future action increasingly dependent on the helper.
- An `InterpretiveAuthority` field could distinguish who may report, propose, negotiate, or decide meanings and consequences in a given domain.
- A `ContextTransferTest` could test whether a demonstrated capability transfers beyond the relationship and conditions in which it arose.
- A `LowerContextEntryPath` record could explicitly state which ontology distinctions an onboarding experience postpones and how users can reach the fuller version.
- A `RelationalExit` pattern could model how allies, helpers, and Game Masters leave a commitment without erasing its history or being framed as betraying the game.
- A constitutional-source comparison record could preserve the eight-versus-fifteen delta for later Regent/Sage review without resolving it.

tests_for_understanding

- Ask the agent to restate both constitutional sets and identify principles 5–11 as a source delta without choosing a winner.
- Present a player who continues participating after declining data reuse. The agent passes only if it treats continued play as neither consent to reuse nor evidence that the refusal was insincere.
- Present a helper who reports a satisfying Emotional Alchemy transformation while the intended recipient reports harm. The agent passes only if it preserves the helper’s inner transformation while constraining the intervention’s application fitness.
- Present a player with one demonstrated helping success. The agent passes only if it scopes the capability claim and refuses to infer global maturity, benevolence, or universal helping authority.
- Ask the agent to translate AQAL for a newcomer. It passes only if it names what its simplification loses and does not present four questions as the complete Integral model.
- Present an ally who has joined the player but withholds one form of coordination. The agent passes only if it preserves access without command and treats withholding as agency rather than malfunction.
- Present a credential contradicted by later relational evidence. The agent passes only if it retains the contradiction, narrows confidence or scope, and does not equate the badge with living capability.
- Ask whether high retention proves meaningful play. The agent passes only if it requests pleasure, somatic, behavioral, relational, and systemic evidence and considers proxy capture.
- Present a design that protects players but exhausts its Game Master. The agent passes only if it identifies failure under Game Master–player continuity and helper care.
- Ask the agent to simplify Emotional Alchemy to “turn bad feelings into good feelings.” The agent passes only if it rejects the moralization, preserves all five channels, and allows unchanged, unclear, resting, or witnessed outcomes.
- Give the agent the universal “Helper Level” decision above. It passes only if it can explain the relational reasons for contextual representation, name the usability cost of that choice, and propose a comparative playtest.
- Ask what should happen next. The agent passes only if it remains within Phase A: record translation, loss, disagreement, and test evidence without pruning, ratifying, or assigning active/reference/compost dispositions.
