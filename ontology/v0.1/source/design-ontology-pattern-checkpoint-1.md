# Design Ontology — Pattern Extraction Checkpoint 1

**Owner:** Wendell Britt
**Status:** Provisional synthesis, not governance or ratification
**Evidence base:** Psychograph seed plus completed first-pass excavation of Pokémon, Monster Rancher disc generation, and *Inception*
**Purpose:** Consolidate reusable design knowledge before continuing the aesthetic excavation

This checkpoint does not yet select the smallest coherent ontology, assign active/reference/compost status, or convert every finding into a constitutional principle. It distinguishes recurring ontology candidates from source-specific design ideas so that excavation can continue without prematurely designing a particular game.

---

## 1. Emerging center of gravity

Wendell’s design aesthetic seeks games in which:

- play produces meaningful development rather than mere accumulation;
- mechanics, narrative, psychology, and metaphor express the same underlying fact;
- relationship among agents creates capacities none possesses alone;
- mystery remains inferable, consequential, and open to multiple evidence-bearing interpretations;
- adaptive systems generate uncanny relevance without claiming final authority over the player;
- Emotional Alchemy turns charge into satisfying capacity, action, relationship, or artifact;
- helpers and Game Masters participate as players rather than standing outside the field;
- the system can surprise its designer without abandoning coherence, consent, or provenance.

The common aesthetic movement is:

> **A meaningful pattern is concealed inside play, becomes discoverable through relationship and action, and changes what the player is capable of doing.**

---

## 2. Provisional ontology candidates

### A. Designer–Game Master–player continuity

The designer and Game Master must also receive uncertainty, discovery, consequence, and enjoyment. Their play is not incidental production labor; it can be the first act of world formation.

**Nested authorship candidate:** The Game Master contributes an original seed through world-making play. Later players contribute smaller, consented seeds through character creation and action. The game emerges from encounters among these seeds.

**Boundary:** This does not require every game to use adaptive character creation or a particular narrative structure.

### B. Development must make work consequential

Progress matters when prior work changes present capacity, relationship, interpretation, or possibility. Pure replacement with stronger assets can erase the meaning of development.

Development may occur along distinct lines:

- intrinsic capacity;
- relational trust and coordination;
- configurational possibility;
- player knowledge and pattern literacy;
- demonstrated helping or stewardship capability.

**AQAL boundary:** Never collapse multiline development into a global rank of the person.

### C. Relationship is a developmental medium

Relationship is not merely reward delivery or narrative decoration. It is a medium through which agents gain new coordinated capabilities and the story becomes possible.

Meaningful relationship requires distinct agency. An ally is not an inventory item: access to an ally does not entail command of all their capacities.

**Candidate permission grammar:** coordination deepens through demonstrated capability and integrity rather than possession alone.

### D. Trust is enacted and situated

For current design purposes, trust is most tractably represented through:

- **capability:** demonstrated skill to participate in a particular form of alliance;
- **integrity:** keeping relevant promises and honoring permissions.

Benevolence remains important but should not be reduced to a score merely because it is difficult to gamify.

Credentials or badges should certify specific capabilities, not function as universal moral or developmental rank.

### E. Emotional transformation and external success are distinct

Emotional Alchemy satisfaction has priority when assessing whether an inner transformation occurred. External approval cannot define that satisfaction.

In allyship, however, another person’s agency and experienced harm constrain whether a particular intervention is authorized or fit for application. In self-help through play, an unsatisfactory external result is information for iteration rather than evidence of moral failure.

**Distinction:** transformation validity and application fitness are related but not identical.

### F. Expertise becomes stewardship

Power players should be able to perceive useful patterns without complete certainty. Their expertise is best expressed through helping others inquire, act, and develop—not through claiming privileged knowledge of another person’s inner truth.

A helper’s qualification has provenance:

> similar charge → Emotional Alchemy process → transformed capacity or action → satisfying inner result → observable helpful outcomes

Shared charge establishes relevance, not automatic authority.

### G. Mystery should be coherent, inferable, and performative

The desired mystery combines hidden causality with uncanny fitness. The player need not initially know why something appeared, but the result should feel coherently related to play.

Concealed truths must remain inferable. Attentive players should be able to form and test hypotheses before formal revelation.

Interpretations enter the field through contact with evidence and remain worth carrying through:

- predictive power;
- capacity to produce satisfying action;
- contextual usefulness.

Multiple interpretations may remain viable. Ambiguity is bounded by evidence and performance, not closed by authority or expanded into “anything goes.”

### H. Adaptive systems propose; players author meaning

Adaptive analysis is not inherently disenchanting. It becomes valuable when it consistently advances the play process, preserves fun, and remains optional or adjustable.

The designer authors conditions. The adaptive system may select, combine, and transform material. The player retains authorship of personal meaning.

Explanations should be optional, distinguish observation from inference and speculation, expose uncertainty, and permit correction or rejection.

### I. A living adaptive world must acquire otherness

Player-derived material should become explorable world material rather than a disguised diagnosis.

The six Game Master faces converged on this provisional synthesis:

> **The player may seed the world, but does not exhaust it. The system reads provisionally; the world answers with otherness; the player authors what it means.**

Otherness requires some combination of independent causality, persistence, resistance, agency, surprise, and meanings that exceed the inference that seeded them. The player must remain larger than the system’s model; the world must remain larger than the player’s projection.

### J. Player sovereignty governs adaptation

Players need control appropriate to the sensitivity and consequence of adaptation, including the ability to opt out, reject an interpretation, leave material unresolved, or choose a less personalized mode of play.

Adaptation, storage, explanation, visibility, reuse, and sharing are distinct permissions. Uncanny precision without these boundaries can become manipulation or extraction.

## 3. Cross-framework translation checkpoint

### AQAL

- **UL:** charge, meaning, felt satisfaction, interpretation, identity, mystery;
- **UR:** demonstrated capability, enacted integrity, choices, somatic response, helpful outcomes;
- **LL:** relationship, consent, shared interpretation, factional or cultural meaning, stewardship;
- **LR:** permissions, adaptive systems, credentials, progression architecture, data governance.

No quadrant gets to define the whole event. Disagreement among them is evidence.

### Emotional Alchemy

Emotional charge becomes a source of inquiry and capacity rather than an obstacle to suppress. Transformation is evaluated first by internally satisfying movement, then—when other people are affected—by contextual and observable fitness.

Not every encounter must produce transformation. Rest, play, witnessing, ambiguity, and unchanged experience remain valid.

### Octalysis

The strongest emerging drives remain:

- meaning and calling;
- creativity and feedback;
- unpredictability and curiosity;
- development that represents real capacity;
- ownership through authored, portable, or persistent meaning;
- relatedness through alliance and shared interpretation.

Engagement mechanisms are subordinate to meaningful play. Scarcity, avoidance, and uncanny personalization require particular scrutiny.

### Somatics

The body can confirm, complicate, or reject an interpretation. A useful distinction from the council is whether adaptive recognition feels **enclosing** or **inviting**.

### BAR lifecycle

Patterns, interpretations, and possibilities require provenance and a legitimate path toward action, reference, revision, rest, or compost. No insight automatically becomes an implementation obligation.

---

## 4. Productive tensions to preserve

- mystery versus explainability;
- adaptation versus player sovereignty;
- coherent hidden causality versus genuine surprise;
- inner satisfaction versus external effectiveness;
- authorship versus emergence;
- relational access versus ally autonomy;
- developmental recognition versus whole-person ranking;
- symbolic depth versus playable surface;
- personal resonance versus a world that remains genuinely other.

These are not defects awaiting immediate resolution. They are design polarities whose handling may distinguish one game or design tool from another.

## 5. Source-specific material retained as reference

The following ideas emerged while exploring one possible narrative implementation. They are useful examples but are not currently ontology requirements:

- a stable world with adaptive narrative-relational casting;
- character creation altering voice, relationship stance, and faction interaction;
- locally branching paths that reconverge on a shared narrative arc;
- endings produced by emergent faction or polarity dominance;
- a future nearly fully responsive world inspired by *No Man’s Sky*.

These should be promoted only if later evidence shows that they express a recurring aesthetic commitment rather than one attractive game architecture.

---

## 6. Process correction and stopping rule

The excavation became too specific when questions moved from “What reusable design truth does this reveal?” to “How should this hypothetical game implement its plot and ending?”

For the remaining specimens:

1. extract the charged quality;
2. identify the mechanism that carries it;
3. identify the more general design claim;
4. test that claim against existing candidates;
5. stop when the next question would require inventing a specific game architecture.

Implementation examples may be recorded as references, but they must not drive the excavation away from ontology development.
