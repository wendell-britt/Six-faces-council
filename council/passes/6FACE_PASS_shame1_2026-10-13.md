## What the research found

The research file holds the sources. In short:

- Nineteen shame offers were found. The large ones are for licensed clinicians: Brené Brown's Daring Way requires a
  licence, PESI sells the Certified Shame-Informed Treatment Specialist credential as clinical training (£344.99), and
  the Lyon and Rubin Healing Shame Practitioner path runs through clinical workshops ($475).
- Coach-level offers are narrow and priced at $79 to $150 a session or $99 to $249 a month: men's recovery from
  pornography, money shame for women, a women's "unshaming" community, sex-addiction recovery.
- The field's verbs are heal, release, reduce and build resilience. Only Lyon and Rubin ("transform it into an Ally")
  come close to treating shame as something that becomes useful. No one found sells shame as fuel or uses the word
  metabolize.
- Play is almost absent: one card deck for counsellors and a free youth board game.
- Shame in allyship is a thin niche, and the two offers found are dormant or unverified (Ackerman's 2022 course for
  white people, the Compassionate Anti-Racism Project).
- Existential Kink: Carolyn Elliott died on 26 July 2026 (Jake Kobrin's remembrance, dated 30 July 2026, confirmed by
  fetch). The Immortal College still sells her programs and says nothing about who leads it now. PENETRATE, the
  six-month coach certification, lists no price. Certified coaches publish no prices. Elliott calls shame "the magic
  killer". The steady criticism is victim-blaming and blindness to race, class and structure, and the careful coaches
  fence EK off from abuse and oppression.

## Shaman

**Reserves becomes Burning.** The reading says he has been storing this and never named the day. The store is real: the
MTGOA book already says the allies who finished "allied with the guilt, the shame, the avoidance, and alchemized them",
the appendix already credits Existential Kink for the daemons' spirit, and the Fear to Joy notes already hold his own
3-2-1 on the Player as the author of self-sabotage. Pragya's sentence did not add a new idea. It named the one the work
was already carrying.

**The felt read is relief, and the risk is a new costume.** Burning warns against a true sentence repeated until it
consumes its subject. If "shame" becomes a brand he wears, it goes stale the way a slogan does. If it stays the word he
listens for in a session, it keeps working.

**The Shaman recommends his own verb.** The field heals, releases and resists shame. He metabolizes it, and the house
rule in bars-engine already says emotional energy is fuel, not judgment. That verb is his and nobody in the sample uses
it. The Shaman's standing test holds: "metabolize" says something about what a person can learn to do, and says nothing
is wrong with them.

## Architect

**Following becomes Stalemate.** The pattern broke open with the walk and the daemons series; the work now is to follow
what they opened, and Stalemate warns of seeking one game while wearing the face of another. A shame page bolted beside
the forest walk would be the second face.

**The mechanism already exists.** The walk's six questions end at the six self-sabotaging beliefs, and every one of them
(not capable, not good enough, not worthy, not ready, don't belong, insignificant) is a shame sentence. The Architect
reads the walk as a shame map that has not said its name. No new product is needed for the positioning; the walk, the
Meet Your Daemons series and the session already carry it.

**The third position is between clinic and slogan.** The field splits into licensed treatment and narrow recovery coaching.
Wendell holds neither credential and needs neither for what he does. His third position is coaching that works with
shame as energy through named practices (the 3-2-1, WAVE, the daemons, EK's turn), with a written scope line and a
referral for trauma treatment. That keeps him out of the clinical lane without apologising for being outside it.

**The Architect notes a later shape.** No shame credential for coaches who are not clinicians was found. A training for
other coaches is a possible later product. It waits until his own clients show what the method does, and it is deferred
here with a return date, not raised as a question.

## Diplomat

**Estrangement becomes Spending.** The reading says a conclusion was reached from a distance, and that this has a price
not yet named. The distance is that the council has read the field from websites and has not asked one client whether
the word lands. The price of "shame" is that some people hear accusation in it.

**The bridge is the life, then the shame.** People come for the life they want, which is the walk's first question.
Shame comes in as what stands in the way, which is the walk's last question. The Diplomat would keep that order in every
piece of copy: the promise first, the shame named as what stands between.

**On allyship shame the Diplomat sides against the Challenger.** The "white shame" label draws hostile press (the
research found one such piece), and Portland's community is already wary. Allyship shame works as a second door
for people who arrive through the book and the podcast, not as the headline for strangers.

**Existential Kink is kin, and the kin are grieving.** EK's coaches lost their teacher in July. Any public mention of
EK credits it as a lineage with care and does not position against it.
