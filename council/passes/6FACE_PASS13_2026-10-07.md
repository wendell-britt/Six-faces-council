## Shaman

**The cast is Arrived, unchanging.** The pull model just landed, and the tradition reads a finished figure as the moment it
starts moving. The plumbing is done enough; what moves next is the council's relationship to its own mistakes.

**The felt sense is that Wendell is the council's alarm.** Every process change on record started with his
irritation: "These questions shouldn't be showing up here" (2026-10-03), "These tests are a little weenie"
(2026-10-03), "If you need rulings from me this should've gone to the council?" (2026-10-04), and this morning's ask
for root cause, not patches. The council learns when he is annoyed enough to say so. That costs him the most and
reaches the council last.

**The risk is a retrospective that reads as penance.** The standing test applies: does it say something is wrong
with them, or something about what I have learned? A look back at 32 overrules can turn into a list of faults. The
Shaman's own lesson holds here too: 15 overrules on 2026-10-06 were most of them the WAVE battle finding its feel,
and an overrule in design exploration is Wendell playing, not the council failing.

**Recommendation:** the council looks at its own record on a rhythm of its own, framed as what was learned, so the
signal reaches the council before it reaches Wendell's patience.

## Architect

**The cast is Depths, becoming Mutuality.** Under the pickup failure there was another failure, and under that one the same
shape: a loop with no step that closes it. The movement arrives when the council stops adding and reads what it has.

**The loop exists in parts and nothing joins them.** An overrule is recorded by `pull.py`. A session writes a
`lessons_pending` entry when it remembers to. `collect_lessons.py` lists them when someone runs it. Folding one into
`faces.yaml` waits on a reviewed edit. Each link works; nobody owns the chain, so nine lessons have waited three
days. The same shape broke pickup: each hop worked alone and nothing guaranteed the whole.

**The record cannot answer the first question a retrospective asks.** Which face's positions get overruled most?
Resolved entries carry the decision, the date and the record file, never the face or the pass, and the position
row leaves the open list when it resolves. Stats show 26 records with no cause and 83 with no convener. The
instrument is half blind, and the cheapest fix is clerical: `pull.py` copies `faces`, `pass` and the cause onto each
resolved entry as it records it.

**The third position is a measured pass.** The binary on offer is another rule in CLAUDE.md or another routine. The
third position is a pass whose subject is the ledger: the six faces read the council's own overrules, steers,
deferrals, incidents and unfolded lessons, and each face asks of the council what it asks of a feature.

**Recommendation:** a retro pass, fed by one report (`council/retro.py`) that prints the counts above, run on the
daily session's Grow up day. The report comes after the first pass by hand, built from what that pass needed.

## Challenger

**The cast is Banter, becoming Anticipation.** The exchange is the whole of it: Wendell's overrule and the council's answer to it
are the process. The good part has started (the data exists) and the event (anyone reading it) has not.

**What breaks: a retro that writes more prose.** The hostile review's sixth assumption was that a failure gets fixed
by writing a rule, and pickup.md became an incident log that every session pays to reread. A retro pass is a machine
for producing rules. If it adds paragraphs to CLAUDE.md, it makes the problem it exists to fix. The standing
position `pickup-mechanism-before-rule` already says a mechanism comes before a sentence; the retro must obey it
first.

**Anti-pattern: grading the council by its overrule rate.** A falling overrule rate can mean the council learned, or
that it stopped proposing anything Wendell might flip. Positions that only restate his last ruling would score
perfectly and be worthless.

**Falsification test, with the blocker it resolves.** The blocker is the nine unfolded lessons and the overrules no
one has traced to a face. Run the first retro by hand on the 32 overrules. If they show no repeating pattern (each
one a different face, cause and reason), the retro pass is not worth a rhythm, and the council folds lessons and
stops there. If two or more overrules share a face and a reason, the pattern is the first lesson the retro earns.

**Dissent:** the Architect's script should not exist before the hand pass shows what to count. The Challenger holds
this against building `retro.py` in the same breath.

## Regent

**The cast is Gathering, unchanging.** Many rules gathered, and the question of why the council came. It came to turn intent
into work with fewer questions to Wendell. Every rule is measured against that.

**Obligations that bind the retro.** `faces.yaml` changes only by Wendell's ruling, so a lesson the retro proposes
reaches him as a lesson position, as `lesson-challenger-ladder` and `lesson-diplomat-levels` did. The reserved list
holds. The daily run's limits say "never retire or delete anything", and the principle of transcend and include says
superseded work is "retired with its reason, never deleted". Those two disagree about whether the
council may prune its own working rules, and the record does not settle which wins. That is a question for Wendell.

**Definition of done for a process change:** a mechanism, a test that would show it failed, the incident written in
an incident log rather than in CLAUDE.md, and a count of rule lines before and after.

**The cadence needs no new routine.** The hostile review found three pickup routines left switched on. The retro does
not get a routine of its own. It runs on the daily session's Grow up day, which already exists for what he called "increase out
throughout or quality capacity- operating from higher levels" (`council/daily.yaml`).

**Every pass grades the tests the last one set,** including passes about the council. Passes 10 to 12 skipped it.

## Diplomat

**The cast is Unbroken, becoming Unsaid.** Every line sends and the return has nowhere to land. Wendell sends steers and
overrules; the council sends positions and questions. What never comes back to him is what his ruling changed.

**The return should land where he already looks.** When a steer becomes a lesson, the board shows it as a lesson
position with his words beside the lesson, and the retro's report shows which of his rulings changed which face. He
should be able to see the council learning from him without reading a ledger.

**The other projects need the lessons too.** Lessons from the WAVE battle, friendcraft and bars-engine sit in other repos' records.
`collect_lessons.py` reaches them; the retro includes them, so a lesson learned in one project reaches every face in
every project.

**Recommendation:** fold the nine pending lessons now, each as a lesson position on the board that stands unless he
flips it, quoting the sentence he was answering.

## Sage

**The cast is Footing, becoming Windfall.** The ground matters and the step is what keeps it safe; done in the right order, more
comes back than goes in.

**Contributions kept.** From the Shaman, the framing and the reason: the council should hear its own signal before it
reaches Wendell's patience. From the Architect, the diagnosis (a loop with no step that closes it), the clerical fix
to the record, and the measured pass. From the Challenger, the order (by hand first, script after) and the test that
could retire the whole idea. From the Regent, the cadence on an existing day, the definition of done, and the
question. From the Diplomat, the return to Wendell and the reach across repos.

**Dropped:** nothing.

**Dissent:** the Architect wanted the report built with the first pass; the Challenger held that a script written
before the hand pass would count whatever is easy to count. The Sage orders them: the hand pass first, the script from what it
needed. Both stand as recorded.

**The answer to "how do we use the process to improve the process":** the council plays its own game on itself.
Its record is the player's history: overrules are the hits it took, steers are the hints, deferrals are what the
player saved for later, incidents are the bugs. A retro pass turns the faces on that record the way they turn on a
feature, and its outputs go where every other output goes: lessons to the board as positions, mechanisms to pull
requests, the one question to Wendell.

**The next step:** run the first retro pass by hand, in its own thread, on the 32 overrules, the 9 pending lessons and
the 6 pickup incidents.
