Wendell called this pass on 2026-10-10 in the Sprout Game project chat ("Let’s do the studio and sprout at the same
time"), and the project coordinator started the thread. The question: define the Marketer role and run Sprout's private hook test,
per his board answer `st-marketing-start` = a hook test now, kept private. This is the second pass on the studio.

**Casts** (`cast.py --json`, recorded in the ledger): Shaman 30 Burning, line 6, becomes 55 Fullness. Architect 50 The
Cauldron, line 3, becomes 64 Almost. Challenger 23 Erosion, lines 5 and 6, becomes 8 Alliance. Regent 41 Spending,

## Scorecard

Pass one's three tests fall due 2026-10-17 and none can be graded yet. This pass adds no structure beyond the role card,
so it changes none of them.

## Anchor

Sprout Plot is a cozy farming RPG in a Taoist cultivation world, built so that play teaches the five-element ontology
of Emotional Alchemy (sprout `docs/design.md`, Overview). The Marketer's job is to say that to a stranger in one line.

## Shaman

**Burning becomes Fullness.** The same true sentence repeated burns up its subject. The design says "giving back
pays" in many ways; a hook has to say it once.

**The felt read sits in the split.** A stranger feels the game in the split of the mock: warm farm on the left, dark forest on the right,
the traveler on the seam. The line has to name what the split costs. Line C does: "Your village thrives by draining the
forest."

## Architect

**The Cauldron becomes Almost.** Raw food goes in and a meal comes out, and care is wanted where the crossing
looks finished. The role card is the vessel; the hook is the first dish cooked in it.

**The role card is written.** `council/roles/marketer.md`: purpose, domain, accountabilities, limits and cost, with no meetings.
It owns the hook, the capsule mock, devlog and store drafts, and one folder per game. It acts at gates 1, 3 and 5,
the socialized gates the first pass named.

## Challenger

**Erosion becomes Alliance.** The ground under a test goes when nobody says what it can't show. So, said plainly:
the cold readers were agents, not strangers.

**The test shows less than it seems.** All three readers scored 4 of 5, so the interest score separated nothing.
The twist account did. Lines A and B failed it: A's reader saw only that "the cost of the farm falls on nature", and B's
reader "would not know whether it is a penalty, a moral choice, or a story hook". C's reader gave back the whole twist
unprompted. The kill line was written before the readers ran (`/mnt/project-files/studio/hook-test/hook-test.md`).

## Regent

**Spending becomes Fog.** The price has to be named. The mock uses prototype art whose source is unconfirmed
(sprout `docs/license-audit.md`), so it can never be released as it stands, and the role card says so for every mock.

**The reserved list holds.** Nothing went out. The mock and line sit in the project folder and on the board. Real strangers are
consent and release, so the next step past agent readers is his.

## Diplomat

**Drained becomes Retreat.** The explaining goes on and the basin is dry: cut words a stranger can't use. "Push
hands" and "Taoist cultivation" meant nothing to the readers in a hook. They belong on the store page, with a picture.

**The readers asked for two changes.** All three found the wound's glow unexplained; none saw the settlement wall, the premise's
first fact. The next mock puts the gate between farm and forest and a spirit rising from the wound.

## Sage

**Dispersal becomes Unbroken.** What was held rigid comes apart, and force has to learn timing. The Marketer is now
a role with a card, and Sprout has a hook that passed its kill line: *Your village thrives by draining the forest. Farm,
spar and befriend its spirits to pay the debt back.* The Shaman gave the split as the felt read, the Architect the card,
the Challenger the limits of agent readers, the Regent the art-source rule, the Diplomat the cuts and the next mock.
Nothing was dropped.
