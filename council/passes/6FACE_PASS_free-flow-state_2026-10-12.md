## The short answer

The record says the two are different, and the daemon work needs them kept apart.

Free-flowing qi and aliveness is a body report. Your ruling makes it the leftover class: four sensations are blocked, and "everything else is free-flowing qi and aliveness". It is one class for all five channels and it answers whether charge is moving or held at a place in the body. A gold state is one of five named feelings (Triumph, Bliss, Wonder-Excitement, Poignance, Peace), each at the top of a channel's ladder, and the book names them both as charge ladders and as satisfactions a reader wants (`book-golds-satisfactions`, your answer: both). The ontology's own vocabulary already holds the two apart: a `SomaticReport` is first-person bodily evidence, a `SatisfactionReport` is first-person evidence about an Emotional Alchemy result, and a `DesiredStateReport` is a reported desired state that is not evidence the state was reached.

The relation runs one way at most. A gold felt with none of the four blocks present would sit inside "everything else", and free flow is wider than the golds: plain moving anger is free-flowing and is not Triumph. Whether a gold ever sits beside a blocked sensation is not on the record and only you or the play can say. The build does not depend on it, because the game keeps two reports and derives neither from the other.

What the daemon work leans on is the exit. A daemon encounter ends when the player says the daemon is satisfied (`sb-satisfied-is-theirs`), and the body answer at the return is data beside that, never a condition of it. If free flow and a gold were the same report, the game would have two exits and could not tell a daemon's satisfaction from the player arriving at a feeling. In Sprout it would also mint coin from a report, because Sprout's gold is minted by grounding (`ya-road-gold`) and `ya-gets-us` calls every gold state a brick on the road.

When a player reports aliveness, nothing is blocked, so the game sends them to no daemon and no block work, takes the word as given, checks and scores nothing, and may offer one line to name what is there, which the player can leave.

On the smaller question: there is no pair of different channels that are neither ke nor sheng neighbours. On your map the five channels make ten pairs, five sheng and five ke. The only pair outside both is one channel picked twice.

## Positions (each stands unless you flip it)

1. `ff-two-reports` (Architect, Sage). The body report and the gold report are two separate reports the game keeps apart. The body report is one of five answers about a place in the body: tension, constriction, strength, numbness, or free-flowing qi and aliveness. A gold state is a named feeling at the top of a channel's ladder and a satisfaction the player may want. The game never derives one from the other. Why: your ruling that "everything else is free-flowing qi and aliveness" makes free flow one leftover class for every channel, while the golds are five; your answer of "both" on `book-golds-satisfactions`; and the ontology's separate types `SomaticReport`, `SatisfactionReport` and `DesiredStateReport`.

2. `ff-aliveness-is-theirs` (Regent, Challenger). A player's report of aliveness is taken as given at every stage. The game does not check it against strength or numbness, unlocks nothing on it and does not score it. It is saved as data when the record is on, as any other answer is. Why: your rulings of 12 October. Of strength: "strength feels good". Of numbness: "its one of the last charges that people learn to metabolize. It's the chargeless charge." The early game does not ask a player to find either, so an early "alive" can hold either and the game cannot tell. Also `bmw-number-stays` and `sb-satisfied-is-theirs`, where the game does not score.

3. `ff-no-daemon-on-aliveness` (Architect, Diplomat). A player who reports aliveness at the scan is not sent to a daemon or to block work, because nothing is holding the charge. The clean up move is not pressed on a charge that is not blocked. Happy Apples stays open to every channel as the move that opens it up, the player may go on up the W.A.V.E. ladder, and stopping at any rung is complete. If a move later blocks, the daemon path opens as `sb-enter-at-daemon` has it. This refines the blocked-move pass, which named the clean up move as the second half of the free move; the second half is offered where something is blocked. Why: your overrule of `oag-open-up-tools`, "The EA move for each of the channels is a clean up move as clean up moves are for dissolving and Alchemizing blocks", and "The general moves for happy apples can apply to all the channels as an opening up move"; `oag-wave-ladder` and `wave-order`, where any rung may be the last.

4. `ff-name-what-is-here` (Diplomat, Shaman). After an aliveness report the game may offer one line, "what is here", and never requires it. The player answers in their own words. The five gold states show as cards only if the player says they do not know, and up to three may be picked. The game does not choose a gold for the player from the body report, the channel or the charge number. The line's wording is the council's placeholder and is yours to set. Why: `sb-words-then-cards` and `bmw-player-names`, where the game never guesses; your steer on `cc-feeling`, "We should be able to chose up to 3 options here", which was about what you want to feel and which the council carries over as its own reading; `book-golds-satisfactions`; your Shaman lesson of 9 September, an offer is not a requirement.

5. `ff-gold-minted-by-grounding` (Regent, Architect). Where a game counts gold, gold comes from grounding a charge or from a real step the player marks done, and from nothing else. Neither a report of aliveness nor naming a gold state mints it. The felt gold state and the counted gold stay two uses of one word. Why: `ya-road-gold` (grounding any charge, or a real step, yields gold), `ya-mode3-gold`, and `ya-gets-us`, which links the felt state and the coin on purpose, so the join sits at grounding and never at the report.

6. `ff-every-pair-has-a-relation` (Architect, Challenger). Any two different channels are a sheng pair or a ke pair, so no pair of channels falls outside both. On your map the five channels make ten pairs, five sheng and five ke. A player who picks any two channels gets the question `sb-compound-prevails` already asks, which holds the other or which leads, in the player's own words, and the game runs that channel's free move first. A player who names the direction the cycle does not run is taken at their word. The only pair outside both is one channel picked twice, which is one channel. Why: counted by script on the standard cycles, with every pair appearing once (the table above); `sb-compound-prevails`; and `bmw-player-names`, where the game does not guess for the player.

Support and what would show each false. Positions 1 and 2 rest on your rulings and your answers, quoted above, plus the ontology's types, which are the council's earlier work. Position 3 rests on your quoted words and one refinement the council makes, which the Regent holds. Position 4 rests on your answers plus a pattern carried over from `cc-feeling`, marked as the council's. Position 5 rests on `ya-road-gold` and `ya-gets-us`. Position 6 rests on arithmetic checked by script; the cycle orders are the standard ones and are not in this repo, so a different order in your canon would change the table, and the pass has not read the Guide's ke notes. Position 1 would be false if play shows a player who reports a gold and free flow as one answer every time.
