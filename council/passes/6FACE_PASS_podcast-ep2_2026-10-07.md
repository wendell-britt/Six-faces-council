thumbnail is made the same way every time, so the next episode starts from a move and not from a blank page.

## The faces

### Shaman, casting Endurance, becoming Erosion

Endurance says this exchange has happened before, and it has: the
Flirtcraft podcast went through covers, a title question and an upload list one day ago. Erosion warns that the
ground goes when nobody names it. The Shaman reads the episode's ground as one claim, that help does not come from
altitude, and every draft has to carry it. A thumbnail that sells the episode with a hook from Orange, such as a
shocked face or a number, would contradict what Tom and Wendell spend an hour saying.

### Challenger, casting Muster, becoming Bearing

Muster says the fact that would organise this has not been said. The
Challenger names it: nobody knows yet what a thumbnail for this show does on YouTube, because the show has one
episode up and no numbers. So the move does not claim a formula. It renders each draft again at phone search size,
and a hook that cannot be read there is cut before it reaches him. The Challenger's falsifier for the move is the
second episode: if its session needs to ask him anything this move's file could have answered, the move is
incomplete.

### Regent, casting Feeding, becoming Adornment

Adornment is the thumbnail itself: dressing that does real work. The
Regent holds the reserved list. The title and thumbnail publish under his name, so each is a question. The
description names Tom, which is a person named in public, so it stands on the board only as a position he can
flip, and it leaves out Tom's partner's name and his parents' medical details. Uploading is his alone, so it goes
on Your steps.

### Architect, casting Retreat

Retreat says the timing decides what is kept. The Architect builds the least that repeats:
one render script that reads a spec file, three layouts taken from the live `/mastering-allyship` page, and a
contact sheet. Nothing is built for YouTube's API, analytics or scheduling until a second episode shows the need.
The script sits in the home repo so every project's podcast uses the same one.

### Diplomat, casting Depths, becoming Alliance

Depths says the bottom has another bottom. The Diplomat reads the episode's
second bottom as the elder care half, which is what most people will search for, and puts it in the recommended
title. Alliance is Tom: the copy says where to find him the way he asked, in person in New Paltz, and the notes
suggest he sees it before it goes up.

### Sage, casting Unbroken, becoming Fellowship

Unbroken warns of a line that sends with nowhere to land. The Sage's
synthesis is that the title and thumbnail are drafted as a pair, so each carries what the other does not: the title
carries the search, and the thumbnail carries the hook, "How long do you want to feel this?" Fellowship is two
people walking the same way without saying so, which is this episode, and the reason the guest's name is on every
thumbnail.

## Dissent check

The pass was unanimous, which is a warning sign. The nearest dissent is the Challenger's: the recommended thumbnail
is a text-only design, and the faces of the two people talking may do more on YouTube than any hook. The council
cannot test that without a still from the video, so the thumbnail question offers it as an option.
