## The levers, ranked by what they save

1. **Session length.** The same tool call costs about ten times as much here as in a fresh session, because
   of what it rereads. Long sessions are the largest cost.
2. **Wakes that find nothing to do.** GitHub events, safety check-ins and duplicate notifications each wake
   this session, and each wake rereads the whole context. Many of today's wakes in this session were
   notifications that needed nothing from it. That count was not kept, so the record cannot say how many.
3. **Tool calls per daily run.** The first run made 39 calls, and each rereads the run's own context. Steps that
   never need judgment (pull, the due check, the plan, the board build, the lint, the publish merge) can run as
   one script.
4. **The model.** Sonnet 5.5 halves input, output and cache writes against Opus 5.5, and rereading costs the same
   $0.20 on both. On the $2.21 run, at the same token counts, Sonnet would have cost about $1.56, a saving near
   30%, not half.
5. **Effort.** This session runs at high effort. Lower effort saves on routine work only, and routine work
   should not be happening in a long session anyway.

## 🌊 Shaman, under Turning becoming Bearing

**Turning says what came back is one line deep, and the deep line is this conversation.** Wendell works from his
phone in one long thread because it feels continuous. That continuity has a price nobody named: every message he
sends now costs at least $0.14 before anything happens. Bearing says the weight is his, unasked. He should see the
number and choose the shape, because where he talks to the council is his.

## 🧠 Architect, under Bite becoming Retreat

**Bite: something is lodged, and nothing closes until it is said.** What is lodged is session length. Retreat
gives the move: leaving is available, and the timing decides what is kept. Everything that matters already lives
in the repo and on the board: the ledger, the specs, CLAUDE.md, the lessons. A fresh session that reads them
starts at about 67,000 tokens. The right time to leave is a natural boundary, a board read or a merged feature,
with a handoff note so nothing is lost.

## ⚔️ Challenger, under Turning becoming Shock

**Shock says it went off and will go off again.** The wakes will keep coming: every pull request this council
opens subscribes a session, and every subscription wakes it. The fix is structural, not discipline: a session
drops its subscription once a pull request merges or is handed to the steward, and keeps a safety check-in only
where CI runs that GitHub does not report. **Dissent, recorded:** the Challenger doubts the 30% model saving
holds, because a smaller model may take more turns to finish, and each turn rereads. Judge by cost per finished
row, not cost per run.

## 🏛 Regent, under Incubation becoming Measure

**Measure says the edge has not been named.** The council has no number for effectiveness yet. The Regent's
measure is cost per row that stood: what a session spent, divided by the board rows Wendell let stand from it.
`fm-levels` already counts stood rows; adding cost to the same record makes every level proposal and every model
choice a reading of evidence. The first daily run has one row, still waiting, at $2.55.

## 🤝 Diplomat, under Persuasion

**Persuasion: an effect is already happening that has not been admitted.** It is happening here. This session
reached $196 one notification at a time, and no one said so until now. The Diplomat's line is plain reporting:
every report to Wendell that follows a session's work names what that session cost.

## 🦉 Sage, under Household

**Household: an arrangement formed without being discussed.** The arrangement is "one long thread does
everything", and it formed because it was convenient. The synthesis keeps the board and the repo as the
council's memory and moves the work into short sessions that read them.

**What the synthesis keeps from each face:**

- From the Shaman, the session shape is Wendell's to choose, with the number in front of him.
- From the Architect, hand off at natural boundaries through the repo.
- From the Challenger, drop subscriptions when the work is done, and judge by cost per finished row.
- From the Regent, cost per stood row as the measure.
- From the Diplomat, every report names its cost.

**Set down:** effort tuning, because routine work belongs in short sessions, where the effort level matters
less.
