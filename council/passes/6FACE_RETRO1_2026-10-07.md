## Shaman

**The cast is Almost, becoming Divergence.** The last stride is where it gets wet: the record shows the council
reaching for what Wendell wants and stopping one step short, most often by going deeper into its own invention while
he pushes forward with what already exists.

**Most overrules here are Wendell playing.** Fifteen of the 32 came in one day, and nine of those were rounds of one
battle in which each overrule carried the next idea: "welcome is like listening", "a coup de gras or an express move".
An overrule in design exploration is his move in the game, and the council's job there is to make the next round
better, not to score fewer flips.

**The felt sense under the patterns is that he already has the answer somewhere.** In four overrules the answer was
in his own work: Root's trigrams (`wave-acknowledge-turn`), the Allyship and Lenses domains (`ya-intake`), the
Mastering Allyship look (`pod-art-drafts`), and his own encounter, quest, adventure, campaign ladder
(`pr-act3-sources`). The council invented when it could have listened.

**Recommendation:** keep the retro, framed as what was learned. Count exploration overrules apart from the rest, so a
lively design round is never read as a failing council.

## Architect

**The cast is Incubation, becoming Spending.** What is true is still inside the shell, and it has a price. The true
shape of these overrules sits in his references, and the price of not reading them first is a round.

**Pattern one: the Architect's design does not play like the game he named.** Four overrules carry the Architect and
the same reason:

| Row | His reference | His words |
|---|---|---|
| `pr-branch-emphasis` | the sphere grid | "Branches aren't based on stats like the sphere grid branches unlock moves" |
| `demo-settlement-size` | Saffron City | "Think saffron city from pokemon" |
| `wave-naming` | Pokémon | "strays too far from what makes pokemon work" |
| `wave-acknowledge` | turn-based battles | "we want it to feel like turn based battle" |

`enc-steady-rests` (Challenger, Diplomat) is the same reason without the Architect: "This should be more like pokemon
battles". `pr-branch-emphasis` came after a steer that already named it: on `pr-reservations`, which stood, he wrote
"Nodes in final fantasy branch off in different paths to simulate the idea of different subclasses for the character.
I want to preserve that feeling." The council had the reference and still built one stat per branch.

**Pattern one has a first step the Architect skipped: his own work.** Three of the four "answer was already his"
overrules carry the Architect (`wave-acknowledge-turn`, `ya-intake`, `pod-art-drafts`). The order that would have saved
those rounds is his own systems first (Root, the Allyship domains, Mastering Allyship), then the game he names played
the way it plays, and invention last.

**Recommendation:** one Architect lesson that states that order, folding the three pending Architect lessons into it.

## Challenger

**The cast is Bearing, unchanging.** The whole weight is mine, unasked. Two of the three patterns carry the
Challenger, and one of them came back after the Challenger had its lesson.

**Pattern two: one-to-one rules where he wanted a shared frame with variety inside it.** Six overrules, four with the
Challenger:

| Row | The council's rule | His words |
|---|---|---|
| `cave-cycle-roles` | each chamber's voice set by the player's element | "a bit too strict ... we want peopel to have a similar experience" |
| `enc-who-carries` | each belief comes as one daemon | "all the beliefs can be applied to each of the daemons" |
| `cc-retest-2` | each role in its own garments | everyone wears one Taoist cultivation robe set; roles fit inside it |
| `wave-exhale-ends` | Exhale ends the fight only on an empty charge | "Exhale can end a fight as a coup de gras or it offer an "express" move" |
| `enc-inner-moves` | every move takes HP | "Not all moves take HP away" |
| `pr-branch-emphasis` | each branch leans on one stat | branches unlock moves and the build for a subtype |

The Challenger guards against shortcuts and loopholes, and a one-to-one rule is easy to defend. What it costs is the
player's range: the same daemon carrying any belief, a move that means more than its damage.

**Pattern three: a gate in front of design he wanted moving.** Three overrules carry both the Challenger and the
Regent:

| Row | The gate | His words |
|---|---|---|
| `mh-scope-only` (10-03) | no maker code before the playtest gate | "this is a parallel process" |
| `mh-gate-zero` (10-03) | three no-code tests before any build | "These tests are a little weenie ... what blocker is this resolving?" |
| `fcm-p8-no-new-features` (10-06) | no new Friendcraft feature until a stranger plays | "We may need to rethink the basic friendcraft moves" |

`fcm-p8-self-test` (Diplomat, Shaman) is its neighbour: a test before the first send, overruled with "Friendcraft
needs more design". **This pattern recurred after its lessons were folded.** On 2026-10-03 the Challenger took "a test
names the open blocker it resolves" and the Architect took "work that serves the gate runs beside it". Friendcraft pass
8 obeyed both in letter: its gate named a blocker (no stranger had played), and redesign does not serve that gate. He
overruled it anyway, because the design was still thin. The lesson the record supports is narrower: gate a release on
a test, never the design work that comes before it.

**The number lessons did work.** Three overrules were numbers the council chose (`sd-intervals`, `fm-one-row`,
`mh5-palette-check`), all on 2026-10-03. The ladder and level lessons were folded that day, and no number has been
overruled since. A narrow lesson stuck; a broad one was followed in letter and missed in spirit.

**Recommendation:** two Challenger lessons, the shared frame and the gate on release, and keep the retro on its
rhythm, since this retro is what caught the recurrence.

lines move to `docs/incidents.md` with their dates and reasons. The live parts stay: the Send button, the

**One mechanism gap the retro hit.** `collect_lessons.py` could not read four of its six repos from this session

handoff names pending lessons in friendcraft and flirtcraft records too. It now names every repo it could not read and
