## The short answer

The record already says what the player meets. A part is using their charge for a job of its own, the game tells the player what that job is and who it works for, and the player works it through the 3-2-1 until they say the part is satisfied. Then the game returns them to the exact spot. This pass adds eight positions that join the new sensation-first flow to that path. No question reached you.

The three candidates (refuse, bargain, take the joystick) are each true of some daemons and none is true of all seven. The Protector keeps you out of range. The Controller says not yet and has no arms of its own. The Skeptic doubts the evidence because it doubts everything. The Fixer is the Controller's arms: it turns the feeling into a problem and holds you until it is handled. The Victim turns what it cost into standing. The Damaged Self says it was probably me and adds the hit to its case that something in you is broken. The Emotional Body hands the feeling off before it has arrived. So the screen does not pick one of the three. The player says what they met in their own words, and then the game tells them whose job it is.

## Positions (each stands unless you flip it)

1. `sb-enter-at-daemon` (Architect, Regent). When a free move blocks, the block work opens at the daemon. The sensation, the place, the channel and any words the player gave are carried in and not asked again. Why: oag-block-carry does exactly this, and oag-gate already says a block does not ask the element's job again.
2. `sb-game-tells-job` (Diplomat). At the daemon the player says in their own words what is in the way, and then the game tells them the daemon's job and who it works for. The player is never asked to supply either. Why: your overrule of oag-daemon-step on 7 October: "We should be telling them what the daemon's job is. They don't provide that info. Who it works for is also something that's supplied to them".
3. `sb-words-then-cards` (Challenger, Diplomat). The player picks the daemon the way they pick the channel: their own words first, and the seven cards only when they say they do not know, and more than one may be picked. Neither the four sensations nor the five marks reorder the cards. Why: your ruling today that the channel is picked "by vibe at first", and bmw-player-names, where the game never guesses for the player.
4. `sb-one-at-a-time` (Architect, Regent). With more than one daemon picked, one is worked at a time and the others wait at the door. The depth cap counts blocks inside blocks, and at the cap the game names it once, offers outside support and pauses. Why: the wave room already holds up to three creatures with a fourth waiting at the door, and your overrule of oag-wave-depth: "cap the depth so players don’t get lost in the minutiae". The cap number is the council's three and the notice wording is yours.
5. `sb-no-pass-at-daemon` (Regent, Shaman). In the body-map flow a named daemon is not walked past. The player may say it is not a daemon and carry on as an ordinary block. Once it is named, the encounter is the 3-2-1 in the shared chamber, Step back pauses and never skips, and nothing calls the player back to a paused encounter. Why: your words on cave-daemon-wall: "We shouldn't have a skip. If a daemon pops up then it needs to be worked with. We can pivot to a 321 discussion with the part until is feels satisfied".
6. `sb-satisfied-is-theirs` (Regent, Challenger). The encounter ends when the player says the part is satisfied, as the player sets the charge number, and the game does not score it. Back at the spot, the player is asked again where it is and whether it is tension, constriction, strength, numbness or free-flowing qi and aliveness. The free move is offered again and never required. A sensation that stays is saved as data. Why: bmw-number-stays, oag-release, and bmw-whose-charge, where the question reads the player's own charge beside the part's.
7. `sb-compound-prevails` (Architect, Sage). When the player picks two channels, the game asks which holds the other, or which leads, in the player's words, and runs that channel's free move first. The other waits. Why: your directional compound rulings, which replace the earlier chat suggestion of starting with the stronger feeling.
8. `sb-none-found-is-data` (Challenger). A free move that blocks with no part found is saved as blocked with none named. A player may also pick the nearest daemon, and the game works with that reading however loose it is. The "I'm not sure" card opens Face it on whatever is in the way, in the player's words. Why: your ruling that moves fail only when a part takes the joystick is the frame, and this is its test.
