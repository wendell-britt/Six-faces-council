## 🌊 Shaman, on felt sense

**A checklist can become a second inbox.** What Wendell asked for is relief from reaching, and a list of
chores would be the same reaching in a new place. So the list holds only what nobody else can do: his
accounts, his money, his eyes on the result. Everything a session can do, it does.

**Every step says where it is safe to stop.** A list run from a phone gets interrupted. Knowing that
stopping does no harm is what lets a person start.

## 🧠 Architect, on structure

**The pass now has three outputs, and each has its own surface.** Questions go to the board, where he
decides. Steps go to a checklist, where he acts. Product goes to a pull request. The board's rule that
questions and product stay apart extends to a third type.

**Spec kit is adopted, not invented** (E1). Specs live in `.specify/specs/<feature>/` in every repo, as
bars-engine already keeps them. The home repo carries a lean template in `council/spec-kit/`, synced like
the skill. A repo with its own template keeps it and adds three council sections: the ask in his words,
whose decision each one is, and how we will know it failed.

**Eight stages, each with owning faces and a gate,** live in `council/pipeline.yaml`: intake, spec,
falsify, plan, build, verify, ship, close. Every task in `tasks.md` is tagged `[claude]` or `[wendell]`
(E2).

**The checklist keeps its rows in a database, not in its page.** A session adds a list with one write and
never republishes the page. His ticks and notes come back the same way.

**The flirtcraft migration belongs in the production build now.** Migrations were manual because of the
shared-server risk, and his answer tonight removed that risk. The session's permission check refused the
change (E7), so it goes to him as a question.

## ⚔️ Challenger, on what gets wrong

**Spec kit's interview collides with the reach test** (E3). Five clarifying questions per feature is the
reaching he built the council to stop. The pipeline keeps the interview and answers it from the record,
and only what survives reaches the board.

**A spec kit can become ceremony.** bars-engine has 421 of them. The pipeline runs forward only: no
existing spec is retrofitted, and a fix that changes no behaviour skips the spec.

**The steps in tonight's first list were written without seeing Vercel's screens.** Steps two and three
name menus from documentation, not from sight. The note box on every step exists for that, and a note is
a defect report.

**Falsification tests.** The pipeline fails if the next feature reaches a build before its steps are on
the list, or if he has to ask in chat what to do. The checklist fails if he cannot finish tonight's list
from his phone up to step seven, which says it needs a computer.

## 🏛 Regent, on what to preserve

**A secret never sits in a step.** The page makes the sign-in secret on his device and stores nothing.

**The reserved list governs shipping.** A feature that touches money, a live paywall, prose or a person's
name ships only on a board question he answers. Every other feature ships as a position he can flip, by
the precedent of E6.

**A repo's own rules win.** bars-engine keeps its Verification Quests and its Build/Infra Blocker
Protocol. Friendcraft's manuscript never runs the pipeline.

## 🎭 Diplomat, on what bridges

**Each step reads as an instruction a person can follow without context:** a verb first, then where, what
to enter, and what he will see when it worked. The page says plainly that Claude reads the ticks, so he
never has to report back.

**The Diplomat dissents from the Architect** on where the steps live. One place to look is kinder, so the
Diplomat would make the steps a tab on the board.

## 📖 Sage, panoramic

**What each face gave.** The Shaman limited the list to what only he can do and added the safe stopping
point. The Architect separated the three outputs, adopted bars-engine's spec kit, and wrote the stages.
The Challenger found the interview's collision with the reach test and set the tests. The Regent kept
secrets off the page and tied shipping to the reserved list. The Diplomat set how a step reads.

**The dissent is the Diplomat's.** It would put the steps on the board as a tab. The Architect keeps them on their own
page, because a step is done inside another app, such as Vercel, while the board is read to decide. The
position stands as the Architect's, and he can flip it.
