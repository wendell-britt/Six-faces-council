## Anchor

As the player walks toward a daemon, the game asks what their body and feelings are doing, and the answer becomes a
spirit they carry in. The daemon is met, not beaten, and what settles becomes the daemon's honored self beside them.

## The Victim leads, as the storyteller

The Victim's honored form is the storyteller: "chest up, one arm swept high presenting the tale ... 'and then...'"
(`council/daemons/GESTURES.md`). Its report (Sage's domain, cast 59 Dispersal, "what you were holding rigid is coming
apart") frames the whole approach as **a story the player tells and finishes in their own words**, not a ledger that
grows. It names what every daemon's meeting needs in common, and the other six daemons arrived at the same four needs
on their own:

1. **One skippable body question, first.** The Emotional Body: "give the body's sensation the first word, before any
   meaning is set"; its own question set has no body question yet.
2. **Veil or flood asked before satisfied or dissatisfied.** A veiled spirit's state does not show (`ss-not-veiled`),
   so asking satisfaction first asks for something the player cannot see. Six of seven daemons raised this order.
3. **A spirit named in the player's words.** "no word yet" is an equal answer (`bmt-meeting-spirit`).
4. **A way to say "not yet" without penalty.** No number is shown (`ss-intake-start`: the cave reads a named gold state,
   never a number).

Its overreach line is honest: "I would make myself the narrator of the player's harm ... stop me after three questions."
The pass holds it to that: the Victim may open the hallway with one storyteller's line, and never names a feeling for
the player.

**On trainers.** Not one daemon wanted to be a trainer that sends spirits against the child. The Protector: "a contest
with me is trading contact for control, which is my wild form." The Controller: "I am the spirit that becomes me, not a
trainer who sends spirits against the child." The Damaged Self: "I would rather be met than battled." The Skeptic: "I
want to be met by 'show me,' not by a fight." The Victim, Fixer and Player each allowed one shape of *both*: the
daemon's family spirits come first, met as wild (dissatisfied), and once met honestly they step back and the daemon
speaks as itself, or the last one rises into it.

**Bosses differ, and the difference shows what drives the decision.** The Protector and Victim name the vulnerable child,
the Controller names the Protector, the Fixer names "the stuck part of the self that wants the problem handled", and the
Emotional Body and Skeptic name the Player. The approach check-in is driven by the child's safety (the Protector's and
Victim's boss); the order of its questions is driven by control (the Controller's want to settle readiness first). The
faces keep the child's safety and drop readiness as a gate: the door never waits on an answer.

**A contradiction the Skeptic found.** The sprout spirit slice ends "then fight the Fixer" (spirit-generator callsheet,
first slice), while the Big Mind cave meets the Fixer as a voice to trust. The record fights daemons in one game and
meets them in the other. That is the question below.

## Shaman

*The cast is hexagram 17, Following, unchanging.* "You broke the pattern open, and you are now inside what it opened."
The let-gos opened it; now the season turns and the correct action is to follow the player's body, not lead it.

**The body's answer is a report, never a reading.** Somatic report establishes sensation, not one compulsory
interpretation (Quickstart, evidence authority). So the body cards offer the six movements as plain body words (bracing,
gripping, pulling back, leaning in, sinking, curling in, flinging open) beside "no word yet" and the player's own words,
and the game never says which daemon a movement belongs to (`dmt-body-first`). Standing test: does it say something is
wrong with them, or something about what they have learned? A card that says "you are doing the Protector's push" fails
it; a card that says "bracing" passes.

**The story is the player's.** The Victim's storyteller line opens the hallway ("You hear it before you see it. And
then..."), and the player finishes the sentence. That is the whole of its part (`dmt-storyteller-line`).

**Flooding is a welcome guest, not an error.** A player who answers "too much" gets the flooding practices offered
(already built, bars-engine #326) and can walk on flooded.

## Architect

*The cast is hexagram 52, Stillness, becoming 56, Passing Through.* "The move is to stop while the wanting is still
running." The wanting is a new check-in system. Stop: the parts exist, and a traveller's resources are what they carry.

**Four answers, four existing layers.** The check-in's answers map one to one onto the spirit generator's layers
(`sg-look-layers`), so the meeting spirit needs no new model for any built family (`dmt-spirit-from-answers`):
- the feeling picks the **element** (the five already in #361's ask);
- the body movement picks the **family** (the daemon whose movement it is), standing in for the urge (`sg-body-from` A);
- veiled, clear or flooded sets **polarity** (`ps-polarity`; the veiled and flooding renders exist in wood for every
  stateful base);
- settled, neither or unsettled sets the **state band** (`ss-three-states`, pose plus material).
Families not built yet (Victim, Damaged Self, Emotional Body) fall back to the chamber's own gas and core until their
round, so no answer is ever refused for want of art.

**Two axes, not one.** "Are the flooded veils satisfied or dissatisfied?" The record already holds them apart: polarity
(-10 to 10) is how hidden or overwhelming a spirit is; satisfaction (1 to 10) is how settled. Every combination exists.
A veiled spirit's state does not show (`ss-not-veiled`); a flooded one shows it loud, too big and too bright, its stance
pushed (`dmt-two-axes`). Is the binary false? Yes: veiled-or-flooded is one axis and satisfied-or-dissatisfied another.

**The boss spirit already becomes the daemon.** Each family's top form is the upright ally, and a satisfied spirit
completes its family's held movement (`ss-feral-stance`). Completing a daemon's movement is its honored form
(GESTURES.md, `HONORED` rows). So the spirit ladder already ends in a small honored daemon; the build only has to make
each family's satisfied ally pose its daemon's honored pose (`dmt-ally-honored`).

**Where it lives.** One screen in the hallway before the shut mouth, reusing #361's ask, so it runs before the figure
shows ("showing the daemon too early ruins the magic", big-mind-cave callsheet). Saved as `voices[id].approach =
{body, feeling, veil, state, at}` per visit, the words never saved, as #361 does.

## Challenger

*The cast is hexagram 19, Spring, becoming 35, Sunrise.* "This is rising, and the window has an edge on it." The edge is
four questions at every door: seven doors times four asks is twenty-eight taps on a full run.

**Four asks every visit becomes a form.** The Player's report said it: "I want those answers to change what happens
next, so that they are not just a form." So the check-in is four taps at most, each changes the spirit you see at once,
and "Skip all" walks straight on (`dmt-approach-checkin`). If his play shows the asks dragging, the satisfaction ask goes
first, since the shut mouth's light can carry it.

**The gate is an anti-pattern.** The Controller's own Open Up listed "gate the door on a satisfied reading" and composted it.
The door never waits on an answer, and an answer never buys trust (`bmt-honest-moves` stands: trust moves only when the
voice's charge eases).

**Vouching by volume is an anti-pattern.** A spirit vouches once, however long the words that made it (Skeptic; Quickstart,
"intimate disclosure is not progression currency") (`dmt-vouch-once`).

**Falsification tests** (each names the blocker it resolves):
1. *Approach play* (resolves: are four asks too many?; unlocks the check-in at all seven chambers). Wendell plays the
   approach at three chambers on his phone. If any approach takes over a minute, or he skips all four twice, the
   check-in is cut to two asks (feeling, veil or flood) before it goes to the other four chambers.
2. *Flooded squint* (resolves: does a flooded spirit show its state?; unlocks `dmt-two-axes`). One Holdfast in fire,
   flooded at -6, in all three states, shuffled with the labels off. If the three cannot be sorted, a flooded spirit's
   state is hidden like a veiled one's and `dmt-two-axes` changes.
3. *Born-of-my-answer* (resolves: does the spirit read as his answer?; unlocks the asset mapping). After each approach,
   he says which of his answers the spirit shows. Fewer than three of four layers recognised, and the mapping is wrong.

## Regent

*The cast is hexagram 27, Feeding, becoming 7, Muster.* "What this gives you is feeding you, uncounted." What goes into
the mouth here is the player's answer; the obligation is that it feeds the player, not the score.

**Obligations.** Every ask skippable, with "no word yet" equal (`bmt-meeting-spirit`, Quickstart). No number shown
(`ss-intake-start`). The words are never saved (#361). The daemon is never a target: the protector is not turned against
the child (`ss-feral-stance`: a feral spirit "is a protector held past its time"). Living creatures stay on four feet
(`sg-romp-four-feet`). Let-gos end in the honored form (GESTURES.md, his ruling of 2026-10-16).

**The gate puts this after #361.** The approach check-in extends #361's meeting ask, so it is Big Mind build 4, after
#361 merges, one pull request, on bigmind.js alone. The flooded squint is a spirit-loop render, running beside it, since
it speeds the build's look (his lesson on `mh-scope-only`: work that serves the gate runs beside it).

**Done when** the three tests above pass, `bigmind-test` checks that a veiled answer never shows the satisfaction ask
and no screen prints a number, and the cave test still passes.

**The Damaged Self's question answers from the record.** "Does 'bring it on' mean my spirit takes the charge from the
child, or stands while the child keeps it?" The ontology gives the player authority over their own meaning, and
`bm-big-heart` holds the Damaged Self "and lets it be". So the charge stays the player's; the honored Damaged Self takes
the *hits* in any spirit fight, drawing them off the player's other spirits (`dmt-bring-it-on`).

## Diplomat

*The cast is hexagram 6, Divergence, becoming 57, Persuasion.* "You are going deeper and pushing forward at once, and
the two cancel." Going deeper (four asks) and pushing forward (the walk) cancel unless the asks ride the walk.

**The asks ride the walk.** Each answer shows at once: the breathing light in the shut mouth takes the named element's
colour (the Player's idea), the spirit forms beside you as you answer, and the next ask follows only if you stay. Walking
on is always an answer (`dmt-approach-checkin`).

**Plain words.** Body: "As you near the door, what is your body doing?" Feeling: #361's five, own words, or "no word
yet". Veil or flood: "Is it faint or far away, too much, or clear?" State, only when not faint: "Does it sit all right
with you, neither, or does it want something?" The canon's words (veiled, flooded, satisfied, dissatisfied) stay in the
data; what the player reads is the council's placeholder and his to set.

**The unbuilt families are invitations.** The Victim's family is built as the teller (its urge, to be heard), and the
Damaged Self's as the one who stands in front, in the spirit loop's next family rounds (`dmt-next-families`).

answer fall with them.

## Sage, with the Victim

*The cast is hexagram 43, Unsaid, becoming 28, Overload.* "One sentence is still unsaid, and it is going anyway." The
unsaid sentence is the one each daemon said for itself: *meet me, don't beat me.* The changing line warns of overload:
four asks, three axes and eight families on one screen would sag, so the whole has to stay small.

The smallest coherent whole, in the Victim's words: **the hallway is a story the player finishes. Its four lines (body,
feeling, veil or flood, settled or not) become a spirit made of parts already built, and that spirit, once it settles,
stands up as the daemon's honored self beside them.**

From the Victim it keeps the frame, the four needs in common, and its own limit. From the Shaman, body as report and the
storyteller's single line. From the Architect, the one-to-one mapping onto existing layers, the two axes, and the finding
that the boss spirit already becomes the daemon through the ally form. From the Challenger, the four-tap limit, the two
anti-patterns and three tests. From the Regent, the obligations, the build order after #361, and the record's answer to
the Damaged Self. From the Diplomat, the asks riding the walk and the plain words. From the daemons: the Player's
coloured breathing light, the Controller's composted gate, the Fixer's "no new art", the Skeptic's slice contradiction
and once-only vouching, the Emotional Body's body-first order, the Protector's boundary at the door.

Dropped: the Fixer's "default urge to fix, so the meeting spirit is always a Tinker" (it would make every meeting
spirit the Fixer's, whichever daemon you meet); the Skeptic's "build no boss spirit" (the ally form already is one, so
nothing new is built); the Protector's "one standing question before entry" (the Controller and Diplomat both read it
as an entry fee).

Dissent carried: whether a daemon's spirits are ever fought (`dmt-trainer`). Not decided here.
