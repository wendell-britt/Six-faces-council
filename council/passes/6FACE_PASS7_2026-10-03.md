## 🌊 Shaman, under The Well

**The Well says the water is there and the reach is broken, and that is root-game exactly.** Everything the
first step needs exists (E2), packed where no review can see it (E1). The Player, sent on what Wendell would
enjoy watching, chose root-game: it is his own game, and the work ends in a side-by-side clip he can feel.

## 🧠 Architect, under Measure becoming Depths

**Measure asks where the edge is, so the test is one step and no more:** make the ruler whole, exactly as
root-game pass one wrote it. Depths warns of a bottom under the bottom, which is the archive (E1). The first
move is to unpack the handoff into the repo as files, which root-game's own CLAUDE.md already assumes.

## ⚔️ Challenger, under Measure becoming The Well

**A test of the council needs a verdict nobody can argue with.** The Skeptic, sent on that question,
chose root-game for the same reason: three gates, each a command that passes or fails (the self-test, the
shipped baseline reproduced, and single-gate contacts reported against HANDOFF.md). The pass mark is set
now, before any work: all three gates pass, or the difference is explained in numbers.

## 🏛 Regent, under The Well becoming Turning

**Turning says what came back is one line deep, and the first step is meant to be that small.** Root-game's
working agreements bind the work: find causes by tracing, keep every win in HANDOFF.md, ship to the preview
page first. Nothing here touches the reserved list, and nothing needs a step from Wendell.

## 🎭 Diplomat, under Emergence becoming Understatement

**The Diplomat dissents.** Emergence warns against skipping a stage, and the stage nobody has run yet is
Wendell's own: the Your steps tab has one list and no ticks. The flirtcraft deploy alert would exercise every
part, including a step only he can take (which phone channel he reads). A test that never touches his part
leaves the most important surface untested.

## 📖 Sage, under Stalemate becoming Sunrise

**Stalemate warns against seeking one game while wearing the face of the other.** Two games are in play: the
council's test and root-game's hands. They keep separate scorecards. The council is graded on its process
(casts, daemons, pipeline, steps); the game is graded on its own pass marks.

**What each face gave.** The Shaman found the reach problem and carried the Player's pick. The Architect
held the edge at one step and made unpacking the first move. The Challenger and the Skeptic set a pass mark
that is a command. The Regent bound the work to root-game's agreements. The Diplomat named the untested
surface.

**The dissent is the Diplomat's.** It would test on the flirtcraft alert instead. The Sage carries it as a sequence:
root-game first for a verdict nobody can argue with, then the flirtcraft alert as the second test, which
exercises the steps list.
