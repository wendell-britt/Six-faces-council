## Shaman (63 Arrived > 9 Restraint)

**Arrived reads as the hollow being done enough that it now wants to move.** Ari's five asks are the place becoming somebody's. The changing lines turn to Restraint, a small rule holding a large force, and the small rule here is the other's yes.

**The felt sense under the notes is ownership and rest.** Ari asks to design "our room", to move what is in it, to look like themself, and for the spirits to have a place of their own. Each is the same wish: this is ours, and I can rest in it. The tired eyes come first in that wish, because a place you cannot rest your eyes in is not yours yet.

**Spells name the felt shift that already happens.** When a yes flies a light into the Between, Ari wants that to feel like casting. The Shaman backs calling the shared acts spells in the hollow's own words, as long as a spell never asks for a stronger feeling (crafting pass, dissent seed).

The Shaman supports the round, comfort and home first.

## Architect (16 Anticipation > 33 Retreat)

**Anticipation is stored energy before release, and the becoming hexagram says keep something by leaving early with it.** The Architect reads it as: take the smallest part of the notes now and leave the rest stored on the callsheet, not built.

**Captions are the cheapest real change.** Every sheet and line of text in the Between gets one reading mode: text appears line by line at a pace the player sets, with a tap to show it all at once and a still mode that shows everything. The pace is stored per player, visible and reversible (QUICKSTART, pacing adaptation). This touches copy and the sheet component, not the scene.

**Moving your own items is a placement rule, not an editor.** Items already carry a maker. A drag on your own item moves it, saved to the store; a shared item moves only as a proposal the other says yes to. No free-build mode until the place has more pieces to arrange.

**Natural spirit movement is a path change.** The walkers follow paths from their seed and the shared clock. Easing, pauses, sniffing at items and turning toward a person who arrives make them read as alive, and both screens still agree because the clock drives it.

**A formation is a recipe card first.** A formation is two moves cast together into one effect. Build nothing until Ari reads a card (`cc-no-joint-before-ari`); a card costs an afternoon and tests the idea.

**Procedural worlds are the ship's, not the Between's.** The hollow is one small place; render distance does not apply. Generating places only as far as the player travels belongs to the ship's world, after Chapter 1 and the engine question (council #302).

The Architect supports one round (captions, own-item moving, spirit paths) and stores the rest.

## Challenger (10 Footing > 4 Fog)

**Footing says conduct is the whole content: where you put your weight, in what order.** The becoming hexagram, Fog, warns that we are guessing where we could ask. The Challenger names three guesses.

**Guess one: that Ari's play counts as Wendell's look.** The gate exists because building outran looking. Ari looking is real evidence, but the rule names his look. Only he can say whether Ari's play opens the gate.

**Guess two: what "dance" meant.** The record holds no dance. Building partner dance on Root from a word we cannot place would be the self-inflicted error. Ask Ari through the formation card.

**Guess three: that "look just like them" means a photo.** A photo likeness is personal data with its own permissions (QUICKSTART, privacy and consent), and a face-from-photo tool is a large build. "Look and feel" may mean choices that feel like them: hair, skin, build, clothes, how they move. Default to choices; never take a photo.

**Anti-patterns:** qi as a meter that rises with practice (a report of aliveness buying power, against `ff-gold-minted-by-grounding`); spells that need both people present to work at all (locks out the one resting); an editor for the hollow before there is furniture to arrange (an empty toolbox).

**Falsification tests:** T1, Ari reads a formation card and either wants to cast it or says it misses (by 2026-10-28). T2, Ari and Wendell each read one sheet in caption mode on a phone and say whether it was easier on the eyes (after the round ships).
