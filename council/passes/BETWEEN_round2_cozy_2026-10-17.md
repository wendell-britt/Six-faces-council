## What it lacks

Intrinsic pleasure, restated for round 2: being met somewhere you want to stay. Leads: the two people together, Ari's
expectations named first, since she will judge it.

1. **No place to settle.** The dome is a temple-sized hall built for one person breathing, with nothing soft and
   nothing at human scale. A cozy space is small, enclosed on most sides, with one view out: a nook, cushions or a
   blanket on the floor, a hearth or a lamp you sit near, something to lean on.
2. **The light is flat.** One warm colour everywhere, no pools of light and shadow, no dusk outside the door, nothing
   flickering. Cozy light is low, warm, uneven, with a cool world beyond it.
3. **It is silent and still.** No sound at all (fire crackle, rain, water, a creature's purr), no motes, no fireflies,
   no leaves moving. Nothing tells you the place is alive when neither of you does anything.
4. **The Between has no personality.** It breathes and changes colour. It does not notice you arrive, follow you, nuzzle,
   sleep, play or show delight when fed. A creature people love has a face (or the hint of one) and responds to them.
5. **The two of you cannot be close.** You can walk, and that is all. No sitting side by side, no waving, no holding
   hands, no leaning, no lying back to look up together. Being near each other changes nothing around you.
6. **The acts are forms.** Tending is a menu and a text box. The verbs that make gardens fun are missing: carrying a
   light in your hands, setting it down, watering a plant, feeding the creature from your palm, watching a seed open.
7. **Nothing to play together.** No small shared game for when you are both there: passing a light back and
   forth, lighting lanterns together, building something, calling the spirits to sit with you.
8. **Nothing is yours.** You cannot place a cushion, hang a lantern, choose a colour or keep a keepsake. The garden looks
   the same on day one and day thirty, so it never becomes a home the two of you made.
9. **Little trace of the other when they are away.** "While you were away" is a list. Footprints, a still-warm cup, a
   note left on your cushion, a lantern they lit for you would carry the same news as a feeling.
10. **The words are clinical.** "Worked-through charge", "the store did not answer", "propose", "retire" read as an app.
    The copy needs the garden's own voice.

Games that already do each of these well, as reference for the build and the prompt: Animal Crossing (nesting,
keepsakes), Spiritfarer (a creature you feed and hug, hand-made verbs), Sky: Children of the Light (sitting together,
holding hands, light passed between strangers), A Short Hike (small, warm, alive), Kind Words (notes left for someone),
Unpacking (a home told through objects).
