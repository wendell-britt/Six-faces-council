## Shaman (63 Arrived > 3 Sprouting, line 3)

**The cast points at the order of work.** The ember-and-lantern pass is finished, and finishing it is what sets the ship moving. Sprouting warns against reading three borrowed games and an unbuilt world as a verdict, either on the design or on a player whose ship is still small.

**The Player is the one in the cockpit.** In `council/daemons/daemons.yaml` (id `player`, the Vulnerable Child) the Player says "I want this and I do not want to be careful about it." Gummi blocks suit that voice because they are toys. The ship should feel like a den the child built, with the seven daemons around it. They are not inside it as machinery. The cabin works as a home only if it echoes the home the record already has. That home is the WAVE room, with its rug, its seat and "Take it home" from anywhere (bars-engine `content/ontology-game/cave/LEVELS.md`; `council/callsheets/cave-levels.md`). I recommend the WAVE room as the ship's cabin, with no second home built beside it.

**Enchantment comes from the ship knowing you.** Research shows the KH1 garage drove a reviewer away after one try, and warp-once flight became skippable filler (research §1, failure modes). The patterns that read as relationship are NMS's grown Living Ship and the Starcruiser's guest greeted by name (§2, §3). So the ship greets the Player with something they made and never with something they lack. Travel is a passage with weather, using the crafting pass's Shaman line "sheng becomes weather, not multiplier" (`council/passes/6FACE_PASS_crafting-charge1_2026-10-18.md`). Arrival is a threshold the Player may pause at. Fog on the chart lifts when the Player goes looking. It never lifts as a completion percentage. That keeps QUICKSTART principle 9, "Mystery rewards inquiry" (`ontology/v0.1/QUICKSTART.md`).

**Each practice yields a kind of thing, never an amount.** From my side:
- A finished W.A.V.E. gives the cabin's light and air, in the element of its move (`move-engine.ts:27-51`, per `cc-move-sets-element`).
- A 3-2-1 gives a window. Its three persons are three views (face it, talk to it, be it, `Clean321Flow.tsx`), so a window fits it.
- A daemon met and honored can come aboard as a passenger in its honored pose, by the Player's invitation (`GESTURES.md` l.35-39).

A daemon is never a hull plate, an engine or a part. A part can be scrapped, and nothing in the record lets a person's daemon be scrapped (`dmt-trainer`: "the daemon is never fought"). Charge intensity sets look only (`cc-intensity-look`). Presets and fictional feelings make the same component (`cc-charge-only`).

**The mechanic diagnoses when the ship's condition reports on the person.** These hooks fail: "321 / let-go as the repair or re-fuel rite", fuel gauges, hull damage, and parts locked behind unfinished practice. Each one turns an undone practice into a visible fault in the Player's own vessel. The standing test asks whether the design says something is wrong with them, or something the designer learned. Under that test a dim cabin, an empty fuel line or "repair needed" says something is wrong with them. NMS's Quicksilver dailies and real-time organ phases (research §2) would make inner work a chore and add a streak. QUICKSTART rules that out ("Intimate disclosure is not progression currency"). A 3-2-1 that ends with no shift must still yield a window, because no-shift is a valid finding. Skipping stays legal (`bmt-321-offer`).

**My standing test passes four plain rules.** First, nothing on the ship decays from absence: `rc-stale-neglect` governs the shared Between, not a private world. Second, the ship can dock and rest with no cost. Third, refusing a practice closes no route, so travel to every charted place needs only cockpit and engine, which every Player has from the start (the KH1 minimum, research §1). Fourth, a component the Player lets go "went to ash" and composts, owing nothing (`cc-no-failure`). Under these rules a sparse ship tells the designer what this Player has chosen to show. It does not say what the Player lacks.

**The four parts get these verdicts.**
- **Vehicle:** support with conditions. It is the Player's den with the WAVE room as its cabin, it has no fuel economy, and its condition never mirrors practice.
- **Inner world:** support with conditions. Places are named by the Player (QUICKSTART principle 10). The five element settings are ports (`element-settings.md`). The cave stays the only port in the Chapter 1 demo.
- **Practice-to-component mapping:** support for kinds (light, window, passenger). I oppose any mapping that makes a daemon a part or makes a bigger feeling buy a better part.
- **Bounds:** support. The bounds are visible caps on space, as in KH1's grid. They are never caps on feeling, and they include no counts compared between players.

**One question only Wendell can answer.** Is the ship alive, a kin that grows with the Player like the NMS Living Ship, or a craft the Player builds and owns like a Gummi ship? The record cannot settle it. It names the Player and the daemons, but it has no ruling on whether a vessel has a voice. Giving it one adds a character to his canon, which is owner authority and canonical prose under QUICKSTART's escalation list. The answer changes whether the ship speaks, whether it has satisfaction states like spirits (`spirit-generator.md`), and whether the Player can compost it.

**Test for the next pass, dated 2026-10-25.** A player starts the smallest slice, does no practice, and uses only a preset or skips. The test passes if that player can board, fly to one charted place, arrive, and dock, with no on-screen string naming a daemon, a lack or a repair, and nothing on the ship changing for the worse during or after a seven-day absence. It fails on any blocked route, any word from {low, empty, damaged, repair, need, not enough, locked, failed}, or any decay over time. Check it two ways: a string grep over the slice's text files comes back clean, and Wendell plays one no-practice run and reports what he felt in the cabin.

## Architect (4 Fog > 40 Thaw, lines 4 and 6)

Fog says we are guessing where we could ask, and the record answers most of this. Embers, moves, spirits, rooms and caps already exist, so the vessel should be built from them and not from a new currency. Thaw says the knot has loosened and the window is short. The ember slice from the crafting pass is not yet in main, so the vessel's types should be cut alongside it now, before the ember settles into a Between-only shape.

**Objects and state.** All of these are proposals. None is built.
- `Ember`: the existing record from the crafting pass. It is a worked charge: {move, from, to, source, state}. It has no text field, and intensity sets only its look (`cc-intensity-look`; `council/passes/6FACE_PASS_crafting-charge1_2026-10-18.md`, Architect).
- `Component`: an ember given a form. Fields: {id, kind, element, emberId, look, state}. States: `loose | fitted | resting | composted`. It carries no power number.
- `Socket`: a cell on the hull, {cell, accepts: kind[]}. A socket is a place a part can go. It does not rank the part.
- `Vessel`: {hullSize, sockets, components}. States: `unlaunchable | ready | docked | underway`. To be ready it needs a Helm and a Sail. That is Gummi's "cockpit and engine" rule and the only gate.
- `InnerWorld`: the set of the player's Places. It is private (a `BetweenPrivate`-style key, bars-engine `src/lib/between/rules.ts`).
- `Place/Port`: {id, setting (one of the five elements), origin}. States: `unseen | charted | visited | fallow`. `fallow` keeps a place without staleness pressure, because staleness belongs to shared space only (`rc-stale-neglect`).
- `Route`: an edge between Places. States: `uncrossed | crossed`. After the first crossing, travel is direct, like KH's Warp-G. A crossing is an encounter that ends. It is never a quest that completes.
- Existing objects keep their own axes. A `Spirit` is crew: it can sit in a Berth by its satisfaction state, and having it aboard does not mean the player can command it. A `Daemon` is never a resource and never fought. `Lantern` stays a Between object. `mana` comes from dissolving and goes to the pilot's stats, never to the hull (`sd-mana-to-stat`). A `BAR` is the outer-world kernel.

**How the move sets kind and element.** Each move in bars-engine `src/lib/quest-grammar/move-engine.ts:27-51` carries `primaryWaveStage` and an element. Store only the move id and derive the rest:
- kind from stage: wakeUp gives Lens, cleanUp gives Keel, growUp gives Hold, showUp gives Helm;
- element from `element` (Transcend moves) or `toElement` (the ten translate moves);
- finding: `PersonalMoveType` has no openUp, so no move yields a Sail. Sail comes only from a whole WAVE run, and its element comes from the charge's feeling (Anger is Fire, and so on);
- reject a move whose `fromElement` does not match the charge, as in the crafting pass.

| Practice | Record it already leaves | What it yields | Component kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| Daemon spar or meeting | `letGoVoice`, honored pose (`bigmind.js`) | A charted Place (that daemon's hall) and a Figurehead look | None. It adds world, not parts. |
| 321 | Clean321 session (bars-engine `src/actions/clean321.ts`) | One Ember with the declared move | Lens, Keel, Hold or Helm, by the move's stage |

| Outer-world deeds | A BAR, or a quest with attestation (`sg-work-grows`) | A Route to an outer Port, and a log entry | None |

**Contracts.** Ontology principle 5 says what a game tracks and rewards is what it treats as real. So the vessel treats as real only that a practice was performed and that its encounter ended.
- Tracks: the move, the practice record id and lineage (`emberId`).
- Rewards: one component per ended encounter, whatever the charge size or reported shift.
- Gates: Helm plus Sail to launch, plus the caps below.
- Permits: only the maker edits.
- Does not track: intensity as power, text, "integrated", or any count shown against another player.
- Conflict found: Clean321 mints a bonus diamond when the charge drops by more than 2 (bars-engine `src/components/clean321/Clean321Flow.tsx`). That pays for a reported shift. The vessel must not inherit it.

**Scout lines.**
- Snap grid and fit rule: `scout: 5`, about 150 lines of TypeScript, rendered with pixi.js, which bars-engine already has. No search was spent on a ready-made library.
- Part art: `scout: 4`, Kenney Space Kit (CC0, https://kenney-assets.itch.io/space-kit), rendered to sprites through the vendored blender skills. Confirm the download format.
- Route map: `scout: 2`, @xyflow/react, which bars-engine already has.
- Place art: `scout: 2`. The five element settings exist as designs (`council/callsheets/element-settings.md`); the cave is built. The three procedural-planet repos have unchecked licences, so skip them.
- Component table: `scout: 5`, a typed table in code. No Prisma model.

**Where the three references fit.**
- Gummi fits: the minimum-parts gate, caps that grow at story beats (KH's COM levels tied to Big Mind chambers, not to grind), per-kind maximums, sample ships to edit, and warp after the first crossing. The editor friction KH was criticised for is the risk we copy.
- NMS adjacency fits only as weather. Neighbouring parts whose elements feed each other in the generating cycle change the look and weather, never a percentage, following the Shaman's crafting-pass reading. NMS slot classes (C to S) and supercharged slots do not fit, because they are rank. The Living Ship fits as a third state. Its real-time gates do not: they break saves, and they press on a pace the player should set.
- Starcruiser's finale-first design fits: work backward from the centre of the self in bars-engine `content/ontology-game/cave/LEVELS.md`. The Datapad fits as a private ship's log. Its single storyline and its price do not fit.

**False binaries.**
1. Built vs grown ship. The third state: parts are grown by practice, and placement is built by hand. A resting part is kept but not fitted.
2. Ship vs world. The third state: a docked vessel becomes a Place.
3. Reward vs no reward for inner work. The third state: the yield is form (kind and look), not strength.
4. Fitted vs lost. The third states: resting and composted.
5. Arrival vs completion. A crossing ends an encounter. It completes nothing.

**Smallest playable slice.** A WAVE run in the WAVE room gives a Sail, and one 321 gives a part typed by its move. The player fits both on a 3x3 hull and launches along one Route from home to the Metal cave, the only setting built.
- Files: bars-engine `src/lib/vessel/types.ts`, `fit.ts` (pure `fit(vessel, component, cell)` and `canLaunch(vessel)`), `fromEmber.ts` (uses `getMoveById`), and a pixi page under `/ontology-game/vessel`.
- Test: `src/lib/vessel/__tests__/vessel.test.ts`, run with tsx like `test:quest-grammar`. V1: two embers with the same move, at intensity 2 and 9, give components that are deep-equal except for id and look. V2: `canLaunch` is false without a Helm and a Sail. V3: a cleanUp move gives a Keel with the derived element. V4: no openUp move maps to a kind. V5: an occupied cell rejects a fit. V6: the serialized vessel contains no text and no intensity. V7: a crossed Route writes `crossed` and leaves every quest status unchanged.

**Verdicts.**
- (1) Vehicle: support with conditions. Spirits are crew, not parts, and intensity never sets power.
- (2) Inner world: support. Places are charted by daemon meetings, and the cave stays the only setting in the demo.
- (3) Practices to components: support with conditions. Sail needs its own path because move-engine has no openUp, and the Clean321 bonus diamond must not carry over.
- (4) Bounds: support. Caps grow at story beats, adjacency gives weather, and there is no staleness in private space.

**Only Wendell can answer.** Do ship parts lift `cc-between-only`? That ruling keeps crafting inside the Between for now, and a private vessel is crafted outside it. The record cannot settle it, because it is his own ruling.

**Test for the next pass, dated 2026-10-25.** Wendell plays the slice on his iPhone, from one WAVE run and one 321 to a launched vessel at the Metal cave, in under ten minutes and with no instruction screen. The design is falsified if he does not launch, or if he names fitting parts as the friction (KH1's Gummi Garage failure repeating).

## Challenger (25 Candour, no changing lines)

*"Your next move has no calculation in it, and that is the exposure."* This question comes from desire, not from the record. The way to answer it honestly is to work out what it costs and which open work it pushes back.

**Strip the image.** Said plainly, the claim is this: doing 321, WAVE or daemon work gives you parts; parts build a vehicle; the vehicle moves you between places you also build. Apply my term test (`council/faces.yaml`, challenger) and two of the nouns have no verb under them. "Ship" has the verb "travel", but the Chapter 1 demo has one place to travel to: the cave stays the only setting, and the other four lands are post-demo stretch goals (`council/callsheets/element-settings.md` l.68-69). "Emergent bounded ecology" names no action at all; only the phrasing holds it up. "Player-built inner world" does have a verb, "place objects", and the hollow already has it (bars-engine `public/between/hollow.js`, the `cc-hearth-slice` ember-to-lantern path).

**Breakpoints and anti-patterns.**
- **Inner work becomes a farm.** If 321 gives hulls and WAVE gives engines, the player does the practice to get the part. Whichever practice is cheapest gets repeated, and the 321 turns into a form to fill in. That breaks the ontology's rule that "Intimate disclosure is not progression currency" (`ontology/v0.1/QUICKSTART.md`). It also breaks `cc-intensity-look`.
- **Resources that reward disclosure.** If a deeper charge drop or a longer "Be it" gives a better part, the game pays people to perform pain. `completeClean321` already gives a bonus diamond when charge drops by more than 2 (bars-engine `src/actions/clean321.ts`). Copying that rule into ship power is where the leak would start.
- **Daemons as loot.** If a daemon drops a component, it has been fought. That breaks `dmt-trainer` ("the daemon is never fought").
- **Travel as filler.** Reviewers called KH flight repetitive and skippable once warps unlock, and KH3 hired a shooter studio and was still panned (research §1). With one destination, every trip would be filler.
- **Cost spiral.** KH3's free editor "can quickly spiral out of control". The spiral that matters here is ours. J5 cloth is at round 13b for one robe (`council/callsheets/INDEX.md`). Wendell rejected the abstract garden ("a bit too abstract"). The scout's 2D pixi editor will draw the same reaction, and pull three.js and art rounds in behind it.
- **Real-time gates.** The NMS egg ripens on a clock, and skipping time breaks saves. A hull that grows "if you practise tomorrow" is a streak, and a streak is coercion dressed as care.
- **Starcruiser.** The price was high and the story single, so nobody came back. The real lesson is that it was designed backward from a finale every guest sees. This ship has no finale.
- **Scope creep.** The ship competes for build time with the hollow's embers and lanterns (bars-engine #366/#367, `bh-device-gate`) and with the cave. It also renames both of them.

**Scheduling truth.** The open strands are the Chapter 1 demo (the Kickstarter video waits on his go; `council/callsheets/chapter1-demo.md`), spirit round 21 (waits on his look; `council/loops/spirit-generator.json`), the Between lantern slice, and J5 cloth round 13b. What is scarce is his looks, not build hours. A ship adds a fifth strand waiting for his eyes. By being exciting, this question avoids the Kickstarter gate.

**Cheapest test that could show the whole concept wrong.** Paper only, no build. Wendell does two short 321s. Before one, he sees a card saying "this yields a hull plate"; before the other, no card. Afterwards he writes one line each: did the card change which charge he chose, or how much he wrote? If it did, part 3 as asked (each practice yields its own components) is wrong at the root, because the practice is now steered by the reward. It is one person and one evening, so it is weak evidence. But it is the only evidence that bears on the risk that would sink the concept.

**My standing test applied.** Test 1 above settles whether part 3 may tie each practice to its own component (the open question under `cc-charge-only` and QUICKSTART). The answer decides whether parts come from which practice was done or only from finishing one. Test 2 below settles whether travel has a destination before the demo. That decides whether part 1 is built now or parked with the element settings. I propose no other tests. Anything more would be for show.

**Verdicts.**
1. *Ship as vehicle:* **defer.** It has nowhere to go until a second land exists. I dissent from the obvious design. If a vessel comes, let it be the WAVE room itself, a home that moves, with no editor. "Take it home" (bars-engine `content/ontology-game/cave/LEVELS.md` l.29-50) already does the travelling.
2. *Player-built inner world:* **support with conditions.** It is the hollow and lanterns under another name. Grow that and do not start a second world.
3. *Practices to components:* **oppose as framed.** Finishing a practice gives one ember, and the move sets its element (`move-engine.ts` l.27-51). Which practice you did, how deep you went and how much you wrote never set part type or power. A preset counts as the same floor.
4. *Bounds:* **support with conditions.** The caps go on the build: stacked KH1-style limits, a cockpit and an engine as the only required parts. Nothing caps the practice: no daily limit, no clock, no streak, nothing that fails at random.

**Only Wendell can answer this.** When you picture the ship, is it the place you go back to (the WAVE room that carries you), or a craft you fly between places? The record has his taste for embodied places, but nothing on flight as play.

**Dated test for the next pass (Test 2, due 2026-10-25).** Count the destinations a ship could reach in the build as it stands on that date, with the cave and the hollow counting as two. Fewer than three destinations means the ship is parked as a post-Kickstarter stretch goal next to `element-settings.md`. It decides whether the next pass builds the vessel or only the hollow.

A saved practice record is evidence that something happened in the past. It is never a yes to turning that practice into ship parts later.

**Binding rules.** All of these are obligations, not preferences:
- **Charge and crafting (`cc-*`):** a louder feeling changes only the look (`cc-intensity-look`). Everything comes from charge, with presets as the floor (`cc-charge-only`). There is no random failure, and a part that is let go composts (`cc-no-failure`). Crafted items stay private (`cc-pouch-private`). Moving charge across games needs its own permission record first (`cc-between-only`). The stricter licence rule holds (`cc-licence-conflict`).
- **Spirits:** `sg-charge-disc` and `sg-overcharge` cap how much spirit a player can take on. Only a fully revealed, elemented spirit can be dissolved (`spirit-dissolve.md`).
- **Daemons:** daemons are sparred, never fought (`dmt-trainer`, `game-demo.md:87`).
- **The ontology:** disclosure is not currency (QUICKSTART), and lineage is kept (principle 11).
- **Non-AI first class:** the non-AI path is a full mode, and "the system never generates a turn" (`Clean321Flow.tsx` l.9).
- **Licence:** only own work and CC0 ship (QUICKSTART). Kenney Space Kit is CC0 and allowed. CC-BY is not allowed, even though `council/toolbox.yaml` l.7 permits it.
- **Process:** questions go to the board. The Chapter 1 demo comes first, with the cave as its only setting (`chapter1-demo.md`). Element settings come after the demo (`element-settings.md` l.68-69). The device gate (`bh-device-gate`) applies.

**What the ship would strain.**
1. Gummi ship flight is a shooter. Any turret aimed at a daemon, or at a "hazard" that is a daemon, breaks "never fought".
2. No Man's Sky's slot and upgrade loop pays for volume. More sessions or higher charge buying a better hull would make disclosure into currency.
3. Travelling "between places" pulls toward building the four element lands now, which breaks the demo-first ruling.

What is merely preferred: a 3D hull, free flight, procedural planets. All three can wait.

**Provenance.** Every component carries a lineage record with five fields: the practice (WAVE step, 3-2-1 phase or daemon spar), the move and its element (`move-engine.ts:27-51`), the source session id, the date, and the player's own label. If the source is deleted, the part keeps a tombstone and stays usable, because deletion is not composting (QUICKSTART). A stored record is never put to a new purpose, such as analytics or a shared world, without a fresh grant.

**Verdicts on the four parts.**
1. **Vehicle: support, with conditions.** It is a way to get around the map that already exists: from the WAVE room, through the hall of doors, to the Big Mind cave (`LEVELS.md`, `lv-home-portal`). It is not a combat craft, and it reaches no new lands before the demo.
2. **Inner world: support, with conditions.** The world is private by default, grows only from acts the player authored, and composting is a valid end state. No screen reads the world back as a diagnosis.
3. **Practice to component: support only in this form.** The practice and move set what kind of part it is. Nothing sets its size: no counts, no intensity, no streaks. A skipped 3-2-1 never blocks a part (`bmt-321-offer`). A preset gives the same result as a worked charge. Spirit parts come only from revealed spirits and stay within `sg-overcharge`.
4. **Bounds: support.** The bounds are visible and are themselves obligations. Nothing is compared between players. Neglect produces weather, not penalties. Stopping is always allowed.

**Phase gates.**
- **G0, scope:** a scout line, a table mapping each component to its practice and its binding row, and the ember slice merged (bars-engine #379 plus the hearth).
- **G1, probe:** a pure data model plus tests that lineage is present on every part, that intensity does not change stats, that a skip does not block, that an unrevealed spirit cannot become a part, and that no field counts anything.
- **G2, preview:** a page that passes `voice_lint`, a paper route that plays whole, and iPhone-size WebKit.
- **G3:** `bh-device-gate` on his iPhone.
- **G4:** his look, recorded on the board.

**Definition of done for the first slice.** One practice, worked or preset, gives one part. The part snaps onto a hull in the WAVE room. The vessel carries the player from the WAVE room to the Big Mind door. Tapping the part shows its lineage. Composting the part leaves the vessel able to fly. ## Diplomat (2 Bearing > 6 Divergence, lines 2, 4, 5, 6 changing)

**The cast.** "The whole weight of this is yours, unasked" fits a design where the newcomer would carry the whole job: inner work, crafting, and a world, all before they have flown once. "Going deeper and pushing forward at once" names the risk. If the ship asks for depth (321, WAVE, daemons) and progress (parts, travel) in the same five minutes, each one undoes the other. So the doorway has to be light, and depth stays optional.

**The five-year-old doorway.** "You get a little ship. You can fly it to places inside you. You can paint it and add parts whenever you like."

**Translation for a skeptical newcomer.** "It's a ship you build and fly through a map of your own moods, and you decide how much of yourself goes into it." No therapy words, no AI words. In this copy "inner" means "yours", not "deep".

**Onboarding, first five minutes.** Do what KH3 does and hand over a premade sample ship ready to fly (research, "Premade samples to edit"). Minute one: pick one of three sample ships by colour and shape. Minute two: fly it from the WAVE room dock to one place (the cave, the only setting in Chapter 1 per `element-settings.md`). Minute three: change one part using a preset. Minutes four and five: an offer, never a gate: "Want to make a part from something you're carrying? You can also skip this." A newcomer who has done no inner work flies the same ship at the same quality. KH1 shows what goes wrong otherwise: one reviewer quit its editor after one try, and players skipped the flights once warps unlocked (research, failure modes).

**Accessible routes.** Every component gets four ways in that share one quality floor: preset, symbolic (choose a colour or element and nothing else), fictional ("a feeling my character has"), and worked (daemon spar, 321, WAVE). This extends `cc-charge-only`, which makes presets the floor, and the result equals a worked one (`council/callsheets/rows/crafting-charge-2026-10-18.json`). A player who skips 321 loses nothing; it is already "skippable, never a gate" (`bmt-321-offer`). A player who can't do breath practice (asthma, panic, trauma, or plain refusal) needs a WAVE route without breathing: the seven steps as cards, read or tapped, with Exhale as a gesture or a rest. Nonverbal and second-language players need icon-and-colour input with no typing. Disabled players need the editor to work without fine motor control: snap to slots, no 3D dragging. Culturally guarded players get a "keep it private" switch that hides every source, built on `cc-pouch-private`.

**Non-AI and paper are first class.** bars-engine `CLAUDE.md` says "The non-AI version is not a fallback".
