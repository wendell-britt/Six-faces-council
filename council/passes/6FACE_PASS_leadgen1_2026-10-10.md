# Six-face pass: lead generation 1, people to the coaching walk

Wendell asked on 2026-10-10 at 21:55 UTC in the Wendell's Coaching Practice project: "I now just need to get people to
the site with the least amount of money and the highest surface area". He has been "failing at this for 6 years", and
"Everyone wants to sell me a strategy guide and I have a few inside of our library as well". The project coordinator
started the thread. This is the first pass on lead generation. The plan it weighs is
`/mnt/project-files/lead-gen-research/coaching-pipeline-plan.md`, and its stage zero is bars-engine PR #273.

**Casts** (`council/iching/cast.py --json`, recorded in the ledger): Shaman 42 Windfall, line 4, becomes 25 Candour.
Architect 51 Shock, line 5, becomes 17 Following. Challenger 23 Erosion, no changing lines. Regent 20 Witnessing, lines
4, 5 and 6, becomes 16 Anticipation. Diplomat 25 Candour, no changing lines. Sage 34 Vigour, lines 2, 3 and 4, becomes
24 Turning.

## Scorecard

No earlier pass on lead generation set tests. The coaching game passes set none that this pass grades.

## Anchor

The walk at masteringallyship.com/coaching gives a visitor the six unpacking questions and ends at a session with
Wendell at four prices (`cf-six-questions`, `cf-one-door`, `cg-money`). The job now is to bring people to it for little
money and from many places, and to know which places worked.

## Shaman

**Windfall becomes Candour.** More is arriving than he put in: four podcast episodes, a backer list, a walk that
already does what a first session does. The move with no calculation in it is the plain invitation, and that is the
exposure.

**The felt read is fatigue with plans.** Twelve plans sit in the Library and six years sit behind them. A thirteenth
plan dressed as a strategy would land as one more guide someone wants to sell him. The plan has to read as a few
invitations he can send this week, with the walk as the gift inside them.

**The Shaman recommends the letter first.** The first pipe is people who already know him, and the letter asks them to walk the forest and
say where it stuck. Asking for their sentence gives them something to do that is not buying.

## Architect

**Shock becomes Following.** The pattern broke open when the walk went live on 10 October; now the work is inside what
it opened. The machine has no instrument panel yet.

**What must be true.** Each visit has to carry where it came from, and each booking has to carry the same source. On 10
October Vercel answered "Web Analytics not found" for the bars-engine project, and the repository holds no analytics
code. PR #273 adds Vercel Web Analytics and passes the utm tags to Calendly at the moment of the click. It stores
nothing the visitor writes, so `cg-browser-only` holds.

**The structure is one tag per link.** Every link to the walk carries a tag: `utm_source` names the pipe (letter, podcast, guest, live) and
`utm_campaign` names the item (backers, ep4, a show's name). A Monday read, the same shape as Flirtcraft's
`promo-monday-numbers`, reports visits by source and bookings by source.

## Challenger

**Erosion comes without change.** The ground under this is going and nobody has said so: the Library's plans erode each other.
Every one of the twelve ends at a next step and none records a result. The Dream 100 has four of a hundred names. The
Ideal Reader Matrix in the June strategy spec still says "NOT YET POPULATED".

**The anti-pattern is starting a new plan before the last one produced a number.** The falsification test for this plan is
dated: by 2026-11-07 the Monday reads show at least one pipe that sent a visitor who booked, or the plan's pipes are
wrong and the next pass says which.

**The Challenger dissents on the first pipe.** It would start with guesting on other people's shows. The backers and the
inner circle have known his work for years, and a letter to them tests the walk more than it finds new clients.
Guesting is the only pipe in the plan that reaches people who have never heard of him.

**Paid ads wait.** Paying to send strangers to a page whose booking rate on warm visitors is unknown buys a number
nobody can read. `cg-money` and the reserved money rule keep spending with Wendell in any case.

## Regent

**Witnessing becomes Anticipation.** The place he watches from is itself in view: a backer who has waited for a book
will read a coaching invitation against that wait. The good part has started and the event has not.

**The obligation is to the backers.** The board's `ship-order` and `ship-pace` questions are still open, and 247 backers are owed a book.
The letter must name the book's state honestly or say nothing about it. It must not read as a sale to people still
waiting on what they paid for.

**Two rules are non-negotiable.** The letter goes out under his name, so he rewrites it and sends it; no session sends anything to a
backer. Each pipe keeps a results line in the plan file every Monday, so this plan cannot join the twelve. The earlier
plans stay in the Library as reference and are not deleted (transcend and include).

**Stage zero is the phase gate.** It comes before any pipe that costs money: Web Analytics is switched on and PR #273 is merged.
The free pipes run beside the gate, because the letter and the podcast links speed the first numbers (the Regent's
lesson of 2026-10-03, "Work that serves the gate in front of it runs beside that gate").

## Diplomat

**Candour comes without change.** The next move has no calculation in it. The Diplomat agrees that the invitation leads, and
asks who it leaves out.

**The visitor who is not ready is excluded.** That is the visitor who walks the forest, finds the belief and is not ready to pay today. The walk gives
that person nothing to keep and nothing that brings them back. Most people are in that place on a first visit
*(the council's claim, not a measurement)*.

**A keep step is the bridge.** At the centre, "Send this to myself" opens the visitor's own mail app with what they carried
in, the same pattern as `cf-your-words-email`, and stores nothing. A step further is an opt-in to the seven-email "Meet
Your Daemons" series, which needs a list and so changes `cg-browser-only`.

**The Diplomat dissents on the keep step.** It would offer the series opt-in, because a person who is not ready today
needs a relationship over weeks more than a copy of one evening. The other faces lean to the mail-app keep step, which
changes no ruling.

**The copy names the walk plainly.** Every caption and show note says the walk plainly, for example "Walk the six questions I ask in a
first session", never a funnel word.

## Dissent check

The pass is not unanimous. The Challenger dissents on the first pipe and the Diplomat dissents on the keep step. All
five agree on measuring first and on no paid ads now; that agreement is a flag, and the Sage notes that none of the
plan's pipes has a number behind it yet.

## Sage

**Vigour becomes Turning.** Force is available and unaimed; what comes back is one line deep. The plan aims the force at
one line at a time and reads what returns each Monday.

**The synthesis keeps something from each face.** It keeps the Shaman's invitation that asks for a sentence before a sale. The Architect's tags and the
Monday read. The Challenger's dated test and the hold on paid ads. The Regent's honest-letter obligation, the results
line, and running the free pipes beside the gate. The Diplomat's excluded visitor and the keep step. Nothing was
dropped.

**Two dissents go to Wendell.** They are which pipe gets his hours first (the Challenger against the rest), and whether the keep step
stops at his own mail app or becomes a list (the Diplomat against the rest).

**Three tasks change.** PR #273 waits on Web Analytics being switched on and on review. The plan file gains a "Results"
section that a Monday session fills. The podcast strand adds the tagged walk link to its show notes and captions.
