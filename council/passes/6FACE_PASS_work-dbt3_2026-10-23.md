- The Player is the vulnerable child (`daemons.yaml` 348-351; `questions.yaml` 17-21): "the centre the others work for or against". Its state is "No daemon has the joystick" (`daemons.yaml` 358 onward).

**Position `tw-emotion-mind-place`.** Feeling arises in the Player. The Emotional Body daemon is the daemon that took over handling that feeling to protect the child. DBT's emotion mind is the state when the Emotional Body has the joystick, and reasonable mind is a state when another daemon leads (the record does not say which; Controller and Skeptic are the nearest, and the doc leaves it unmapped). Wise Mind and Big Mind are the trajectory past the Player, where the daemons stand around the child as spirits and no daemon holds the joystick. The WAVE room's validate-first order (doc 3.6) is the Emotional Body's honoured form, "release in its time": the feeling is let finish arriving before any inquiry. DBT's three words stay cited and do not become cave names.

The record does not say what the Emotional Body protects (no line in `daemons.yaml` or `questions.yaml`). That is left for the daemon interviews and is not a question for him now, because no build in this extension waits on it.

## Findings kept from each face

- **Shaman (weighted).** The Player is the vulnerable child, so no inquiry is ever aimed at the Player. The Work questions a thought, and a thought line is a daemon's line ("I always fail" is the Damaged Self's or the Controller's). Turnarounds are offered on the daemon's line. "Turnaround to the self" must never read as "I did this to myself" about a hurt in the event card. A streak shown as a number that drops to zero says something is wrong with the player (the Shaman's standing test). Show the runs as kept.
- **Architect.** Completion is measured by steps finished in the loop, never by what was written. A preset line, a picture or a line the game cannot read completes a step the same as typed words, so the system still reads no free text. Emotion mind is a state flag on the Emotional Body daemon (who has the joystick). It is not a new character.
- **Challenger (dissent on streaks).** A visible streak counter that resets is loss aversion, the black-hat drive the doc leaves unused (3.5, D8), even with no guilt message. Falsification test: a player who breaks a run sees nothing that reads as lost. The game offers only the paced-breathing and paired-relaxation parts.
- **Regent (weighted).** Child and trauma tiers stay closed until a named clinician reads them (`tw-clinician-named`, `tw-tier-gate`). A sitting ended by Calm, Step back, a flooding route or a danger hard stop counts as declared rest. It never breaks a run and never costs a reward. DBT's names (DEAR MAN, GIVE, FAST, TIPP, Wise Mind) are Linehan's. They stay in a lineage column and on the works-cited page. Player-facing names come from a term pass, not from this pass (`tw-permission-on-objection`).
- **Diplomat (weighted).** DEAR MAN was split between Challenger and Diplomat. All of it moves to the Diplomat; the Challenger keeps the body-first skills and opposite action. A preset line always counts as completing a step, so a child or a classroom player never has to disclose to finish. "Turnaround to the other" is never offered toward a person who harmed the player (it carries over from 3.7).
