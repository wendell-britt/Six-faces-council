## 🌊 Shaman, under Bite becoming Nightfall

**Bite says something is lodged and nothing closes until it is named.** What is lodged here is Wendell's
absence. The session's work has to close something he can see on his phone, so each run finishes one change
and adds one board row, never a report of activity. Nightfall says a light is being kept covered, and that
light is the abandoned work (E1). Open up exists to uncover it. A covered light is still lit, though: abandoned
work goes to the board as found, never retired by the session.

## 🧠 Architect, under Bearing becoming Sprouting

**Bearing puts the weight on whoever is holding it, so each run carries one move and no more.** The run
re-weighs what is due, then takes the day's move in Wendell's order: Wake up, Open up, Clean up, Grow up, Show
up. When the day's move finds nothing, it does research, his "something new". Sprouting warns that early work
looks like a mess. The first two weeks will find more than they can finish, and that state is normal. Every
change goes on a strand branch with its own pull request. The steward in #16 (E3) keeps those branches
current, so the session builds on it rather than beside it.

## ⚔️ Challenger, under Restraint

**Restraint names a small rule nobody revisited, holding everything.** Two such rules hold here, and both
stay. Merging into main stays with Wendell. #16 says so, and the pipeline's ship stage merges only at a board
read. A cloud session cannot push tags (E4), so Clean up cannot retire a branch; it can only list one. The
session works inside both rules and says when one blocks a move.

**Dissent, recorded.** Three writers now touch this repo: the Sprout session, the steward and the daily
session. The Challenger would have the daily session write no code until #16 merges, because #16's merge
driver is what keeps three writers from colliding on the board's data.

## 🏛 Regent, under Estrangement becoming Burning

**Estrangement warns of a conclusion reached from a distance, and the daily session works at a distance by
design.** It never acts on the reserved list (E5). It never changes another session's rows or branches. In
bars-engine, whose owner is johnair01 (E2), its reach is Wendell's call, so that one is a question. A project
with a deferral waiting is skipped until its date, as the council lesson of 2026-10-03 requires. Burning warns
against the same true sentence consuming its subject, so a move that finds nothing real two runs in a row says
so once on the board rather than every day.

## 🤝 Diplomat, under Incubation becoming The Well

**Incubation says what is true is still inside the shell, and The Well says the reach is broken.** Finished
work that never reaches him is the broken reach. Each run adds at most one row to the board: a position that
merges at his next board read unless he flips it, or a finding for him to read. A daily session that added ten
rows a day would bury the board, and he works from his phone.

## 🦉 Sage, under Erosion becoming Stillness

**Erosion says the ground is going and nobody has said so.** The ground here is the open work across six
repos, ten pull requests in one of them alone (E1). Stillness gives the move: stop while the wanting is still
running. Each run takes one move, finishes one change, adds one row and stops, however much more it found.

**What the synthesis keeps from each face:**

- From the Shaman, one finished change per run, and abandoned work brought to light, never retired.
- From the Architect, the daily rotation in Wendell's order, research as the fallback, and building on #16.
- From the Challenger, merging stays with Wendell, and the tag limit is stated.
- From the Regent, the reserved list, other sessions' work left alone, the deferral lesson, and bars-engine
  put to Wendell.
- From the Diplomat, one row per run.

**Sequencing, from the Challenger's dissent:** the build waits for #16 to merge.
