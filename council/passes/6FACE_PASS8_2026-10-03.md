back and the council can weigh in on them in new context."* The pipeline ran in full: spec kit at
`.specify/specs/spaced-deferral/`, the cheap test before the board was built, and the build on
`feature/spaced-deferral`. No daemons were sent. The question is narrow, and the record answered most of it.

## 🌊 Shaman, under Overload

**Overload says the beam is carrying more than its posts hold, and a board of returning items is that beam.**
Wendell deferred the oracle drafts because he is out of practice, which is a weight he named himself. An item
that comes back bare puts the same weight back on him and adds the work of remembering why he set it down.
So the returning row shows his own last steer, in his words, before anything the council says. The "when I
ask" option stays on the list, because some weight he should be able to put down without a date.

## 🧠 Architect, under Persuasion becoming Mutuality

**Persuasion points at an effect already happening, and the board already brings items back (E1).** Spaced
repetition changes only the arithmetic of the date. The smallest build keeps one rule, a week and then
double the last wait, in two places that name each other: the board's script, which shows him the date, and
`due.py`, which records it. The re-weigh needs no new store. It is one field, `review`, on the row the board
already renders. Mutuality says the movement arrived because something stopped, and here a deferral that
stops a project is exactly what produces the data, so every deferral is kept in `history`.

## ⚔️ Challenger, under Bearing becoming The Well

**Bearing says the weight is mine unasked, and the weight here is the numbers.** Wendell asked for spaced
repetition. He did not ask for 7 days or for doubling. Those are the council's, and they go on the board as
position `sd-intervals`, which he can flip. The Well warns that the water can be there while the reach is
broken. The risk is a re-weigh that finds nothing and pads, so the cheap test ran first (E2). It passed, and
it caught the reach failing in the other direction: records from before the deferral (E3).

**Dissent, recorded.** The schedule never retires an item by itself, so the sixth spaced deferral of an item waits
224 days. The Challenger would cap it and ask him to retire or take it up. No cap ships, because a
cap is a number inferred on his behalf (council lesson of 2026-08-22). The count of deferrals is on the row,
and he can retire an item any time.

## 🏛 Regent, under Drained becoming Depths

**Drained warns of explaining that continues after the basin is dry.** A re-weigh that restates the old case
in new words is exactly that, so `review` holds what changed with its source, one line per face, and a
recommendation from a fixed list: take it up, defer again, retire, or reshape the question. If nothing
changed, the box says so in one line. Depths found a bottom under the bottom twice in this pass. The first
was the same-day records (E3). The second was a sentence in the spec's first draft that claimed more than the record
holds (E4). Every deferral stays in `history`, also after he takes the item up, because the lesson he ruled today
says the pattern is data about priority. Board data is written on main only; this branch carries code.

## 🤝 Diplomat, under Fullness

**Fullness says the work is at its peak and something is being saved.** What is being saved is the re-weigh
itself, if it waits for a session he happens to open. An item can then sit on the Open tab bare, with only a
note that the council has not re-weighed it yet. A weekly scheduled session would bring every item back with
its reading done. That session spends his account's usage, so it passes the reach test and goes to him as
question `sd-when`. The Diplomat would choose the weekly session.

## 🦉 Sage, under Footing becoming Burning

**Footing says the ground matters and play keeps it safe.** The play is in the casts: each return gets fresh
casts, so the faces read an old item from a new angle rather than repeating themselves. Burning names the
failure this feature could cause, the same true sentence said every month until it consumes its subject.
The fixed list of recommendations and the "nothing changed" line are the guard.

**What the synthesis keeps from each face:**

- From the Shaman, his own steer shown first.
- From the Architect, one rule in two named places, and no new store.
- From the Challenger, the cheap test, the numbers marked as the council's, and the cap dissent recorded.
- From the Regent, the review's fixed shape, and the history kept for good.
- From the Diplomat, the weekly session, put to him as a question.

**Set down:** the Challenger's cap, for the reason given above.

## Cast grading (`iching-grade`, second of three)

**Two casts changed what a face did.** The Challenger's Bearing put the numbers on the board as the
council's. Without it they would have shipped as a design decision. The Regent's Depths sent a second look at
the spec's own sentences, which found the overclaim in E4. Neither change can be shown to depend on the cast rather
than on the rules already in force. Read the result as weak evidence.
