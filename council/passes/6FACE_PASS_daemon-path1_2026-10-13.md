## Anchor

## The faces

**Shaman (17 Following, to 3 Sprouting).** The reading says the player is now inside what the door opened, and that is the
point of a door from home: the room is safe ground, and the path is where the player follows something down. The Shaman
holds that the figures are presences, not obstacles, so each must feel met before it moves. That is already the rule: a
voice steps aside only when its charge has eased to 3 or below (Big Mind steer, 12 October 22:39). The Shaman asks that the
child at the centre not be asked anything. The child is seen, and the player self arrives beside it.

**Architect (15 Understatement).** What is underplayed here is that nearly every piece exists. The path is one more place in
#309's `LEVEL` table: `daemons: { kind: "path", from: "home", stops: [the seven], ends: "centre" }`, built with the tunnel
engine and `goTo`. Each stop is #301's meeting, and a block in it opens the sit-down that #296 built. What is new is small:
a second arch in the room, a path of seven chambers, the refusal path, the meter and saving carried from `bigmind.js`, and
the centre's child. The Architect builds it stacked on #309, so the two levels share one `goTo` and one room.

**Challenger (64 Almost, to 4 Fog).** Nearly there, and the last stride gets wet: seven full meetings in a row is long, and a
player who stalls at the fourth must not lose the first three. Each daemon passed stays passed, and the path reopens at the
next figure. A daemon left mid-meeting waits, the way `inner-room-lobby` describes. No figure may be skipped
(`cave-daemon-wall`, `fold-not-now`); leaving is always allowed.

**Regent (11 Mingling, to 27 Feeding).** This is working because the rules already agree. The order is ruled

the player self). The Regent sees no rule Wendell has to make.

**Diplomat (50 The Cauldron, to 64 Almost).** The transformation is carried by its handles, the W.A.V.E. steps. When a voice
speaks out of turn at a figure, that is the emergent blocker Wendell names, and `bmw-block` already says where it goes: the
