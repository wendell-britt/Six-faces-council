# Ship reference designs: Gummi Ship, No Man's Sky, Galactic Starcruiser

Gaps are flagged. NMS Fandom wiki returned HTTP 402, so NMS figures come from search snippets and forum posts (lower confidence).

## 1. Kingdom Hearts Gummi Ship

**Acquisition.**
- KH1: blocks come from shops, treasure chests and destroyed enemy ships. Enemies may also drop a blueprint. [khwiki Gummi Ship](https://www.khwiki.com/Gummi_Ship)
- KH1 blueprints also come from travelling between worlds, talking to certain characters, finding Warp Holes and finishing Gummi Missions. Each world's set of three missions pays out blueprints or blocks. [Gamer Guides KH1.5](https://gamerguides.com/kingdom-hearts-hd-15-remix/guide/kingdom-hearts-final-mix/side-quests/gummi-ship)
- KH2: blocks come only from Gummi Missions, plus special blocks for certain enemies or scores. Blueprints are awarded only by specific missions. [khwiki](https://www.khwiki.com/Gummi_Ship)
- KH3: the editor opens from the world map, and you can edit Samples/Specials blueprints or build from Originals. Regular blueprints are sold in the Moogle shop. Cheaper blueprints come from photographing constellations in flight. Builds cost munny plus Gummi materials, which enemies and treasure spheres drop. [GoSunoob](https://www.gosunoob.com/kingdom-hearts-3/gummi-ship-customization-materials-blueprints/)

**Editor constraints (KH1).**
- Every ship needs a cockpit and an engine. [Gamer Guides](https://gamerguides.com/kingdom-hearts-hd-15-remix/guide/kingdom-hearts-final-mix/side-quests/gummi-ship)
- The build area starts small. SYS UP1 gives 8x8x8 and SYS UP2 gives 10x10x10. [Gamer Guides](https://gamerguides.com/kingdom-hearts-hd-15-remix/guide/kingdom-hearts-final-mix/side-quests/gummi-ship)
- COM upgrades raise the limits (same page):

| Level | Saved blueprints | Max blocks | Power level |
|---|---|---|---|
| COM LV1 | 1 | 100 | 3 |
| COM LV2 | 5 | 150 | 5 |
| COM LV3 | 10 | 200 | 8 |

- Individual block types have per-type maximums, for example Fire-G max 6. Navi-G pieces are key items and do not count toward the block limit. [Fire-G / Navi-G wiki pages via search](https://khwiki.com/Navi-G)
- KH2 puts weapons and engines in a separate "special" slot embedded in generic blocks. Ships carry a limited number of abilities and up to two Teeny Ships. [khwiki](https://www.khwiki.com/Gummi_Ship)
- KH3 has no hard cap in the sources I found, only cost. One guide warns it "can quickly spiral out of control and end up costing too much." [GoSunoob](https://www.gosunoob.com/kingdom-hearts-3/gummi-ship-customization-materials-blueprints/)
- Weight and balance are not mentioned in any source I found. Treat as unverified.

**Travel link.**
- KH1: you fly each route until you earn that world's Warp-G, which allows direct warping afterward. Routes contain Heartless ships, Warp Holes that detour, and Monstro can swallow the ship. [khwiki](https://www.khwiki.com/Gummi_Ship)
- KH2: finishing the simplest version of a Gummi Mission unlocks a world for warping. Missions are scored on medals, kills and points. [khwiki](https://www.khwiki.com/Gummi_Ship)
- KH3: travel splits into open-ish exploration and a larger combat phase. [Wikipedia KH3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_Hearts_III)
- Worlds unlock with story progress. KH1 ties upgrades to story beats: Palette and SYS UP2 unlock after Riku's second fight, SYS UP1 and COM LV3 after locking Agrabah's keyhole. [Gamer Guides](https://gamerguides.com/kingdom-hearts-hd-15-remix/guide/kingdom-hearts-final-mix/side-quests/gummi-ship)

**Loved and hated.**
- Hated: one reviewer called the KH1 Gummi Garage tedious because of cumbersome controls and stopped customizing after one try. Others called the flight sections repetitive and skippable once warp points unlocked. [Gaming Pastime](https://gamingpastime.com/kingdom-hearts-ps4-review/), [Push Square](https://www.pushsquare.com/features/i-played-kingdom-hearts-for-the-very-first-time-heres-what-i-liked-and-what-i-didnt)
- KH3 response: the Gummi Ship was a common criticism of earlier games, so the team hired developers of the shooter Einhander and driving-game engineers. Co-director Tai Yasue promised more customization options. [Wikipedia KH3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_Hearts_III)
- KH3 still drew criticism. Game Informer panned the Gummi mode and GamesRadar called it "bland". [Wikipedia KH3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_Hearts_III)
- Loved: the sources I found give no direct evidence of what players loved. That is a gap.
- Blueprint sharing: no source found. KH3 sharing is unverified. Do not cite.

## 2. No Man's Sky

**Timeline.**
- Foundation (Nov 2016) added modular bases and customizable freighters. Frontiers (Sep 2021) added settlements. Next (Jul 2018) added fleets of ships. Living Ship (Feb 2020) added sentient ships. Synthesis (Nov 2019) added starship upgrading. [Wikipedia NMS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No_Man%27s_Sky)
- The galaxy is generated from a single seed by deterministic algorithms, so every player sees the same place. [Wikipedia NMS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No_Man%27s_Sky)
- Players craft upgrades to suit, multitool and ship from blueprints plus resources. Bought ships and multitools have more slots. [Wikipedia NMS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No_Man%27s_Sky)

**Classes and slots (forum-grade, may predate patches).**
- Supercharged slots by class: C has 1, B has 2, A has 3, S has 4. Exosuits always have 3. [Destructoid via search](https://destructoid.com/?p=590998)
- Compatible technology modules sharing an edge on the inventory grid get a percentage boost, and more shared edges give more. A supercharged slot multiplies a module's effect. [Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/275850/discussions/0/3819656934431326801)
- Players debate which beats the other. An optimizer site claims about 25-30% for supercharge, unverified. [NMS Optimizer](https://nms-optimizer.app/)

**Living Ship ("grown, not built").**
- The Void Egg costs 3,200 Quicksilver at the Space Anomaly, and Quicksilver comes mainly from daily community research missions at about 250 each. [PC Gamer via search](https://pcgamer.com/no-mans-sky-void-egg-quicksilver)
- Holding the egg and warping starts "Melody of the Egg". You gather twelve transmissions, each with a glyph, in random order, then enter glyphs into a Portal. [Gameranx](https://gameranx.com/features/id/192653/article/no-mans-sky-how-to-hatch-your-own-living-ship-starbirth-guide/)
- The ship matures through four real-time organ phases plus two final stages. Time-skipping the clock causes bugs. The quest runs once per save, and later ships need a new egg plus 10,000 nanites. [Ginx](https://www.ginx.tv/en/living-ship-starbirth-quest-walkthrough), [PC Gamer](https://pcgamer.com/no-mans-sky-void-egg-quicksilver)
- Detail comes from 2020 guides and may have changed.

**Bases and bounds.**
- Foundation introduced modular bases that recruited aliens can crew. [Wikipedia NMS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No_Man%27s_Sky)
- Forum posts give a 3,000-part cap per base, which also matters for uploading to servers. The save-wide total is disputed between 16,000 and 20,000. A 300-unit radius around the base computer is a separate limit. [Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/275850/discussions/0/1629663905410014127)
- Players work around the cap with several nearby base computers. [Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/275850/discussions/0/1629663905410014127)
- I found no source on base power in this pass. Freighter bases, settlements and Exocraft customization are named in the Wikipedia timeline only.

## 3. "Starship cruiser"

**Reading used:** Star Wars: Galactic Starcruiser (Disney "Halcyon").
- **Other referents, low relevance:**
  - A 1977 Ramtek arcade game, Star Cruiser, a one-on-one ship duel. [Arcade Museum](https://arcade-museum.com/Videogame/star-cruiser)
  - Starship Creator Warp II (1999), a Star Trek game where you design a ship and send it on missions. [search result summary](https://www.entertainmentearth.com/product/star-trek-starships-jemhadar-cruiser-vehicle-with-collector-magazine/dc629886) (weak source)

**Design.**
- Guests picked a storyline: Resistance, Smuggler or First Order. It was set between The Last Jedi and The Rise of Skywalker. Bridge piloting became "Bridge Ops" training. Lightsaber training was kept, but duels were cut. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Wars:_Galactic_Starcruiser)
- The GDC panel called it a "living-theater game" and compared it to a single-player RPG with MMO elements. The Datapad app records choices that shape each guest's story. Each cruise had at least 50 players over two nights. [Laughing Place](https://www.laughingplace.com/w/news/2022/03/28/star-wars-galactic-starcruiser-gdc/)
- The design started from the finale, a live stunt sequence every guest sees, and worked backward through the paths. [Laughing Place](https://www.laughingplace.com/w/news/2022/03/28/star-wars-galactic-starcruiser-gdc/)
- Imagineers said guests have individual and group agency within fixed endings, so paths branch and converge. Real guests revealed things playtesting missed. [Laughing Place search summary](https://www.laughingplace.com/w/news/2022/03/28/star-wars-galactic-starcruiser-gdc/)
- Players who engaged the First Order villain Lt. Croy got a shout-out by name in the Day 2 finale. [Theme Park Insider via search](https://www.themeparkinsider.com/flume/202202/8806/)

**Why it closed.**
- Minimum rate was $4,800 for two for two nights. It had 100 rooms, with build cost estimates of $350M to $1B. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Wars:_Galactic_Starcruiser)
- Disney announced closure in May 2023. The last voyage was Sept 28 and the hotel closed Sept 30, 2023. Josh D'Amaro said it "didn't perform how we wanted it to." Write-down was about $250M. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Wars:_Galactic_Starcruiser)
- Critics cited price and a single storyline that discouraged repeat visits. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Wars:_Galactic_Starcruiser)

**Praise.**
- Polygon called it "something of a dream come true for a Star Wars fan" and praised the story arc. It won a Thea Award in Nov 2022. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Wars:_Galactic_Starcruiser)
- "Being known by name" is supported only by the Croy shout-out above. "Choices mattered" rests on the design intent in the GDC panel, not on guest testimony.

## Design patterns worth stealing

- **Unlock capacity with story beats.** KH1's COM and SYS upgrades arrive with specific boss and keyhole milestones, so growth and plot move together. [Gamer Guides](https://gamerguides.com/kingdom-hearts-hd-15-remix/guide/kingdom-hearts-final-mix/side-quests/gummi-ship)
- **Hard minimum parts, free everything else.** "Cockpit and engine" is the only gate in KH1, and wings, weapons and decoration are optional. [Gamer Guides](https://gamerguides.com/kingdom-hearts-hd-15-remix/guide/kingdom-hearts-final-mix/side-quests/gummi-ship)
- **Several caps at once.** KH1 stacks block count, power level, saved blueprints and per-type maximums. [Gamer Guides](https://gamerguides.com/kingdom-hearts-hd-15-remix/guide/kingdom-hearts-final-mix/side-quests/gummi-ship)
- **Cheap path beside the costly path.** KH3 blueprints can be bought or earned by finding constellations, so exploration substitutes for money. [GoSunoob](https://www.gosunoob.com/kingdom-hearts-3/gummi-ship-customization-materials-blueprints/)
- **Premade samples to edit.** KH3 offers Samples and Specials as starting points, so newcomers skip the blank grid. [GoSunoob](https://www.gosunoob.com/kingdom-hearts-3/gummi-ship-customization-materials-blueprints/)
- **Route cleared once, then warp.** The KH1 Warp-G rewards a first crossing with free later travel. [khwiki](https://www.khwiki.com/Gummi_Ship)
- **Adjacency and supercharged slots.** NMS makes layout itself the puzzle: where a module sits matters, not just what it is. [Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/275850/discussions/0/3819656934431326801)
- **Grown ship via a slow quest.** The Void Egg plus real-time organ phases makes the ship a relationship, not a purchase. [Ginx](https://www.ginx.tv/en/living-ship-starbirth-quest-walkthrough)
- **Quest gated by a soft currency.** Quicksilver (daily missions) paces the egg without blocking progress. [PC Gamer](https://pcgamer.com/no-mans-sky-void-egg-quicksilver)
- **Work backward from a shared finale.** Galactic Starcruiser designed the stunt every guest sees first, then the branching paths into it. [Laughing Place](https://www.laughingplace.com/w/news/2022/03/28/star-wars-galactic-starcruiser-gdc/)
- **A record of choices carried by an app.** The Datapad stores each guest's choices to shape their story. [Laughing Place](https://www.laughingplace.com/w/news/2022/03/28/star-wars-galactic-starcruiser-gdc/)
- **Procedural variety inside one seed.** NMS keeps a deterministic galaxy so everyone shares the same places. [Wikipedia NMS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No_Man%27s_Sky)

## Known failure modes

- **Awkward editor controls.** KH1's Gummi Garage drove a reviewer away after one try. [Gaming Pastime](https://gamingpastime.com/kingdom-hearts-ps4-review/)
- **Travel layer that feels like filler.** KH's flight is called repetitive and skippable once warps unlock. [Push Square](https://www.pushsquare.com/features/i-played-kingdom-hearts-for-the-very-first-time-heres-what-i-liked-and-what-i-didnt)
- **Fixing it once does not fully fix it.** KH3 hired shooter specialists, yet Game Informer and GamesRadar still panned the mode. [Wikipedia KH3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_Hearts_III)
- **Cost spirals in free editors.** KH3 guides warn custom builds can get too expensive. [GoSunoob](https://www.gosunoob.com/kingdom-hearts-3/gummi-ship-customization-materials-blueprints/)
- **Real-time gates break saves.** The Starbirth quest warns that skipping time causes bugs. [PC Gamer](https://pcgamer.com/no-mans-sky-void-egg-quicksilver)
- **Build caps collide with sharing.** NMS bases over 3,000 parts cannot be uploaded, and players route around the cap with several base computers. [Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/275850/discussions/0/1629663905410014127)
- **Single storyline, high price, no replay.** Galactic Starcruiser at $4,800 and one story discouraged repeat visits. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Wars:_Galactic_Starcruiser)
- **App glitches blocking the story.** Reviewers said the Datapad app's glitches kept them from engaging. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Wars:_Galactic_Starcruiser)
