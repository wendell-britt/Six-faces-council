# Spirit look round 17: art the round needed and did not have

Round 17 builds the fifth body family, the Skeptic's (`sg-body-from` A: the urge picks the family), starting with its
first base. The family has no name, no animals and no concept art in the record, so this round sets them as drafts that
stand unless Wendell flips them, and builds the first base from the prompt below (docs/loops.md, "When a round needs
art that does not exist").

- **Family name: the Hedge.** To hedge is to hold back from committing, and the Skeptic is the daemon that holds back
  until it is sure.
- **Bases: llama, hedgehog, Pallas's cat.** Each is an animal that draws back before it trusts: the llama pulls its head
  back on its long neck and turns it aside, the hedgehog draws in behind its spines, the Pallas's cat flattens its ears
  and gives a long sideways look. All three stand on four feet here.
- **The llama comes first**: its long neck carries the Skeptic's pull further than any other body could, and its tall
  curved ears read at 96 px.
- **The Skeptic's movement is pull** (`council/daemons/GESTURES.md`: chest and head drawn back and turned aside, elbows
  pulled behind, hands drawn in to the belly, weight on the back knee). A dissatisfied Hedge holds that pull and pushes
  it further: the neck drawn back over the shoulders, the head turned away and the nose tucked, ears pinned flat, the
  forefeet braced out in front and the weight sunk onto the hind legs, the way a llama refuses the lead rope. A
  satisfied Hedge has let the pull complete: it draws toward what it was unsure of, neck reaching forward, head turned
  toward you, ears up and forward.

The Hedge keeps every spirit's shared pieces: a lidded eye with a dark iris and almost no white, the yin-yang ringed by
the eight trigrams, a cloth wrap, prayer beads with one jade bead and a red tassel. Its own pieces are the narrowed core
and a hand lens on a cord.

## Gas and core (the family's, the same for every base)

- Gas: the motes drawn into a question mark, a hook curling up and back over a short stem, and a dot of motes below it.
- Core: an almond-cut gem shaped like a narrowed eye, two bronze lids closing over it from above and below so only a
  slit shows, the yin-yang and trigrams in the slit.
