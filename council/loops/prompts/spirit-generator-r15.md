# Spirit look round 15: art the round needed and did not have

Round 15 builds the fourth body family, the Controller's (`sg-body-from` A: the urge picks the family), starting with
its first base. The family has no name, no animals and no concept art in the record, so this round sets them as
drafts that stand unless Wendell flips them, and builds the first base from the prompt below (docs/loops.md, "When a
round needs art that does not exist").

- **Family name: the Holdfast.** A holdfast is the grip that keeps hold, and the Controller is the daemon that grasps.
- **Bases: badger, meerkat, koala.** Each is an animal that holds on: the badger keeps its ground and does not let go,
  the meerkat stands sentry, rigid and tall with its chin up, the koala grips with both hands. All three stand on four
  feet here.
- **The badger comes first**: its striped face reads at 96 px, and its stiff, set stance is the Controller's.
- **The Controller's movement is grasp** (`council/daemons/GESTURES.md`: fists clenched and set on the hips, elbows
  out wide, chest and chin up, feet together, rigid and tall). A dissatisfied Holdfast holds that grasp and pushes it
  further: legs locked straight, all four feet drawn in together under it, chest and chin raised high, back stiff, tail
  up, claws dug in. A satisfied Holdfast has let go: low and easy, feet set wide, head level and soft, tail down, claws
  flat. That matches the integration animation, which starts with the Controller's fist opening (bars-engine #351).

The Holdfast keeps every spirit's shared pieces: a lidded eye with a dark iris and almost no white, the yin-yang ringed
by the eight trigrams, a cloth wrap, prayer beads with one jade bead and a red tassel. Its own pieces are the clasped
core and a ring of iron keys.

## Gas and core (the family's, the same for every base)

- Gas: the motes are drawn in tight to a clenched fist, four knuckles in a row along its top, the fingers curled under,
  the thumb laid across them, a short wrist trailing back.
- Core: a faceted gem cut square, gripped by four iron claws that reach round from behind its rim and close over its
  face, the yin-yang and trigrams between them.
