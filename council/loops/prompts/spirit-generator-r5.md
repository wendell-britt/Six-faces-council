# Spirit look round 5: art the round needed and did not have

Round 5 reworks the heads of the Shellback family's three bases after the players' look at round 3 ("the big eyes are
too Disney like and want something a bit more realistic. The mouth is also strange to them"). The tortoise has a
concept, "Taoist Tortoise - Young Adult Concept.png" in the cave studio inbox. The armadillo and the pangolin have none,
so round 5 builds their heads from the descriptions below (rule in docs/loops.md, "When a round needs art that does not
exist"). Each prompt makes one concept sheet in the same style as the tortoise, so the three read as one family.
Drop the results in the cave studio inbox; the next head round builds from them.

## Armadillo sage

> A young adult nine-banded armadillo as a Taoist sage, standing on all fours, three-quarter view facing right, on a
> plain warm off-white background. Painted concept art in the style of a Studio Ghibli character sheet, soft
> watercolour shading, clean ink outline. Realistic armadillo head: long narrow tapering snout with a small dark nose
> pad, a domed bony head shield, tall upright leathery ears, small dark eyes set deep under the head shield with no
> white showing, a closed mouth as a thin seam along the jaw. Eight armour bands across the back between two tiled
> shields, moss and small white flowers growing in the joints, a yin-yang with the eight trigrams worked into the
> shield on its flank. A cream shawl with a green-grey hem, a string of large red-brown prayer beads with one jade
> bead, a red tassel and a bronze gourd hanging from the shell. Calm, wise, a little shy. Leave out any text or lettering.

## Pangolin sage

> A young adult ground pangolin as a Taoist sage, standing on all fours, three-quarter view facing right, on a plain
> warm off-white background. Painted concept art in the style of a Studio Ghibli character sheet, soft watercolour
> shading, clean ink outline. Realistic pangolin head: small cone-shaped head with a long tapering snout, small dark
> eyes set close to the snout with heavy lids and no white showing, a tiny closed mouth at the snout's tip, scales
> starting at the crown and running down the neck. Rows of overlapping olive-bronze scales pointing back, moss and
> small white flowers in the gaps, a long broad scaled tail curling round, big curved front claws. A yin-yang with the
> eight trigrams worked into the scales on its flank. A cream shawl with a green-grey hem, a string of large red-brown
> prayer beads with one jade bead, a red tassel and a bronze gourd. Calm, wise, a little shy. Leave out any text or lettering.

## Tortoise face, close up (optional)

The concept shows the face small. A close-up would pin down the eye and mouth that the players asked for:

> Close-up portrait of the same young adult Taoist tortoise sage's head and neck, side three-quarter view facing right,
> painted concept art, Studio Ghibli style, soft watercolour. Realistic tortoise head: domed skull, a slightly hooked
> beak-like upper jaw overlapping the lower, small nostrils at the snout's tip, a long closed mouth seam curving up a
> little at the corner, small brown eyes set under a heavy upper lid and brow scale with almost no white showing, a
> pale cream throat, dark olive spots on green skin. Leave out any text or lettering.
