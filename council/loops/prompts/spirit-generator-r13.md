# Spirit look round 13: art the round needed and did not have

Round 13 builds the third body family, the Fixer's (`sg-body-from` A: the urge picks the family), starting with its
first base. The family has no name, no animals and no concept art in the record, so this round sets them as drafts
that stand unless Wendell flips them, and builds the first base from the prompt below (docs/loops.md, "When a round
needs art that does not exist").

- **Family name: the Tinker.** The board's loot example already names "a Tinker's claw shard" (`sg-loot-offering`),
  and the Fixer is the daemon that turns a feeling into a problem to solve.
- **Bases: raccoon, beaver, aye-aye.** Each is an animal that fixes with its hands: the raccoon handles and washes
  everything it finds, the beaver builds and patches, the aye-aye taps and probes with one long finger. All three
  stand on four feet.
- **The raccoon comes first**: its mask and ringed tail read at 96 px, and its hands are the Fixer's reach.
- **The Fixer's movement is reach** (`council/daemons/GESTURES.md`: pitched far forward, both long arms out to the
  problem, head up, back leg trailing). A dissatisfied Tinker holds that reach and pushes it further: chest dropped
  low, both forepaws stretched far ahead, head thrust out at the problem, hindquarters high and trailing. A satisfied
  Tinker has finished the reach: it stands square and level, head up, paws back under it, tail lifted.

The Tinker keeps every spirit's shared pieces: a lidded eye with a dark iris and almost no white, the yin-yang ringed
by the eight trigrams, a cloth wrap, prayer beads with one jade bead and a red tassel. Its own pieces are the cog and
the tool roll.

## Gas and core (the family's, the same for every base)

- Gas: the motes stream forward in a long reaching arm that splays into five fingers at its far end, thickest at the
  shoulder.
- Core: a faceted gem cut as a cog, a flat disc ringed with twelve square teeth, the yin-yang and trigrams on its face.
