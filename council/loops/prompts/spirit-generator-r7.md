# Spirit look round 7: art the round needed and did not have

Round 7 puts one nation style over every Shellback stage. The record names the style in one line (`sg-look-layers`:
"Meridia stone and terraces") and the nation in bars-engine `src/lib/nation/nations.ts` ("The Golden Noon. Clarity,
trade, and exchange."). No concept art exists for any nation's look, so round 7 builds Meridia's from the description
below (rule in docs/loops.md, "When a round needs art that does not exist"). Drop the result in the cave studio inbox;
a later round rebuilds the Meridia layer from it, and the same prompt with the nation swapped serves the other four.

## Meridia sage

> The Taoist tortoise sage from the attached concept, met in Meridia, the Golden Noon, a nation of clarity, trade and
> exchange built in sandstone terraces. Same character, same three-quarter view facing right, plain warm off-white
> background, painted concept art in the style of a Studio Ghibli character sheet, soft watercolour shading, clean ink
> outline. Keep its own colours and shell. Add Meridia's regional dress in sandstone and gold: a gold sun crest with
> twelve short rays standing behind its head like noon light; a collar of three stepped sandstone rings at the neck,
> each with a thin gold lip, like terraced hills; a red cord of square-holed gold trade coins hanging from the shell in
> place of a bell; a small terraced sandstone plinth under one foot. Warm noon light from above. Calm, open, a trader's
> easy confidence. Leave out any text or lettering.

Attach "Taoist Tortoise - Young Adult Concept.png" (cave studio inbox) as the character reference.
