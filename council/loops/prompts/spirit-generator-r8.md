# Spirit look round 8: art the round needed and did not have

Round 8 builds the second body family, the Player's Romp (`sg-body-from` A: the urge picks the family, the family has
two or three animal bases), starting with its fox base. The Romp's bases are the fox, the otter and the kid goat. None
has concept art, so round 8 builds the fox from the description below (rule in docs/loops.md, "When a round needs art
that does not exist"), and the otter and kid goat prompts are written now so their art can be made before their round.
Each prompt makes one concept sheet in the same style as the Taoist Tortoise, so the two families read as one world.
Drop the results in the cave studio inbox; the round that builds each base rebuilds it from its sheet.

The Romp is the Player's family: it plays. Where the Shellback is calm and sits, the Romp is mid-bound, ears up,
tail out. It keeps the shared pieces of every spirit: a lidded eye with a dark iris and almost no white, a yin-yang
ringed by the eight trigrams for its core, a cloth wrap, prayer beads with one jade bead, and a red tassel.

## Fox

> A young adult red fox as a playful Taoist wanderer, caught mid-bound with its front paws lifted, three-quarter view
> facing right, on a plain warm off-white background. Painted concept art in the style of a Studio Ghibli character
> sheet, soft watercolour shading, clean ink outline. Realistic fox head: a narrow pointed muzzle with a small black
> nose, tall triangular ears with black backs, amber eyes with dark irises filling the eye and no white showing, a
> white cheek ruff and white bib, black stockings on the legs. One big brush of a tail, longer than the body, with a
> white tip, carried high in a curl. A short cream scarf knotted at the neck with a green-grey hem, a string of large
> red-brown prayer beads with one jade bead, a red tassel, and a small round jade token on a cord at its hip showing a
> yin-yang ringed by the eight trigrams. Mischievous, curious, about to pounce. Leave out any text or lettering.

## How round 8 builds the fox without the sheet

The fox is built from the first prompt: a narrow pointed muzzle, tall pointed ears, a white bib and cheek ruff, dark
stockings and ear backs, and a tail longer than the body with a pale tip, carried high. Its element changes the coat,
the tail and the aura the way it changes the Shellback's shell: fire's tail burns at the tip, water's tail trails a
ribbon of water, wood's ruff and tail carry leaves and small flowers, metal wears engraved plates on its shoulders and
a blade crest on its tail, earth carries amber crystals along its spine. The Romp's gas drifts in a looping trail like
a swishing tail, and its core is cut as a fox's jewel (the onion-tipped star ball, hoshi no tama, that foxes carry in
Japanese folk tales) in place of the Shellback's hexagonal gem.
