# Spirit look round 19: art the round needed and did not have

Round 19 builds the sixth body family, the Victim's (`sg-body-from` A: the urge picks the family), starting with its
first base. The family has no name, no animals and no concept art in the record, so this round sets them as drafts that
stand unless Wendell flips them, and builds the first base from the prompt below (docs/loops.md, "When a round needs
art that does not exist").

- **Family name: the Ballad.** `dmt-next-families` builds the Victim's family as the teller, its urge to be heard. A
  ballad is a sorrow sung so that it is heard, and the honored Victim is the storyteller (GESTURES.md, his note of
  2026-10-17: "They've become the storyteller").
- **Bases: donkey, basset hound, elephant.** Each carries a heavy, hanging body and a voice: the donkey is the beast of
  burden who brays, the basset hound hangs its ears and long face and bays, and the elephant lowers its trunk and
  remembers everything, then trumpets. All three stand on four feet here.
- **The donkey comes first**: its long ears hang out to the sides when it sinks and stand straight up when it brays, so
  its state reads at 96 px, and the burden it carries is the tales.
- **The Victim's movement is yield, collapsed** (`council/daemons/GESTURES.md`: sunk into the knees, back rounded, head
  hung, arms hanging long and heavy). A dissatisfied Ballad holds that collapse and pushes it further: chest and rump
  sunk low on bent legs, the back rounded, the head hung to the ground and turned a little away, the ears hanging out to
  the sides, the tail limp. A satisfied Ballad has let the yield become the story: chest up, head lifted high and turned
  toward you with the nose raised as if braying, ears up and forward, tail swinging out.
- **Its upright ally takes the Victim's HONORED pose** (`dmt-ally-honored`; GESTURES.md, "yield becomes the story"): the
  storyteller, a jester or performer, chest up, one arm swept high presenting the tale, the other palm up, "and then...".

The Ballad keeps every spirit's shared pieces: a lidded eye with a dark iris and almost no white, the yin-yang ringed by
the eight trigrams, a cloth wrap, prayer beads with one jade bead and a red tassel. Its own pieces are the bell core and
a bundle of scrolls tied with a red cord.

## Gas and core (the family's, the same for every base)

- Gas: the motes drawn into a teardrop, point up, and three small dots of motes trailing away from it to the side, as a
  told sorrow trails off: "and then...".
- Core: a teardrop-cut gem, point up, hung in the mouth of a small bronze bell, the yin-yang and trigrams on the gem.

## Donkey

> plain warm off-white background. Painted concept art in the style of a Studio Ghibli character sheet, soft watercolour
> shading, clean ink outline. Realistic donkey: a sturdy barrel body on straight legs with small dark hooves, a short
> thick neck with a short upright dark mane, a long heavy head with a pale mealy muzzle and pale rings round large dark
> eyes with no white showing, very long ears with dark edges and tips, a thin tail ending in a dark tuft. A plain grey-dun
> coat with a dark stripe down the back crossed by a dark bar over the shoulders, a pale belly. A short cream scarf with
> a green-grey hem, a string of large red-brown prayer beads with one jade bead, a red tassel, two rolled scrolls tied
> with a red cord hung at its side, and at its chest a teardrop-cut gem hung in the mouth of a small bronze bell, showing
> a yin-yang ringed by the eight trigrams. Head low and ears drooping out to the sides: it has carried a lot. Leave out
> any text or lettering.
