# Jev asset trial: the mark

Fixed 2026-10-14, before the model has read any row of the scoring pile. Not edited after the run. Ruling `jev-asset-numbers` = C (Wendell, 2026-10-04): the mark is fixed in a dated file, then the model runs once on a separate scored pile.

## What is scored

- Pile: `council/jev/scoring_pile_2026-10-14.json`, frozen 2026-10-14. 19 rows: 2 positives (AS-011, AS-032) and 17 agreeing rows. AS-024 is out (cannot-tell, stale doc line).
- The model reads each row's doc line and the code lines that decide it, without the label or the `why`, and answers `agrees`, `disagrees` or `cannot tell`, with one sentence of reason.
- A flag is `disagrees`. `cannot tell` is not a flag. On a positive row it counts as a miss; on an agreeing row it is reported and is not a false flag.
- The model runs once on the pile. No rerun, no prompt change after seeing results. A failed run (crash, truncated output) is re-run unchanged and said so in the report.
- No injected or constructed positives in the headline. If any are used to test the plumbing, they are labelled injected and kept out of the counts.

## The mark

The trial passes when both hold:

1. **Catches:** the model flags both positives, 2 of 2.
2. **False flags:** the model flags at most 1 of the 17 agreeing rows.

Anything else is a fail, stated as a fail. The mark is not adjusted afterwards for any outcome.

## What the result can and cannot say

Counts with 95% exact (Clopper-Pearson) intervals, no bare percentages:

| Result | Reads as |
|---|---|
| Catches 2 of 2 | true catch rate plausibly 16% to 100% |
| Catches 1 of 2 | plausibly 1% to 99% |
| 0 false flags of 17 | true false-flag rate plausibly 0% to 19% |
| 1 false flag of 17 | plausibly 0.1% to 29% |
| 2 false flags of 17 | plausibly 1.5% to 36% |

Two positives cannot show a catch rate. A pass means: the model found the two known disagreements without burying them in noise. It does not mean the model is reliable on unseen docs. Say so in the report.

## Baseline and report

- The constant-extraction script (ruling `jev-asset-beat-script`) runs on the same 19 rows. The report lists, row by row, what the model flagged that the script did not and what the script flagged that the model did not.
- Reader budget: 19 rows is far under any budget, so it does not bind here. The flags-per-reader-hour figure waits for a larger pile.
- Whether the trial "earns its place" (it must catch something the script misses) is decided from the report on the board, not by this mark.
