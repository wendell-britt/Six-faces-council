# Daemon gestures: the six fundamental movements

Wendell, 2026-10-14 23:35, on the first warp sheet: "they need to be more dramatic and different from each other. They
are quite subtle nods but if you want to create a gestural language we might want to use the building blocks of the
Six Fundamental Movements of contacting as a starting point with the context that the daemons emerge as a way to
protect the vulnerable child through egos development until they need to be renegotiated with and integrated for the
child to move into adulthood. This is essentially one of the things we are gamifying."

## Source

Ruella Frank's developmental movements, from Gestalt therapy's study of contact between infant and caregiver: Ruella
Frank, *Body of Awareness: A Somatic and Developmental Approach to Psychotherapy* (GestaltPress, 2001), and Ruella
Frank and Frances La Barre, *The First Year and the Rest of Your Life: Movement, Development, and Psychotherapeutic
Change* (Routledge, 2011). The six are **yield, push, reach, grasp, pull, release**. Each is a way of making and
leaving contact; each can be held incomplete, and the held form shows in posture. (Cited from memory; not yet checked
against the books' text.)

## The position (stands unless he flips it)

Each daemon wears one movement held stuck in its protective form. It once protected the vulnerable child; held past
its time, it blocks the child's way into adulthood. Integration in play is the movement completing: a push that lands,
a grasp that lets go. The poses are rows of `WARP` in bars-engine `content/ontology-game/cave/character.py`.

| Daemon | Movement held | The body |
|---|---|---|
| Protector | push | a stop: both palms out at shoulder height, fingers up, arms nearly straight, set into the push over a bent front knee (his look, 2026-10-14: the first, straight-armed try "looks like a zombie") |
| Controller | grasp | fists clenched and set on the hips, elbows out wide, chest and chin up, feet together, rigid and tall (his look, 2026-10-16: the fists drawn to the chest "still looks weird") |
| Skeptic | pull | chest and head drawn back and turned aside, elbows pulled behind, hands drawn in to the belly, weight on the back knee |
| Fixer | reach | pitched far forward over a bent front knee, both long arms out to the problem, head up, back leg trailing |
| Victim | yield, collapsed | sunk into the knees, back rounded, head hung, arms hanging long and heavy |
| Damaged Self | release refused | curled forward, arms wrapped round its own middle, head down and turned away, knees drawn in |
| Emotional Body | release too soon | arms flung wide and up, palms open, chest thrown up and head back, up on the front foot |
| Player | all six available | the child's proportions, soft, upright and open, palms turned forward |
