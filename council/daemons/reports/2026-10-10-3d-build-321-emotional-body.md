3. Make "I" an alternation, not a destination. The player steps into the daemon, speaks, then steps back to their own place and hears it said [2]. This is also the way out.
4. Keep a visible way back to "it" at every stage. It is a pause, not a skip, which keeps Wendell's no-skip ruling.
5. Reuse the chat port's before and after charge reading as a hand-on-body dwell in the scene.
