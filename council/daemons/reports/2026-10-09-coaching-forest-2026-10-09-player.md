## The cast

The face cast hexagram 18, Stale Air: "This went off from being left alone." For me, at the Diplomat face, that reads as a warning about the chat-like feel of the current page. Air that sits under a mountain goes stale when nothing moves through it. The forest should move the visitor, so each screen needs a reason to step through and a way out of the room.

## What I want here

I want to arrive somewhere that feels like it was built for me, and I want to walk toward the middle without being sold to on the way. I want the centre to tell me something true about what I came for.

## What the superpower does with it

My cell here is The Connector. For this page, that means the forest does not sort me into a product. It asks one honest question per screen, lets me answer in my own words. The current map asks me to pick a situation, then a body place, then a texture, then a feeling, before anything true is said back. That is a quiz with a sales page at the end. On a phone, three to five screens: forest edge asks what has been stuck; a path, choose the one that fits; a clearing where I write one honest sentence; the centre shows my sentence back and a single door.

## What would make it fun

Let the centre be a reflection rather than a menu: my own sentence in larger type, with the forest light on it, and one quiet door. The price stays off the centre screen. Fun is the feeling that the forest noticed what I said and handed it back better.
