**What I want here:** I want running the studio to be fun, and I want saying what I want to be a normal move at every stage, not only at intake.

**What the superpower does with it:** It burns the charge rather than managing it. The Alchemist keeps one feature in the room and does not open a second.

**What already is the meta-game:**
- The board is the world map. Feature requests play as battles of at most three questions a round (pipeline.yaml, `feature_request`).
- Agent limits are levels. A limit starts low and rises as the record shows useful work and careful token use (faces.yaml, diplomat lesson of 2026-10-03).
- The five moves: one move per daily run, with a level, a limit and a record (pipeline.yaml, `five_moves`).
- The deferral ladder: a deferred item returns by itself, and the pattern of deferrals is data about priority (pipeline.yaml, `defer`).
- Stage owners: intake to close, each stage owned by faces (pipeline.yaml, `stages`). That already has the shape of a film or TV pipeline. Kegan and film stages can be tags on these stages rather than a new structure.

**What to cut:**
- A second scoreboard. The board already scores.
- Kegan labels on people. The council never rates a person (faces.yaml, `standing`).
- Role cards that add meetings. Holacracy should cut handoffs, not add them.
- Anything touching reserved items: money, names, prose, consent (faces.yaml, `reserved`).
- Any session that watches the game itself. The audit finds 84% of spend is rereading context.

**What would make it fun:** Each closed battle prints a one-line receipt on the board: the question answered, the face that moved it, the agent's level change, and the session's cost, read from the platform after the work.
