## The cast
Hexagram 56, Passing Through: a stranger is judged fast. Weigh the first ten seconds above everything and demand proof for each claim.

## The interview
1. How long does a stranger give the page? Usually 10 to 20 seconds; NN/g says "Users often leave Web pages in 10-20 seconds" [1]. Measured.
2. Will they scroll to the booking section? Less each screen: 57% of viewing time above the fold, 74% in the first two screenfuls [2]. The tiers sit at the bottom under a five-station game.
3. Do they trust a stranger's face? Real-person photos drew attention; stock photos were ignored [3]. Attention only, not trust.

## Wake Up
The header carries a booking path. The page has no photo of Wendell and no testimonial. NN/g's trust article was rate-limited, so no trust claims.

## Open Up
One hero line and one button; a one-screen forest entrance; Wendell's photo at the centre; a quiz as the way in; tools folded into the game; a sticky Book link; cut the tool cards; testimonials; fade the game in after a click.

## Clean Up
- Choice overload does not justify cutting the four tiers: mean effect "virtually zero" [4]; overload is likelier with complex sets and uncertain preferences [5]. Four price points of one session are a simple set.
- "One call to action always wins": no source found. Unlinked folklore.
- The cost of many buttons is attention, not proven conversion loss.

## Grow Up
Fast judgement: measured [1]. Attention drops below the fold: measured [2]. Real faces draw attention: measured [3]. Hidden content hurts when the path is unclear: NN/g guidance [6]. Immersive pages, quizzes as lead-in, testimonials: no linkable source; the game is a bet. If the forest hides the booking path, [6] and [1] predict losses.

## Show Up
1. State the value in one sentence on the first screen, with one forest image [1].
2. Put Wendell's real photo in view, not stock [3].
3. Keep one quiet visible booking link at all times; tiers one tap away [6].
4. Move the tool cards behind a fold so booking moves up [2].
5. Run a two-version test on tier clicks and game starts.

## For the Player
Protects a stranger from hunting for the way in and from claims dressed as research.

## Your questions
1. How many visitors a month?
2. Does Wendell have a testimonial he can name and use?

## Where you would overreach
Refusing every claim as unproven until nothing could change, when Wendell's felt sense of the forest is a legitimate design choice.
