## Clean Up
Failures, named:
1. Suppression trap. "Show it the exit on an exhale" with a goal-based verdict is a suppression instruction in costume. Suppression sits near zero effect [1]. Fix: the verdict never rules on the feeling's right to exist. Step "validate" already does that, so the exit means release of the story or action, not of the sensation.
2. Rate-everything-aligned. Nothing in the loop costs the player for "aligned". Fix: make "stay" require a 3-2-1 output, a concrete next action, so the cheap answer is not the easy one.
3. Endless restart. No exit condition exists. Fix below.
4. "Fully metabolized" is unmeasurable. A 0 on a 0-10 scale invites a false zero. Composted: "fully", and the zero target.
Blocker: the restart cap and the "done" definition. Both are undefined.

## Grow Up
Read the Webb meta-analysis behind [1] for who benefits and who does not. Measure per session: rounds used, before/after delta, and the player's own "does this still pull?" yes/no.

## Show Up
1. Define done as one of three: charge down by 2 or more, OR the player says "it can stay with me, it has no job", OR the round cap. Never "zero".
2. Cap at 3 rounds per sitting, then "park it at the door" saves it for later. That stops the phone loop.
3. Reword the exit as "let go of its claim on your next move", not "it is wrong to feel this". The sensation stays validated.
4. Alignment test asks about action ("does acting on this serve the goal?") not about the feeling.
5. Cheapest falsification test: 5 paper runs with Wendell on his own real charges, counting rounds to stop and whether any "exit" felt like pushing down. If any run passes round 3 or feels like suppression, the rules in 1-4 fail and the loop needs redesign. Passing unlocks building the room screen.
