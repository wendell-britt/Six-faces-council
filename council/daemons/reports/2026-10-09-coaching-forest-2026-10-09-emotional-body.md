## The cast
Hexagram 25, Candour, says the next move carries no calculation, and that is the exposure. The forest should ask the visitor for no strategy, only one honest act at a time, and let Wendell show up at the centre with no sales polish.

## The interview
The page today reads the visitor's feeling as a sign (five situations, six places, five textures, five jobs) before they have had it. CoachingMap.tsx has 33 buttons or headings. The weight sits on choosing, not on arriving.

## Wake Up
The first screen makes the visitor diagnose themselves before they have felt anything. Prices and booking doors at every station bring the head's question into the first minute. Natural scenes restore attention through soft fascination [1], which needs few elements, not many.

## Open Up
- Threshold: a dark screen, one line ("Whatever brought you here came with you"), one slow breath the visitor taps to begin.
- Path: three or four screens, each one thing: feel your feet; where is it in you; what is it like.
- Clearing: the feeling named in the visitor's own words, and one line saying that feeling's job.
- Waiting there: Wendell's face or voice, one sentence from him, one door.
- Fast lane: a small "I know what I need" at the threshold.
- Compost: the five-situation menu, the five-station map, the tier list, the free-door lists.

## Clean Up
- The plain lines and the situation sorter are the head explaining the body; remove them from the path.
- Hick's law: decision time grows with the number of options [3]; one act and one forward button per screen.
- Show no price until the clearing, then one door.
- Keep "something else" and "skip" on every step, since a body scan can feel like judgement.

## Grow Up
Measure how many reach the clearing, how many use the fast lane, and where they stop. Read the ritual evidence properly [2].

## Show Up
1. Replace the first screen with a threshold: one line, one breath, one tap, plus "I know what I need".
2. Cut the path to screens with one act each: breath, where it sits (PLACES), what it is like (TEXTURES).
3. Name it at the clearing: one feeling from FEELINGS, its job line, the visitor's own sentence.
4. Put Wendell at the centre: one sentence and one door; show the tier list only on the next tap.
5. Record where visitors leave so the next pass uses numbers.

## For the Player
It protects a stranger from being sorted, priced and explained at before they have felt anything, and makes the way in a walk rather than a shop.
