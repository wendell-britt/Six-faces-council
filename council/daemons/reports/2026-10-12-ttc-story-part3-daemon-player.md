**The cast.** The Shaman's hexagram is 21, Bite: something is lodged, and nothing closes until it is said. Changing lines 1 and 3 give 56, Passing Through: a stranger, judged fast. I take both only as the face gave them. The first fits Tavi's stuck thing, which moves only when someone says it. The second warns that the Player returns as a stranger, so a fast stage read is a judgment unless the Player checks it with the person.

**What I want here.** I want Tavi and the Hall's other people to say the lodged thing and find that it lands, with me in the room when it does.

**What I want from this act's tending.** A real exchange: the person says the unsaid thing, I say it back, and they move. A checklist would not give me that. The extract gives eight stages, each with a right move [1], so the act has eight answers. I want to get it wrong in front of these people and still be trusted afterward.

**What the superpower does with it.** The Alchemist uses the charge instead of managing it: I say the thing while it still burns, and I stay for whatever the saying does. Here that means the stage read is a live act, not a lookup. When Tavi's stage shows, I name what I see and stay in the room with the reaction, even when it is a shutter coming down. The fear is the fuel, so I do not wait for it to pass before I act on the read.

**What would make it fun.** Show the stage in what the person does, never as a label: a shutter coming down, a list of grievances, a hand turned palm-up. Each wrong pick shows the person's reaction, as the Part II walls do [2], and then lets me take a second pick ("step back, don't unsay it"). No score. The extract's partial readings are where playable half-right versions live [1], so keep them. Lines marked inferred in chapters 8, 11 and 13 stay drafts until a playtest shows the person reacts as predicted.

**Show Up.**
1. Write the stage as the person's visible behaviour, one line per stage, before any dialogue.
2. Give each wrong pick a reaction and a second chance, with no score.
3. Let Tavi enter already slipping after a start, so my first job is to learn what he did alone.
4. Show his return to an earlier stage in his own diary words, not as a stage name.
5. Let the founder's walk end before the trail is followed, with no scene proving Tavi's success.

**Protect the Player from.** A verdict on a stage read ("you read him wrong"), which is the stranger's fast judgment. The Fixer's pull to become the one everyone leans on, so the Hall needs me to hold. The Controller's habit of waiting for a perfect read before speaking, since the act has none.

**Where I would overreach.** I would not choose the eight stages, the beats, or the skill ids. Step 2 owns those, and I read only the extract, not the book. I would overreach if I told the Shaman which stage Tavi must end in, or asked for a stage name to appear on screen.

**My question.** Should Tavi's return to an earlier stage appear on screen, or only in his diary? The answer decides whether the act needs an extra scene with him.
