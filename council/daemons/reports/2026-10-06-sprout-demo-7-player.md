## The cast

Hexagram 55, Fullness: "This is at its peak and you are saving some of it." The traditional reading warns that abundance at its height begins to wane, so spend it while it is full. For Acknowledge, that means the moment the name lands is the peak, and the design should spend it fully on the screen instead of rationing it behind a quiz.

## What I want here

I want to pick a move, watch the spirit answer, and feel the name land like a hit. I do not want to be tested on it, and I do not want to read a manual for it.

## What the superpower does with it

The Rule-Changer (Architect) moves the condition so nobody has to be brave twice. Here the rule changes so the player never has to guess a name. Wendell's own words fit: the system generates the spirit in line with the charge the player is feeling.

What makes a battle read as turn-based, and what a non-violent move can carry (my synthesis, with sources where I read them):

1. **Alternating exchange.** I act, then it acts, and both are visible. Slay the Spire shows each foe's plan before you act: "an icon indicating how much damage and how many attacks they're going to do" ([Roy Tang](https://roytang.net/2019/02/slay-the-spire/)). Into the Breach shows every attack before you commit ([games.gg](https://games.gg/into-the-breach/)). Naming can carry this fully: the spirit's turn is the charge it wears, shown as a line, a posture and a feeling icon.
2. **Readable menu with tradeoffs.** Undertale's ACT "opens a sub-menu with one or more possible actions that vary depending on the targeted monster" ([Undertale search summary via Fandom/Konstanz results](https://undertale.fandom.com/wiki/Encounter)). WAVE is already that menu. Each step has a cost or a payoff.
3. **Information that becomes power.** Persona's One More system "makes information valuable" ([MegaTen Wiki](https://megatenwiki.com/wiki/One_More)). Octopath foes carry weaknesses "revealed when the party first encounters that foe," and a Break makes them lose their next turn ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Octopath_Traveler)). Welcome reveals the feeling, and the element stance then pays off. A non-violent move carries this well.
4. **Resource tension.** Octopath's Boost Points are banked and spent ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Octopath_Traveler)). Darkest Dungeon turns stress into a pressure that makes heroes "refuse commands" ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darkest_Dungeon)). Sprout already has HP, and its pressure can carry a nerve or composure meter.
5. **A punctuating hit with feedback.** Persona's reward chain is down, then One More, then All-Out Attack ([MegaTen Wiki](https://megatenwiki.com/wiki/Press_Turn_System)). Acknowledge can supply this beat.
6. **An alternative win.** Undertale: monsters "can typically be Spared only after the player has ACTed one or more times" (same Fandom page). Exhale is the Spare.

## What would make it fun

Make Acknowledge a Break. Name the spirit by pressing the move, and the game then reveals its name. When the name lands, the spirit flinches, loses its next turn, and the Witness line appears. Make the hit bigger when the stance matches the revealed feeling. This is the Persona and Octopath payoff, carried by a name.

## Show Up

1. Show the foe's intent every turn: one icon for its feeling and one short line for what its charge will do.
2. Make Acknowledge the main move. Its power rises if Welcome came first and if the stance matches the feeling.
3. When the name lands, play a full beat: screen flash, the name in large type, the spirit staggered, and its next turn skipped.
4. Keep Welcome and Validate free of HP cost, but let them bank a visible point (Boost style) that Acknowledge or Exhale can spend.
5. After Validate, show the Witness Book entry and the Exhale choice as the "spare" ending, with a distinct victory jingle.

## My question

Should a landed name always skip the spirit's next turn, or only when the stance matches its feeling? The answer decides whether the type chart is a bonus or a gate for Acknowledge.
