## The cast

Hexagram 43 "Unsaid" reads as the tension between charge held back and charge that must express itself anyway. The Alchemist's work is to burn the feeling as fuel and say the unsaid thing while it is still burning, using the heat itself to give the words force.

## What I want here

The Player wants to meet a random daemon encounter on a phone and work with the charge without managing it—to see the daemon, name it, and choose how to work with this emotion right now. Each encounter should feel fresh even in the tenth run because the Player's own move is new.

## What the superpower does with it

The Alchemist takes the charge as fuel and works it through WAVE. Welcome shows what the daemon is (type, element, belief). Acknowledge names it in one sentence while the charge is still moving; the heat gives the words force. Validate adds it to a record (pokedex), making the pattern knowable. Exhale offers release (let the charge go, gain calm) or integrate (burn it for fuel, gain a resource, carry the cost).

## What would make it fun

One concrete change: make release and integration equally satisfying and visibly different. Release brings calm; integration brings clarity or power. The Player should feel both work and choose based on what they need from this charge right now. Repetition becomes play when the Player's response changes each time, not when the daemon does.

## Show Up

1. Tap to encounter: daemon appears on the strip
2. Tap daemon: Welcome reveals its archetype and element
3. Speak its name: Acknowledge—one sentence—in the pokedex
4. Set it in the ring: Validate updates standing with this archetype
5. Exhale: choose release (gain calm) or integrate (gain fuel/resource)

## My question

Should daemon battles focus on teaching WAVE (the four breaths), or should they also surface the five-element wisdom running through Sprout Plot's design? This changes whether each daemon gets a fixed element-emotion pair or whether the Player discovers it through play and sees the cycle emerge from many encounters.
