## The cast
Hexagram 31, Mutuality, changing to 28, Overload: influence begins in stillness, and the forest cannot carry the visitor's whole need on one screen.

## The interview
The crisis note sits at the bottom. go() always smooth-scrolls. Each station click unmounts the pressed button and focus is not moved. The tiers open in new tabs without saying so. The map says "no account and no AI".

## Wake Up
A forest with fewer sections would drop the 988 note and the credits. Motion ignores reduced-motion settings. A keyboard user loses their place after every station. The "no AI" sentence is the promise the Portland audience can check.

## Open Up
A fixed 988 line on every screen; a skip link to the tiers inside the forest; a text-only version; scroll with no animation; the centre as the visitor's own sentence rather than the booking; all four prices on the first screen; art loaded only when asked.

## Clean Up
Compost a centre that is only the booking: it claims to know the visitor and is the funnel shape the Player distrusts. Compost hidden prices and a single route in. Keep 988, the credits, the prices and the no-AI line reachable.

## Grow Up
Test with reduced motion on, keyboard only, a screen reader, a throttled phone, and 375 px, confirming 988 shows without scrolling [1][2][3].

## Show Up
1. 988 in plain text on the first screen.
2. One quiet path to the tiers on the first screen.
3. Wrap motion in prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference; scroll instantly otherwise [1][3].
4. Move focus to each new screen's heading [2].
5. Static art or CSS, no video or sound; credits and the no-AI line in the footer.

## For the Player
Protects a person in distress from a centre that pushes booking, a keyboard user from getting lost, a slow phone from a heavy scene, and a visitor who distrusts AI or funnels.

## Your questions
1. Should the centre be the booking, or a sentence the visitor writes?
2. Should the prices appear on the first screen, or after the first station?

## Where you would overreach
Burying the forest under warnings until no one enters it.
