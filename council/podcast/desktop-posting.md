## What the thread writes for each batch

One handoff file in `/mnt/project-files/podcast/<episode>-handoff.md`, shaped like the ep 2 one: the override rule, the
files on his Mac, a schedule table, the steps per post kind, and every caption whole. It is a prompt he pastes into Claude
on his Mac (Claude desktop app with computer use on, or Claude in Chrome). The thread attaches the file and says in one
line what the Mac needs.

## Rules every handoff carries (from the ep 2 file)

- Claude on Desktop stops before each Schedule or Publish click, shows a screenshot of the finished composer, and clicks
  only after Wendell says "go" for that post. His go on the post is the publishing act.
- It never types a password or approves a login or two-factor prompt, and touches no settings, billing or ads.
- A missing file or a changed screen is reported, not guessed at. A missing cover or clip goes back to the thread to
  re-render.
- X is not in Business Suite: the handoff lists the X captions for him to post by hand.
- Business Suite posts to Pages and linked Instagram accounts, not to a personal Facebook profile. A profile post is a
  separate task in the handoff, done at facebook.com, with the same go rule.

## What the thread checks first (Challenger)

Every `[episode link]` filled or marked "ask Wendell", Instagram captions saying "link in bio", clips and covers named
exactly as on his Mac, no guest family or health detail, and `python3 council/tools/voice_lint.py` on every caption.
Words that go out under his name are his: a thread drafts them from the recording or his own text and never invents a
claim about the show.
