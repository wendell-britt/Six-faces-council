## Round 0, concept (2026-10-24, done)

- Page: https://claude.ai/artifact/KjBp8CUMPrA3AQwRjf47MG (source `/mnt/project-files/play-to-make/open-hatch.html`,
  built by the thread's build.py from a template; stills, their sources and `art-director-review.md` beside it).
- The idea: the onboarding is a door into the face ships, not a school beside the game. On deck after the Chapter 1
  lantern beat, a crew member carrying a crate that glows the player's lantern color steps down through a hatch.
  Following them is the choice (pm-seam-is-hatch). Below, in the stern cabin (Concept), the crew member takes the
  player's lantern apart in the air and the player tunes it; the deck light changes through a skylight
  (pm-remix-first).
- Ladder: Passenger, Stowaway, Deckhand (unpaid play on practice copies), then Crew, Officer, Shipwright =
  artist corps Contractor, Associate, Member (pm-ladder-is-the-corps). Works with no AI (pm-hatch-without-ai).
- Reference board: Mario Maker, Baba Is You, Factorio, Opus Magnum, TIS-100, Townscaper, Cocoon, Viewfinder,
  Roblox Studio, plus Dreams, Kodu and LittleBigPlanet in text. Seam text came from the research worker's own
  knowledge and was not checked against sources.
- Art director's open notes on the stills: still 1's deck is a straight plank box and the hull window echoes round
  6's cutaway; the hatch lid reads as a clam shell; the player is about 40px tall on a phone. Still 2: two shape
  choices are faint, the flicker beads read as fairy lights, the ribs as a birdcage. Robed figures are stiff.
- scout: rung 2 (we have it). The face ships already hold the seven stages as cabins, crew as roles and crates as work.
